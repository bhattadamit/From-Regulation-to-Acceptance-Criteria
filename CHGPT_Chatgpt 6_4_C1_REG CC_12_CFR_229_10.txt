# **Business requirements: Regulation CC next-day availability (12 CFR § 229.10)**

**Initiative:** Implement Regulation CC next-day availability in the Deposit Hold Engine\
**Business domain:** US Retail / Commercial Deposits\
**Role:** Senior Business Analyst\
**Regulatory reference:** 12 CFR Part 229, specifically § 229.10\
**Effective rules baseline:** November 20, 2026

## **1. Business objective**

Implement automated deposit availability rules to ensure that eligible deposits receive next-business-day availability in accordance with Regulation CC § 229.10.

The Deposit Hold Engine must identify qualifying deposits, calculate the applicable availability date, override standard deposit hold schedules where required, and maintain an auditable record of the availability decision.

The solution must prevent the bank from imposing holds that extend beyond the regulatory availability deadline for funds covered by § 229.10.

## **2. Regulatory requirements and business rules**

The engine must support the following next-day availability categories.

| Deposit category                                                  | Regulatory requirement                                                              |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Cash deposited in person to a bank employee                       | Available no later than the first business day following the banking day of deposit |
| Electronic payments (including qualifying ACH and wire transfers) | Available no later than the business day the bank receives the payment              |
| US Treasury checks                                                | Next-business-day availability                                                      |
| US Postal Service money orders                                    | Next-business-day availability, subject to applicable deposit conditions            |
| Federal Reserve Bank checks                                       | Next-business-day availability                                                      |
| Federal Home Loan Bank checks                                     | Next-business-day availability                                                      |
| State and local government checks                                 | Next-business-day availability, subject to applicable deposit conditions            |
| Cashier's, certified and teller's checks                          | Next-business-day availability, subject to applicable deposit conditions            |
| First portion of other check deposits                             | First $275 of aggregate daily check deposits must be available next business day    |

**Important threshold update:** The first $275 threshold reflects the Regulation CC inflation adjustment effective July 1, 2025. The previous threshold was $225.

The engine should distinguish between:

- Funds that qualify for next-day availability under § 229.10.
- Funds subject to the general availability requirements under § 229.12.
- Funds potentially subject to extended holds under other applicable Regulation CC provisions.

## **3. User stories and Gherkin acceptance criteria**

The following stories are organized by functional capability and are intended to be suitable for Jira refinement and development.

### **EPIC 1: Deposit classification and eligibility determination**

**US-001: Identify cash deposits eligible for next-day availability**

**As a** Deposit Operations stakeholder,\
**I want** the Deposit Hold Engine to identify cash deposits received in person by a bank employee,\
**So that** qualifying cash deposits receive next-business-day availability.

**Acceptance Criteria**

```
Feature: Next-day availability for cash deposits

  Scenario: Cash deposited in person at a bank branch
    Given a customer deposits cash into an eligible deposit account
    And the cash is received in person by a bank employee
    When the Deposit Hold Engine processes the deposit
    Then the deposit must be classified as Regulation CC 229.10(a) eligible
    And the availability date must be the first business day
        following the banking day of deposit
    And no standard check hold schedule may extend this deadline

  Scenario: Cash deposit received after the branch cutoff
    Given a customer deposits cash after the bank's established cutoff time
    When the deposit is processed
    Then the engine must determine the applicable banking day
        in accordance with Regulation CC
    And calculate availability based on that banking day

  Scenario: Cash deposited through an ATM
    Given a customer deposits cash through an ATM
    When the Deposit Hold Engine processes the deposit
    Then the engine must not automatically classify the deposit
        as cash received in person by a bank employee
    And the applicable availability rules must be evaluated separately
```

**US-002: Identify electronic payments eligible for availability under Regulation CC**

**As a** Deposit Operations stakeholder,\
**I want** the engine to identify qualifying electronic payments,\
**So that** electronic funds are made available within the required regulatory timeframe.

**Acceptance Criteria**

```
Feature: Electronic payment availability

  Scenario: Qualifying electronic payment received
    Given an eligible deposit account receives an electronic payment
    And the bank has received the payment
    When the Deposit Hold Engine processes the transaction
    Then the transaction must be classified under Regulation CC 229.10(b)
    And the funds must be made available no later than the business day
        the bank receives the payment

  Scenario: Electronic payment received on a non-business day
    Given an electronic payment is received on a day that is not
        a banking business day
    When the engine determines the availability date
    Then the applicable banking day and payment receipt rules
        must be applied
    And the engine must not incorrectly treat the non-business day
        as a business day

  Scenario: Electronic payment incorrectly routed to check hold processing
    Given a transaction is identified as a qualifying electronic payment
    When the Deposit Hold Engine evaluates availability
    Then the transaction must bypass check-based hold schedules
    And the electronic payment availability rule must take precedence
```

**US-003: Identify qualifying next-day check deposits**

**As a** Deposit Operations stakeholder,\
**I want** the engine to recognize checks qualifying for next-day availability,\
**So that** eligible check deposits receive the availability required by § 229.10(c).

**Acceptance Criteria**

```
Feature: Next-day availability for qualifying checks

  Scenario Outline: Qualifying check deposited
    Given a customer deposits a <check_type>
    And the check satisfies the applicable regulatory eligibility conditions
    When the Deposit Hold Engine evaluates the deposit
    Then the deposit must be classified as next-day eligible
    And the applicable funds must be available no later than
        the first business day following the banking day of deposit

    Examples:
      | check_type                  |
      | US Treasury check           |
      | USPS money order            |
      | Federal Reserve Bank check  |
      | Federal Home Loan Bank check|
      | State or local government check |
      | Cashier's check              |
      | Certified check              |
      | Teller's check               |

  Scenario: Qualifying check deposited in person
    Given a qualifying cashier's check is payable to the customer
    And the check is deposited into an account held by the payee
    And the check is deposited in person to a bank employee
    When the Deposit Hold Engine evaluates the deposit
    Then the check must qualify for next-day availability
    And the availability date must be calculated accordingly

  Scenario: Check does not satisfy required eligibility conditions
    Given a deposited check belongs to a next-day check category
    But one or more applicable regulatory eligibility conditions
        are not satisfied
    When the Deposit Hold Engine evaluates the deposit
    Then the engine must not automatically grant next-day eligibility
    And the deposit must be evaluated under the applicable
        alternative Regulation CC availability rules
    And the eligibility failure reason must be recorded
```

**Implementation note:** Check classification must use instrument type, paying bank information, payee information, deposit account ownership, and deposit channel where applicable. Instrument type alone is insufficient to establish eligibility.

### **EPIC 2: First $275 next-day availability**

**US-004: Provide next-day availability for the first $275 of check deposits**

**As a** deposit account holder,\
**I want** the first $275 of eligible daily check deposits to be available on the next business day,\
**So that** I receive the minimum funds availability required by Regulation CC.

**Acceptance Criteria**

```
Feature: First $275 next-day availability

  Scenario: Single check deposit below $275
    Given a customer deposits a check for $200
    And the check is not otherwise eligible for next-day availability
    And the customer is not subject to an applicable regulatory exception
    When the Deposit Hold Engine processes the deposit
    Then $200 must be made available no later than the first
        business day following the banking day of deposit

  Scenario: Single check deposit above $275
    Given a customer deposits a check for $1,000
    And the check is not otherwise eligible for next-day availability
    And the customer is not subject to an applicable regulatory exception
    When the Deposit Hold Engine processes the deposit
    Then $275 must be made available no later than the first
        business day following the banking day of deposit
    And the remaining $725 must be evaluated under applicable
        Regulation CC availability requirements

  Scenario: Multiple check deposits on the same banking day
    Given a customer makes multiple eligible check deposits
    And the aggregate amount of those deposits exceeds $275
    When the Deposit Hold Engine calculates next-day availability
    Then the engine must aggregate the qualifying daily check deposits
    And ensure that at least $275 is available by the next business day
    And must not incorrectly apply a separate $275 allowance
        to each individual check

  Scenario: Multiple check deposits totaling less than $275
    Given a customer deposits three checks totaling $250
    And the checks are not otherwise eligible for next-day availability
    And no applicable regulatory exception applies
    When the Deposit Hold Engine processes the deposits
    Then the entire $250 must be available by the next business day

  Scenario: Customer deposits both qualifying and non-qualifying checks
    Given a customer deposits a qualifying Treasury check for $500
    And deposits an ordinary check for $1,000
    When the Deposit Hold Engine calculates availability
    Then the qualifying Treasury check must receive next-day availability
    And the engine must separately evaluate the $275 minimum
        availability requirement for other applicable check deposits
    And must prevent duplicate counting of the same funds
```

**US-005: Aggregate deposits across multiple transactions and channels**

**As a** Deposit Operations stakeholder,\
**I want** the engine to aggregate eligible deposits across the customer's deposit activity for the banking day,\
**So that** the $275 minimum is applied consistently rather than independently to each transaction.

**Acceptance Criteria**

```
Feature: Daily deposit aggregation

  Scenario: Deposits made at multiple branches
    Given a customer deposits checks at two different branches
    And both deposits occur on the same banking day
    When the engine calculates the first $275 availability
    Then both deposits must be considered in the daily aggregation
    And the first $275 must not be duplicated

  Scenario: Deposits made through multiple channels
    Given a customer deposits checks through a branch and an eligible ATM
    And both deposits are associated with the same banking day
    When the engine calculates next-day availability
    Then eligible deposits must be aggregated appropriately
    And channel-specific regulatory conditions must be applied

  Scenario: Subsequent deposit after initial allocation
    Given the customer has already received the $275 next-day allocation
    And the customer makes another eligible check deposit
    When the engine processes the subsequent deposit
    Then the engine must consider the previous allocation
    And must not grant an additional $275 allocation for the same
        banking day
```

### **EPIC 3: Business-day and availability-date calculation**

**US-006: Calculate the regulatory next-business-day availability date**

**As a** Deposit Operations stakeholder,\
**I want** the engine to calculate next-day availability using the bank's business calendar,\
**So that** availability dates comply with Regulation CC business-day requirements.

**Acceptance Criteria**

```
Feature: Regulatory business-day calculation

  Scenario: Deposit made on Monday
    Given a qualifying deposit is received on Monday
    And Monday is a banking business day
    And Tuesday is a banking business day
    When the engine calculates availability
    Then the availability date must be Tuesday

  Scenario: Deposit made on Friday
    Given a qualifying deposit is received on Friday
    And Saturday and Sunday are not banking business days
    And Monday is a banking business day
    When the engine calculates availability
    Then the availability date must be Monday

  Scenario: Deposit made before a federal holiday
    Given a qualifying deposit is received on Friday
    And Monday is a federal holiday observed by the bank
    When the engine calculates availability
    Then the availability date must be Tuesday

  Scenario: Deposit made on the day before a holiday weekend
    Given a qualifying deposit is received on Thursday
    And Friday is a banking business day
    And Monday is a federal holiday
    When the engine calculates availability
    Then the availability date must be Friday

  Scenario: Deposit received after the established cutoff
    Given a customer makes a deposit after the applicable cutoff time
    When the engine determines the banking day of deposit
    Then the engine must apply the bank's established cutoff rules
        consistent with Regulation CC
    And calculate the availability deadline using the resulting banking day
```

**US-007: Support configurable banking calendars and cutoff times**

**As a** Deposit Operations administrator,\
**I want** the engine to use approved banking calendars and deposit cutoff configurations,\
**So that** availability calculations remain consistent with the bank's operating rules and regulatory requirements.

**Acceptance Criteria**

```
Feature: Banking calendar configuration

  Scenario: Standard banking business day
    Given the bank's business calendar identifies a date as a business day
    When the Deposit Hold Engine calculates availability
    Then the date must be included in the business-day calculation

  Scenario: Weekend exclusion
    Given the date falls on Saturday or Sunday
    When the Deposit Hold Engine calculates availability
    Then the date must not be counted as a business day

  Scenario: Federal holiday exclusion
    Given the date is a federal holiday recognized by the bank
    When the Deposit Hold Engine calculates availability
    Then the date must not be counted as a business day

  Scenario: Calendar configuration update
    Given an authorized administrator updates the banking calendar
    When the updated configuration becomes effective
    Then future availability calculations must use the updated calendar
    And historical availability decisions must remain unchanged

  Scenario: Cutoff configuration update
    Given an authorized administrator updates an applicable deposit cutoff
    When the configuration becomes effective
    Then subsequent deposit transactions must use the new cutoff
    And the configuration change must be auditable
```

### **EPIC 4: Hold calculation and regulatory prioritization**

**US-008: Override standard holds when next-day availability applies**

**As a** Deposit Operations stakeholder,\
**I want** Regulation CC next-day availability rules to take precedence over standard hold schedules,\
**So that** the bank does not unnecessarily delay the release of regulated funds.

**Acceptance Criteria**

```
Feature: Regulatory hold prioritization

  Scenario: Standard hold conflicts with next-day availability
    Given a deposit qualifies for next-day availability
    And the standard deposit hold schedule specifies availability
        after the next business day
    When the Deposit Hold Engine calculates the hold
    Then the regulatory next-day availability rule must take precedence
    And the applicable funds must be available by the regulatory deadline

  Scenario: Standard hold is shorter than regulatory deadline
    Given a deposit qualifies for next-day availability
    And the standard hold schedule releases the funds earlier
        than the regulatory deadline
    When the Deposit Hold Engine calculates availability
    Then the earlier permitted availability date must be retained

  Scenario: Mixed eligible and non-eligible funds
    Given a customer deposits a check for $2,000
    And $275 qualifies for next-day availability under the minimum
        availability requirement
    And the remaining amount is subject to a standard availability schedule
    When the engine calculates the deposit hold
    Then $275 must be available by the next business day
    And the remaining $1,725 must be assigned its applicable
        availability date

  Scenario: Existing hold conflicts with regulatory availability
    Given a deposit has an existing hold
    And a subsequent eligibility determination establishes
        that some funds qualify for next-day availability
    When the engine recalculates the deposit
    Then the applicable funds must be released by the regulatory deadline
    And the hold must be adjusted accordingly
    And the adjustment must be recorded in the audit history
```

**US-009: Apply regulatory exceptions appropriately**

**As a** Deposit Operations stakeholder,\
**I want** the engine to evaluate applicable Regulation CC exceptions,\
**So that** next-day availability rules are applied correctly without overriding legally permitted exceptions.

**Acceptance Criteria**

```
Feature: Regulatory exception evaluation

  Scenario: New account exception applies
    Given a customer account meets the regulatory definition of a new account
    And an applicable Regulation CC exception affects the funds
    When the Deposit Hold Engine evaluates availability
    Then the engine must apply the applicable new account provisions
    And must not automatically grant an otherwise applicable
        minimum availability amount where the exception permits otherwise

  Scenario: No regulatory exception applies
    Given a deposit qualifies for next-day availability
    And no applicable regulatory exception exists
    When the Deposit Hold Engine evaluates the deposit
    Then the next-day availability requirement must be enforced

  Scenario: Exception applies only to part of a deposit
    Given a deposit contains funds subject to different regulatory treatments
    When the engine evaluates the applicable exception
    Then the exception must be applied only to the funds
        for which it is legally permitted
    And unaffected funds must retain their applicable availability treatment

  Scenario: Exception reason is unavailable
    Given a deposit is flagged as potentially subject to a regulatory exception
    But the required exception reason or supporting information is missing
    When the engine evaluates availability
    Then the engine must not silently apply an unsupported exception
    And the transaction must follow the approved exception-handling process
```

**Important:** Exception handling requires a separately governed rules matrix. In particular, new account, large deposit, repeated overdraft, reasonable cause to doubt collectibility, emergency conditions, and other applicable exceptions must not be treated as interchangeable. Their applicability to specific § 229.10 requirements must be validated individually.

### **EPIC 5: Deposit-level availability allocation**

**US-010: Maintain separate availability amounts for a deposit**

**As a** Deposit Operations stakeholder,\
**I want** the engine to maintain separate availability amounts and dates for portions of a deposit,\
**So that** funds subject to different regulatory requirements can be released independently.

**Acceptance Criteria**

```
Feature: Deposit-level funds allocation

  Scenario: Deposit has multiple availability dates
    Given a customer deposits a check for $5,000
    And $275 is subject to next-day availability
    And the remaining $4,725 is subject to a later availability date
    When the engine calculates availability
    Then the deposit must have at least two availability allocations
    And each allocation must have its own amount and availability date
    And the total allocated amount must equal the original deposit amount

  Scenario: Partial release of funds
    Given a deposit has multiple availability allocations
    And the first allocation reaches its availability date
    When the engine executes the availability release
    Then only the matured allocation must be released
    And the remaining amount must retain its applicable hold

  Scenario: Recalculation does not duplicate funds
    Given a deposit has already been allocated into multiple portions
    When the Deposit Hold Engine recalculates availability
    Then the total available and held amounts must reconcile
        to the original deposit amount
    And previously released funds must not be released again
```

**US-011: Support real-time availability determination**

**As a** Deposit Operations stakeholder,\
**I want** the Deposit Hold Engine to calculate availability at deposit-processing time,\
**So that** customers receive accurate availability information without manual intervention.

**Acceptance Criteria**

```
Feature: Real-time availability calculation

  Scenario: Successful next-day availability calculation
    Given a deposit transaction has been received
    And all required deposit attributes are available
    When the engine processes the deposit
    Then the engine must calculate the applicable availability date
    And return the next-day availability amount
    And return the remaining held amount
    And return the applicable regulatory rule identifier

  Scenario: Missing deposit attributes
    Given a deposit transaction is missing a required classification attribute
    When the engine attempts to determine eligibility
    Then the engine must invoke the approved exception-handling process
    And must not incorrectly classify the deposit
    And the processing outcome must be recorded

  Scenario: Duplicate deposit processing request
    Given a deposit has already been processed successfully
    When the same deposit transaction is submitted again
    Then the engine must recognize the duplicate transaction
    And must not create duplicate availability allocations
    And must preserve the original availability decision
```

### **EPIC 6: Audit, reporting and regulatory traceability**

**US-012: Maintain an auditable record of next-day availability decisions**

**As a** Compliance Officer,\
**I want** the engine to record the regulatory rationale for each availability decision,\
**So that** the bank can demonstrate compliance with Regulation CC.

**Acceptance Criteria**

```
Feature: Regulation CC audit trail

  Scenario: Successful next-day availability determination
    Given a deposit qualifies for next-day availability
    When the Deposit Hold Engine processes the deposit
    Then the audit record must capture:
      | Attribute                    |
      | Deposit transaction ID       |
      | Customer account identifier  |
      | Deposit amount               |
      | Deposit date and time        |
      | Deposit channel              |
      | Instrument classification    |
      | Regulatory rule applied      |
      | Next-day eligible amount     |
      | Calculated availability date |
      | Hold amount                  |
      | Hold release date            |
      | Processing timestamp        |

  Scenario: Regulatory exception applied
    Given a deposit is subject to an applicable regulatory exception
    When the engine calculates availability
    Then the audit record must capture the exception category
    And the reason for applying the exception
    And the applicable regulatory reference
    And the resulting availability date

  Scenario: Availability decision is recalculated
    Given an availability decision has already been recorded
    When the engine recalculates availability
    Then the previous decision must remain available for audit
    And the revised decision must be recorded
    And the reason for the change must be captured

  Scenario: Unauthorized modification of regulatory configuration
    Given a user does not have the required administrative permissions
    When the user attempts to modify a regulatory availability rule
    Then the modification must be rejected
    And the attempted action must be logged
```

### **EPIC 7: Regulatory threshold and rule configuration**

**US-014: Configure the $275 next-day availability threshold**

**As a** Deposit Operations administrator,\
**I want** the first $275 threshold to be configurable,\
**So that** the bank can implement regulatory inflation adjustments without requiring application code changes.

**Acceptance Criteria**

```
Feature: Configurable regulatory thresholds

  Scenario: Apply current $275 threshold
    Given the effective Regulation CC threshold is $275
    When an eligible ordinary check deposit is processed
    Then the engine must apply the $275 next-day minimum availability rule

  Scenario: Apply threshold based on effective date
    Given a new regulatory threshold has been approved
    And the new threshold has a future effective date
    When a deposit is processed before that effective date
    Then the existing effective threshold must be applied

  Scenario: Apply updated threshold after effective date
    Given a new regulatory threshold has been approved
    And the effective date has been reached
    When a deposit is processed
    Then the new threshold must be applied

  Scenario: Unauthorized threshold modification
    Given a user does not have regulatory configuration permissions
    When the user attempts to modify the threshold
    Then the system must reject the modification
    And record the attempted change

  Scenario: Threshold change audit
    Given an authorized administrator updates the threshold
    When the change is saved
    Then the system must record the previous value
    And the new value
    And the effective date
    And the administrator identifier
    And the approval reference
```

## **9. Definition of Done**

A user story should not be considered complete until:

- Business rules have been reviewed and approved by Deposit Operations.
- Regulatory interpretation has been approved by Legal/Compliance.
- Gherkin acceptance criteria pass in QA.
- Availability dates are validated against the banking calendar.
- Available and held amounts reconcile with the original deposit.
- Regulatory exceptions are tested.
- Audit records are generated successfully.
- Core banking integration is validated.
- No unauthorized extension of next-day availability deadlines is possible.
- UAT sign-off has been obtained from business stakeholders.

## **10. Senior BA recommendations**

I would highlight five design considerations before handing these stories to engineering.

1\. **Do not hardcode $275.** Store the threshold with an effective date and regulatory source. This makes future inflation adjustments easier to implement.

2\. **Separate eligibility from availability calculation.** One component should determine whether a deposit qualifies under § 229.10; another should calculate the amount and date. This makes the engine easier to test and maintain.

3\. **Make availability allocation amount-based, not transaction-based.** This is especially important for the $275 aggregate daily requirement and deposits involving multiple checks.

4\. **Treat regulatory priority as an explicit engine capability.** A standard product hold schedule should never inadvertently override an applicable regulatory deadline.

5\. **Build regulatory traceability into the decision itself.** Store the rule identifier, rule version, eligibility result, amount, date, and exception rationale with every calculation.

**Regulatory validation note:** These stories are a functional requirements baseline, not a legal opinion. Before implementation sign-off, Legal/Compliance should validate the precise conditions in § 229.10(c), including check eligibility, deposit channels, special deposit slip requirements where applicable, and the interaction with §§ 229.13 and 229.19. In particular, do not assume that every check in a listed instrument category qualifies regardless of how it is deposited.

The most important architectural principle is that the Deposit Hold Engine should calculate **the legally required available amount by the legally required date**, rather than simply assigning one hold period to an entire deposit.
