### Epic: Regulation E Error Resolution (12 CFR § 1005.11) — Card & ACH Disputes Platform

---

### Story 1: Error Classification and Scope Validation

**As a** Dispute Intake Specialist,

**I want** the platform to classify incoming consumer claims against covered Regulation E error types and filter out excluded inquiries,

**So that** only eligible electronic fund transfer (EFT) disputes enter the Reg E resolution workflow.

#### Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Feature: Reg E Error Classification and Scope Filtering

  Scenario Outline: Classify covered Reg E error types
    Given a consumer submits a dispute claim for a card or ACH transaction
    When the claim reason is "<Covered_Error_Reason>"
    Then the system classifies the claim as an eligible Regulation E error
    And routes the claim into the Reg E Error Resolution workflow

    Examples:
      | Covered_Error_Reason                                                                           |
      | Unauthorized electronic fund transfer                                                          |
      | Incorrect electronic fund transfer to or from the consumer's account                           |
      | Omission of an electronic fund transfer from a periodic statement                              |
      | Computational or bookkeeping error made by the financial institution relating to an EFT        |
      | Receipt of an incorrect amount of money from an electronic terminal                            |
      | Electronic fund transfer not identified in accordance with § 1005.9 or § 1005.10(a)            |
      | Request for documentation required by § 1005.9 or § 1005.10(a) or EFT clarification            |

  Scenario Outline: Exclude non-covered inquiries from Reg E error workflow
    Given a consumer contacts the bank regarding their account or an EFT
    When the inquiry type is "<Excluded_Inquiry_Type>"
    Then the system does not classify the inquiry as a Regulation E error
    And routes the request to standard customer service instead of the Reg E workflow

    Examples:
      | Excluded_Inquiry_Type                                            |
      | Routine inquiry about the consumer's account balance             |
      | Request for information for tax or other recordkeeping purposes  |
      | Request for duplicate copies of documentation                    |

```

### Story 2: Notice of Error Intake, Timeliness Validation, and Written Confirmation

**As a** Dispute Intake Specialist,

**I want** the system to validate the completeness and 60-day timeliness of oral or written error notices and manage optional written confirmation requirements,

**So that** the bank complies with notice requirements under § 1005.11(b).

#### Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Feature: Notice of Error Validation and Written Confirmation

  Scenario: Validate required fields on a standard notice of error
    Given a consumer submits an oral or written notice of error under § 1005.11(a)(1)(i)-(vi)
    When the notice enables the bank to identify the consumer's name and account number
    And indicates why the consumer believes an error exists
    And includes to the extent possible the type, date, and amount of the error
    Then the system accepts the notice as complete and logs the receipt timestamp

  Scenario: Validate required fields for a documentation or clarification request
    Given a consumer submits a request for documentation or clarification under § 1005.11(a)(1)(vii)
    When the notice enables the bank to identify the consumer's name and account number
    And indicates the documentation or clarification requested
    Then the system accepts the notice without requiring error type, date, or amount

  Scenario: Validate 60-day timeliness from periodic statement or passbook documentation
    Given an alleged error first appeared on a periodic statement or passbook documentation required by § 1005.9
    When the bank receives the consumer's notice of error no later than 60 calendar days after sending the statement or providing the passbook documentation
    Then the system marks the notice as timely under Regulation E

  Scenario: Validate 60-day timeliness following a documentation or clarification response
    Given the bank previously sent requested documentation or clarification under § 1005.11(a)(1)(vii)
    When the consumer submits a subsequent notice of error based on that information no later than 60 calendar days after the bank sent the information
    Then the system marks the subsequent notice of error as timely under Regulation E

  Scenario: Require written confirmation for an oral notice of error
    Given a consumer provides an oral notice of error
    When the bank's policy requires written confirmation within 10 business days of the oral notice
    Then the system prompts the agent to inform the consumer of the 10-business-day written confirmation requirement
    And provides the address where the written confirmation must be sent
    And sets a 10-business-day tracking timer for receipt of the written confirmation

```

### Story 3: Standard Investigation SLA Calculation and Extended Time Periods

**As a** Dispute Operations Manager,

**I want** the platform to automatically calculate the initial investigation deadline (10 vs. 20 business days) and maximum investigation deadline (45 vs. 90 calendar days) based on transaction and account attributes,

**So that** investigators meet all statutory deadlines under § 1005.11(c)(1)–(3).

#### Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Feature: Regulation E Investigation SLA Calculation

  Scenario: Standard 10-business-day and 45-calendar-day SLA window
    Given a timely notice of error is received on a standard consumer account
    And the disputed EFT is not a point-of-sale debit card transaction
    And the disputed EFT was initiated within a state
    And the disputed EFT did not occur within 30 days after the first deposit to the account
    Then the system sets the initial determination/provisional credit deadline to 10 business days from receipt
    And sets the maximum extended investigation deadline to 45 calendar days from receipt

  Scenario: New account extension (within 30 days of first deposit)
    Given a timely notice of error involves an EFT to or from the account within 30 calendar days after the first deposit to the account was made
    Then the system sets the initial determination/provisional credit deadline to 20 business days from receipt
    And sets the maximum extended investigation deadline to 90 calendar days from receipt

  Scenario Outline: 90-day extended investigation window for foreign or POS debit card transactions
    Given a timely notice of error is received for an account opened more than 30 days prior to the EFT
    When the disputed EFT "<Transaction_Condition>"
    Then the system sets the initial determination/provisional credit deadline to 10 business days from receipt
    And sets the maximum extended investigation deadline to 90 calendar days from receipt

    Examples:
      | Transaction_Condition                             |
      | Was not initiated within a state                  |
      | Resulted from a point-of-sale debit card transaction |

```

### Story 4: Provisional Credit Issuance, Exclusions, and Consumer Notification

**As a** Dispute Investigator,

**I want** the platform to manage provisional credit eligibility, enforce the $50 unauthorized-transfer withholding rule and statutory exceptions, and trigger the 2-business-day notice,

**So that** consumers receive timely provisional funds and disclosures when investigations extend beyond 10 (or 20) business days.

#### Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Feature: Provisional Credit Processing and Consumer Notification

  Scenario: Issue full provisional credit when investigation exceeds initial window
    Given an investigation cannot be completed within 10 business days (or 20 business days for a new account)
    And written confirmation was received (if required)
    And the account is not subject to Regulation T (12 CFR part 220)
    When the system processes provisional credit by the 10th (or 20th) business day from receipt
    Then the system credits the consumer's account for the full amount of the alleged error, including interest where applicable
    And grants the consumer full use of the credited funds during the investigation
    And triggers a consumer notification to be sent within 2 business days stating the amount and date of the provisional crediting

  Scenario: Withhold up to $50 for unauthorized EFT when § 1005.6(a) is satisfied
    Given an investigation into an alleged unauthorized EFT extends beyond the initial 10- or 20-business-day window
    And the bank has a reasonable basis for believing an unauthorized EFT occurred
    And the bank has satisfied the liability conditions of § 1005.6(a)
    When the system calculates the provisional credit amount
    Then the system allows withholding a maximum of $50.00 from the provisionally credited amount

  Scenario: Omit provisional credit when required written confirmation is not received
    Given the bank required written confirmation within 10 business days of an oral notice of error
    And the bank informed the consumer of the requirement and provided the mailing address upon oral notice
    When 10 business days elapse without receipt of written confirmation
    Then the system waives the provisional credit requirement while continuing the investigation up to the applicable 45- or 90-day limit

  Scenario: Omit provisional credit for Regulation T accounts
    Given the alleged error involves an account subject to Regulation T (12 CFR part 220)
    When the investigation extends beyond 10 business days
    Then the system does not require provisional crediting of the account

```

### Story 5: Four-Walls Investigation Scope for Unaffiliated Third-Party Transfers

**As a** Dispute Investigator,

**I want** the platform to identify when an alleged error involves a third-party transfer with no agreement between the bank and the third party,

**So that** I know a review of the bank's own records satisfies the investigation requirement under § 1005.11(c)(4).

#### Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Feature: Investigation Scope for Third-Party Transfers

  Scenario: Satisfy investigation via internal records review for unaffiliated third party
    Given a notice of error concerns an EFT to or from a third party
    And the transfer is not covered by § 1005.14
    And there is no agreement between the bank and the third party for the type of EFT involved
    When the investigator completes a review of the bank's own records regarding the alleged error
    Then the system allows the investigator to close the investigation phase based solely on the internal records review

```

### Story 6: Error Confirmation, Final Credit, and Consumer Results Reporting

**As a** Dispute Investigator,

**I want** the platform to enforce the 1-business-day error correction rule and 3-business-day results reporting rule when an error is confirmed,

**So that** confirmed errors are resolved and communicated within statutory timelines under § 1005.11(c)(1) and (c)(2)(iii)–(iv).

#### Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Feature: Confirmed Error Correction and Reporting

  Scenario: Correct confirmed error within 1 business day (no prior provisional credit)
    Given the bank completes its investigation and determines that the alleged error occurred
    And no provisional credit was previously issued
    When the determination is recorded in the system
    Then the system schedules and posts the account correction within 1 business day of the determination
    And triggers a results report to the consumer within 3 business days after completing the investigation

  Scenario: Finalize provisional credit within 1 business day and report within 3 business days
    Given the bank completes its investigation and determines that the alleged error occurred
    And a provisional credit was previously issued
    When the determination is recorded in the system
    Then the system removes the provisional hold / finalizes the credit within 1 business day of the determination
    And triggers a results report to the consumer within 3 business days after completing the investigation stating that the provisional credit has been made final

```

### Story 7: Denial or Partial Adjustment Procedures and Provisional Credit Reversal

**As a** Dispute Investigator,

**I want** the platform to generate a compliant written explanation, handle document requests, and enforce the 5-business-day overdraft protection period when reversing provisional credit after finding no error or a different error,

**So that** the bank complies with § 1005.11(d).

#### Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Feature: No Error or Different Error Procedures and Provisional Credit Reversal

  Scenario: Generate written explanation and document rights upon finding no error or different error
    Given the bank determines that no error occurred or that an error occurred in a manner or amount different from that described by the consumer
    When the investigation is completed
    Then the system generates a results report within 3 business days that includes a written explanation of the bank's findings
    And includes notice of the consumer's right to request the documents the bank relied on in making its determination

  Scenario: Fulfill consumer request for relied-upon documents
    Given the bank issued a written explanation for finding no error or a different error
    When the consumer requests copies of the documents the bank relied on
    Then the system logs the document request and tracks prompt delivery of the document copies to the consumer

  Scenario: Debit provisional credit and apply 5-business-day check/preauthorized transfer honor period
    Given the bank previously issued a provisional credit
    And subsequently determines that no error occurred or a smaller error occurred
    When the system debits the provisionally credited amount (or the excess portion)
    Then the system sends a notification to the consumer stating the date and amount of the debiting
    And notifies the consumer that the bank will honor checks, drafts, or similar instruments payable to third parties and preauthorized transfers from the account without overdraft charges for 5 business days after the notification
    And configures the core banking system for 5 business days after notification to honor such items without overdraft fees, up to the amount that would have been paid had the provisionally credited funds not been debited

```

### Story 8: Reassertion of Previously Resolved Errors

**As a** Dispute Intake Specialist,

**I want** the platform to flag when a consumer reasserts a previously resolved error and distinguish between closed claims and claims based on newly provided § 1005.11(a)(1)(vii) documentation,

**So that** the bank enforces § 1005.11(e) without denying valid post-clarification disputes.

#### Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Feature: Reassertion of Error Handling

  Scenario: Close reasserted error when bank previously complied with all Reg E requirements
    Given a consumer submits a notice of error that is identical to a previously resolved error
    And the system verifies that the bank fully complied with all § 1005.11 requirements during the prior resolution
    And the new assertion is not based on documentation or clarification newly provided under § 1005.11(a)(1)(vii)
    Then the system flags the claim as a previously resolved reassertion
    And allows the agent to close the claim with no further Regulation E investigation obligations

  Scenario: Accept reasserted error following receipt of requested documentation or clarification
    Given a consumer previously requested documentation or clarification under § 1005.11(a)(1)(vii)
    And the bank sent the requested information
    When the consumer asserts an error based on that information within 60 days of the bank sending it
    Then the system treats the submission as a valid, timely notice of error requiring full § 1005.11 investigation

```
