### Epic: Regulation CC Next-Day Funds Availability Engine (12 CFR § 229.10)

---

### Story 1: Cash Deposit Availability Rules

**As a** Deposit Operations Compliance Officer,

**I want** the deposit hold engine to assign funds availability dates for cash deposits based on whether the deposit was made in person to a bank employee,

**So that** the bank complies with 12 CFR § 229.10(a)(1) and (a)(2).

#### Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Feature: Cash Deposit Availability (12 CFR 229.10(a))

  Scenario: Cash deposit made in person to a depositary bank employee
    Given a customer makes a deposit by cash into an account on a banking day
    And the deposit is made in person to an employee of the depositary bank
    When the deposit hold engine calculates the funds availability date
    Then the funds shall be made available for withdrawal not later than the first business day after the banking day of deposit

  Scenario: Cash deposit not made in person to a depositary bank employee
    Given a customer makes a deposit by cash into an account on a banking day
    And the deposit is not made in person to an employee of the depositary bank
    When the deposit hold engine calculates the funds availability date
    Then the funds shall be made available for withdrawal not later than the second business day after the banking day of deposit

```

### Story 2: Electronic Payment Availability Rules

**As a** Deposit Operations Compliance Officer,

**I want** the deposit hold engine to trigger next-day availability for electronic payments only upon receipt of both finally collected funds and credit instructions,

**So that** the bank complies with 12 CFR § 229.10(b)(1) and (b)(2).

#### Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Feature: Electronic Payment Availability (12 CFR 229.10(b))

  Scenario: Electronic payment received with collected funds and credit information
    Given an electronic payment is sent for deposit into a customer account
    And the bank has received payment in actually and finally collected funds
    And the bank has received information on the account and amount to be credited
    When the deposit hold engine establishes the banking day of receipt and calculates availability
    Then the funds shall be made available for withdrawal not later than the first business day after the banking day on which the electronic payment was received

  Scenario: Electronic payment with partial actually and finally collected funds
    Given an electronic payment is sent for deposit into a customer account
    And the bank has received information on the account and amount to be credited
    And the bank has received only a portion of the payment in actually and finally collected funds
    When the deposit hold engine evaluates the receipt of the electronic payment
    Then the bank shall consider the electronic payment received only to the extent of the actually and finally collected funds
    And only that collected portion shall be scheduled for availability not later than the first business day after the banking day of receipt

  Scenario: Electronic payment missing credit information or collected funds
    Given an electronic payment is initiated for a customer account
    And either actually and finally collected funds have not been received or information on the account and amount to be credited has not been received
    When the deposit hold engine evaluates the payment
    Then the electronic payment shall not be marked as received for next-day availability calculation

```

### Story 3: Unconditional Next-Day Check Availability (U.S. Treasury & On-Us Checks)

**As a** Deposit Operations Compliance Officer,

**I want** the deposit hold engine to assign next-day availability to U.S. Treasury checks payable to the account holder and qualifying "on-us" branch checks regardless of whether they are deposited in person,

**So that** the bank complies with 12 CFR § 229.10(c)(1)(i) and (c)(1)(vi).

#### Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Feature: Unconditional Next-Day Check Availability (12 CFR 229.10(c)(1)(i) & (vi))

  Scenario: U.S. Treasury check deposited into a payee account
    Given a check drawn on the Treasury of the United States is deposited into an account on a banking day
    And the account is held by a payee of the check
    When the deposit hold engine calculates the funds availability date
    Then the funds shall be made available for withdrawal not later than the first business day after the banking day of deposit

  Scenario: Check drawn on and deposited at the same depositary bank within the same state or check processing region
    Given a check is deposited in a branch of the depositary bank on a banking day
    And the check is drawn on the same branch or another branch of the same depositary bank
    And both branches are located in the same state or the same check processing region
    When the deposit hold engine calculates the funds availability date
    Then the funds shall be made available for withdrawal not later than the first business day after the banking day of deposit

```

### Story 5: State/Local Government & Cashier's/Certified/Teller's Checks (Including Special Deposit Slip Rules)

**As a** Deposit Operations Compliance Officer,

**I want** the deposit hold engine to validate payee status, geographic rules, in-person channel, and special deposit slip/envelope requirements for state/local government checks and cashier's, certified, or teller's checks,

**So that** the bank complies with 12 CFR § 229.10(c)(1)(iv), (c)(1)(v), (c)(2), and (c)(3).

#### Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Feature: State/Local Government and Cashier's/Certified/Teller's Check Availability (12 CFR 229.10(c)(1)(iv)-(v), (c)(2) & (c)(3))

  Scenario: State or local government check deposited in person meeting all requirements
    Given a check drawn by a state or a unit of general local government is deposited on a banking day
    And the check is deposited into an account held by a payee of the check
    And the depositary bank is located in the same state that issued the check or the same state as the issuing unit of general local government
    And the deposit is made in person to an employee of the depositary bank
    And any special deposit slip or deposit envelope required by the bank under 12 CFR 229.10(c)(3) was used
    When the deposit hold engine calculates the funds availability date
    Then the funds shall be made available for withdrawal not later than the first business day after the banking day of deposit

  Scenario Outline: Cashier's, certified, or teller's check deposited in person meeting all requirements
    Given a "<check_type>" is deposited on a banking day
    And the check is deposited into an account held by a payee of the check
    And the deposit is made in person to an employee of the depositary bank
    And any special deposit slip or deposit envelope required by the bank under 12 CFR 229.10(c)(3) was used
    When the deposit hold engine calculates the funds availability date
    Then the funds shall be made available for withdrawal not later than the first business day after the banking day of deposit

    Examples:
      | check_type       |
      | cashier's check  |
      | certified check  |
      | teller's check   |

  Scenario: Qualifying state/local government or cashier's/certified/teller's check not deposited in person
    Given a check meets all payee, geographic, and special deposit slip/envelope requirements of 12 CFR 229.10(c)(1)(iv) or (c)(1)(v)
    And the deposit is not made in person to an employee of the depositary bank
    When the deposit hold engine calculates the funds availability date
    Then the funds shall be made available for withdrawal not later than the second business day after the banking day of deposit

  Scenario: Special deposit slip or envelope required by the bank but omitted by the customer
    Given the depositary bank requires a special deposit slip or deposit envelope identifying the check type for state/local government or cashier's/certified/teller's checks
    And the bank has either provided the slip/envelope to customers or informed customers how it may be prepared or obtained and made it reasonably available
    And a customer deposits an otherwise qualifying check without the required special deposit slip or deposit envelope
    When the deposit hold engine evaluates the deposit for 12 CFR 229.10(c)(1)(iv) or (v) eligibility
    Then the check shall not qualify for next-day availability under paragraphs (c)(1)(iv) or (c)(1)(v)

```

### Story 6: Aggregate $275 Next-Day Check Availability Rule

**As a** Deposit Operations Compliance Officer,

**I want** the deposit hold engine to aggregate all non-next-day checks deposited across all accounts of a customer on a single banking day and make the lesser of $275 or the aggregate amount available on the next business day,

**So that** the bank complies with 12 CFR § 229.10(c)(1)(vii).

#### Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Feature: Aggregate $275 Next-Day Check Availability (12 CFR 229.10(c)(1)(vii))

  Scenario: Aggregate non-qualifying checks on one banking day exceed $275 across customer accounts
    Given a customer deposits one or more checks on a single banking day across one or more accounts held by the customer
    And those checks are not otherwise subject to next-day availability under 12 CFR 229.10(c)(1)(i) through (vi)
    And the aggregate amount of those checks deposited on that banking day is greater than $275
    When the deposit hold engine calculates next-day availability for the customer's banking day deposits
    Then exactly $275 of those funds shall be made available for withdrawal not later than the first business day after the banking day of deposit

  Scenario: Aggregate non-qualifying checks on one banking day are $275 or less across customer accounts
    Given a customer deposits one or more checks on a single banking day across one or more accounts held by the customer
    And those checks are not otherwise subject to next-day availability under 12 CFR 229.10(c)(1)(i) through (vi)
    And the aggregate amount of those checks deposited on that banking day is $275 or less
    When the deposit hold engine calculates next-day availability for the customer's banking day deposits
    Then the full aggregate amount of those checks shall be made available for withdrawal not later than the first business day after the banking day of deposit

  Scenario: Checks already receiving next-day availability under (c)(1)(i)-(vi) are excluded from the $275 aggregation
    Given a customer deposits a check qualifying for next-day availability under 12 CFR 229.10(c)(1)(i) through (vi) on a banking day
    And on the same banking day the customer also deposits checks not subject to next-day availability under paragraphs (c)(1)(i) through (vi)
    When the deposit hold engine calculates the $275 next-day availability allowance
    Then the check qualifying under paragraphs (c)(1)(i) through (vi) shall receive full next-day availability independently
    And its dollar amount shall not consume any portion of the $275 allowance applied to the remaining checks

```
