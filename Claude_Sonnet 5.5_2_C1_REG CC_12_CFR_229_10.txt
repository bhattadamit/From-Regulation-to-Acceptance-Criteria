# Regulation CC Next-Day Availability (12 CFR 229.10): User Stories

**Epic:** REGCC-100, Next-Day Funds Availability in the Deposit Hold Engine
**Prepared:** November 20, 2026 | **Status:** Draft for Compliance / Product / Engineering review

## 1. Scope and assumptions

**In scope:** The rules in §229.10: cash deposits (a), electronic payments (b), next-day check categories and the minimum amount (c).

**Out of scope (separate epics):** §229.12 schedules for the remaining funds, §229.13 exception holds, §229.13(g) hold notices, and nonproprietary-ATM extensions (§229.12(f)). Returned-item and reversal handling is also out of scope. This epic must hand off a clean "remaining amount" to those flows.

**Assumptions to validate with Compliance:**
- A1. The minimum amount in §229.10(c)(1)(vii) has been $275 since July 1, 2025. It is a configurable, effective-dated parameter and is never hard-coded.
- A2. The Reg CC definitions of *business day* (not Sat/Sun/federal holiday) and *banking day* (a business day the bank is open for substantially all banking functions) apply.
- A3. The check deposit cutoff is configurable per channel. The regulation does not allow it to be earlier than 2:00 PM local time. Default is 2:00 PM CT, to be confirmed against §229.19(a).
- A4. The per-category conditions for deposits not made in person (§229.10(c)(2)) are encoded as configuration rules. Compliance signs off on the mapping before go-live.
- A5. Where one customer holds several accounts, the $275 minimum is aggregated per customer per banking day (per the regulatory text). The allocation order across accounts is an open question (OQ-1).

## 2. Reference data (configurable)

| Parameter | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Minimum amount (next-day) | $275, effective 2025-07-01 | §229.10(c)(1)(vii) |
| Check cutoff time | 14:00 CT | Bank policy, ≥ 2 PM per §229.19 |
| Federal holiday calendar | Fed-published, loaded annually | §229.2 |

**2026 calendar fixtures used in the examples:** Thanksgiving is Thu 2026-11-26. Christmas is Fri 2026-12-25.

## 3. User stories

### US-01: Business day and banking day determination
**As a** deposit operations analyst, **I want** the engine to determine the banking day of a deposit and the next business day correctly, **so that** every availability date is computed from a single compliant calendar.

*Notes: This is the foundation story. All other stories depend on it.*

```gherkin
@regcc @229.2 @calendar
Feature: Banking day and next business day determination

  Background:
    Given the check deposit cutoff is 14:00 CT
    And the federal holiday calendar for 2026 is loaded
    And Thursday 2026-11-26 is a federal holiday

  Scenario Outline: Banking day assignment by receipt time
    When a deposit is received on <received_date> at <received_time> CT
    Then the banking day of deposit is <banking_day>
    And the next business day is <next_business_day>

    Examples:
      | received_date | received_time | banking_day | next_business_day | note                          |
      | 2026-11-18    | 10:15         | 2026-11-18  | 2026-11-19        | normal weekday                |
      | 2026-11-18    | 14:00         | 2026-11-18  | 2026-11-19        | at cutoff counts as on time   |
      | 2026-11-18    | 14:01         | 2026-11-19  | 2026-11-20        | after cutoff rolls forward    |
      | 2026-11-20    | 11:00         | 2026-11-20  | 2026-11-23        | Friday skips weekend          |
      | 2026-11-21    | 09:00         | 2026-11-23  | 2026-11-24        | Saturday rolls to Monday      |
      | 2026-11-25    | 10:00         | 2026-11-25  | 2026-11-27        | skips Thanksgiving            |
      | 2026-11-25    | 15:30         | 2026-11-27  | 2026-11-30        | after cutoff, holiday + weekend |
      | 2026-12-24    | 09:00         | 2026-12-24  | 2026-12-28        | Christmas Friday + weekend    |

  Scenario: Holiday calendar is missing for the deposit year
    Given no holiday calendar is loaded for the year of the deposit
    When a deposit is received
    Then the engine does not assign an availability date
    And the deposit is routed to the exception queue "CALENDAR_MISSING"
    And an alert is sent to Deposit Operations
```

### US-02: Cash deposits available next business day (§229.10(a))
**As a** compliance officer, **I want** cash deposited to a customer account to be available by the start of the next business day, **so that** the bank meets the Reg CC cash availability requirement.

```gherkin
@regcc @229.10a @cash
Feature: Next-day availability for cash deposits

  Background:
    Given account "A-1001" is an open transaction account
    And the bank's cash deposit channels are "Teller" and "Proprietary ATM"

  Scenario: Cash deposited at a teller window
    Given the available balance of "A-1001" is $100.00
    When $500.00 in cash is deposited through "Teller" on 2026-11-18
    Then the ledger balance increases by $500.00 immediately
    And a hold of $500.00 is placed with release date 2026-11-19 at start of business
    And on 2026-11-19 at start of business the available balance is $600.00

  Scenario: Cash deposited on a Friday
    When $200.00 in cash is deposited on Friday 2026-11-20
    Then the hold release date is Monday 2026-11-23

  Scenario: Cash deposited at a proprietary ATM after the ATM cutoff
    Given the ATM cash cutoff is 14:00 CT
    When $300.00 in cash is deposited at a proprietary ATM on 2026-11-18 at 16:45 CT
    Then the banking day of deposit is 2026-11-19
    And the hold release date is 2026-11-20

  Scenario: Cash deposited at a nonproprietary ATM is not handled by this rule
    When cash is deposited at a nonproprietary ATM
    Then the engine routes the item to the extended-hold rules (out of scope for REGCC-100)
```

### US-03: Electronic payments available per §229.10(b)
**As a** payments operations lead, **I want** incoming wire transfers and ACH credits to be made available according to the regulatory timing, **so that** electronic funds are never held longer than allowed.

*Note: The availability offset for wires and ACH credits is parameterized (see A4 and OQ-2). Compliance must confirm the exact timing language before go-live. The scenarios below assume availability is no later than the start of business on the next business day for any credit received after the posting cutoff.*

```gherkin
@regcc @229.10b @electronic
Feature: Availability of wire transfers and ACH credits

  Scenario Outline: Electronic credit availability
    Given account "A-2002" is an open transaction account
    When a <payment_type> credit of $2,500.00 is received for "A-2002" on <received_date> at <received_time> CT
    Then the credit is posted to the ledger balance
    And the funds are available no later than <available_by>

    Examples:
      | payment_type | received_date | received_time | available_by                  |
      | Fedwire      | 2026-11-18    | 10:00         | rule: electronic_wire_offset  |
      | ACH          | 2026-11-18    | 10:00         | rule: electronic_ach_offset   |
      | ACH          | 2026-11-20    | 10:00         | start of business per rule    |

  Scenario: Electronic credit is never held longer than the configured regulatory offset
    Given a risk review flag is raised on an incoming ACH credit
    When the hold engine computes availability
    Then the release date never exceeds the §229.10(b) release date
    And any additional hold requires an approved exception under §229.13 (out of scope)
```

### US-04: Next-day availability for specified check types (§229.10(c)(1)(i)–(vi))
**As a** compliance officer, **I want** U.S. Treasury checks, U.S. Postal Service money orders, Federal Reserve Bank checks, Federal Home Loan Bank checks, state and local government checks, and cashier's, certified and teller's checks to be available the next business day, **so that** the bank does not apply longer holds to checks Reg CC protects.

```gherkin
@regcc @229.10c1 @next-day-checks
Feature: Next-day availability for specified check categories

  Background:
    Given the bank's next-day check category table is loaded
    And account "A-3003" has payee name "Jordan Reyes"

  Scenario Outline: Eligible check deposited in person to a bank employee
    When a <check_type> for $4,000.00 payable to "Jordan Reyes" is deposited in person to a teller on 2026-11-18 at 11:00 CT
    Then the entire $4,000.00 is placed on a hold with release date 2026-11-19
    And the hold reason code is "REGCC_10C1_NEXT_DAY"
    And the minimum-amount rule (US-06) is not applied to this item

    Examples:
      | check_type                    |
      | U.S. Treasury check           |
      | U.S. Postal Service money order |
      | Federal Reserve Bank check    |
      | Federal Home Loan Bank check  |
      | State government check        |
      | Local government check        |
      | Cashier's check               |
      | Certified check               |
      | Teller's check                |

  Scenario: Eligible check that fails the payee-and-account condition
    Given the bank requires the check to be payable to the account holder for next-day treatment
    When a cashier's check payable to "Acme Supplies LLC" is deposited to account "A-3003"
    Then the item is NOT classified as next-day under §229.10(c)(1)
    And the item is treated as a non-next-day check and enters the US-06 aggregation

  Scenario: Unclassifiable instrument type
    When a check is deposited whose instrument type cannot be determined
    Then the engine treats it conservatively as a non-next-day check
    And the item is flagged "TYPE_REVIEW" for operations to reclassify
    And if reclassified as next-day, availability is recalculated to the correct date
```

### US-05: Next-day treatment for deposits not made in person (§229.10(c)(2))
**As a** product owner, **I want** the engine to apply the correct conditions when eligible checks arrive through ATM, mail or remote deposit capture, **so that** channel differences do not produce wrong holds.

*Note: Rules per channel are configuration (see A4).*

```gherkin
@regcc @229.10c2 @channel
Feature: Channel-specific conditions for next-day check categories

  Background:
    Given the channel rules table is loaded and approved by Compliance

  Scenario Outline: Eligible check by channel
    When a U.S. Treasury check for $1,200.00 is deposited through <channel> on 2026-11-18 before cutoff
    Then the next-day eligibility result is <eligible>
    And the hold release date is <release_date>

    Examples:
      | channel            | eligible | release_date            |
      | Teller             | yes      | 2026-11-19              |
      | Proprietary ATM    | per rule | per channel rule        |
      | Mail               | per rule | per channel rule        |
      | Mobile RDC         | per rule | per channel rule        |

  Scenario: Channel rule missing for a new channel
    When a deposit arrives from a channel with no rule configured
    Then the engine applies the most conservative compliant treatment
    And raises an alert "CHANNEL_RULE_MISSING" to Compliance
```

### US-06: Minimum amount of $275 per customer per banking day (§229.10(c)(1)(vii))
**As a** compliance officer, **I want** the first $275 of aggregate non-next-day check deposits made on a banking day to be available the next business day, **so that** customers have access to a minimum amount regardless of any longer hold on the rest.

```gherkin
@regcc @229.10c1vii @minimum-amount
Feature: Minimum amount ($275) next-day availability

  Background:
    Given the minimum amount is configured as $275.00 effective 2025-07-01
    And customer "C-5005" owns accounts "A-5001" and "A-5002"

  Scenario: Single check larger than the minimum
    When customer "C-5005" deposits one personal check for $1,000.00 to "A-5001" on Friday 2026-11-20 at 10:00 CT
    Then $275.00 is released at start of business on Monday 2026-11-23
    And the remaining $725.00 is handed to the schedule engine as "REMAINING_AFTER_MINIMUM"

  Scenario: Single check smaller than the minimum
    When customer "C-5005" deposits one personal check for $150.00 on 2026-11-18
    Then the full $150.00 is released at start of business on 2026-11-19
    And the remaining amount handed to the schedule engine is $0.00

  Scenario: Several checks on the same banking day are aggregated
    When customer "C-5005" deposits personal checks of $100.00, $120.00 and $200.00 on 2026-11-18
    Then the total non-next-day deposits for the banking day are $420.00
    And $275.00 in total is released on 2026-11-19
    And $145.00 is handed to the schedule engine

  Scenario: Aggregation across accounts of the same customer
    When customer "C-5005" deposits a $200.00 check to "A-5001" and a $200.00 check to "A-5002" on 2026-11-18
    Then $275.00 in total is released on 2026-11-19
    And the allocation across accounts follows the configured allocation rule
    And the engine never releases more than $275.00 in total for the banking day

  Scenario: Amount is calculated on the banking day, not the calendar day
    When customer "C-5005" deposits a $100.00 check on 2026-11-18 at 10:00 CT
    And deposits a $300.00 check on 2026-11-18 at 15:00 CT
    Then the first check belongs to banking day 2026-11-18
    And the second check belongs to banking day 2026-11-19
    And each banking day has its own $275.00 minimum

  Scenario: Next-day category checks do not consume the minimum
    When customer "C-5005" deposits a $5,000.00 cashier's check and a $400.00 personal check on 2026-11-18
    Then the cashier's check is released in full on 2026-11-19
    And $275.00 of the personal check is also released on 2026-11-19
    And $125.00 of the personal check is handed to the schedule engine

  Scenario: Threshold change takes effect by date
    Given the minimum amount is $225.00 before 2025-07-01 and $275.00 from 2025-07-01
    When a check is deposited on 2025-06-30
    Then the $225.00 minimum is used
    When a check is deposited on 2025-07-01
    Then the $275.00 minimum is used
```

### US-07: Hand-off of the remaining amount to downstream hold logic
**As a** hold engine developer, **I want** the next-day rules to produce a clear split of each deposit into "released next day" and "remaining" amounts, **so that** §229.12 schedules and §229.13 exceptions can be applied only to the remaining funds.

```gherkin
@regcc @handoff
Feature: Deposit split output contract

  Scenario: Deposit is split into released and remaining components
    Given a deposit of $1,000.00 in non-next-day checks on 2026-11-18
    When the next-day rules finish
    Then the engine emits a split record with:
      | component  | amount   | release_date | rule_code             |
      | released   | $275.00  | 2026-11-19   | REGCC_10C1VII_MIN     |
      | remaining  | $725.00  | pending      | HANDOFF_SCHEDULE_ENGINE |
    And the sum of the components equals the deposit amount

  Scenario: No component is lost or double counted
    Given any deposit processed by the next-day rules
    Then the released amount plus the remaining amount equals the deposit amount to the cent
    And no amount is released more than once

  Scenario: Downstream engine unavailable
    Given the schedule engine cannot be reached
    When the next-day rules finish
    Then the released component is still processed
    And the remaining component is queued for retry with the status "PENDING_DOWNSTREAM"
    And the customer is never given a longer hold on the released component
```

### US-08: Funds release at start of business and available balance update
**As a** customer, **I want** my funds to show as available at the start of the business day on which they are due, **so that** I can withdraw them without delay.

```gherkin
@regcc @availability @release
Feature: Timely release of holds

  Scenario: Scheduled release at start of business
    Given a hold of $275.00 on account "A-6006" with release date 2026-11-19
    When the start-of-business processing for 2026-11-19 runs
    Then the hold is released
    And the available balance increases by $275.00
    And the release timestamp is recorded

  Scenario: Release is not blocked by batch failure
    Given the start-of-business batch for 2026-11-19 fails
    When the failure is detected
    Then the failover process releases all holds due on 2026-11-19 within the configured SLA
    And the incident is logged for Compliance review

  Scenario: Withdrawal of released funds
    Given funds were released at start of business on 2026-11-19
    When the customer attempts to withdraw those funds by ATM, debit, check or transfer
    Then the engine does not block the withdrawal because of a Reg CC hold
```

### US-09: Effective-dated, versioned regulatory parameters
**As a** compliance manager, **I want** Reg CC dollar thresholds and calendars to be configurable with effective dates and approvals, **so that** the next five-year inflation adjustment (July 1, 2030 under the current cycle) can be implemented without a code release.

```gherkin
@regcc @configuration

    Given the current minimum amount is $275.00 effective 2025-07-01
    When Compliance submits a new value effective on a future date
    Then the change requires a second approver (maker-checker)
    And the previous value remains in force until the effective date
    And both versions are retained permanently

  Scenario: Retroactive change is rejected
    When a user submits a parameter version with an effective date in the past
    Then the system rejects the change unless it is approved as a documented correction

  Scenario: Parameter lookup is by deposit date
    Given multiple versions of a parameter exist
    When the engine evaluates a deposit

```

## 4. Non-functional requirements

- **NFR-1 (Accuracy):** The release amount and date must match a Compliance-approved test suite at 100% before go-live.
- **NFR-2 (Timeliness):** Start-of-business releases complete within the configured SLA, with failover (US-08).
- **NFR-3 (Retention):** Decision records are kept per the bank's record-retention schedule, with a minimum of 5 years assumed.
- **NFR-4 (Performance):** The next-day rules add no more than the agreed latency to real-time deposit posting.
- **NFR-5 (Testability):** The business calendar and clock are injectable so that all scenarios above can be automated.

## 6. Open questions

- **OQ-1:** How should the $275 be allocated across multiple accounts or joint holders? Options are deposit order, largest account, or pro rata. Needs a Compliance decision.
- **OQ-2:** Confirm the exact §229.10(b) timing for wire and ACH credits, and the Fed Reserve and ACH posting-time dependencies.
- **OQ-3:** Confirm the per-channel conditions for §229.10(c)(2) deposits (ATM, mail, RDC) and the payee-match requirement in the bank's current Funds Availability Disclosure.
- **OQ-4:** Is the check cutoff the same for all channels? Mobile RDC and ATM cutoffs often differ.
- **OQ-5:** Should the engine recalculate holds for items reclassified after posting, and what customer communication is needed?
