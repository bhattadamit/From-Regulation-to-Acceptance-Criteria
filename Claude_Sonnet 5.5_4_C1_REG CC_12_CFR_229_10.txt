# Reg CC Next-Day Availability (12 CFR 229.10): User Stories for the Deposit Hold Engine

## Scope and assumptions

- **In scope:** Determining next-day availability under §229.10 (cash, electronic payments, and specified check types) and setting the availability date. Also in scope are the interaction with the §229.13 exception holds and the audit trail.
- **Out of scope:** Second-day and local/nonlocal schedules (§229.12), exception-hold decisioning rules (§229.13) beyond the override interaction, and hold notice generation (§229.13(g)). Each of these should be its own epic.
- **Thresholds are configuration, not code.** The dollar amounts below reflect my understanding of the July 2025 inflation adjustment ($275 aggregate daily check amount, $6,725 for new accounts and large deposits). **Compliance must confirm against the current Federal Register notice and Reg CC text before go-live.**
- **"Next business day" means the business day after the banking day of deposit.** Business days are Monday to Friday excluding federal holidays (§229.2(g)). A deposit received after the cutoff is treated as made on the next banking day. Cutoff is configurable, no earlier than 2:00 p.m. local at a branch and no earlier than 12:00 noon at an ATM or other off-premises location.
- **"Available" means funds are available for all uses, including cash withdrawal and paying checks,** with no partial-availability logic inside this epic.

## Configuration parameters

| Parameter | Default (verify) | Reference |
|---|---|---|
| `NEXT_DAY_AGGREGATE_CHECK_MIN` | $275 | §229.10(c)(1)(vii) |
| `NEW_ACCOUNT_NEXT_DAY_CAP` | $6,725 | §229.13(a) |
| `CUTOFF_BRANCH` | 14:00 local | §229.19(a) |
| `CUTOFF_ATM` | 12:00 local | §229.19(a) |
| `HOLIDAY_CALENDAR` | Federal Reserve holiday calendar | §229.2(g) |

## Epic: Next-Day Availability Determination

### Story 1: Business-day and banking-day calculation

**As a** deposit operations analyst
**I want** the hold engine to compute the banking day of deposit and the next business day from a maintained calendar
**So that** every next-day availability date is correct across weekends and federal holidays.

```gherkin
Feature: Banking day and next business day calculation

  Background:
    Given the holiday calendar contains "2026-11-26" as Thanksgiving Day
    And the branch cutoff hour is 14:00 local time

  Scenario Outline: Compute banking day and next-day availability date
    Given a deposit is received on "<receipt_date>" at "<receipt_time>" at a branch
    When the engine computes the banking day of deposit
    Then the banking day of deposit is "<banking_day>"
    And the next-day availability date is "<available_date>"

    Examples:
      | receipt_date | receipt_time | banking_day | available_date | note                          |
      | 2026-11-18   | 10:00        | 2026-11-18  | 2026-11-19     | Wednesday before cutoff       |
      | 2026-11-18   | 15:30        | 2026-11-19  | 2026-11-20     | Wednesday after cutoff        |
      | 2026-11-20   | 10:00        | 2026-11-20  | 2026-11-23     | Friday, skips weekend         |
      | 2026-11-20   | 15:30        | 2026-11-23  | 2026-11-24     | Friday after cutoff           |
      | 2026-11-25   | 10:00        | 2026-11-25  | 2026-11-27     | Skips Thanksgiving            |
      | 2026-11-21   | 09:00        | 2026-11-23  | 2026-11-24     | Saturday receipt              |
      | 2026-11-26   | 09:00        | 2026-11-27  | 2026-11-30     | Holiday receipt               |

  Scenario: Branch not open on a business day
    Given the branch is closed to the public on "2026-12-24" which is not a federal holiday
    When a deposit is received at that branch on "2026-12-24"
    Then the banking day of deposit is the next day the branch is open to the public
```

### Story 2: Cash deposits

**As a** deposit operations analyst
**I want** cash deposits made in person to a bank employee to be available the next business day
**So that** the bank complies with §229.10(a).

```gherkin
Feature: Next-day availability for cash deposits

  Scenario: Cash deposited in person to an employee
    Given an account holder deposits $2,000.00 in cash at a teller window on a business day before cutoff
    When the hold engine evaluates the deposit
    Then the deposit is classified as "CASH_IN_PERSON"
    And the availability rule applied is "REGCC_229.10(a)"
    And the full $2,000.00 is available on the next business day

  Scenario: Cash deposited at an ATM is not in-person-to-employee
    Given an account holder deposits $500.00 in cash at a proprietary ATM
    When the hold engine evaluates the deposit
    Then the deposit is classified as "CASH_ATM"
    And the engine does not apply rule "REGCC_229.10(a)"
    And the deposit is routed to the ATM cash availability rule defined in the §229.12 epic

  Scenario: Cash deposit into a new account
    Given the account was opened 10 business days ago
    And a customer deposits $9,000.00 in cash in person to an employee
    When the hold engine evaluates the deposit
    Then the full $9,000.00 is available on the next business day
    And the new-account exception is not applied to the cash deposit
```

*Note for Compliance: Reg CC permits second-business-day availability for cash taken at an ATM or other non-employee-staffed location (§229.12(d)). Confirm the bank's policy for ATM cash.*

### Story 4: Government and bank-issued checks

**As a** deposit operations analyst
**I want** U.S. Treasury checks, state and local government checks, cashier's, certified, and teller's checks, Federal Reserve Bank and Federal Home Loan Bank checks, and postal money orders to be available the next business day when the conditions of §229.10(c) are met
**So that** the bank complies with the rule for low-risk check types.

```gherkin
Feature: Next-day availability for specified check types

  Scenario Outline: Eligible check type deposited into the payee's account
    Given a check of type "<check_type>" for $<amount>
    And the check is payable to the account holder
    And the check is deposited "<channel>"
    When the hold engine evaluates the deposit
    Then the availability rule applied is "REGCC_229.10(c)"
    And the funds are available on the next business day

    Examples:
      | check_type              | amount   | channel                    |
      | US_TREASURY             | 4000.00  | in person to an employee   |
      | US_TREASURY             | 4000.00  | at an ATM                  |
      | STATE_LOCAL_GOVERNMENT  | 2500.00  | in person to an employee   |
      | CASHIERS_CHECK          | 8000.00  | in person to an employee   |
      | CERTIFIED_CHECK         | 8000.00  | in person to an employee   |
      | TELLERS_CHECK           | 3000.00  | in person to an employee   |
      | FRB_OR_FHLB_CHECK       | 15000.00 | in person to an employee   |
      | USPS_MONEY_ORDER        | 700.00   | in person to an employee   |

  Scenario: Eligible check deposited by someone other than the payee
    Given a cashier's check payable to a third party
    And it is deposited into an account where the account holder is not the payee
    When the hold engine evaluates the deposit
    Then the next-day rule "REGCC_229.10(c)" is not applied
    And the item is routed to the §229.12 availability rules

  Scenario: Eligible check type deposited at an ATM or by mail
    Given a cashier's check payable to the account holder
    And it is deposited at an ATM
    When the hold engine evaluates the deposit
    Then the item is flagged "NEXT_DAY_REQUIRES_EMPLOYEE_DEPOSIT"
    And the engine applies the bank's configured rule for non-employee deposits
    And the rule applied is recorded on the hold record

    Given a state government check
    And the item lacks the information the bank requires to treat it as a state or local government check
    When the hold engine evaluates the deposit
    Then the item is not treated as next-day eligible
    And the item is routed to the §229.12 availability rules
```

*Business rule for Compliance to confirm:* Treasury checks are next-day eligible regardless of deposit channel when deposited into the payee's account. For cashier's, certified, teller's, state/local government, and Federal Reserve/FHLB checks, next-day treatment depends on the deposit method and on payee conditions under §229.10(c). I've encoded this as a configurable channel eligibility matrix and have not hard-coded it.

### Story 6: Aggregate daily next-day amount for other checks

**As a** deposit operations analyst
**I want** the first $275 (configurable) of aggregate check deposits per account per banking day, not otherwise next-day eligible, to be available the next business day
**So that** the bank complies with the minimum-amount provision of §229.10(c)(1)(vii).

```gherkin
Feature: Aggregate daily minimum next-day availability

  Background:
    Given NEXT_DAY_AGGREGATE_CHECK_MIN is $275.00

  Scenario: Single check below the minimum
    Given an account has no other deposits on the banking day
    When a personal check for $200.00 is deposited
    Then $200.00 is available on the next business day

  Scenario: Single check above the minimum
    When a personal check for $1,000.00 is deposited
    Then $275.00 is available on the next business day
    And the remaining $725.00 is routed to the §229.12 availability rules

  Scenario: Multiple checks in one banking day
    Given a personal check for $200.00 is deposited at 10:00
    When a second personal check for $150.00 is deposited at 11:00 on the same banking day
    Then the aggregate next-day amount across both deposits is capped at $275.00
    And $200.00 from the first deposit and $75.00 from the second are available next business day
    And $75.00 of the second deposit is routed to the §229.12 availability rules

  Scenario: Aggregate cap is not consumed by items already next-day eligible
    Given a cashier's check for $5,000.00 qualifies under "REGCC_229.10(c)"
    When a personal check for $250.00 is deposited on the same banking day
    Then the cashier's check does not reduce the $275.00 aggregate allowance
    And $250.00 of the personal check is available on the next business day

  Scenario: Deposits on different banking days
    Given a personal check for $275.00 deposited on Monday
    When another personal check for $275.00 is deposited on Tuesday
    Then each deposit receives its own $275.00 allowance for its banking day
```

*Note: The $275 allowance is separate from, and in addition to, the new-account cap in Story 7. Confirm sequencing with Compliance.*

### Story 8: Exception holds overriding next-day treatment

**As a** compliance officer
**I want** safeguard exceptions to be able to extend next-day availability only for the permitted reasons, with a mandatory reason code
**So that** the bank holds funds only when Reg CC allows.

```gherkin
Feature: Exception hold override of next-day availability

  Scenario Outline: Permitted exception extends the hold
    Given a deposit is eligible for next-day availability
    And the exception "<exception>" is triggered
    When the hold engine evaluates the deposit
    Then the availability date is extended according to the configured exception period
    And the hold record contains reason code "<reason_code>"
    And a hold notice is queued for the notice service

    Examples:
      | exception              | reason_code               |
      | LARGE_DEPOSIT          | EXC_LARGE_DEPOSIT         |
      | REDEPOSITED_CHECK      | EXC_REDEPOSIT             |
      | REPEATED_OVERDRAFT     | EXC_REPEATED_OVERDRAFT    |
      | RECEIPT_OF_NOTICE_DOUBT| EXC_REASONABLE_DOUBT      |
      | EMERGENCY_CONDITIONS   | EXC_EMERGENCY             |

  Scenario: Reasonable-doubt exception without documented basis
    Given a deposit is eligible for next-day availability
    And the "RECEIPT_OF_NOTICE_DOUBT" flag is set with no reason text
    When the hold engine evaluates the deposit
    Then the engine rejects the override
    And the deposit remains available on the next business day
    And an exception is logged for operations review

  Scenario: Exceptions do not apply to ineligible deposit types
    Given an incoming wire for $50,000.00
    When the "LARGE_DEPOSIT" exception is triggered
    Then the engine does not extend the hold on the wire
```

*Note for Compliance: Apply only exceptions Reg CC permits for each deposit type. Cash and electronic payments are generally not subject to the large-deposit or redeposit exceptions. Encode the permitted-exception matrix as configuration.*

## Non-functional requirements

- **Accuracy:** 100% of decisions must pass the regression suite built from the Examples tables above and Compliance-supplied test cases.
- **Performance:** Hold determination returns within the SLA for real-time deposit channels (to be set with the channel owners).
- **Calendar controls:** Holiday calendar changes require maker-checker approval, and the calendar is loaded at least a year ahead.
- **Retention:** Audit records retained per the bank's Reg CC and recordkeeping policy.
- **Testing:** Include boundary tests at the cutoff minute, at exactly $275.00, and at exactly the new-account cap.
