## Epic: Regulation CC Next-Day Funds Availability Engine (12 CFR § 229.10)

### Story 1: Cash Deposit Availability

**As a** Deposit Operations Manager,

**I want** the deposit hold engine to assign availability dates for cash deposits based on whether the deposit was made in person to a bank employee,

**So that** customers receive timely access to cash funds in compliance with 12 CFR § 229.10(a).

#### Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Feature: Cash Deposit Availability (12 CFR § 229.10(a))

  Scenario: Cash deposit made in person to a bank employee
    Given a customer deposits cash into an account on banking day "D"
    And the deposit is made in person to an employee of the depositary bank
    When the deposit hold engine calculates the funds availability date
    Then the funds must be made available for withdrawal no later than business day "D + 1"

  Scenario: Cash deposit not made in person to a bank employee
    Given a customer deposits cash into an account on banking day "D"
    And the deposit is not made in person to an employee of the depositary bank
    When the deposit hold engine calculates the funds availability date
    Then the funds must be made available for withdrawal no later than business day "D + 2"

```

### Story 2: Electronic Payment Availability

**As a** Payment Processing Lead,

**I want** the deposit hold engine to recognize when an electronic payment is legally "received" and make those funds available by the next business day,

**So that** incoming electronic credits comply with 12 CFR § 229.10(b).

#### Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Feature: Electronic Payment Availability (12 CFR § 229.10(b))

  Scenario: Electronic payment received with collected funds and credit instructions
    Given an electronic payment is initiated for deposit into a customer account
    And the bank has received payment in actually and finally collected funds on banking day "D"
    And the bank has received information on the account and amount to be credited on banking day "D"
    When the deposit hold engine processes the receipt of the electronic payment
    Then the electronic payment is recorded as received on banking day "D"
    And the collected funds must be made available for withdrawal no later than business day "D + 1"

  Scenario: Partial receipt of actually and finally collected funds
    Given an electronic payment instruction is for "$1,000.00" with account and amount information received
    And the bank has only received "$600.00" in actually and finally collected funds on banking day "D"
    When the deposit hold engine processes the electronic payment
    Then the bank records receipt of the electronic payment only to the extent of "$600.00"
    And "$600.00" must be made available for withdrawal no later than business day "D + 1"

  Scenario: Collected funds received without account and amount credit information
    Given the bank receives payment in actually and finally collected funds on banking day "D"
    And the bank has not yet received information on the account and amount to be credited
    When the deposit hold engine evaluates the payment status
    Then the electronic payment is not yet considered received under § 229.10(b)(2)
    And next-day availability clock "D + 1" does not start until both conditions are met

```

**I want** checks drawn on the U.S. Treasury to receive next-day availability regardless of deposit channel when deposited into a payee's account,

**So that** government benefit and tax refund deposits comply with 12 CFR § 229.10(c)(1)(i).

#### Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Feature: U.S. Treasury Check Availability (12 CFR § 229.10(c)(1)(i))

  Scenario: U.S. Treasury check deposited into payee's account (any channel)
    Given a check drawn on the Treasury of the United States is deposited on banking day "D"
    And the check is deposited into an account held by a payee of the check
    When the deposit hold engine calculates availability (whether deposited in person or not in person)
    Then the funds must be made available for withdrawal no later than business day "D + 1"

```

### Story 5: State and Local Government Checks, Cashier's, Certified, and Teller's Checks (Including Special Deposit Slip Rules)

**As a** Retail Banking Product Owner,

**I want** the deposit hold engine to evaluate payee ownership, state jurisdiction (for government checks), deposit channel, and special deposit slip usage for state/local government and official bank checks,

**So that** next-day and second-business-day availability rules are enforced per 12 CFR § 229.10(c)(1)(iv)–(v), (c)(2), and (c)(3).

#### Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Feature: Government and Official Bank Checks Availability (12 CFR § 229.10(c)(1)(iv)-(v), (c)(2), & (c)(3))

  Scenario: State or local government check deposited in person with all criteria met
    Given a check drawn by a state or unit of general local government is deposited on banking day "D"
    And the check is deposited into an account held by a payee of the check
    And the depositary bank is located in the same state that issued the check (or same state as the issuing local government)
    And the deposit is made in person to an employee of the depositary bank
    And any required special deposit slip or envelope identifying the check type is used (or not required by the bank)
    When the deposit hold engine calculates the funds availability date
    Then the funds must be made available for withdrawal no later than business day "D + 1"

  Scenario Outline: Cashier's, certified, or teller's check deposited in person with all criteria met
    Given a "<check_type>" is deposited on banking day "D"
    And the check is deposited into an account held by a payee of the check
    And the deposit is made in person to an employee of the depositary bank
    And any required special deposit slip or envelope identifying the check type is used (or not required by the bank)
    When the deposit hold engine calculates the funds availability date
    Then the funds must be made available for withdrawal no later than business day "D + 1"

    Examples:
      | check_type       |
      | cashier's check  |
      | certified check  |
      | teller's check   |

  Scenario: Eligible government or official check NOT deposited in person
    Given a state/local government check (meeting same-state rules) or a cashier's, certified, or teller's check is deposited on banking day "D"
    And the check is deposited into an account held by a payee of the check
    And the deposit is not made in person to an employee of the depositary bank
    When the deposit hold engine calculates the funds availability date
    Then the funds must be made available for withdrawal no later than business day "D + 2"

  Scenario: Special deposit slip prerequisite validation
    Given the depositary bank requires a special deposit slip or deposit envelope for state/local government, cashier's, certified, or teller's checks
    And the bank provides the slip/envelope or informs customers how to prepare/obtain it and makes it reasonably available
    When a customer deposits an otherwise eligible check in person without the required special deposit slip or envelope
    Then the deposit hold engine does not grant next-day availability under § 229.10(c)(1)(iv) or (v)

```


### Story 7: Next-Day Availability of Up to $275 for Non-Qualifying Checks

**As a** Core Banking Product Manager,

**I want** the deposit hold engine to aggregate all check deposits made on a single banking day across all accounts of a customer that do not otherwise qualify for next-day availability, and release the lesser of $275 or the aggregate deposit amount on the next business day,

**So that** customers receive their mandatory $275 next-day cash-out allowance under 12 CFR § 229.10(c)(1)(vii).

#### Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Feature: $275 Next-Day Check Availability Rule (12 CFR § 229.10(c)(1)(vii))

  Scenario: Single non-qualifying check deposit exceeding $275
    Given a customer deposits a single check of "$500.00" on banking day "D"
    And the check is not subject to next-day availability under § 229.10(c)(1)(i) through (vi)
    And the customer makes no other check deposits on banking day "D"
    When the deposit hold engine calculates next-day availability under § 229.10(c)(1)(vii)
    Then "$275.00" (the lesser of $275 or $500.00) must be made available for withdrawal no later than business day "D + 1"

  Scenario: Single non-qualifying check deposit less than $275
    Given a customer deposits a single check of "$180.00" on banking day "D"
    And the check is not subject to next-day availability under § 229.10(c)(1)(i) through (vi)
    And the customer makes no other check deposits on banking day "D"
    When the deposit hold engine calculates next-day availability under § 229.10(c)(1)(vii)
    Then "$180.00" (the lesser of $275 or $180.00) must be made available for withdrawal no later than business day "D + 1"

  Scenario: Multiple non-qualifying check deposits across multiple accounts of the same customer on the same banking day
    Given a customer holds Account A and Account B at the depositary bank
    And on banking day "D", the customer deposits a "$200.00" non-qualifying check into Account A
    And on banking day "D", the customer deposits a "$300.00" non-qualifying check into Account B
    When the deposit hold engine aggregates non-qualifying check deposits across all accounts of the customer for banking day "D"
    Then the aggregate non-qualifying check deposit amount is "$500.00"
    And a total of "$275.00" across the customer's accounts must be made available for withdrawal no later than business day "D + 1"

  Scenario: Mix of qualifying next-day checks and non-qualifying checks on the same banking day
    Given on banking day "D", a customer deposits a "$1,000.00" U.S. Treasury check into their account (qualifying under § 229.10(c)(1)(i))
    And on the same banking day "D", the customer deposits a "$400.00" personal check (not qualifying under § 229.10(c)(1)(i)–(vi))
    When the deposit hold engine calculates next-day availability for banking day "D"
    Then the full "$1,000.00" from the U.S. Treasury check is made available no later than business day "D + 1"
    And an additional "$275.00" from the "$400.00" personal check is made available no later than business day "D + 1"
    And the total amount made available on business day "D + 1" is "$1,275.00"

```
