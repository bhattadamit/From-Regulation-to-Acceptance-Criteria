# **Business Requirements: Regulation CC Next-Day Availability — 12 CFR § 229.10**

**Role:** Senior Business Analyst\
**Business capability:** Deposit Hold Engine\
**Regulatory scope:** 12 CFR § 229.10 — Next-day availability\
**Objective:** Implement deposit availability rules that calculate the required funds availability date based on deposit type, deposit method, qualifying characteristics, and the banking day on which the deposit or payment is received.

## **1. Business rules and interpretation**

The deposit hold engine must support the following availability requirements.

| Deposit type / scenario                                                                                                                         | Required availability                                                           |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Cash deposited in person to a bank employee                                                                                                     | No later than the next business day                                             |
| Cash not deposited in person to a bank employee                                                                                                 | No later than the second business day                                           |
| Electronic payment received                                                                                                                     | No later than the next business day                                             |
| U.S. Treasury check deposited by payee                                                                                                          | No later than the next business day                                             |
| U.S. Postal Service money order deposited in person by payee                                                                                    | No later than the next business day                                             |
| Federal Reserve Bank / Federal Home Loan Bank check deposited in person by payee                                                                | No later than the next business day                                             |
| State or local government check meeting applicable conditions, deposited in person                                                              | No later than the next business day                                             |
| Cashier's, certified, or teller's check meeting applicable conditions, deposited in person                                                      | No later than the next business day                                             |
| Qualifying checks deposited at another branch of the same bank in the same state or check processing region                                     | No later than the next business day                                             |
| Qualifying USPS, Federal Reserve/Federal Home Loan Bank, state/local government, or cashier's/certified/teller's checks not deposited in person | No later than the second business day                                           |
| Eligible portion of other check deposits                                                                                                        | Up to $275 per banking day, subject to the aggregate customer-level calculation |

**Important implementation distinction:** The $275 provision is not a blanket next-day availability amount for every deposit. It is the lesser of $275 or the aggregate amount deposited on a banking day to all accounts of the customer by checks not subject to next-day availability under § 229.10(c)(1)(i)–(vi).

# **2. Epic: Regulation CC Next-Day Availability**

**Epic description**

Implement rules in the deposit hold engine to identify deposits and electronic payments that qualify for next-day or second-business-day availability under 12 CFR § 229.10.

The engine must evaluate deposit characteristics and calculate the applicable availability deadline, ensuring that funds are made available no later than the deadline specified by the regulation.

## **3. User stories and Gherkin acceptance criteria**

### **US-01: Cash deposited in person**

**User story**

As a deposit operations customer,\
I want cash deposited in person to a bank employee to be available by the next business day,\
So that qualifying cash deposits receive the availability required under Regulation CC.

**Regulatory reference:** § 229.10(a)(1)

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Next-day availability for in-person cash deposits

  Scenario: Cash deposited in person to a bank employee
    Given a customer deposits cash into an account
    And the cash deposit is made in person
    And the deposit is made to an employee of the depositary bank
    When the deposit hold engine evaluates availability
    Then the funds must be available for withdrawal
    No later than the business day after the banking day
    On which the cash was deposited
```

### **US-04: Determine when an electronic payment is received**

**User story**

As a deposit operations analyst,\
I want the deposit hold engine to recognize an electronic payment as received only when both collected funds and crediting information have been received,\
So that the availability deadline is calculated using the regulatory definition of receipt.

**Regulatory reference:** § 229.10(b)(2)(i)–(ii)

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Electronic payment receipt determination

  Scenario: Both payment and account information received
    Given the bank has received payment in actually and finally
          collected funds
    And the bank has received information on the account
        and amount to be credited
    When the deposit hold engine evaluates the electronic payment
    Then the electronic payment must be recognized as received

  Scenario: Payment received but account information missing
    Given the bank has received payment in actually and finally
          collected funds
    And the bank has not received information on the account
        and amount to be credited
    When the deposit hold engine evaluates the electronic payment
    Then the electronic payment must not be recognized as received

  Scenario: Account information received but payment not collected
    Given the bank has received information on the account
        and amount to be credited
    And the bank has not received payment in actually and finally
        collected funds
    When the deposit hold engine evaluates the electronic payment
    Then the electronic payment must not be recognized as received

  Scenario: Payment partially received in finally collected funds
    Given the bank has received only a portion of the payment
        in actually and finally collected funds
    And the bank has received information on the account
        and amount to be credited
    When the deposit hold engine evaluates the electronic payment
    Then the bank must recognize receipt only to the extent
         that payment has been received in actually and finally
         collected funds
```

### **US-11: $275 next-day availability for other check deposits**

**User story**

As a deposit operations customer,\
I want the deposit hold engine to calculate the qualifying portion of other check deposits eligible for next-day availability,\
So that customers receive the lesser of $275 or the aggregate amount of qualifying deposits across all their accounts on a banking day.

**Regulatory reference:** § 229.10(c)(1)(vii)

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Next-day availability for qualifying portions
         of other check deposits

  Scenario: Aggregate eligible check deposits exceed $275
    Given a customer deposits checks not subject to next-day
          availability under paragraphs (c)(1)(i) through (vi)
    And the aggregate amount deposited across all customer accounts
        on the banking day exceeds $275
    When the deposit hold engine calculates next-day availability
    Then $275 of the aggregate deposit amount must be available
    No later than the business day after the banking day
    On which the funds were deposited

  Scenario: Aggregate eligible check deposits are below $275
    Given a customer deposits checks not subject to next-day
          availability under paragraphs (c)(1)(i) through (vi)
    And the aggregate amount deposited across all customer accounts
        on the banking day is less than $275
    When the deposit hold engine calculates next-day availability
    Then the entire aggregate amount must be available
    No later than the business day after the banking day
    On which the funds were deposited

  Scenario: Aggregate eligible check deposits equal $275
    Given a customer deposits checks not subject to next-day
          availability under paragraphs (c)(1)(i) through (vi)
    And the aggregate amount deposited across all customer accounts
        on the banking day equals $275
    When the deposit hold engine calculates next-day availability
    Then $275 must be available
    No later than the business day after the banking day
    On which the funds were deposited

  Scenario: Aggregate deposits across multiple accounts
    Given a customer deposits qualifying checks into multiple accounts
    And the accounts belong to the same customer
    And the deposits occur on the same banking day
    When the deposit hold engine calculates next-day availability
    Then the engine must aggregate the qualifying check deposits
         across all accounts of that customer
    And the next-day available amount must be the lesser of
        $275 or the aggregate deposit amount
```

### **US-12: Second-day availability for qualifying checks not deposited in person**

**User story**

As a deposit operations customer,\
I want qualifying USPS money orders, Federal Reserve Bank checks, Federal Home Loan Bank checks, state and local government checks, and cashier's, certified, or teller's checks deposited without in-person delivery to an employee to be available by the second business day,\
So that eligible non-in-person check deposits receive the applicable Regulation CC availability.

**Regulatory reference:** § 229.10(c)(2)

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Second-business-day availability for qualifying
         checks not deposited in person

  Scenario Outline: Qualifying check not deposited in person
    Given a customer deposits a <check_type>
    And the deposit meets the applicable payee requirements
    And the deposit meets the other applicable requirements
        of paragraphs (c)(1)(ii) through (v)
    And the check is not deposited in person
        to an employee of the depositary bank
    When the deposit hold engine evaluates availability
    Then the funds must be available for withdrawal
    No later than the second business day after the banking day
    On which the funds were deposited

    Examples:
      | check_type                       |
      | U.S. Postal Service money order  |
      | Federal Reserve Bank check      |
      | Federal Home Loan Bank check    |
      | State government check         |
      | Local government check         |
      | Cashier's check                 |
      | Certified check                 |
      | Teller's check                  |
```

### **US-13: Special deposit slip or deposit envelope requirements**

**User story**

As a deposit operations administrator,\
I want the deposit hold engine to account for the bank's special deposit slip or envelope requirements for applicable checks,\
So that next-day availability is applied only when the applicable regulatory conditions are satisfied.

**Regulatory reference:** § 229.10(c)(3)(i)–(ii)

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Special deposit slip and deposit envelope requirements

  Scenario: Bank requires special deposit slip for government check
    Given a customer deposits a state or local government check
    And the depositary bank requires a special deposit slip
        or deposit envelope for this check type
    When the deposit hold engine evaluates next-day availability
    Then the deposit must satisfy the applicable special slip
         or envelope requirement before receiving
         next-day availability

  Scenario: Bank requires special deposit slip for cashier's,
            certified, or teller's check
    Given a customer deposits a cashier's, certified, or teller's check
    And the depositary bank requires a special deposit slip
        or deposit envelope for this check type
    When the deposit hold engine evaluates next-day availability
    Then the deposit must satisfy the applicable special slip
         or envelope requirement before receiving
         next-day availability

  Scenario: Bank does not require special deposit slip
    Given a customer deposits an otherwise qualifying check
    And the depositary bank does not require a special deposit slip
        or deposit envelope
    When the deposit hold engine evaluates availability
    Then the deposit must not be rejected for failure
         to provide a special deposit slip or deposit envelope

  Scenario: Bank requires special deposit slip and makes it available
    Given the depositary bank requires a special deposit slip
        or deposit envelope
    When the bank communicates its deposit requirements
    Then the bank must either provide the slip or envelope
         to its customers
    Or inform customers how the slip or envelope may be
       prepared or obtained
    And make the slip or envelope reasonably available
```

| BR-08 | Special deposit requirements | Identify whether special slips or envelopes are required and whether the requirement is satisfied.          |
| BR-09 | Availability calculation     | Calculate the next or second business day based on the applicable rule.                                     |
| BR-10 | Availability amount          | Calculate the applicable qualifying amount, including the lesser-of-$275 calculation.                       |

# **7. Assumptions, dependencies, and scope boundaries**

To keep implementation aligned with the supplied regulatory text, the following distinctions should be maintained.

**A. Business-day calculations**

- The engine must calculate deadlines relative to the banking day on which the deposit is made or electronic payment is received.
- The availability deadline is expressed in business days, not calendar days.
- The applicable banking-day and business-day calendar definitions must be supplied by the bank's existing calendar capability; those definitions are not included in the provided excerpt.

**B. Electronic payment processing**

- Payment receipt requires both actually and finally collected funds and account/amount information.
- Receipt is limited to the extent that actually and finally collected funds have been received.
- The electronic payment availability clock should not be started solely by receipt of payment instructions or account information.

**C. Check classification**

- The check classification service must distinguish checks covered by § 229.10(c)(1)(i)–(vi) from other checks.
- Payee, issuing entity, deposit method, branch location, state, and check processing region are relevant attributes.
- The special slip/envelope requirement must be configurable because the supplied text makes it conditional on whether the bank requires it.

**D. Customer aggregation**

- The $275 calculation must operate at the customer level, across all customer accounts.
- The calculation must aggregate qualifying deposits on the same banking day.
- The calculation applies only to checks outside the categories listed in § 229.10(c)(1)(i)–(vi).

**E. Explicit scope exclusions**

The following topics are not specified in the supplied excerpt and therefore should not be introduced as acceptance criteria for this delivery:

- Exception holds and extended availability.
- New-account rules.
- Large-deposit exception rules beyond the specific $275 provision.
- Funds availability disclosures.
- Notice requirements.
- ATM-specific availability rules beyond the cash distinction expressly stated.
- Non-proprietary check processing arrangements not addressed in the supplied text.
- Other sections of Regulation CC.

# **8. Definition of Done**

The epic is complete when:

- All deposit categories in § 229.10(a), (b), and (c) are supported.
- In-person and non-in-person cash deposits receive the appropriate availability deadline.
- Electronic payment receipt is determined using both regulatory receipt conditions.
- All six qualifying check categories in § 229.10(c)(1)(i)–(vi) are supported.
- Non-in-person deposits meeting § 229.10(c)(2) receive second-business-day availability.
- The $275 calculation aggregates qualifying check deposits across all customer accounts.
- Special deposit slip/envelope requirements are configurable and evaluated.
- Availability calculations use the correct banking day and business-day sequence.
- All Gherkin scenarios pass functional testing.
- Regression testing confirms existing deposit hold engine functionality remains intact.
