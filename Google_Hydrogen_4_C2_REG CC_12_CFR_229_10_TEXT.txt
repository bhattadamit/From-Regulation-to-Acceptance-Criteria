### Story 1: Cash Deposit Availability

**As a** Deposit Operations Compliance Officer

**I want** the deposit hold engine to assign availability dates to cash deposits based on whether the deposit was made in person to a bank employee

**So that** customers receive next-day or second-business-day access to cash funds in compliance with 12 CFR 229.10(a).

#### Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Feature: Cash Deposit Funds Availability (12 CFR 229.10(a))

  Scenario: Cash deposit made in person to a bank employee
    Given a cash deposit is received on a banking day
    And the deposit is made in person to an employee of the depositary bank
    When the deposit hold engine evaluates the transaction
    Then the funds shall be made available for withdrawal not later than the 1st business day after the banking day of deposit

  Scenario: Cash deposit not made in person to a bank employee
    Given a cash deposit is received on a banking day
    And the deposit is not made in person to an employee of the depositary bank
    When the deposit hold engine evaluates the transaction
    Then the funds shall be made available for withdrawal not later than the 2nd business day after the banking day of deposit

```

### Story 2: Electronic Payment Availability

**As a** Deposit Operations Compliance Officer

**I want** the deposit hold engine to determine receipt of electronic payments based on collected funds and credit instructions and assign next-day availability

**So that** electronic deposits are made available on time under 12 CFR 229.10(b).

#### Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Feature: Electronic Payment Funds Availability (12 CFR 229.10(b))

  Scenario: Electronic payment received with collected funds and credit information
    Given an electronic payment is initiated for deposit into an account
    And the bank has received payment in actually and finally collected funds
    And the bank has received information on the account and amount to be credited
    When the deposit hold engine records the banking day of receipt
    Then the collected funds shall be made available for withdrawal not later than the 1st business day after the banking day of receipt

  Scenario: Electronic payment with partial or uncollected funds
    Given an electronic payment is initiated for deposit into an account
    And the bank has received information on the account and amount to be credited
    And only a portion of the payment is in actually and finally collected funds
    When the deposit hold engine evaluates the payment
    Then the bank shall treat the payment as received only to the extent of the actually and finally collected funds
    And only that collected portion shall be scheduled for availability not later than the 1st business day after the banking day of receipt

```

### Story 5: State and Local Government Check Availability

**As a** Deposit Operations Compliance Officer

**I want** the deposit hold engine to enforce payee, state-location, deposit channel, and special deposit slip rules on state and local government checks

**So that** availability dates comply with 12 CFR 229.10(c)(1)(iv), (c)(2), and (c)(3).

#### Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Feature: State and Local Government Check Availability (12 CFR 229.10(c)(1)(iv), (c)(2), & (c)(3))

  Scenario: State or local government check deposited in person meeting all requirements
    Given a check drawn by a state or a unit of general local government is deposited on a banking day
    And the check is deposited into an account held by a payee of the check
    And the depositary bank is located in the state that issued the check or the same state as the issuing unit of general local government
    And the deposit is made in person to an employee of the depositary bank
    And any bank-required special deposit slip or deposit envelope identifying the check type is used
    When the deposit hold engine evaluates the deposit
    Then the funds shall be made available for withdrawal not later than the 1st business day after the banking day of deposit

  Scenario: State or local government check not deposited in person meeting all other requirements
    Given a check drawn by a state or a unit of general local government is deposited on a banking day
    And the check is deposited into an account held by a payee of the check
    And the depositary bank is located in the state that issued the check or the same state as the issuing unit of general local government
    And the deposit is not made in person to an employee of the depositary bank
    And any bank-required special deposit slip or deposit envelope identifying the check type is used
    When the deposit hold engine evaluates the deposit
    Then the funds shall be made available for withdrawal not later than the 2nd business day after the banking day of deposit

  Scenario: State or local government check deposited without required special deposit slip
    Given a check drawn by a state or a unit of general local government is deposited on a banking day
    And the depositary bank requires a special deposit slip or deposit envelope for this check type
    And the bank provides the slip/envelope or informs customers how to prepare/obtain it and makes it reasonably available
    And the customer does not use the required special deposit slip or deposit envelope
    When the deposit hold engine evaluates the deposit
    Then the check shall not qualify for next-day availability under 12 CFR 229.10(c)(1)(iv) or second-day availability under 229.10(c)(2)

```

### Story 8: $275 Next-Day Availability Rule for Non-Qualifying Checks

**As a** Deposit Operations Compliance Officer

**I want** the deposit hold engine to aggregate all check deposits made by a customer across all their accounts on a single banking day that do not qualify for next-day availability under (c)(1)(i)–(vi) and make up to $275 available the next business day

**So that** the customer receives the mandatory $275 next-day release required by 12 CFR 229.10(c)(1)(vii).

#### Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Feature: $275 Next-Day Availability on Aggregate Non-Qualifying Checks (12 CFR 229.10(c)(1)(vii))

  Scenario: Aggregate non-qualifying checks on a single banking day exceed $275
    Given a customer deposits one or more checks across one or more of their accounts on a single banking day
    And none of those checks qualify for next-day availability under 12 CFR 229.10(c)(1)(i) through (vi)
    And the aggregate amount of those checks is greater than $275
    When the deposit hold engine calculates next-day availability for that banking day
    Then $275 of the aggregate check deposits shall be made available for withdrawal not later than the 1st business day after the banking day of deposit

  Scenario: Aggregate non-qualifying checks on a single banking day are $275 or less
    Given a customer deposits one or more checks across one or more of their accounts on a single banking day
    And none of those checks qualify for next-day availability under 12 CFR 229.10(c)(1)(i) through (vi)
    And the aggregate amount of those checks is $275 or less (for example, $180)
    When the deposit hold engine calculates next-day availability for that banking day
    Then the entire aggregate amount ($180) shall be made available for withdrawal not later than the 1st business day after the banking day of deposit

  Scenario: Customer deposits both qualifying next-day checks and non-qualifying checks on the same banking day
    Given a customer deposits a $1,000 U.S. Treasury check into a payee account on a banking day
    And the customer also deposits a $500 check not subject to next-day availability under 12 CFR 229.10(c)(1)(i) through (vi) on the same banking day
    When the deposit hold engine calculates next-day availability for that banking day
    Then the full $1,000 from the Treasury check shall be made available not later than the 1st business day after the banking day of deposit
    And an additional $275 from the non-qualifying check shall be made available not later than the 1st business day after the banking day of deposit

```

### Story 9: Special Deposit Slip Configuration and Customer Notice Controls

**As a** Deposit Product Manager

**I want** the deposit hold engine and channel systems to enforce special deposit slip/envelope rules only when the bank provides or informs customers how to obtain/prepare them and makes them reasonably available

**So that** the bank satisfies the prerequisite conditions of 12 CFR 229.10(c)(3).

#### Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Feature: Special Deposit Slip Prerequisite Enforcement (12 CFR 229.10(c)(3))

  Scenario: Bank enforces special deposit slip requirement with customer availability controls active
    Given the bank configures a special deposit slip or deposit envelope requirement for state/local government checks or cashier's/certified/teller's checks
    And the slip or envelope identifies the type of check
    And the bank either provides the slip/envelope to customers or informs customers how it may be prepared or obtained and makes it reasonably available
    When a customer deposits an applicable check without using the special deposit slip or deposit envelope
    Then the deposit hold engine shall treat the special deposit slip condition as unmet

  Scenario: Bank has not satisfied special deposit slip availability or notice prerequisites
    Given a customer deposits a state/local government check or cashier's/certified/teller's check meeting all other criteria of 12 CFR 229.10(c)(1)(iv) or (v)
    And the bank either does not provide the special deposit slip/envelope or has not informed customers how to prepare/obtain it and made it reasonably available
    When the deposit hold engine evaluates the deposit without a special deposit slip
    Then the engine shall not deny next-day or second-day availability on the basis of a missing special deposit slip

```
