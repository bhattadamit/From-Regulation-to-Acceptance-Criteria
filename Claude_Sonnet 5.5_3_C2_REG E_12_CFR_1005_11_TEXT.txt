# Regulation E Error Resolution (12 CFR 1005.11): User Stories and Acceptance Criteria

Acceptance criteria are drawn only from the § 1005.11 text you supplied. Terms the text doesn't define are flagged in the open items at the end rather than assumed.

---

## Epic 1: Error Intake and Classification

### US-01: Classify a notice as a covered error
**As a** disputes analyst
**I want** the platform to classify each incoming notice against the defined error types
**So that** only covered notices enter the Reg E error resolution workflow.

```gherkin
Feature: Error type classification (§ 1005.11(a))

  Scenario Outline: Notice alleges a covered error type
    Given a consumer notice alleging "<error_type>"
    When the analyst classifies the notice
    Then the notice is flagged as a Reg E error
    And the error type "<error_type>" is recorded on the case

    Examples:
      | error_type                                                                          |
      | Unauthorized electronic fund transfer                                               |
      | Incorrect electronic fund transfer to or from the account                           |
      | Omission of an electronic fund transfer from a periodic statement                   |
      | Computational or bookkeeping error by the institution relating to an EFT            |
      | Consumer received an incorrect amount of money from an electronic terminal          |
      | EFT not identified in accordance with § 1005.9 or § 1005.10(a)                      |
      | Request for documentation or clarification, including to determine if an error exists |

  Scenario Outline: Notice is an excluded inquiry
    Given a consumer inquiry that is "<inquiry_type>"
    When the analyst classifies the inquiry
    Then the inquiry is not flagged as a Reg E error
    And no error resolution deadlines are started

    Examples:
      | inquiry_type                                      |
      | A routine inquiry about the account balance       |
      | A request for tax or other recordkeeping info     |
      | A request for duplicate copies of documentation   |
```

## Epic 2: Notice Validity and Timeliness

### US-02: Validate an oral or written notice of error
**As a** disputes analyst
**I want** the platform to validate each notice for timing and content
**So that** we comply with this section for every qualifying notice.

```gherkin
Feature: Notice of error validation (§ 1005.11(b)(1))

  Scenario Outline: Notice is accepted through any channel
    Given a notice of error received by "<channel>"
    When the platform validates the notice
    Then the notice is eligible for error resolution

    Examples:
      | channel |
      | oral    |
      | written |

  Scenario: Notice received within 60 days of the statement
    Given the periodic statement or passbook documentation first reflecting the alleged error was sent on a known date
    And the notice is received no later than 60 days after that date
    When the platform validates timeliness
    Then the notice is marked timely

  Scenario: Notice received after 60 days
    Given the notice is received more than 60 days after the statement or passbook documentation first reflecting the alleged error was sent
    When the platform validates timeliness
    Then the notice is marked untimely under § 1005.11(b)(1)(i)
    And the case is routed for manual review

  Scenario: Notice identifies the consumer
    Given a notice that enables identification of the consumer's name and account number
    When the platform validates content
    Then the identification requirement is marked satisfied

  Scenario: Notice explains why an error exists
    Given a notice that is not a documentation or clarification request under § 1005.11(a)(1)(vii)
    When the platform validates content
    Then the platform requires the reason the consumer believes an error exists
    And the platform captures the type, date, and amount of the error to the extent possible

  Scenario: Documentation or clarification request needs no error details
    Given a notice that is a request described in § 1005.11(a)(1)(vii)
    When the platform validates content
    Then the platform does not require the reason, type, date, or amount
```

### US-03: Timeliness of a notice based on requested documentation
**As a** disputes analyst
**I want** the 60-day window to restart when the consumer's notice follows information they requested
**So that** follow-on notices are judged on the correct deadline.

```gherkin
Feature: Notice of error following a documentation or clarification request (§ 1005.11(b)(3))

  Scenario: Notice received within 60 days of the requested information
    Given a notice of error based on documentation or clarification the consumer requested under § 1005.11(a)(1)(vii)
    And the institution sent the requested information on a known date
    When the notice is received no later than 60 days after that date
    Then the notice is marked timely

  Scenario: Notice received more than 60 days after the requested information
    Given a notice of error based on requested documentation or clarification
    When the notice is received more than 60 days after the institution sent the information
    Then the notice is marked untimely under § 1005.11(b)(3)
```

### US-04: Optional written confirmation of oral notices
**As a** disputes operations manager
**I want** to configure whether written confirmation is required for oral notices
**So that** the platform handles the confirmation process and its effect on provisional credit.

```gherkin
Feature: Written confirmation of oral notice (§ 1005.11(b)(2))

  Scenario: Institution requires written confirmation
    Given the institution is configured to require written confirmation
    When a consumer gives oral notice of error
    Then the platform informs the consumer of the requirement during the oral notification
    And the platform provides the address where confirmation must be sent
    And the platform starts a 10-business-day confirmation timer

  Scenario: Institution does not require written confirmation
    Given the institution is not configured to require written confirmation
    When a consumer gives oral notice of error
    Then no confirmation timer is started
    And the case proceeds under § 1005.11(c)

  Scenario: Written confirmation not received in time
    Given written confirmation was required for an oral notice
    And 10 business days have passed without receipt of confirmation
    When the platform evaluates provisional credit obligations
    Then the platform records that provisional credit is not required under § 1005.11(c)(2)(i)(A)
```

## Epic 3: Investigation Timelines

### US-05: Standard 10-business-day determination
**As a** disputes analyst
**I want** the platform to track the 10-business-day investigation clock
**So that** determination, correction, and reporting meet the required deadlines.

```gherkin
Feature: Ten-day investigation period (§ 1005.11(c)(1))

  Scenario: Case clock starts at receipt
    Given a valid notice of error
    When the institution receives the notice
    Then the platform starts the investigation clock from the date of receipt
    And the case is flagged for prompt investigation

  Scenario: Determination within 10 business days
    Given an open case with no provisional credit issued
    When the institution determines within 10 business days of receipt whether an error occurred
    Then the platform records the determination date

  Scenario: Report results after investigation
    Given the investigation is completed
    When the determination is recorded
    Then the platform requires the results to be reported to the consumer within 3 business days after completion
    And the platform raises an alert if the deadline is at risk

  Scenario: Correct a confirmed error
    Given the institution determined that an error occurred
    When the determination is recorded
    Then the platform requires the error to be corrected within 1 business day after the determination
    And the platform raises an alert if the deadline is at risk
```

### US-06: 45-day investigation with provisional credit
**As a** disputes analyst
**I want** the platform to support the extended 45-day investigation only when its conditions are met
**So that** we remain compliant when we cannot finish within 10 business days.

```gherkin
Feature: Forty-five day investigation period (§ 1005.11(c)(2))

  Scenario: Extend the investigation to 45 days
    Given the institution cannot complete its investigation within 10 business days of receiving the notice
    When the analyst elects the 45-day period
    Then the platform sets the investigation deadline to 45 days from receipt of the notice
    And the platform requires provisional credit, consumer notice, and use of funds conditions to be met

  Scenario: Provisional credit issued
    Given the 45-day period is elected
    When the platform processes provisional credit
    Then the account is credited with the amount of the alleged error, including interest where applicable
    And the credit is posted within 10 business days of receipt of the notice

  Scenario: Withhold up to $50 for suspected unauthorized transfer
    Given the institution has a reasonable basis for believing an unauthorized EFT occurred
    And the institution has satisfied the requirements of § 1005.6(a)
    When the platform calculates the provisional credit
    Then the platform allows a maximum of $50 to be withheld from the amount credited

  Scenario: No withholding without both conditions
    Given the alleged error is an unauthorized EFT
    And either the reasonable basis or the § 1005.6(a) requirement is not documented
    When the platform calculates the provisional credit
    Then no amount is withheld from the credit

  Scenario Outline: Provisional credit exception
    Given the 45-day period is elected
    And "<exception>"
    When the platform evaluates provisional credit
    Then provisional credit is not required

    Examples:
      | exception                                                                                           |
      | Written confirmation was required but not received within 10 business days of an oral notice        |
      | The alleged error involves an account subject to Regulation T (12 CFR part 220)                     |

  Scenario: Notify the consumer of provisional credit
    Given a provisional credit has been posted
    When the platform generates the consumer notice
    Then the notice states the amount and date of the provisional credit
    And the notice is sent within 2 business days after the provisional credit
    And the consumer has full use of the funds during the investigation

  Scenario: Correct the error and report results
    Given the investigation is completed within the 45-day period
    When an error is determined to have occurred
    Then the platform requires correction within 1 business day after the determination
    And the platform requires results to be reported within 3 business days after completing the investigation
    And the report includes notice that a provisional credit has been made final, if applicable
```

### US-07: Extended time periods for special cases
**As a** disputes analyst
**I want** the platform to apply extended deadlines automatically
**So that** investigation clocks reflect the transaction characteristics.

```gherkin
Feature: Extension of time periods (§ 1005.11(c)(3))

  Scenario: 20 business days for transfers near first deposit
    Given the notice of error involves an EFT to or from the account within 30 days after the first deposit to the account was made
    When the platform sets investigation deadlines
    Then 20 business days applies in place of 10 business days under both § 1005.11(c)(1) and (c)(2)

  Scenario Outline: 90 days in place of 45 days
    Given the 45-day period is elected
    And the notice involves an EFT that "<condition>"
    When the platform sets the investigation deadline
    Then the deadline is 90 days from receipt of the notice

    Examples:
      | condition                                                    |
      | was not initiated within a state                             |
      | resulted from a point-of-sale debit card transaction         |
      | occurred within 30 days after the first deposit was made     |

  Scenario: Combined extensions
    Given an EFT occurred within 30 days after the first deposit to the account
    When the 45-day period is elected
    Then the provisional credit deadline is 20 business days
    And the investigation deadline is 90 days

  Scenario: No extension conditions apply
    Given the transfer meets none of the extension conditions
    When the platform sets deadlines
    Then the standard 10 business day and 45 day periods apply
```

### US-08: Scope of investigation for third-party transfers
**As a** disputes analyst
**I want** the platform to identify when an internal records review is sufficient
**So that** we apply the correct investigation scope.

```gherkin
Feature: Investigation scope (§ 1005.11(c)(4))

  Scenario: Review of own records is sufficient
    Given the alleged error concerns a transfer to or from a third party
    And there is no agreement between the institution and the third party for that type of EFT
    And the transfer is not covered by § 1005.14
    When the analyst completes a review of the institution's own records
    Then the platform records that the investigation requirements are satisfied

  Scenario: Agreement exists with the third party
    Given the alleged error concerns a transfer to or from a third party
    And an agreement exists between the institution and the third party for that type of EFT
    When the analyst scopes the investigation
    Then the platform does not apply the own-records-only provision

  Scenario: Transfer covered by § 1005.14
    Given the transfer is covered by § 1005.14
    When the analyst scopes the investigation
    Then the platform does not apply the own-records-only provision
```

## Epic 4: Outcomes When No Error or a Different Error Is Found

### US-09: Written explanation of findings
**As a** disputes analyst
**I want** the results letter to include required content when no error, or a different error, is found
**So that** consumers receive compliant explanations and know their document rights.

```gherkin
Feature: Written explanation of findings (§ 1005.11(d)(1))

  Scenario Outline: Determination differs from the consumer's allegation
    Given the institution determined that "<outcome>"
    When the platform generates the results report
    Then the report includes a written explanation of the institution's findings
    And the report notes the consumer's right to request the documents the institution relied on
    And the standard § 1005.11(c) timing requirements still apply

    Examples:
      | outcome                                                                  |
      | no error occurred                                                        |
      | an error occurred in a manner different from that described by consumer  |
      | an error occurred in an amount different from that described by consumer |

  Scenario: Consumer requests supporting documents
    Given the consumer has requested the documents relied upon
    When the platform receives the request
    Then the platform creates a task to provide copies promptly
    And the task is tracked until copies are sent
```

### US-10: Reverse a provisional credit
**As a** disputes analyst
**I want** the platform to enforce required notices and item-honoring when debiting a provisional credit
**So that** reversals comply with § 1005.11(d)(2).

```gherkin
Feature: Debiting provisional credit (§ 1005.11(d)(2))

  Scenario: Notify the consumer on debit
    Given the institution determined that no error, or a different error, occurred
    And a provisional credit is outstanding
    When the platform debits the provisionally credited amount
    Then the platform notifies the consumer of the date and amount of the debiting

  Scenario: Notice of five-business-day honoring period
    Given the platform is debiting a provisional credit
    When the platform generates the debit notice
    Then the notice states that the institution will honor checks, drafts, or similar instruments payable to third parties for five business days after the notification
    And the notice states that the institution will honor preauthorized transfers for five business days after the notification
    And the notice states that no overdraft charge will result from honoring those items

  Scenario: Honor items during the five-business-day period
    Given the debit notice was sent
    And an item is presented within five business days after the notification
    When the platform evaluates the item
    Then the item is honored if the institution would have paid it had the provisionally credited funds not been debited
    And no overdraft charge is applied as a result of the debit

  Scenario: Item would not have been paid anyway
    Given the debit notice was sent
    And an item is presented within five business days after the notification
    And the institution would not have paid the item even if the provisional credit had not been debited
    When the platform evaluates the item
    Then the platform is not required to honor the item
```

## Epic 5: Closure and Reassertion

### US-11: Handle reassertion of the same error
**As a** disputes analyst
**I want** the platform to recognize reasserted errors after full compliance
**So that** we avoid duplicate investigations while preserving the documentation-request exception.

```gherkin
Feature: Reassertion of error (§ 1005.11(e))

  Scenario: Same error reasserted after full compliance
    Given a closed case in which the institution fully complied with the error resolution requirements
    When the consumer later reasserts the same error
    Then the platform records that no further responsibilities exist under § 1005.11
    And no new investigation clock is started

  Scenario: Error asserted after documentation or clarification is provided
    Given the consumer receives information under § 1005.11(a)(1)(vii)
    When the consumer asserts an error after receiving that information
    Then the platform treats the assertion as a new notice of error
    And the timeliness rules in § 1005.11(b)(3) apply

  Scenario: Prior compliance was incomplete
    Given a closed case in which a required step was not completed
    When the consumer reasserts the same error
    Then the platform does not apply the no-further-responsibility provision
    And the case is routed for compliance review
```

## Open Items to Resolve with Compliance

These are not defined in the supplied text, so the criteria above deliberately don't specify them:

1. **"Business day" definition.** The text uses the term but doesn't define it. The calendar and holiday logic needs a compliance-approved definition.
2. **Start of the clock.** Confirm how "receipt" is determined for oral notices (call timestamp) and for notices received on non-business days.
3. **Interest on provisional credit.** The text says "where applicable" without a calculation method.
4. **Cross-references.** The criteria depend on § 1005.6(a) (unauthorized transfer liability), § 1005.9 and § 1005.10(a) (documentation), and § 1005.14, none of which were supplied. Stories that rely on them (US-06, US-08) will need acceptance criteria once that text is provided.
5. **"Not initiated within a state."** Determining this from card and ACH data (for example, foreign-originated transfers) needs a defined data rule.
6. **Debit notice timing.** The text sets no deadline for notifying the consumer of the debit. Confirm that reporting results within 3 business days covers it, or define a separate rule.
