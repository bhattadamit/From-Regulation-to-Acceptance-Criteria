# Regulation CC Next-Day Availability (12 CFR 229.10): User Stories and Acceptance Criteria

## Scope and assumptions

- Acceptance criteria are based **only** on the § 229.10 text you provided. Anything not in that text is listed under "Gaps and questions" below.
- "Business day" and "banking day" are not defined in the provided text. The criteria assume a **calendar service** that maps a banking day D to "next business day" (D+1 business day) and "second business day" (D+2 business days).
- "Not later than" sets a **maximum** hold. The engine computes a **latest permitted availability date**, and the bank may make funds available sooner.

```gherkin
Background:
  Given the deposit hold engine receives a deposit event with
    | deposit_type | channel | payee_match | amount | banking_day |
  And a calendar service resolves "next business day" and "second business day" after a banking day
```

## Epic 1: Cash deposits (§ 229.10(a))

### US-1.1: Cash deposited in person
**As a** deposit operations manager, **I want** cash deposits made in person to an employee to be available by the next business day, **so that** we comply with § 229.10(a)(1).

```gherkin
Scenario: In-person cash deposit to a bank employee
  Given a cash deposit of $1,000 is made in person to an employee of the depositary bank
  And the banking day of deposit is D
  When the hold engine calculates availability
  Then the latest permitted availability date is the business day after D
  And the full $1,000 is scheduled as available for withdrawal by that date

Scenario: Cash deposit amount is not capped
  Given an in-person cash deposit of $250,000
  When the hold engine calculates availability
  Then the full amount is scheduled for availability by the business day after D
  And no portion is held beyond that date under this rule
```

### US-1.2: Cash deposited not in person
**As a** deposit operations manager, **I want** cash deposits not made in person to an employee to be available by the second business day, **so that** we comply with § 229.10(a)(2).

```gherkin
Scenario: Cash deposit not made in person to an employee
  Given a cash deposit of $500 is made by a channel other than in person to an employee of the depositary bank
  And the banking day of deposit is D
  When the hold engine calculates availability
  Then the latest permitted availability date is the second business day after D

Scenario Outline: Cash deposit timing by in-person indicator
  Given a cash deposit with in_person_to_employee = "<in_person>"
  When the hold engine calculates availability
  Then the latest permitted availability date is the "<deadline>" after the banking day of deposit

  Examples:
    | in_person | deadline                |
    | true      | next business day       |
    | false     | second business day     |
```

## Epic 2: Electronic payments (§ 229.10(b))

### US-2.1: Next-day availability for electronic payments
**As a** deposit operations manager, **I want** electronic payments to be available by the business day after the banking day on which the bank *received* the payment, **so that** we comply with § 229.10(b)(1).

```gherkin
Scenario: Electronic payment received
  Given an electronic payment for $2,000 is received on banking day R
  When the hold engine calculates availability
  Then the latest permitted availability date is the business day after R
```

### US-2.2: Determining when an electronic payment is "received"
**As a** payments analyst, **I want** the engine to treat an electronic payment as received only when both funds and account information have arrived, **so that** the availability clock starts correctly per § 229.10(b)(2).

```gherkin
Scenario: Both conditions satisfied
  Given the bank has received payment in actually and finally collected funds
  And the bank has received information on the account and amount to be credited
  When the hold engine evaluates the payment
  Then the payment is marked "received"
  And the receipt banking day is the banking day on which the later of the two conditions was met

Scenario: Account and amount information missing
  Given the bank has received payment in actually and finally collected funds
  But the bank has not received information on the account and amount to be credited
  When the hold engine evaluates the payment
  Then the payment is not marked "received"
  And no availability clock is started

Scenario: Funds not yet actually and finally collected
  Given the bank has received information on the account and amount to be credited
  But the bank has not received payment in actually and finally collected funds
  When the hold engine evaluates the payment
  Then the payment is not marked "received"
  And no availability clock is started

Scenario: Payment received only in part
  Given account and amount information has been received for $1,000
  And payment in actually and finally collected funds has been received for $600
  When the hold engine evaluates the payment
  Then the bank is treated as having received an electronic payment of $600 only
  And the next-business-day availability clock applies to $600
  And the remaining $400 has no clock under this rule until collected funds are received
```

## Epic 3: Check deposits eligible for next-day availability (§ 229.10(c)(1))

For the in-person and special-slip conditions below, the engine needs deposit attributes: `payee_account_holder`, `in_person_to_employee`, `special_slip_used`, `bank_requires_special_slip`, `depositor_state`, and so on.

### US-3.4: State and local government checks
**As a** deposit operations manager, **I want** state and local government checks to be available next business day only when all four conditions are met, **so that** we comply with § 229.10(c)(1)(iv).

```gherkin
Scenario: State or local government check meeting all conditions

Scenario: Depositary bank located in a different state than the issuer

Scenario: Local government check and bank location
```

### US-3.7: Minimum next-day amount for other checks
**As a** deposit operations manager, **I want** the lesser of $275 or the day's aggregate non-qualifying check deposits to be available next business day, **so that** we comply with § 229.10(c)(1)(vii).

```gherkin
Scenario: Aggregate exceeds $275
  Given on banking day D a customer deposits checks to all of the customer's accounts
  And checks totaling $1,000 are not subject to next-day availability under (c)(1)(i) through (vi)
  When the hold engine calculates availability
  Then $275 is scheduled for availability by the business day after D

Scenario: Aggregate is below $275
  Given the aggregate of such checks deposited on D to all accounts of the customer is $120
  When the hold engine calculates availability
  Then $120 is scheduled for availability by the business day after D

Scenario: Aggregate across multiple accounts
  Given a customer deposits $200 of such checks to account A and $300 to account B on banking day D
  When the hold engine calculates availability
  Then the aggregate considered is $500
  And a total of $275 is made available next business day across the customer's accounts

Scenario: Checks qualifying under (c)(1)(i) through (vi) are excluded from the aggregate
  Given a customer deposits a qualifying Treasury check of $900 and a non-qualifying check of $100 on banking day D
  When the hold engine calculates availability
  Then the Treasury check is available next business day under (c)(1)(i)
  And the aggregate for the (c)(1)(vii) rule is $100 only
```

## Epic 5: Special deposit slip configuration (§ 229.10(c)(3))

### US-5.1: Configure the special slip requirement
**As a** compliance officer, **I want** to configure whether the bank requires a special deposit slip or envelope, **so that** the engine applies conditions (c)(1)(iv)(D) and (c)(1)(v)(C) only when we have opted in.

```gherkin
Scenario: Requirement disabled
  Given the "require special deposit slip" setting is off
  When a state or local government check or a cashier's, certified, or teller's check is classified
  Then the special slip condition is not evaluated

Scenario: Requirement enabled
  Given the "require special deposit slip" setting is on
  When one of these checks is deposited without the special slip or envelope
  Then it does not qualify under (c)(1)(iv) or (c)(1)(v)

Scenario: Setting scope
  Given the "require special deposit slip" setting is on
  When a Treasury check, USPS money order, or Federal Reserve or FHLB check is classified
  Then the special slip condition is not applied
  # (c)(3)(i) covers only state/local government checks and cashier's, certified, or teller's checks
```

### US-5.2: Customer-facing obligations when the slip is required
**As a** compliance officer, **I want** the bank to provide the slip or inform customers how to prepare or obtain it, so that we comply with § 229.10(c)(3)(ii).

```gherkin
Scenario: Cannot enable requirement without a provision method
  Given an administrator turns the "require special deposit slip" setting on
  When the administrator saves the configuration
  Then the system requires one of the following to be recorded
    | The bank provides the slip or envelope to customers |
    | The bank informs customers how the slip or envelope may be prepared or obtained, and makes it reasonably available |
  And the configuration is rejected if neither is recorded
```

## Gaps and questions for compliance and legal

1. **Definitions are missing.** "Banking day," "business day," "check processing region," "actually and finally collected funds," "unit of general local government," and "in person" are not in the text provided. The stories treat them as upstream data or calendar inputs. Please confirm the source for each.
2. **Checks not covered here.** Ordinary personal or business checks beyond the $275 amount have no rule in this text. The engine needs a fallback or handoff to other provisions, or the text you supplied is incomplete.
3. **Payee conditions.** The text requires "an account held by a payee" for several check types. Please confirm how the engine determines this match, especially for joint accounts.
4. **Special slip scope.** The text allows the requirement only for state/local government and cashier's/certified/teller's checks. US-5.1 enforces that scope.
5. **Partial qualification of one check.** The text does not address checks that qualify in part, other than the $275 rule and the partial electronic payment case. I did not invent further behavior.
