# Regulation CC Next-Day Availability (12 CFR 229.10): User Stories and Acceptance Criteria

**Author:** Senior Business Analyst, Deposit Operations | **Date:** November 20, 2026 | **Status:** Draft for Compliance/Product review

---

## 1. Purpose and scope

**Goal:** Make the deposit hold engine assign the correct *next-business-day* availability date to each component of a deposit, as 12 CFR 229.10 requires, and make those decisions auditable.

**In scope:** cash deposits, electronic payments, next-day check types, the "minimum amount" rule, business and banking day logic, effective-dated thresholds, and audit and reason codes.

**Out of scope (separate epics, but this work exposes hooks for them):**
- Exception holds (§229.13: new account, large deposit, repeated overdraft, reasonable cause, emergency conditions)
- The existing §229.12 schedule for non-next-day checks
- Customer disclosures and hold notices (§§229.15–229.18)

## 2. Reference parameters

The agencies adjust the Reg CC dollar thresholds every five years. The current values took effect July 1, 2025, and the minimum amount under §229.10(c)(1)(vii) is $275. These must be stored as **effective-dated configuration**, never hard-coded.

| Parameter | Value | Citation | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `MIN_AMOUNT_NEXT_DAY` | $275 | §229.10(c)(1)(vii) | Previously $225; effective 7/1/2025 |
| `CASH_WITHDRAWAL_AMOUNT` | $550 | §229.12(d) | Reference only, not used in this epic |
| `NEW_ACCOUNT_AMOUNT` / `LARGE_DEPOSIT_THRESHOLD` | $6,725 | §229.13 | Used only by the exception-hold epic |
| `DEPOSIT_CUTOFF_TIME` | 14:00 local | §229.19 | Assumed bank policy; the regulation allows no earlier than 2 p.m. Confirm with Compliance |

**Assumptions and open items for Compliance sign-off**
1. Paragraph-level citations under §229.10(c)(1) should be verified against the current eCFR text before they go into rule metadata. The sub-paragraph lettering is not reproduced here from memory.
2. Whether the $275 minimum amount applies per account or per customer across multiple accounts is a policy decision. It is modeled as a configuration flag (US-08).
3. Channel conditions (employee-attended vs. ATM vs. remote deposit capture) for next-day check types follow the bank's disclosed policy and are configurable (US-05, US-06).
4. Cash deposited at an ATM or otherwise not to an employee is governed by a different timing rule. It is excluded from US-03 pending a confirmed citation.

## 3. Personas

- **Deposit Operations Analyst:** reviews holds and answers customer inquiries
- **Compliance Officer:** needs evidence the engine follows Reg CC
- **Account Holder (Consumer or Business):** expects timely access to funds
- **Hold Engine Product Owner:** owns rule configuration

## 4. Epic: Reg CC Next-Day Availability in the Deposit Hold Engine

### US-01: Apply next-day rules only to Reg CC "accounts"

**As a** Compliance Officer
**I want** the engine to apply §229.10 rules only to transaction accounts covered by Regulation CC
**So that** we do not apply consumer-style availability rules to products outside the regulation.

```gherkin
Feature: Reg CC account eligibility

  Background:
    Given the hold engine has product-to-RegCC-coverage mapping loaded

  Scenario Outline: Coverage determination by product type
    Given an account of product type "<product>"
    When a deposit is received for the account
    Then the engine sets RegCC_covered to "<covered>"

    Examples:
      | product                    | covered |
      | Consumer Checking          | true    |
      | Business Checking          | true    |
      | NOW Account                | true    |
      | Certificate of Deposit     | false   |
      | Savings (non-transaction)  | false   |

  Scenario: Uncovered account bypasses Reg CC logic
    Given an account with RegCC_covered "false"
    When a deposit is received
    Then the engine does not evaluate §229.10 rules
    And the audit record states reason code "ACCT_NOT_REGCC"

  Scenario: Unmapped product fails safe
    Given an account whose product type has no coverage mapping
    When a deposit is received
    Then the engine treats the account as RegCC_covered "true"
    And raises a configuration alert to the Product Owner queue
```

### US-02: Calculate banking day, business day, and next business day

**As a** Hold Engine Product Owner
**I want** a single, tested calendar service for business and banking day determination
**So that** every availability rule computes dates consistently.

*Definitions:* A **business day** is any calendar day except Saturday, Sunday, and a legal federal holiday. A **banking day** is the part of a business day on which the bank is open to carry on substantially all banking functions. Deposits received after the cutoff, or on a non-banking day, are treated as received on the next banking day.

```gherkin
Feature: Banking day and next-business-day calculation

  Background:
    Given the deposit cutoff time is "14:00" America/Chicago
    And the federal holiday calendar for 2026 is loaded
    And "2026-11-26" (Thanksgiving) is a federal holiday

  Scenario Outline: Deposit date assignment
    When a deposit is received on "<received_datetime>"
    Then the deposit's banking day is "<banking_day>"
    And the next business day after the banking day is "<next_business_day>"

    Examples:
      | received_datetime    | banking_day | next_business_day | note                               |
      | 2026-11-18 10:00 CT  | 2026-11-18  | 2026-11-19        | Wednesday, before cutoff           |
      | 2026-11-18 15:30 CT  | 2026-11-19  | 2026-11-20        | After cutoff rolls to next banking day |
      | 2026-11-20 13:59 CT  | 2026-11-20  | 2026-11-23        | Friday before cutoff, spans weekend |
      | 2026-11-20 14:01 CT  | 2026-11-23  | 2026-11-24        | Friday after cutoff                |
      | 2026-11-21 09:00 CT  | 2026-11-23  | 2026-11-24        | Saturday deposit                   |
      | 2026-11-25 11:00 CT  | 2026-11-25  | 2026-11-27        | Skips Thanksgiving                 |
      | 2026-11-26 09:00 CT  | 2026-11-27  | 2026-11-30        | Holiday deposit                    |

  Scenario: Calendar data is missing for the deposit year
    Given the holiday calendar for the deposit year is not loaded
    When a deposit is received
    Then the engine does not guess a date
    And routes the deposit to manual review with reason code "CAL_MISSING"
    And sends a high-priority alert to Deposit Operations
```

### US-03: Next-day availability for cash deposits made to an employee

**As an** Account Holder
**I want** cash I hand to a teller to be available the next business day
**So that** I can use my money promptly, as the regulation guarantees.

```gherkin
Feature: Cash deposits in person (§229.10(a))

  Background:
    Given the account is RegCC_covered
    And the deposit cutoff time is "14:00" America/Chicago

  Scenario: Cash deposited with a teller before cutoff
    Given a cash deposit of $1,500.00 made in person to a bank employee on "2026-11-20 10:00 CT"
    When the hold engine evaluates the deposit
    Then $1,500.00 is assigned availability date "2026-11-23"
    And availability time is "start of business"
    And no hold remains on the funds after that date
    And reason code "CASH_EMPLOYEE_NEXT_DAY" is logged

  Scenario: Cash deposit after cutoff
    Given a cash deposit of $400.00 made to a teller on "2026-11-20 16:00 CT"
    When the hold engine evaluates the deposit
    Then the banking day is "2026-11-23"
    And the availability date is "2026-11-24"

  Scenario: Cash component of a mixed deposit is evaluated independently
    Given a deposit slip containing $300.00 in cash and a $900.00 personal check
    And the deposit is made in person to a teller
    When the hold engine evaluates the deposit
    Then the $300.00 cash component receives next-day availability
    And the check component is evaluated under check rules separately
```

### US-04: Next-day availability for electronic payments

**As an** Account Holder
**I want** incoming wires and ACH credits made available no later than the next business day
**So that** electronic funds are not held longer than permitted.

*Note:* The engine enforces the **latest permitted** date. Bank policy may make funds available sooner, via a configurable earlier-availability rule.

```gherkin
Feature: Electronic payments (§229.10(b))

  Background:
    Given the account is RegCC_covered

  Scenario Outline: Incoming electronic payment
    Given an incoming "<type>" of $<amount> received on banking day "2026-11-24"
    When the hold engine evaluates the credit
    Then the latest permitted availability date is "<latest_date>"
    And reason code "ELEC_PAYMENT_NEXT_DAY" is logged

    Examples:
      | type            | amount    | latest_date |
      | Wire transfer   | 25,000.00 | 2026-11-25  |
      | ACH credit      | 1,200.00  | 2026-11-25  |

  Scenario: Policy configured for same-day availability
    Given the bank policy sets ACH credit availability to "same day"
    When an ACH credit of $800.00 posts on a banking day
    Then the funds are available per the policy
    And the engine never assigns a date later than the Reg CC maximum

  Scenario: Electronic payment received on a non-business day
    Given a wire is received on Saturday "2026-11-21"
    When the hold engine evaluates the wire
    Then the banking day is "2026-11-23"
    And the latest permitted availability date is "2026-11-24"
```

### US-05: Next-day availability for government and Federal Reserve/FHLB checks

**As an** Account Holder
**I want** U.S. Treasury checks, Federal Reserve Bank and Federal Home Loan Bank checks, and state or local government checks available the next business day
**So that** government payments reach me quickly.

```gherkin
Feature: Government and Reserve Bank checks (§229.10(c)(1))

  Background:
    Given the account is RegCC_covered
    And configuration "REQUIRE_PAYEE_MATCH" is true
    And configuration "GOV_CHECK_CHANNELS" lists the channels where next-day treatment applies

  Scenario Outline: Eligible check type deposited to the payee's account
    Given a "<check_type>" for $<amount> deposited to an account owned by the named payee
    And the deposit channel is an eligible channel
    And the deposit banking day is "2026-11-24"
    When the hold engine evaluates the check
    Then the full $<amount> is assigned availability date "2026-11-25"
    And reason code "NEXTDAY_<code>" is logged

    Examples:
      | check_type                  | amount   | code       |
      | U.S. Treasury check         | 3,200.00 | TREASURY   |
      | Federal Reserve Bank check  | 9,000.00 | FRB        |
      | Federal Home Loan Bank check| 7,500.00 | FHLB       |
      | State government check      | 1,850.00 | STATE_GOV  |
      | Local government check      | 640.00   | LOCAL_GOV  |

  Scenario: Government check deposited to an account that is not the payee's
    Given a U.S. Treasury check payable to "Jordan Lee"
    And the deposit is made to an account not owned by "Jordan Lee"
    When the hold engine evaluates the check
    Then the check is not classified as next-day
    And it falls through to the next applicable rule
    And reason code "PAYEE_MISMATCH" is logged

  Scenario: Payee match cannot be determined automatically
    Given the payee name on a state government check is illegible
    When the hold engine evaluates the check
    Then the item is routed to the manual review queue
    And no next-day assignment is made until a reviewer decides
```

### US-06: Next-day availability for cashier's, certified, teller's checks and postal money orders

**As an** Account Holder
**I want** bank-guaranteed instruments available the next business day
**So that** I can rely on them for time-sensitive payments.

```gherkin
Feature: Guaranteed instruments (§229.10(c)(1))

  Background:
    Given the account is RegCC_covered
    And configuration "REQUIRE_SPECIAL_DEPOSIT_SLIP" reflects the bank's disclosed policy

  Scenario Outline: Guaranteed instrument deposited to the payee's account
    Given a "<instrument>" for $<amount> deposited to the payee's account
    And the deposit banking day is "2026-11-20"
    When the hold engine evaluates the item
    Then the full amount is assigned availability date "2026-11-23"
    And reason code "NEXTDAY_GUARANTEED" is logged

    Examples:
      | instrument            | amount    |
      | Cashier's check       | 12,000.00 |
      | Certified check       | 4,500.00  |
      | Teller's check        | 2,250.00  |
      | Postal money order    | 700.00    |

  Scenario: Bank requires a special deposit slip and customer did not use one
    Given configuration "REQUIRE_SPECIAL_DEPOSIT_SLIP" is true
    And a cashier's check is deposited without the required slip or separate identification
    When the hold engine evaluates the item
    Then the item is not classified as next-day
    And reason code "SLIP_REQUIRED_NOT_MET" is logged
    And the item is evaluated under the minimum-amount rule (US-08)

  Scenario: Instrument deposited in a channel the bank does not designate for next-day treatment
    Given the deposit channel is not in the configured eligible channel list for this instrument type
    When the hold engine evaluates the item
    Then next-day treatment is not applied
    And the decision is recorded with the channel and the policy version used
```

### US-08: Apply the $275 minimum amount to other check deposits

**As an** Account Holder
**I want** the first $275 of my check deposits that are not otherwise next-day items available the next business day
**So that** I have guaranteed access to a baseline amount.

*Rules:* The minimum amount is applied once per banking day across the aggregate of other checks, and is withdrawable in any form, including cash. The remainder follows the existing availability schedule.

```gherkin
Feature: Minimum amount rule (§229.10(c)(1)(vii))

  Background:
    Given the account is RegCC_covered
    And MIN_AMOUNT_NEXT_DAY is $275 effective "2025-07-01"
    And configuration "MIN_AMOUNT_SCOPE" is "PER_ACCOUNT"

  Scenario Outline: Single check deposit
    Given a personal check for $<check_amount> deposited on banking day "2026-11-20"
    And the check qualifies for no other next-day rule
    When the hold engine evaluates the deposit
    Then $<next_day> is available on "2026-11-23"
    And $<remaining> is assigned the availability date from the standard schedule

    Examples:
      | check_amount | next_day | remaining |
      | 100.00       | 100.00   | 0.00      |
      | 275.00       | 275.00   | 0.00      |
      | 275.01       | 275.00   | 0.01      |
      | 1,000.00     | 275.00   | 725.00    |

  Scenario: Multiple checks on the same banking day share one minimum amount
    Given checks of $200.00 and $300.00 are deposited to the same account on one banking day
    And neither qualifies for another next-day rule
    When the hold engine evaluates the deposits
    Then a total of $275.00 is available on the next business day
    And $225.00 follows the standard schedule

  Scenario: Minimum amount is reduced by next-day items in the same deposit
    Given a deposit contains a $5,000.00 U.S. Treasury check and a $400.00 personal check
    When the hold engine evaluates the deposit
    Then the Treasury check is available next business day in full
    And the personal check is still eligible for the $275.00 minimum amount
    And $125.00 of the personal check follows the standard schedule

  Scenario: Minimum amount is applied once across deposits on the same banking day
    Given a $250.00 check is deposited at 09:00 and a $250.00 check at 12:00 on the same banking day
    When the hold engine evaluates both deposits
    Then the combined next-day amount does not exceed $275.00
    And the second deposit receives only the unused portion of the minimum amount

  Scenario: Scope set to per customer across accounts
    Given "MIN_AMOUNT_SCOPE" is "PER_CUSTOMER"
    And a customer deposits $250.00 in checks to each of two accounts on one banking day
    When the hold engine evaluates the deposits
    Then the combined next-day amount across both accounts does not exceed $275.00
```

### US-09: Post holds and releases so funds are available at the start of business

**As a** Deposit Operations Analyst
**I want** holds to release automatically at the start of business on the assigned date
**So that** customers can use funds exactly when the regulation requires.

```gherkin
Feature: Hold ledger and release timing

  Background:
    Given the start-of-business release job runs before the first customer-facing channel opens

  Scenario: Mixed deposit produces component-level holds
    Given a $2,000.00 deposit with $500.00 cash and a $1,500.00 personal check
    When the hold engine evaluates the deposit
    Then the ledger shows a $500.00 component with availability "2026-11-23"
    And a $275.00 component with availability "2026-11-23" from the minimum amount rule
    And a $1,225.00 component with the standard-schedule availability date
    And the component amounts sum to $2,000.00

  Scenario: Automatic release at start of business
    Given a hold component has availability date "2026-11-23"
    When the release job executes on "2026-11-23"
    Then the component moves to available balance before the first channel opens
    And a release event is recorded with a timestamp

  Scenario: Release job fails
    Given the release job has not completed by the configured SLA time
    When the SLA breach is detected
    Then a critical alert is sent to Deposit Operations and Compliance
    And a manual release procedure is triggered
    And the incident is recorded as a potential Reg CC timing exception

  Scenario: Component amounts do not reconcile
    Given component amounts sum to a value different from the deposit amount
    When the engine finalizes the evaluation
    Then the evaluation is rejected
    And the deposit is routed to manual review with reason code "RECON_MISMATCH"
```

### US-10: Effective-dated threshold configuration

**As a** Hold Engine Product Owner
**I want** regulatory thresholds stored with effective dates and approval workflow
**So that** the next five-year adjustment is a configuration change, not a code release.

```gherkin
Feature: Threshold configuration management

  Scenario: Engine selects the threshold in force on the deposit's banking day
    Given MIN_AMOUNT_NEXT_DAY has versions:
      | amount | effective_from |
      | 225    | 2020-07-01     |
      | 275    | 2025-07-01     |
    When a check deposit has banking day "2025-06-30"
    Then the engine uses $225
    When a check deposit has banking day "2025-07-01"
    Then the engine uses $275

  Scenario: Configuration change requires dual approval
    Given a user proposes a new MIN_AMOUNT_NEXT_DAY version
    When the user submits the change
    Then it remains inactive until a second authorized approver, who is not the submitter, approves it
    And Compliance is notified of the pending change

  Scenario: Re-evaluating historical deposits uses historical thresholds
    Given an audit request to replay a deposit from "2025-03-10"
    When the engine replays the evaluation
    Then it applies the thresholds and cutoff time in force on that date
    And the result matches the original recorded decision
```

### US-11: Define precedence with exception holds

**As a** Compliance Officer
**I want** a clear, tested interface between next-day availability and exception holds
**So that** exceptions can only extend availability where §229.13 allows, and never by accident.

```gherkin
Feature: Next-day availability and exception hold precedence

  Scenario: No exception applies
    Given no §229.13 exception is flagged for a deposit
    When the engine finalizes availability
    Then the availability dates from the next-day rules stand

  Scenario: Exception flag present from the exception-hold service
    Given the exception-hold service returns exception "<exception>" for the deposit
    When the engine finalizes availability
    Then the engine applies the exception's rules from the exception-hold service
    And records both the original §229.10 date and the extended date
    And the reason codes for both decisions are logged

    Examples:
      | exception        |
      | NEW_ACCOUNT      |
      | LARGE_DEPOSIT    |
      | REASONABLE_CAUSE |

  Scenario: Exception service unavailable
    Given the exception-hold service times out
    When a deposit is evaluated
    Then the engine applies the §229.10 availability dates only
    And flags the deposit for post-evaluation exception review within the same banking day
    And does not apply any extended hold without a documented exception
```

## 5. Non-functional requirements

- **Accuracy:** 100% of regression test cases in the US-02 through US-08 example tables pass before release.
- **Performance:** availability evaluation completes in under 500 ms at p95 for deposits with up to 50 items.
- **Retention:** audit records are retained per the bank's record-retention schedule and are queryable by deposit, account, date, and reason code.
- **Calendar controls:** the holiday calendar is loaded at least 12 months ahead and reviewed annually by Compliance.
- **Security:** configuration changes are role-restricted, dual-approved, and logged.

## 6. Definition of done (epic level)

1. All Gherkin scenarios automated and passing in CI.
2. Compliance sign-off on rule mapping, citation verification, and the open items in section 2.
3. Parallel run against production deposits shows no unexplained variance against the legacy engine, or each variance is documented and approved.
4. Operations runbook covers release-job failure and manual review queues.
5. Disclosure and hold-notice owners confirm customer-facing documents match engine behavior.

1. US-02 and US-10 (calendar and configuration foundation)
