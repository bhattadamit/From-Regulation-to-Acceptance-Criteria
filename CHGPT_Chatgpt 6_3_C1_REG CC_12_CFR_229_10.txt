# **Business Requirements: Regulation CC Next-Day Availability**

**Deposit Hold Engine Implementation — 12 CFR § 229.10**

Regulatory compliance

Deposit operations

User stories + Gherkin

## **1. Business objective**

Implement Regulation CC, 12 CFR § 229.10, in the bank's deposit hold engine to ensure that qualifying deposits receive the required next-day availability treatment.

The deposit hold engine must identify deposits eligible for next-day availability, calculate the legally required availability date, prevent conflicting holds from delaying required funds, and maintain an auditable record of the availability determination.

**Primary business outcome:** Customers receive timely access to funds as required by Regulation CC, while the bank retains the ability to apply permissible exceptions and holds under other applicable provisions of Regulation CC.

### **Regulatory scope**

The implementation should address:

| Regulation          | Requirement                                                                                           |
| ------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| § 229.10(a)         | Next-day availability for qualifying cash and electronic payments                                     |
| § 229.10(b)         | Next-day availability for U.S. Treasury checks and U.S. Postal Service money orders                   |
| § 229.10(c)         | Next-day availability for specified checks, subject to applicable eligibility requirements            |
| § 229.10(c)(1)(vii) | Next-day availability for the first $275 of aggregate check deposits, under the applicable conditions |

**Important implementation consideration:** The $275 threshold reflects the Regulation CC inflation adjustment effective July 1, 2025. It should be maintained as a configurable regulatory parameter rather than hardcoded.

The following stories distinguish mandatory next-day availability from the bank's ability to apply exceptions under §§ 229.13 and other applicable provisions.

## **2. Epics and user stories**

### **Epic 1 — Identify deposits eligible for next-day availability**

**US-01: Identify qualifying deposit types**

As a deposit operations system,\
I want to identify deposits that qualify for next-day availability under Regulation CC § 229.10,\
So that qualifying deposits receive the required funds availability treatment.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Identify deposits qualifying for next-day availability

  Scenario Outline: Identify qualifying deposit instruments
    Given a customer makes a deposit
    And the deposit instrument type is "<instrument_type>"
    And all applicable eligibility conditions are satisfied
    When the deposit is evaluated by the hold engine
    Then the deposit must be classified as "NEXT_DAY_ELIGIBLE"
    And the regulatory classification must reference "12 CFR 229.10"

    Examples:
      | instrument_type              |
      | Cash                          |
      | Electronic Payment            |
      | US Treasury Check             |
      | US Postal Service Money Order |
      | Cashier's Check               |
      | Certified Check               |
      | Teller's Check                |
      | Qualifying State or Local Gov Check |
```

**US-02: Validate eligibility conditions for qualifying checks**

As a deposit operations system,\
I want to validate the eligibility conditions associated with qualifying checks,\
So that next-day availability is granted only when regulatory requirements are satisfied.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Validate qualifying check eligibility

  Scenario: Qualifying cashier's check deposited in person
    Given a customer deposits a cashier's check
    And the deposit is made in person to a bank employee
    And the deposit account meets applicable eligibility requirements
    When the hold engine evaluates the deposit
    Then the cashier's check must qualify for next-day availability

  Scenario: Cashier's check deposited through an ineligible channel
    Given a customer deposits a cashier's check through a deposit channel
    And the channel does not satisfy the applicable regulatory conditions
    When the hold engine evaluates the deposit
    Then the deposit must not automatically receive the in-person cashier's check treatment
    And the applicable alternative availability rules must be evaluated

  Scenario: State or local government check eligibility
    Given a customer deposits a state or local government check
    When the hold engine evaluates the deposit
    Then the issuing government entity's location must be validated
    And the bank's applicable geographic eligibility requirements must be evaluated
    And next-day availability must be assigned only when regulatory conditions are satisfied
```

### **Epic 2 — Calculate next-day availability**

**US-03: Calculate the next-business-day availability date**

As a deposit operations system,\
I want to calculate the next-business-day availability date for qualifying deposits,\
So that customers receive access to funds within the required regulatory timeframe.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Calculate next-day availability

  Scenario: Qualifying deposit made on a banking day
    Given a qualifying deposit is received on Monday
    And Monday is a banking day
    And the deposit is received before the applicable cutoff time
    When the hold engine calculates the availability date
    Then the required availability date must be Tuesday

  Scenario: Qualifying deposit made before a weekend
    Given a qualifying deposit is received on Friday
    And Friday is a banking day
    When the hold engine calculates the availability date
    Then the required availability date must be Monday
    And Saturday and Sunday must not be treated as banking days

  Scenario: Qualifying deposit made before a banking holiday
    Given a qualifying deposit is received on the banking day before a federal banking holiday
    When the hold engine calculates the availability date
    Then the required availability date must be the next banking day
    And the holiday must not be counted as a banking day

  Scenario: Deposit received after the applicable cutoff
    Given a qualifying deposit is submitted after the bank's applicable cutoff time
    When the hold engine determines the deposit receipt date
    Then the deposit must be assigned to the next banking day
    And the availability date must be calculated from that banking day
```

**US-04: Apply availability rules to electronic payments**

As a deposit operations system,\
I want to distinguish electronic payments from check deposits,\
So that electronic payments receive the treatment required under § 229.10(a).

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Electronic payment availability

  Scenario: Electronic payment received by the bank
    Given an electronic payment is received by the bank
    And the payment qualifies under Regulation CC
    When the hold engine processes the payment
    Then the payment must be classified as an electronic payment
    And funds must be available no later than the business day
        following the banking day of receipt
    And check deposit hold rules must not be applied

  Scenario: Electronic payment received on a non-banking day
    Given an electronic payment is received on a non-banking day
    When the hold engine determines the applicable receipt banking day
    Then the payment must be assigned the appropriate banking day
    And availability must be calculated using the applicable regulatory deadline
```

**US-05: Prevent holds from extending required next-day availability**

As a deposit operations system,\
I want to prevent standard deposit holds from overriding mandatory next-day availability,\
So that the bank does not inadvertently delay funds beyond the regulatory deadline.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Protect required next-day availability

  Scenario: Standard hold conflicts with next-day availability
    Given a deposit qualifies for next-day availability
    And a standard deposit hold would make funds available later
    When the hold engine applies the availability rules
    Then the required next-day availability must take precedence
    And the applicable next-day funds must not be delayed by the standard hold

  Scenario: Deposit has a permissible regulatory exception
    Given a deposit qualifies for next-day availability
    And a legally permissible exception under Regulation CC applies
    When the hold engine evaluates the exception
    Then the exception must be evaluated under its applicable regulatory requirements
    And the engine must not apply an exception without satisfying its required conditions
    And the resulting availability schedule must be retained for audit
```

### **Epic 3 — Apply the $275 next-day availability requirement**

**US-06: Make the first $275 available next day**

As a deposit operations system,\
I want to make the first $275 of a customer's aggregate daily check deposits available on the next business day when required by § 229.10(c)(1)(vii),\
So that the bank complies with the minimum next-day availability requirement.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: First $275 next-day availability

  Scenario: Single check deposit greater than $275
    Given a customer deposits a check for $1,000
    And the deposit is subject to the first $275 requirement
    And no permissible exception applies
    When the hold engine calculates availability
    Then $275 must be available no later than the next business day
    And the remaining $725 must be evaluated under the applicable availability rules

  Scenario: Check deposit less than $275
    Given a customer deposits a qualifying check for $200
    And the deposit is subject to the first $275 requirement
    And no permissible exception applies
    When the hold engine calculates availability
    Then the entire $200 must be available no later than the next business day

  Scenario: Check deposit equal to $275
    Given a customer deposits a check for $275
    And the deposit is subject to the first $275 requirement
    And no permissible exception applies
    When the hold engine calculates availability
    Then the entire $275 must be available no later than the next business day

  Scenario: Multiple check deposits on the same banking day
    Given a customer makes multiple check deposits on the same banking day
    And the aggregate deposits total $1,000
    And the deposits are subject to the first $275 requirement
    And no permissible exception applies
    When the hold engine calculates availability
    Then the first $275 of the aggregate deposits must be available
         no later than the next business day
    And the remaining $725 must be evaluated under the applicable availability rules
```

**US-07: Aggregate deposits across multiple transactions**

As a deposit operations system,\
I want to aggregate eligible check deposits made by the same customer on the same banking day,\
So that the $275 minimum availability requirement is correctly applied across multiple deposit transactions.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Aggregate daily check deposits

  Scenario: Multiple deposits across different channels
    Given a customer makes a $100 check deposit at a branch
    And the customer makes a $200 check deposit through an ATM
    And both deposits are assigned to the same banking day
    When the hold engine calculates the daily aggregate
    Then the aggregate amount must be $300
    And the applicable first $275 requirement must be satisfied
    And the engine must not independently apply a separate $275 minimum
        to each transaction

  Scenario: Deposits across different banking days
    Given a customer makes a $200 check deposit on Monday
    And the customer makes a $200 check deposit on Tuesday
    When the hold engine calculates daily aggregate deposits
    Then Monday's and Tuesday's deposits must be evaluated separately
    And the applicable minimum availability requirement must be
        calculated for each banking day

  Scenario: Multiple deposits with different availability classifications
    Given a customer makes multiple deposits on the same banking day
    And some deposits qualify for full next-day availability
    And other deposits are subject to the first $275 requirement
    When the hold engine calculates aggregate availability
    Then funds already receiving full next-day availability must be
        accounted for correctly
    And the customer must receive at least the required next-day amount
    And the same funds must not be counted twice
```

### **Epic 4 — Configure banking calendars and cutoff times**

**US-08: Maintain banking day and holiday calendars**

As a deposit operations administrator,\
I want the hold engine to use an approved banking calendar,\
So that next-day availability calculations exclude non-banking days.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Banking calendar management

  Scenario: Weekend calculation
    Given Saturday and Sunday are configured as non-banking days
    When a qualifying deposit is received on Friday
    Then the next banking day must be Monday

  Scenario: Federal banking holiday calculation
    Given a federal banking holiday is configured in the calendar
    When a qualifying deposit is received on the preceding banking day
    Then the holiday must be excluded from the availability calculation

  Scenario: Calendar update
    Given an authorized administrator updates the banking calendar
    When the updated calendar becomes effective
    Then subsequent availability calculations must use the updated calendar
    And the previous calendar version must remain available for audit
```

**US-09: Apply deposit channel cutoff times**

As a deposit operations system,\
I want to apply the bank's approved cutoff times by deposit channel,\
So that the correct banking day is assigned to each deposit.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Deposit cutoff processing

  Scenario: Deposit received before cutoff
    Given a customer submits a deposit before the applicable cutoff
    When the deposit is processed
    Then the deposit must be assigned to the current banking day

  Scenario: Deposit received after cutoff
    Given a customer submits a deposit after the applicable cutoff
    When the deposit is processed
    Then the deposit must be assigned to the next banking day

  Scenario: Different channels have different cutoff times
    Given branch, ATM, and mobile deposit channels have configured cutoff times
    When deposits are received through each channel
    Then the applicable channel-specific cutoff must be used
    And the cutoff must comply with Regulation CC requirements
```

### **Epic 5 — Manage regulatory exceptions**

**US-10: Evaluate permissible exceptions to next-day availability**

As a deposit operations system,\
I want to evaluate permissible Regulation CC exceptions,\
So that the bank can apply legally authorized holds without violating regulatory requirements.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Regulation CC exception processing

  Scenario: No exception applies
    Given a deposit qualifies for next-day availability
    And no permissible exception applies
    When the hold engine evaluates the deposit
    Then the required next-day availability must be assigned

  Scenario: New account exception
    Given a customer account meets the regulatory definition of a new account
    And the new account exception under Section 229.13(a) applies
    When the deposit is evaluated
    Then the applicable new account availability rules must be applied
    And the exception reason must be recorded

  Scenario: Large deposit exception
    Given a customer makes aggregate check deposits exceeding the
         applicable large-deposit threshold
    And the conditions under Section 229.13(b) are satisfied
    When the hold engine evaluates the deposit
    Then the large-deposit exception may be applied
    And the applicable exception limits must be enforced
    And the exception reason must be recorded

  Scenario: Repeated overdraft exception
    Given the customer's account meets the applicable repeated
         overdraft conditions under Section 229.13(c)
    When the hold engine evaluates the deposit
    Then the repeated overdraft exception may be applied
    And the applicable regulatory limits must be enforced

  Scenario: Exception conditions are not satisfied
    Given a deposit is being considered for a Regulation CC exception
    And the applicable exception conditions are not satisfied
    When the hold engine evaluates the exception
    Then the exception must not be applied
    And the deposit must receive its otherwise applicable availability treatment
```

**US-11: Calculate and enforce exception hold periods**

As a deposit operations system,\
I want to calculate permissible exception hold periods,\
So that exceptions do not exceed regulatory limits.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Exception hold duration

  Scenario: Exception hold is applied
    Given a qualifying Regulation CC exception has been established
    When the hold engine calculates the availability date
    Then the applicable exception period must be calculated
    And the appropriate banking calendar must be used
    And the resulting date must not exceed the applicable regulatory limit

  Scenario: Exception hold exceeds regulatory limit
    Given an exception hold calculation produces an availability date
        beyond the applicable regulatory maximum
    When the hold engine validates the calculated date
    Then the calculation must be rejected
    And the maximum permissible availability date must be enforced
    And a validation event must be logged
```

*Note: Exception processing must incorporate the applicable requirements of § 229.13, including customer-specific eligibility, dollar limits, and required notices. These rules should be implemented and tested separately from the standard § 229.10 treatment.*

## **7. Definition of done**

A story is considered complete when:

- Business requirements are approved by Deposit Operations and Compliance.
- Regulatory eligibility logic is implemented and unit tested.
- Banking-day and cutoff calculations are validated.
- The $275 aggregate minimum availability requirement is tested.
- Applicable exception processing is tested.
- Core banking availability integration is validated.
- Customer available balances are reconciled against hold engine calculations.
- Audit records and compliance reports are validated.
- End-to-end regression testing is complete.
- Compliance signs off on regulatory interpretation and production readiness.

## **8. Senior business analyst recommendations**

I would highlight five implementation risks for the project steering committee:

1. **Do not treat all checks as next-day eligible.** The instrument classification must distinguish checks qualifying under § 229.10(c) from ordinary checks subject only to the first-dollar requirement.
2. **Do not apply $275 independently to every transaction.** The first-dollar requirement involves aggregate daily check deposits. The engine must avoid both under- and over-crediting.
3. **Separate regulatory eligibility from exception processing.** A qualifying check and an eligible exception are separate determinations. The engine must preserve both decisions.
4. **Use effective-dated regulatory parameters.** This is especially important for future inflation adjustments to Regulation CC thresholds.
5. **Test actual customer access, not just the calculated hold date.** A correct hold engine calculation does not guarantee compliance if the core banking system does not release the funds on time.

**Regulatory reference:** [12 CFR § 229.10 — Next-day availability](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/chapter-II/subchapter-A/part-229/section-229.10?utm_source=chatgpt.com) and [12 CFR § 229.13 — Exceptions](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/chapter-II/subchapter-A/part-229/section-229.13?utm_source=chatgpt.com).

*Implementation caveat:* This is a business requirements baseline, not a legal interpretation. Compliance and Legal should validate the precise treatment of each instrument, deposit channel, exception, cutoff, and availability deadline before the requirements are baselined.
