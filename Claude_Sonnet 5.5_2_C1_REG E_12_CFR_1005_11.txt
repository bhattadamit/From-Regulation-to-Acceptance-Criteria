# Regulation E Error Resolution (12 CFR 1005.11): User Stories and Acceptance Criteria

**Platform:** Card and ACH disputes platform
**Prepared:** November 20, 2026
**Status:** Draft for Compliance, Legal, Payments Ops and Engineering review

> This is a BA requirements draft, not legal advice. Compliance should validate every citation against the current eCFR text and the Official Interpretations (Supp. I, comment 11) before build sign-off.

## 1. Scope, assumptions and open questions

**In scope:** Consumer accounts, debit card (POS, ATM, online) and ACH (debit and credit) electronic fund transfers (EFTs) covered by Regulation E.

**Assumptions**
- A1. "Business day" follows 1005.2(d) and is driven by a bank-maintained calendar, not a hardcoded federal holiday list.
- A2. 10-business-day, 45-day and 90-day clocks start when the bank receives the notice. Business-day periods start counting the next business day. 45 and 90 are calendar days.
- A3. Interest on provisional credit is paid only where the account agreement or applicable law requires it. This is a configurable flag.
- A4. Network rules (Visa, Mastercard, NACHA) are treated as downstream constraints. Their deadlines are configurable and are not restated here as law.
- A5. Credit card accounts are handled under Regulation Z (1005.12(a) and 1005.11(f) interplay) and are out of scope. Debit-card-linked overdraft-line scenarios need a Compliance ruling.

**Open questions**
- Q1. Does the bank require written confirmation of oral notices, and at what address?
- Q2. Are prepaid, payroll card and government benefit accounts in scope? They carry different rules (1005.18 and 1005.15).
- Q3. Is there a fraud-risk or provisional-credit hold policy beyond the regulatory exceptions? Reg E has no general "suspected fraud" exception to provisional credit.
- Q4. What is the system of record for the ledger, and is it real-time or batch? This drives the "1 business day" correction story.

## 2. Regulatory clock reference

| Event | Rule | Default | Variant |
|---|---|---|---|
| Consumer notice window | 1005.11(b) | 60 days from the statement on which the error first appears | Extended for extenuating circumstances (e.g., hospitalization, extended travel) |
| Written confirmation of oral notice | 1005.11(b) | 10 business days, if the bank told the consumer at the time of the oral notice | None |
| Investigate and report | 1005.11(c)(1) | 10 business days | Up to 45 days if provisional credit is given |
| Provisional credit | 1005.11(c)(2) | Within 10 business days of notice | 20 business days for new accounts |
| Notify consumer of provisional credit | 1005.11(c)(2) | Within 2 business days of crediting | None |
| Extended investigation | 1005.11(c)(3) | 45 days | 90 days for new accounts, POS debits and foreign-initiated transfers |
| Correct confirmed error | 1005.11(d)(1) | 1 business day after determination | None |
| Report results | 1005.11(d) | 3 business days after completing the investigation | None |
| Honor debits after reversing provisional credit | 1005.11(d)(2) | 5 business days after notifying the consumer | None |
| Record retention | 1005.13 | 2 years | None |

**Worked example (notice received Friday, 11/20/2026; bank closed Thursday 11/26):**
- 10 business days ends **Mon 12/7/2026**.
- 45 days ends **Mon 1/4/2027**.
- 90 days ends **Thu 2/18/2027**.

## 4. User stories

### Epic E1: Intake and eligibility

#### US-01: Log an error notice and start regulatory clocks
**Reg ref:** 1005.11(b), (c)

**As a** dispute intake specialist
**I want** every oral, written or electronic error notice recorded with its receipt timestamp
**So that** all Reg E clocks start from the correct date and nothing is missed.

```gherkin
Feature: Error notice intake

  Background:
    Given the bank business-day calendar is loaded

  Scenario: Oral notice with enough information starts the clocks
    Given a consumer calls and provides name, account number, the transaction, and why they believe it is an error
    When the specialist saves the case
    Then the system records the notice receipt date and time in the bank's time zone
    And the system creates a Reg E case with the channel "oral"
    And the system calculates the 10-business-day, 45-day and 90-day dates

  Scenario Outline: Notice received through any channel
    When a consumer submits an error notice via "<channel>"
    Then the case is created with receipt time equal to the time the bank received it
    Examples:
      | channel            |
      | phone              |
      | branch             |
      | secure message     |
      | mail               |
      | online dispute form|

  Scenario: Notice missing identifying information
    Given the consumer cannot be identified or the account cannot be determined
    When the specialist attempts to save the case
    Then the system blocks the save and lists the missing items
    And the system records the attempted contact in the audit log

  Scenario: Notice received after hours
    Given a notice arrives at 11:30 PM on a business day
    Then the receipt date is recorded as the actual date received
    And the case is not back-dated or forward-dated
```

#### US-02: Classify a contact as a Reg E error or a non-error inquiry
**Reg ref:** 1005.11(a)

**As a** dispute intake specialist
**I want** guided classification of the consumer's claim
**So that** every qualifying error is handled under Reg E and routine inquiries are not.

```gherkin
Feature: Error classification

  Scenario Outline: Qualifying error types
    Given an open intake record
    When the specialist selects "<error_type>"
    Then the case is flagged "Reg E error resolution"
    Examples:
      | error_type                                                      |
      | Unauthorized EFT                                                |
      | Incorrect EFT to or from the account                            |
      | EFT omitted from periodic statement                             |
      | Bank computational or bookkeeping error                         |
      | Incorrect amount dispensed by ATM or electronic terminal        |
      | EFT not properly identified on statement or receipt             |
      | Request for documentation or clarification about an EFT         |

  Scenario Outline: Contacts that are not errors
    When the specialist selects "<contact_type>"
    Then the case is not opened as a Reg E error
    And the system displays "Not a Reg E error. Route to servicing."
    Examples:
      | contact_type                                              |
      | Routine balance inquiry                                   |
      | Duplicate copy request for tax or recordkeeping purposes  |

  Scenario: Specialist is unsure
    When the specialist selects "Unsure"
    Then the system defaults to opening a Reg E case
    And a supervisor review task is created

  Scenario: Classification is audited
    When a classification is saved or changed
    Then the user ID, timestamp, prior value and new value are logged
```

#### US-03: Validate timeliness and extend for extenuating circumstances
**Reg ref:** 1005.11(b) and Official Interpretation 11(b)

**As a** dispute analyst
**I want** the system to test the 60-day window and support documented extensions
**So that** timely claims are never rejected and late claims are handled consistently.

```gherkin
Feature: 60-day notice window

  Scenario: Notice within 60 days
    Given the periodic statement showing the error was sent on 2026-10-15
    And the notice was received on 2026-11-20
    Then the system marks the notice "Timely"

  Scenario: Notice after 60 days with no extenuating circumstances
    Given the notice was received 75 days after the statement was sent
    When the analyst records no extenuating circumstance
    Then the system marks the notice "Untimely"
    And the case routes to supervisor review before any denial
    And the system does not auto-close the case

  Scenario: Extenuating circumstance extends the window
    Given the notice is more than 60 days after the statement
    When the analyst records "Extended hospitalization" with supporting notes
    Then the system marks the notice "Timely by extension"
    And the case proceeds under the standard Reg E clocks

  Scenario: Statement date is unknown
    Given the statement-sent date cannot be found
    Then the system treats the notice as timely
    And creates a task to investigate the missing statement record

  Scenario: Later transfers in a series
    Given the consumer reports an error on an earlier statement that is out of window
    And later unauthorized transfers on subsequent statements are in window
    Then the system evaluates timeliness separately for each transfer
```

#### US-04: Request written confirmation of an oral notice
**Reg ref:** 1005.11(b)

**As a** dispute intake specialist
**I want** the system to track written confirmation when the bank requires it
**So that** the bank preserves its right to withhold provisional credit only where it complied with the disclosure condition.

```gherkin
Feature: Written confirmation of oral notice

  Scenario: Requirement disclosed at the time of the oral notice
    Given the bank policy "require written confirmation" is enabled
    When the specialist completes an oral intake
    Then the system prompts the specialist to read the confirmation script and mailing address
    And the specialist must check "Requirement and address disclosed" to save
    And the system starts a 10-business-day confirmation clock

  Scenario: Requirement was not disclosed
    Given the specialist did not check "Requirement and address disclosed"
    Then the system sets the confirmation flag to "Not enforceable"
    And provisional credit and investigation proceed without waiting for confirmation

  Scenario: Confirmation received on time
    Given a written confirmation arrives within 10 business days
    Then the system attaches it to the case
    And the system marks confirmation "Received"

  Scenario: Confirmation not received
    Given the confirmation clock expires with nothing received
    Then the system marks confirmation "Not received"
    And the case is eligible for the provisional credit exception in US-08
    And the system does not auto-close the investigation
```

#### US-05: Business-day calendar and deadline calculator
**Reg ref:** 1005.2(d), 1005.11(c)

**As a** compliance officer
**I want** a single configurable deadline engine
**So that** every story uses consistent, auditable dates.

```gherkin
Feature: Regulatory deadline engine

  Scenario: Ten business days with a holiday
    Given the bank is closed on 2026-11-26
    And a notice is received on Friday 2026-11-20
    Then the 10-business-day date is Monday 2026-12-07

  Scenario: Calendar-day periods
    Given a notice received on 2026-11-20
    Then the 45-day date is 2027-01-04
    And the 90-day date is 2027-02-18

  Scenario: Calendar change recalculates open cases
    When Operations adds a bank closure date
    Then all open cases with deadlines affected by that date are recalculated
    And the changes are written to the audit log
    And a report lists any case whose deadline moved

  Scenario: Notice received on a non-business day
    Given a notice is received on Saturday
    Then the receipt date is Saturday
    And business-day counting starts on the next business day
```

### Epic E2: Provisional credit

#### US-06: Issue provisional credit within the required period
**Reg ref:** 1005.11(c)(2) and (c)(3)

**As a** dispute analyst
**I want** provisional credit queued and posted automatically when the investigation will exceed 10 business days
**So that** consumers have use of their funds as required.

```gherkin
Feature: Provisional credit

  Scenario: Credit when the investigation is not complete in 10 business days
    Given a timely Reg E case with the amount alleged in error of $250.00
    And the investigation is not complete by business day 8
    When the nightly job runs
    Then the system creates a provisional credit of $250.00 to the consumer account
    And the credit posts no later than business day 10
    And the case is marked "Provisional credit issued" with date and amount

  Scenario: New account extends the credit deadline
    Given the first deposit was made fewer than 30 days before the notice
    Then the provisional credit deadline is 20 business days
    And the investigation deadline is 90 days

  Scenario: Credit includes interest where required
    Given the "pay interest on provisional credit" setting is on
    When the credit posts
    Then the system includes interest from the credit date per the account agreement

  Scenario: Investigation completes early
    Given the bank completes the investigation and corrects the error by business day 10
    Then the system does not require a provisional credit
    And the case moves to US-13

  Scenario: Credit amount equals the alleged amount
    Given the consumer alleges multiple transfers
    Then the system credits the total of the transfers alleged in error
    And each transfer is linked to the credit entry

  Scenario: Deadline at risk
    Given business day 8 has passed with no credit posted and the case is not resolved
    Then the system raises a high-priority alert to the team lead
```

#### US-07: Notify the consumer of provisional credit
**Reg ref:** 1005.11(c)(2)

**As a** compliance officer
**I want** consumers told about the provisional credit within 2 business days
**So that** the bank complies with the notice requirement.

```gherkin
Feature: Provisional credit notice

  Scenario: Notice generated after credit posts
    Given a provisional credit posted on 2026-12-03
    Then the system generates a consumer notice with the credit amount, date, and the consumer's right to full use of the funds
    And the notice is sent no later than 2 business days after posting

  Scenario: Consumer contact preference
    Given the consumer has electronic delivery consent on file
    Then the notice is delivered by the consented electronic channel
    And otherwise by mail or by oral notice recorded in the case

  Scenario: Delivery failure
    Given an email or mail notice bounces
    Then the system creates a follow-up task within 1 business day
    And logs the failed attempt
```

*Note: The two-business-day oral-notice option is implemented only if Compliance confirms it for the bank's disclosure approach.*

#### US-08: Withhold provisional credit when written confirmation is not received
**Reg ref:** 1005.11(c)(2)

**As a** dispute analyst
**I want** the platform to apply the confirmation exception correctly
**So that** the bank neither over-credits nor withholds credit improperly.

```gherkin
Feature: Provisional credit exception

  Scenario: Exception applies
    Given requirement disclosure was recorded under US-04
    And the confirmation clock expired with nothing received
    Then the system suppresses provisional credit
    And the system records the reason code "WRITTEN_CONFIRMATION_NOT_RECEIVED"
    And the investigation continues within the 45/90-day limit

  Scenario: Exception blocked when disclosure was not recorded
    Given requirement disclosure was not recorded
    When an analyst attempts to suppress provisional credit
    Then the system blocks the action

  Scenario: Late confirmation arrives
    Given provisional credit was suppressed
    And written confirmation arrives after the 10 business days
    Then the system asks the analyst to confirm whether to credit
    And the decision and its rationale are logged
```

*Open item:* The late-confirmation policy needs a Compliance decision.

### Epic E3: Investigation

#### US-09: Investigation workflow and record review
**Reg ref:** 1005.11(c)(4)

**As a** dispute investigator
**I want** a structured checklist for the investigation
**So that** every case documents a good-faith, reasonable investigation.

```gherkin
Feature: Investigation workflow

  Scenario: Required review steps
    Given a case is in "Investigation"
    Then the system requires the investigator to complete: review of bank records for the transfer, review of the account history, and review of any authorization on file
    And the case cannot be closed until all required steps are completed

  Scenario: Third-party transfer with no agreement
    Given the alleged error concerns a transfer to or from a third party
    And there is no agreement between the bank and the third party for that type of EFT
    When the investigator selects "Limited record review"
    Then the system accepts review of the bank's own records only
    And requires the investigator to record the basis for the limitation

  Scenario: Third-party agreement exists
    Given the transfer is under a network or processor agreement
    Then the system does not allow "Limited record review"
    And the checklist adds a network or originator inquiry

  Scenario: Investigator adds notes and evidence
    When the investigator uploads evidence
    Then the system stores it with user, time and a document type
```

#### US-10: Apply the 90-day extended investigation period
**Reg ref:** 1005.11(c)(3)

**As a** dispute analyst
**I want** the system to detect when the extended period applies
**So that** investigation deadlines are neither too short nor too long.

```gherkin
Feature: 45 versus 90 day investigation period

  Scenario Outline: Extended period triggers
    Given a timely Reg E case with trigger "<trigger>"
    Then the investigation deadline is 90 days from notice receipt
    Examples:
      | trigger                                          |
      | New account (first deposit under 30 days prior)  |
      | Point-of-sale debit transaction                  |
      | Transfer initiated outside the United States     |

  Scenario: No trigger
    Given none of the extended-period triggers apply
    Then the investigation deadline is 45 days from notice receipt

  Scenario: Trigger data missing
    Given the transaction channel or origin country is not populated
    Then the system defaults the deadline to 45 days
    And a data-quality task is created
```

#### US-11: Card and ACH dispute handling integration
**Reg ref:** 1005.11(c); network rules (configurable)

**As a** dispute investigator
**I want** the Reg E case to drive network actions (card chargeback or ACH return)
**So that** recoveries run in parallel without affecting consumer deadlines.

```gherkin
Feature: Network actions are decoupled from consumer clocks

  Scenario: Card chargeback initiated
    Given a debit card case alleging an unauthorized POS transaction
    When the investigator selects "Initiate chargeback"
    Then the system submits the chargeback within the configured network deadline
    And the Reg E clocks continue unchanged

  Scenario: ACH unauthorized debit return
    Given an ACH debit alleged to be unauthorized
    And the consumer has signed a written statement of unauthorized debit
    When the investigator selects "Return"
    Then the system prepares the return with the configured unauthorized return reason code and the consumer's statement attached
    And the case records the return deadline

  Scenario: ACH return window missed
    Given the ACH return window has passed
    Then the system tells the investigator that a return is no longer available
    And the Reg E obligations to the consumer remain unchanged

  Scenario: Network recovery is pending at the deadline
    Given the Reg E investigation deadline is reached while the network claim is unresolved
    Then the system requires a consumer determination
    And does not wait for the network outcome
```

*Open item:* Payments Operations to confirm return reason codes (e.g., R10, R07, R11), the written-statement requirements and the current network deadlines.

#### US-12: Handle repeat assertions of the same error
**Reg ref:** 1005.11(e)

**As a** dispute analyst
**I want** to detect repeat claims
**So that** the bank applies the repeat-assertion rule only when it is permitted.

```gherkin
Feature: Reassertion of error

  Scenario: Same error asserted again
    Given a closed case for transfer X with a completed investigation and a written determination sent
    When the consumer reasserts the same error on transfer X
    Then the system flags the new contact as a possible repeat
    And the analyst can mark "No further investigation required"
    And the system generates a response notice referencing the earlier determination

  Scenario: New facts or a different transfer
    Given the consumer asserts a different transfer or provides new information indicating a different error
    Then the system opens a new Reg E case with new clocks

  Scenario: Duplicate-detection logic
    Then the system matches on account, transaction reference, amount and date
    And an analyst must confirm the match before any case is declined
```

### Epic E4: Resolution and communications

#### US-13: Error confirmed: correct, make whole and notify
**Reg ref:** 1005.11(c)(1), (d)(1)

**As a** dispute analyst
**I want** the correction and notice to follow automatically once an error is confirmed
**So that** consumers are made whole within the required timeframe.

```gherkin
Feature: Error confirmed

  Scenario: Correction posted within 1 business day
    Given the investigator marks "Error occurred" on 2026-12-02
    Then the system posts the correcting entry no later than 2026-12-03
    And the entry includes any interest and fees the bank imposed as a result of the error

  Scenario: Provisional credit becomes final
    Given a provisional credit exists
    When an error is confirmed
    Then the system marks the credit "Final"
    And no second credit is posted

  Scenario: Partial error
    Given the consumer alleged $500.00 and the investigator confirms $300.00
    Then the system finalizes the credit for $300.00
    And processes the remaining $200.00 under US-14

  Scenario: Result notice
    Then the system sends the consumer a notice within 3 business days after the investigation is completed
    And the notice states the result and that any provisional credit is now final

  Scenario: Fees and overdrafts caused by the error
    Given the error resulted in overdraft or NSF fees
    Then the system reverses those fees and logs each reversal
```

#### US-14: No error found: explain, reverse provisional credit and protect the consumer
**Reg ref:** 1005.11(d)(2)

**As a** dispute analyst
**I want** compliant denial handling
**So that** consumers get a full written explanation and a short protection period before funds are debited.

```gherkin
Feature: No error or different error found

  Scenario: Written explanation
    Given the investigator marks "No error" or "Different error"
    Then the system generates a written explanation of findings
    And the notice tells the consumer they may request the documents relied on
    And the notice is sent within 3 business days after the investigation is completed

  Scenario: Reversal of provisional credit
    Given a provisional credit of $250.00 exists
    When the denial notice is generated
    Then the notice states the date and amount to be debited
    And the system does not debit before the notice is sent

  Scenario: Five-business-day honor period
    Given the notice was sent on 2026-12-10
    Then the system honors checks, drafts and other items that would have been paid but for the reversal, for 5 business days after the notice
    And the system suppresses overdraft and NSF fees caused solely by the reversal during that period, subject to Compliance confirmation

  Scenario: Debit posted after the honor period
    When the 5 business days have passed
    Then the system posts the reversal
    And records the reversal date and amount on the case

  Scenario: Supervisor approval
    Then the denial requires a second-level approval before the notice is released
```

### Epic E5: Controls and audit

#### US-17: SLA monitoring and breach alerts
**Reg ref:** 1005.11(c), (d)

**As a** dispute operations manager
**I want** real-time visibility into approaching and missed regulatory deadlines
**So that** I can prevent violations.

```gherkin
Feature: Deadline monitoring

  Scenario Outline: Escalating alerts
    Given an open Reg E case
    When the "<deadline>" is within <threshold>
    Then the system alerts the assigned analyst and the team lead
    Examples:
      | deadline                          | threshold          |
      | provisional credit (10/20 BD)     | 2 business days    |
      | result notice (3 BD)              | 1 business day     |
      | investigation end (45/90 days)    | 5 calendar days    |

  Scenario: Management dashboard
```

#### US-18: Audit trail and record retention
**Reg ref:** 1005.13

**As a** compliance officer
**I want** an immutable record of every action
**So that** the bank can evidence compliance to examiners.

```gherkin
Feature: Audit and retention

  Scenario: Full activity logging
    When any user or system job changes a case
    Then the system logs the actor, timestamp, field, old value and new value
    And log entries cannot be edited or deleted

  Scenario: Retention
    Then all case records, notices, evidence and logs are retained for at least 2 years from the date of the required action

  Scenario: Legal hold
    Given Legal places a hold on a case
    Then the system prevents purge until the hold is lifted
```

#### US-19: Unauthorized transfer liability determination
**Reg ref:** 1005.6

**As a** dispute analyst
**I want** the platform to calculate consumer liability on confirmed unauthorized EFTs
**So that** consumers are charged only what the regulation allows.

```gherkin
Feature: Consumer liability limits

  Scenario: Reported within 2 business days of learning of card loss or theft
    Given an unauthorized transfer made with a lost or stolen access device
    And the consumer notified the bank within 2 business days of learning of the loss
    Then liability is capped at the lesser of $50 or the amount of the unauthorized transfers before notice

  Scenario: Reported after 2 business days
    Given the consumer notified after 2 business days but within 60 days of the statement
    Then liability is capped at the lesser of $500 or the applicable amount per the tiered rule

  Scenario: Unauthorized transfers shown on a statement and not reported within 60 days
    Then the system flags potential extended liability for transfers occurring after the 60 days
    And requires analyst and supervisor review before applying

  Scenario: Extenuating circumstances
    Given the delay was due to extenuating circumstances
    Then the system applies the extended period under Reg E
    And records the supporting information

  Scenario: Compliance configuration
    Then Compliance owns the rules table, and the platform applies any bank policy of zero liability as the more favorable rule
```

*Note:* This story should be validated against 1005.6 and its commentary. The tiered rule has several nuances, and the rules above are a simplification for scoping.

## 5. Non-functional requirements (apply to all stories)

- **NFR-1 Accuracy:** The deadline engine passes a regression suite of at least 200 date cases, including holidays, weekends and year-end.
- **NFR-2 Availability:** Intake and deadline jobs run daily, including after weekend and holiday outages, with catch-up processing.
- **NFR-3 Security:** Role-based access. Only authorized users can approve denials or suppress provisional credit. PII is encrypted at rest and in transit.
- **NFR-4 Configurability:** Business-day calendar, interest flag, written confirmation policy and template versions are all configuration, not code.
- **NFR-5 Traceability:** Each story is mapped to its Reg E paragraph in a requirements traceability matrix for the compliance testing plan.
- **NFR-6 Reconciliation:** Provisional credits, finalizations and reversals reconcile daily to the general ledger.
