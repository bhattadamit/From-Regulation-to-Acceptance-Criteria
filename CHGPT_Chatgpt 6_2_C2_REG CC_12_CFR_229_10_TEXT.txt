# **Business Requirements: Regulation CC Next-Day Availability — 12 CFR § 229.10**

**Role:** Senior Business Analyst\
**Business capability:** Deposit Hold Engine\
**Regulatory scope:** 12 CFR § 229.10(a), (b), and (c)\
**Objective:** Implement regulatory funds availability rules for cash deposits, electronic payments, specified check deposits, and the applicable $275 next-day availability threshold.

## **1. Business overview**

The deposit hold engine must determine the applicable funds availability deadline based on the deposit type, deposit method, banking day, customer/payee relationship, check characteristics, and other conditions specified in § 229.10.

The engine must calculate the availability date using the distinction between:

- **Banking day:** The day on which the deposit or payment is made or received.
- **Business day:** The deadline unit used to determine when funds must be available.

The requirements below are limited to the supplied regulatory text. They do not introduce additional exceptions, extensions, or requirements from other sections of Regulation CC.

# **2. User stories and acceptance criteria**

## **Epic 1: Cash deposit availability**

### **US-001: Next-day availability for in-person cash deposits**

**As a** deposit customer,\
**I want** cash deposited in person to an employee of the depositary bank to be available by the next business day,\
**So that** I can withdraw deposited cash within the timeframe required by Regulation CC.

**Regulatory reference:** § 229.10(a)(1)

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Next-day availability for in-person cash deposits

  Scenario: Cash deposited in person to a bank employee
    Given a customer deposits cash into an account
    And the deposit is made in person to an employee
        of the depositary bank
    And the deposit occurs on banking day "D"
    When the deposit hold engine calculates availability
    Then the cash deposit must be available for withdrawal
         no later than the business day after banking day "D"

  Scenario: Cash deposit availability is calculated using
            the banking day of deposit
    Given a customer deposits cash in person to an employee
        of the depositary bank
    When the deposit hold engine determines the availability deadline
    Then the calculation must use the banking day
         on which the cash was deposited
    And the deadline must be no later than the following business day
```

### **US-002: Second-business-day availability for non-in-person cash deposits**

**As a** deposit customer,\
**I want** cash deposited without being handed in person to a bank employee to be available by the second business day,\
**So that** the availability timing reflects the applicable regulatory requirement.

**Regulatory reference:** § 229.10(a)(2)

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Availability for non-in-person cash deposits

  Scenario: Cash deposited without in-person delivery to an employee
    Given a customer deposits cash into an account
    And the deposit is not made in person to an employee
        of the depositary bank
    And the deposit occurs on banking day "D"
    When the deposit hold engine calculates availability
    Then the cash deposit must be available for withdrawal
         no later than the second business day after banking day "D"

  Scenario: Non-in-person cash deposit uses second-business-day deadline
    Given a cash deposit is not made in person to an employee
    When the deposit hold engine determines the availability deadline
    Then the applicable deadline must be the second business day
         after the banking day of deposit
```

## **Epic 2: Electronic payment availability**

### **US-003: Next-day availability for electronic payments**

**As a** deposit customer,\
**I want** electronic payments received by the bank to be available by the next business day,\
**So that** funds received electronically can be withdrawn within the regulatory timeframe.

**Regulatory reference:** § 229.10(b)(1)

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Next-day availability for electronic payments

  Scenario: Electronic payment received by the bank
    Given an electronic payment is received by the bank
    And the payment is intended for deposit into a customer account
    And the bank receives the payment on banking day "D"
    When the deposit hold engine calculates availability
    Then the funds must be available for withdrawal
         no later than the business day after banking day "D"

  Scenario: Electronic payment availability is based on receipt
    Given an electronic payment is received by the bank
    When the deposit hold engine calculates the availability deadline
    Then the calculation must use the banking day
         on which the bank received the electronic payment
    And the deadline must be no later than the following business day
```

### **US-004: Determine when an electronic payment is received**

**As a** deposit operations user,\
**I want** the deposit hold engine to determine electronic payment receipt using both final funds and payment information,\
**So that** the availability clock starts only when the regulatory receipt conditions are met.

**Regulatory reference:** § 229.10(b)(2)(i) and (ii)

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Determination of electronic payment receipt

  Scenario: Both final funds and payment information are received
    Given the bank has received payment in actually
          and finally collected funds
    And the bank has received information on the account
        to be credited
    And the bank has received information on the amount to be credited
    When the deposit hold engine evaluates electronic payment receipt
    Then the electronic payment must be considered received

  Scenario: Final funds have not been received
    Given the bank has received information on the account
        to be credited
    And the bank has received information on the amount to be credited
    But the bank has not received payment in actually
        and finally collected funds
    When the deposit hold engine evaluates electronic payment receipt
    Then the electronic payment must not be considered received

  Scenario: Account information has not been received
    Given the bank has received payment in actually
         and finally collected funds
    But the bank has not received information on the account
         to be credited
    When the deposit hold engine evaluates electronic payment receipt
    Then the electronic payment must not be considered received

  Scenario: Amount information has not been received
    Given the bank has received payment in actually
         and finally collected funds
    But the bank has not received information on the amount to be credited
    When the deposit hold engine evaluates electronic payment receipt
    Then the electronic payment must not be considered received

  Scenario: Only a portion of the payment has been received
    Given the bank has received payment in actually
         and finally collected funds for only a portion
         of the electronic payment
    When the deposit hold engine evaluates electronic payment receipt
    Then the bank must consider the electronic payment received
         only to the extent of the actually and finally
         collected funds received
```

## **Epic 4: $275 next-day availability threshold**

### **US-011: Make the lesser of $275 or qualifying aggregate check deposits available next day**

**As a** deposit customer,\
**I want** the applicable portion of my check deposits that are not otherwise subject to next-day availability to be available by the next business day,\
**So that** I can access the minimum amount required under Regulation CC.

**Regulatory reference:** § 229.10(c)(1)(vii)(A) and (B)

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: $275 next-day availability threshold

  Scenario: Aggregate qualifying deposits exceed $275
    Given a customer deposits checks on banking day "D"
    And the checks are not subject to next-day availability
        under paragraphs (c)(1)(i) through (vi)
    And the aggregate amount deposited that banking day
        to all accounts of the customer exceeds $275
    When the deposit hold engine calculates next-day availability
    Then $275 must be available for withdrawal
        no later than the business day after banking day "D"

  Scenario: Aggregate qualifying deposits equal $275
    Given a customer deposits checks on banking day "D"
    And the checks are not subject to next-day availability
        under paragraphs (c)(1)(i) through (vi)
    And the aggregate amount deposited that banking day
        to all accounts of the customer equals $275
    When the deposit hold engine calculates next-day availability
    Then $275 must be available for withdrawal
        no later than the business day after banking day "D"

  Scenario: Aggregate qualifying deposits are less than $275
    Given a customer deposits checks on banking day "D"
    And the checks are not subject to next-day availability
        under paragraphs (c)(1)(i) through (vi)
    And the aggregate amount deposited that banking day
        to all accounts of the customer is less than $275
    When the deposit hold engine calculates next-day availability
    Then the aggregate amount deposited must be available
        for withdrawal no later than the business day
        after banking day "D"

  Scenario: Multiple check deposits across customer accounts
    Given a customer deposits checks into multiple accounts
    And the deposits occur on the same banking day "D"
    And the checks are not subject to next-day availability
        under paragraphs (c)(1)(i) through (vi)
    When the deposit hold engine calculates the next-day availability amount
    Then the engine must aggregate the applicable check deposits
         across all accounts of the customer
    And the next-day availability amount must be the lesser of
         $275 or the aggregate applicable deposit amount
```

## **Epic 5: Second-business-day availability for specified checks not deposited in person**

### **US-012: Second-day availability for qualifying checks not deposited in person**

**As a** deposit customer,\
**I want** qualifying government and bank checks deposited without being handed in person to an employee to be available by the second business day,\
**So that** I can access funds within the applicable regulatory timeframe.

**Regulatory reference:** § 229.10(c)(2)

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Second-business-day availability for qualifying
         checks not deposited in person

  Scenario Outline: Qualifying check deposited without in-person delivery
    Given the deposited check is a "<CheckType>"
    And the check meets the applicable requirements
        under paragraph (c)(1)
    And the check is not deposited in person
        to an employee of the depositary bank
    And the deposit occurs on banking day "D"
    When the deposit hold engine evaluates check eligibility
    Then the check qualifies for second-business-day availability
    And the funds must be available for withdrawal
        no later than the second business day after banking day "D"

    Examples:
      | CheckType                         |
      | U.S. Postal Service money order    |
      | Federal Reserve Bank check        |
      | Federal Home Loan Bank check      |
      | State government check            |
      | Local government check            |
      | Cashier's check                   |
      | Certified check                   |
      | Teller's check                    |
```

**Important rule:** This second-business-day treatment applies only to check deposits described in paragraphs (c)(1)(ii), (iii), (iv), and (v), and that otherwise meet their applicable requirements. It does not extend to Treasury checks or checks covered by paragraph (c)(1)(vi).

## **Epic 6: Special deposit slip or envelope requirements**

### **US-013: Support special deposit slip or envelope requirements**

**As a** deposit operations administrator,\
**I want** the deposit hold engine to recognize whether a special deposit slip or envelope is required for eligible check types,\
**So that** next-day availability eligibility is evaluated according to the conditions specified in Regulation CC.

**Regulatory reference:** § 229.10(c)(3)(i)

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Special deposit slip or envelope eligibility

  Scenario: Bank requires a special deposit slip for a state check
    Given a state government check is deposited
    And the depositary bank requires a special deposit slip
        or deposit envelope
    And the deposit is not accompanied by the required
        special deposit slip or envelope
    When the deposit hold engine evaluates next-day eligibility
    Then the deposit must not qualify for next-day availability
        under paragraph (c)(1)(iv)

  Scenario: Bank requires a special deposit slip for a cashier's check
    Given a cashier's check is deposited
    And the depositary bank requires a special deposit slip
        or deposit envelope
    And the deposit is not accompanied by the required
        special deposit slip or envelope
    When the deposit hold engine evaluates next-day eligibility
    Then the deposit must not qualify for next-day availability
        under paragraph (c)(1)(v)

  Scenario: Special deposit slip is provided when required
    Given a qualifying state or local government check
        or cashier's, certified, or teller's check is deposited
    And the depositary bank requires a special deposit slip
        or deposit envelope
    And the required special deposit slip or envelope is provided
    When the deposit hold engine evaluates next-day eligibility
    Then the special deposit slip or envelope condition is satisfied
```

### **US-014: Support bank obligations for special deposit slips or envelopes**

**As a** deposit operations administrator,\
**I want** the bank's special deposit slip or envelope process to support customer access to the required materials,\
**So that** customers can satisfy the conditions for next-day availability.

**Regulatory reference:** § 229.10(c)(3)(ii)

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Special deposit slip or envelope customer access

  Scenario: Bank provides required special deposit materials
    Given the depositary bank requires a special deposit slip
        or deposit envelope
    When the bank establishes its special deposit process
    Then the bank must provide the special deposit slip
         or deposit envelope to its customers

  Scenario: Bank informs customers how to prepare or obtain
            special deposit materials
    Given the depositary bank requires a special deposit slip
        or deposit envelope
    When the bank establishes its special deposit process
    Then the bank must inform customers how the slip
         or envelope may be prepared or obtained
    And the bank must make the slip or envelope reasonably available
```

# **3. Consolidated business rules**

The following table summarizes the availability rules the deposit hold engine must implement.

| Deposit type                             | Qualifying conditions                                                                                                             | Availability deadline                                |
| ---------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| Cash — in person                         | Deposited in person to an employee                                                                                                | Next business day                                    |
| Cash — not in person                     | Not deposited in person to an employee                                                                                            | Second business day                                  |
| Electronic payment                       | Actually and finally collected funds and account/amount information received                                                      | Next business day                                    |
| U.S. Treasury check                      | Account held by payee                                                                                                             | Next business day                                    |
| USPS money order                         | Payee account; in-person deposit                                                                                                  | Next business day                                    |
| Federal Reserve Bank check               | Payee account; in-person deposit                                                                                                  | Next business day                                    |
| Federal Home Loan Bank check             | Payee account; in-person deposit                                                                                                  | Next business day                                    |
| State/local government check             | Payee account; qualifying state location; in-person deposit; required special slip/envelope if applicable                         | Next business day                                    |
| Cashier's/certified/teller's check       | Payee account; in-person deposit; required special slip/envelope if applicable                                                    | Next business day                                    |
| Same-bank branch check                   | Deposited at a branch and drawn on the same or another branch of the bank; branches in same state or same check processing region | Next business day                                    |
| Other qualifying check deposits          | Aggregate eligible deposits across all customer accounts on a banking day                                                         | Lesser of $275 or aggregate amount next business day |
| Specified checks not deposited in person | Meets applicable requirements under (c)(1)(ii)–(v), but not deposited in person                                                   | Second business day                                  |

# **6. Key business and implementation considerations**

The following are important design considerations for translating the requirements into production functionality.

| Consideration                   | Expected system behavior                                                                                                                               |
| ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Banking day versus business day | Maintain distinct date concepts and use the appropriate date for each calculation.                                                                     |
| Customer-level aggregation      | Aggregate qualifying check deposits across all accounts of the customer, not just the individual deposit account.                                      |
| Electronic payment receipt      | Do not start the availability clock solely because payment information has arrived. Actually and finally collected funds must also have been received. |
| Partial electronic payment      | Recognize receipt only to the extent actually and finally collected funds have been received.                                                          |
| Check classification            | Apply the individual instrument-specific conditions rather than treating all government or bank checks alike.                                          |
| In-person delivery              | Distinguish a deposit made in person to an employee from a deposit that is not made in person to an employee.                                          |
| Special deposit slips           | Apply the slip/envelope condition only when the bank requires it for the applicable check category.                                                    |
| Second-day check treatment      | Restrict this treatment to the categories explicitly listed in § 229.10(c)(2).                                                                         |
| $275 threshold                  | Apply the lesser-of calculation using the aggregate qualifying deposits across all customer accounts on the same banking day.                          |

# **8. Scope boundaries and regulatory interpretation**

To keep the implementation and testing aligned with your instruction to use only the supplied regulatory text, the following items are intentionally excluded.

1. **Other Regulation CC availability requirements:** No assumptions are made about general availability schedules outside § 229.10.
2. **Exception holds:** No exception-hold provisions are introduced.
3. **Account-specific exceptions:** No additional customer, account, or deposit exceptions are introduced.
4. **Cutoff times:** No cutoff-time rules are inferred.
5. **Non-business-day processing:** The engine must use business-day deadlines, but this text alone does not define the complete banking-day and business-day calendar implementation.
6. **Remaining check deposit balances:** The supplied text establishes the lesser-of-$275 rule, but does not specify the availability schedule for the remaining balance.
7. **Electronic payment types:** No additional classification of electronic payment rails is introduced.
8. **Customer notification requirements:** No notification requirements outside the supplied text are added.

**Important BA clarification:** US-013 distinguishes between failure to satisfy a bank-imposed special deposit slip condition and the bank's separate obligation to provide or make those materials reasonably available. The engine should not assume that every qualifying check requires a special slip; the condition applies when the bank requires one.

**Recommended delivery approach:** Implement these stories as five functional components: deposit classification, eligibility determination, customer-level aggregation, availability-date calculation, and regulatory rule traceability. This allows the bank to test each provision independently while maintaining an auditable connection between the engine's decision and the applicable paragraph of § 229.10.
