# **Regulation CC – Next-Day Availability (12 CFR § 229.10)**

**Business Requirements and User Stories for Deposit Hold Engine**

## **1. Objective**

Implement deposit availability rules under 12 CFR § 229.10 in the bank's deposit hold engine to determine the latest permissible withdrawal availability date based on:

- Deposit type (cash, electronic payment, or check).
- Deposit method (in person to an employee or not in person).
- Deposit classification and eligibility criteria.
- Banking day on which the deposit is made or electronic payment is received.
- Applicable business-day availability deadline.
- Special deposit slip or deposit envelope requirements.

The engine must calculate and assign the appropriate availability date for eligible funds.

## **2. Scope and assumptions**

**Regulatory scope:** The acceptance criteria below are based exclusively on the regulatory text supplied for 12 CFR § 229.10.

**Implementation assumptions:**

1. The hold engine receives validated deposit attributes from upstream systems.
2. Banking days and business days are supplied or determined by existing bank calendars.
3. The engine calculates the latest permissible availability date, not necessarily the actual date funds must be released if the bank makes them available earlier.
4. For check deposits subject to the $275 provision, the engine calculates the applicable amount independently of other check funds.
5. No additional regulatory provisions, exceptions, or interpretations are introduced.

# **3. Epic: Implement Regulation CC next-day availability**

**Epic ID:** REGCC-22910

**Epic description:**

As a depositary bank, we need the deposit hold engine to identify deposits qualifying for next-day or second-business-day availability under 12 CFR § 229.10 so that funds are made available for withdrawal within the applicable regulatory timeframe.

**Business outcome:**

The engine accurately classifies deposits and calculates the applicable withdrawal availability deadline.

# **4. User stories and Gherkin acceptance criteria**

## **US-001: Cash deposit made in person to a bank employee**

**Regulatory reference:** § 229.10(a)(1)

**User story**

As a deposit hold engine, I want to identify cash deposits made in person to an employee of the depositary bank so that the deposited funds are available for withdrawal no later than the next business day after the banking day of deposit.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Next-day availability for in-person cash deposits

  Scenario: Cash deposited in person to a bank employee
    Given a customer deposits cash into an account
    And the deposit is made in person to an employee
        of the depositary bank
    And the deposit occurs on a banking day
    When the hold engine calculates the availability date
    Then the funds must be available for withdrawal
         no later than the next business day
         after the banking day of deposit
```

## **US-004: Determine when an electronic payment is received**

**Regulatory reference:** § 229.10(b)(2)(i) and (ii)

**User story**

As a deposit hold engine, I want to determine when an electronic payment is considered received based on actual and final collection of funds and receipt of account and amount information so that the availability clock starts only when the regulatory receipt conditions are satisfied.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Electronic payment receipt determination

  Scenario: Payment and account information both received
    Given the bank has received payment in actually
        and finally collected funds
    And the bank has received information identifying
        the account to be credited
    And the bank has received information identifying
        the amount to be credited
    When the hold engine evaluates the electronic payment
    Then the electronic payment is considered received
    And the receipt date is the banking day
        on which the receipt conditions are satisfied

  Scenario: Payment received but account information missing
    Given the bank has received payment in actually
        and finally collected funds
    And the bank has not received the information
        identifying the account to be credited
    When the hold engine evaluates the electronic payment
    Then the electronic payment is not considered received

  Scenario: Payment received but amount information missing
    Given the bank has received payment in actually
        and finally collected funds
    And the bank has not received the information
        identifying the amount to be credited
    When the hold engine evaluates the electronic payment
    Then the electronic payment is not considered received

  Scenario: Account and amount information received
            but funds are not finally collected
    Given the bank has received information identifying
        the account to be credited
    And the bank has received information identifying
        the amount to be credited
    But the bank has not received payment in actually
        and finally collected funds
    When the hold engine evaluates the electronic payment
    Then the electronic payment is not considered received
```

## **US-011: $275 next-day availability for other check deposits**

**Regulatory reference:** § 229.10(c)(1)(vii)

**User story**

As a deposit hold engine, I want to make the lesser of $275 or the aggregate daily amount of checks not otherwise qualifying for next-day availability available by the next business day so that the applicable portion of customer check deposits receives next-day availability.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: $275 next-day availability for other checks

  Scenario: Aggregate non-qualifying check deposits exceed $275
    Given a customer deposits checks on a banking day
    And the checks are not subject to next-day availability
        under paragraphs (c)(1)(i) through (vi)
    And the aggregate amount of those checks exceeds $275
    When the hold engine calculates next-day availability
    Then $275 of the aggregate amount must be available
         for withdrawal no later than the next business day
         after the banking day of deposit

  Scenario: Aggregate non-qualifying check deposits equal $275
    Given a customer deposits checks on a banking day
    And the checks are not subject to next-day availability
        under paragraphs (c)(1)(i) through (vi)
    And the aggregate amount of those checks equals $275
    When the hold engine calculates next-day availability
    Then $275 must be available for withdrawal
         no later than the next business day
         after the banking day of deposit

  Scenario: Aggregate non-qualifying check deposits are below $275
    Given a customer deposits checks on a banking day
    And the checks are not subject to next-day availability
        under paragraphs (c)(1)(i) through (vi)
    And the aggregate amount of those checks is less than $275
    When the hold engine calculates next-day availability
    Then the entire aggregate amount must be available
         for withdrawal no later than the next business day
         after the banking day of deposit

  Scenario: Customer deposits qualifying and non-qualifying checks
    Given a customer deposits multiple checks on a banking day
    And some checks qualify for next-day availability
        under paragraphs (c)(1)(i) through (vi)
    And other checks do not qualify under those provisions
    When the hold engine calculates next-day availability
    Then the qualifying checks must receive next-day availability
    And the lesser of $275 or the aggregate amount
        of non-qualifying checks must receive next-day availability
```

# **7. Suggested deposit hold engine decision logic**

The following decision flow provides a logical implementation sequence.

**Step 1 – Identify deposit type**

**Cash**

**Electronic**

**Check**

**Step 2 – Apply applicable eligibility rules**

- Cash: Determine in-person employee deposit status.
- Electronic: Confirm final collection and account/amount information.
- Check: Evaluate classification, payee, geography, deposit method, and special slip requirements.

  **Step 3 – Determine availability deadline**

# **Next day**

Next business day after applicable banking day

# **Second day**

Second business day after applicable banking day

**Step 4 – Apply the $275 check provision**

Calculate the lesser of:

- $275
- Aggregate amount of daily checks not qualifying under § 229.10(c)(1)(i)–(vi).

  **Step 5 – Assign availability**

Assign the calculated availability deadline to the applicable deposit amount or deposit component.

# **9. Business analyst considerations and open design decisions**

The supplied regulatory text establishes the eligibility rules and deadlines, but several implementation details need confirmation before development.

| Area                               | Design decision required                                                                                                                        |
| ---------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Banking day determination          | Confirm the source system and definition used to identify the banking day of deposit or electronic payment receipt.                             |
| Business-day calendar              | Confirm which calendar the engine uses to calculate the next and second business days.                                                          |
| Electronic payment receipt         | Define how the engine captures the timestamps for final collection and receipt of account and amount information.                               |
| Electronic payment partial receipt | Support the rule that a bank receives an electronic payment only to the extent it has received payment in actually and finally collected funds. |
| Customer aggregation               | Confirm the customer identifier used to aggregate checks across all customer accounts.                                                          |
| $275 allocation                    | Define the allocation methodology when multiple non-qualifying checks or accounts are involved.                                                 |
| Special deposit slips              | Confirm how the engine receives the bank's special-slip requirement and whether the deposit satisfies it.                                       |
| Branch geography                   | Confirm the authoritative data source for branch state and check processing region.                                                             |
| Mixed deposits                     | Confirm how individual deposit components are tracked when different availability deadlines apply.                                              |

**Important scope clarification:** The supplied text does not establish the treatment of every possible deposit type, exception, or hold scenario. A deposit that does not qualify under one of the specified next-day categories should not automatically be assigned a second-day deadline unless another supplied provision establishes that deadline. The stories above apply the second-day rule specifically to the cash and check categories identified in § 229.10(a)(2) and § 229.10(c)(2).

**Recommended implementation approach:** Build the rules as configurable decision-table logic rather than hardcoding individual deposit scenarios. This will allow the bank to maintain deposit classifications, eligibility conditions, and availability deadlines independently while preserving traceability to the applicable regulatory paragraph.
