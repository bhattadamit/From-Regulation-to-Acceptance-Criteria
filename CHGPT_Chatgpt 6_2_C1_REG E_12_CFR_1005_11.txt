Regulation E Error Resolution — User Stories & Gherkin Acceptance Criteria
Business requirements
Card & ACH
12 CFR 1005.11

Role: Senior Business Analyst, US Banking
Platform: Card and ACH Disputes Management System
Regulatory framework: Electronic Fund Transfer Act (EFTA), Regulation E, 12 CFR §1005.11
Primary objective: Implement a compliant, auditable, end-to-end error resolution process for consumer electronic fund transfer disputes.
1. Business context and objectives
The disputes platform must support the regulatory requirements for investigating and resolving alleged errors involving electronic fund transfers (EFTs) from consumer accounts.

The solution must provide:
1. Consistent intake and classification of Regulation E errors.
2. Accurate calculation and monitoring of regulatory deadlines.
3. Timely investigation and determination of alleged errors.
4. Provisional credit when required.
5. Appropriate consumer notifications.
6. Final credit or debit adjustments.
7. Supporting documentation and investigation audit trails.
8. Evidence of compliance with regulatory timelines.
Scope
Transaction type	In scope
Debit card EFTs	Yes
ATM transactions	Yes
ACH debit transactions	Yes
ACH credit transactions	Where applicable to an alleged EFT error
Unauthorized electronic transfers	Yes
Incorrect or duplicate EFTs	Yes
EFTs not properly identified on statements	Yes
Missing EFTs or incorrect amounts	Yes
Consumer requests for additional EFT information	Yes
Credit card billing disputes	No, generally governed by Regulation Z
Pure merchant dissatisfaction or product-quality disputes	Not a Regulation E error by itself

Important distinction: Card-network chargeback rules and ACH network operating rules may impose additional requirements. The platform should maintain separate operational workflows while ensuring Regulation E obligations are independently satisfied.
2. Regulatory requirements and business rules
The following requirements should drive system configuration and acceptance testing.
Requirement	Regulatory expectation
Consumer notification	Consumer generally must notify the institution within 60 days after the institution sends the periodic statement first reflecting the alleged error, subject to applicable statutory provisions
Investigation	Investigate promptly and determine whether an error occurred
Standard investigation period	10 business days
Extended investigation	Up to 45 calendar days, subject to applicable conditions
New account investigation	20 business days for qualifying transfers within 30 days after the first deposit
Extended investigation period	Up to 90 calendar days for qualifying new-account, foreign-initiated, and point-of-sale debit card transactions
Provisional credit	Generally required within 10 business days if the investigation cannot be completed within the initial period and the applicable conditions are met
Provisional credit notification	Notify the consumer within two business days after providing provisional credit
Provisional credit availability	Consumer must have full use of provisionally credited funds during the investigation
Final determination	Notify consumer within three business days after completing the investigation
Error confirmed	Correct the error within one business day after determining that an error occurred
Error not confirmed	Provide written explanation and advise the consumer of the right to request supporting documents

Implementation clarification: The 45- and 90-day periods are calendar-day investigation limits, not business-day limits. The applicable 10- and 20-business-day initial periods must be calculated using the institution's Regulation E business-day definition.

The platform must also account for the regulatory provision allowing an institution to require written confirmation of an oral notice within 10 business days, provided the consumer is properly informed of the requirement. Failure to receive confirmation may affect the obligation to provide provisional credit; it does not automatically eliminate the institution's other applicable error-resolution obligations.

3. Epics and user stories
The following stories are structured for direct use in Jira or Azure DevOps. Each story includes a business objective and executable-style Gherkin acceptance criteria.
EPIC 1 — Dispute intake and Regulation E eligibility
US-001: Capture a consumer-reported EFT error
User story

As a disputes operations associate,
I want to capture an alleged electronic fund transfer error reported by a consumer,
So that the bank can initiate the Regulation E error-resolution process.

Acceptance criteria
Feature: Regulation E dispute intake

  Scenario: Consumer reports an unauthorized debit card transaction
    Given a consumer has an active deposit account
    And a debit card transaction has posted to the account
    When the consumer reports that they did not authorize the transaction
    Then the platform shall create a dispute case
    And classify the dispute as a Regulation E error
    And record the transaction reference
    And record the reported error amount
    And record the consumer notification date
    And record the notification channel
    And assign a unique dispute case identifier

  Scenario: Consumer reports an incorrect ACH debit
    Given an ACH debit transaction has posted to the consumer account
    When the consumer reports that the amount is incorrect
    Then the platform shall create a Regulation E dispute
    And classify the transaction as ACH
    And capture the disputed amount
    And capture the consumer's explanation
    And initiate the applicable regulatory timeline

  Scenario: Consumer reports a duplicate EFT
    Given two electronic transfers appear on the account
    When the consumer reports that one transfer is duplicated
    Then the platform shall classify the alleged error as a duplicate EFT
    And associate both transaction references with the dispute
    And calculate the disputed amount based on the alleged duplicate

  Scenario: Consumer reports an error orally
    Given a consumer contacts the bank by telephone
    When the consumer reports an alleged EFT error
    Then the platform shall accept the oral notification
    And record the date and time of notification
    And record the employee or channel receiving the notification
    And initiate the applicable Regulation E workflow


US-002: Determine Regulation E applicability
User story

As a disputes operations associate,
I want the platform to determine whether a reported dispute falls within Regulation E,
So that eligible disputes receive the appropriate regulatory treatment.

Acceptance criteria
Feature: Regulation E eligibility assessment

  Scenario: Eligible debit card dispute
    Given the consumer reports an unauthorized debit card EFT
    When the dispute is evaluated
    Then the platform shall identify the dispute as Regulation E eligible
    And route it to the Regulation E workflow

  Scenario: Eligible ACH dispute
    Given the consumer reports an incorrect ACH debit
    When the dispute is evaluated
    Then the platform shall identify the dispute as Regulation E eligible
    And route it to the ACH investigation workflow
    And retain the Regulation E compliance requirements

  Scenario: Merchant dissatisfaction without an EFT error
    Given the consumer reports dissatisfaction with merchandise quality
    And does not allege an unauthorized or otherwise erroneous EFT
    When the dispute is evaluated
    Then the platform shall not automatically classify the case as a Regulation E error
    And shall route the case to the appropriate non-Regulation E workflow

  Scenario: Credit card transaction dispute
    Given the consumer reports a credit card billing dispute
    When the dispute is evaluated
    Then the platform shall identify the transaction as outside the Regulation E workflow
    And route the dispute to the applicable Regulation Z process

  Scenario: Eligibility cannot be determined automatically
    Given the dispute information is insufficient
    When the platform evaluates Regulation E applicability
    Then the case shall be flagged for manual review
    And the platform shall not automatically reject the consumer's claim


US-003: Capture and evaluate the 60-day notification period
User story

As a Regulation E case manager,
I want the platform to calculate the consumer notification period using the applicable periodic statement date,
So that late-reported errors can be identified and appropriately reviewed.

Acceptance criteria
Feature: Consumer notification period

  Scenario: Consumer reports an error within 60 days
    Given the periodic statement first reflecting the alleged error
    And the statement was sent 30 days ago
    When the consumer reports the alleged error
    Then the platform shall identify the notification as within 60 days
    And record the notification as timely

  Scenario: Consumer reports an error after 60 days
    Given the periodic statement first reflecting the alleged error
    And the statement was sent more than 60 days ago
    When the consumer reports the alleged error
    Then the platform shall flag the notification as potentially late
    And route the case for applicable Regulation E liability assessment
    And retain the dispute for review
    And shall not automatically reject the dispute solely based on the date

  Scenario: Periodic statement has not yet reflected the error
    Given the alleged EFT error has not appeared on a periodic statement
    When the consumer reports the error
    Then the platform shall record the notification
    And shall not incorrectly classify the notification as late

  Scenario: Multiple periodic statements are associated with the account
    Given the account has multiple periodic statements
    When the platform evaluates the notification period
    Then it shall use the statement first reflecting the alleged error
    And retain the statement date used in the calculation


EPIC 2 — Regulatory deadline calculation and monitoring
US-004: Calculate the initial 10-business-day investigation deadline
User story

As a Regulation E case manager,
I want the platform to calculate the initial investigation deadline,
So that the bank can complete its investigation within the regulatory timeframe.

Acceptance criteria
Feature: Initial investigation deadline

  Scenario: Standard Regulation E investigation
    Given a Regulation E dispute is received
    And the transaction does not qualify for a special investigation period
    When the case is created
    Then the platform shall calculate the initial deadline as 10 business days
    And use the institution's applicable business-day calendar
    And display the deadline on the case

  Scenario: Weekend is excluded from business-day calculation
    Given a dispute is received before a weekend
    When the platform calculates the 10-business-day deadline
    Then Saturday shall not count as a business day
    And Sunday shall not count as a business day

  Scenario: Bank holiday is excluded
    Given a dispute is received before a recognized bank business-day holiday
    When the platform calculates the deadline
    Then the applicable non-business day shall be excluded
    And the resulting deadline shall reflect the configured business-day calendar

  Scenario: Deadline is approaching
    Given a dispute has an investigation deadline
    And one business day remains
    When the case is displayed to an associate
    Then the platform shall flag the case as approaching its deadline
    And generate the configured operational alert


US-005: Support extended investigation periods
User story

As a Regulation E case manager,
I want the platform to identify disputes eligible for extended investigation periods,
So that the bank can apply the correct regulatory deadline and provisional credit requirements.

Acceptance criteria
Feature: Extended investigation periods

  Scenario: Qualifying new account transaction
    Given the consumer's account is within 30 days after the first deposit
    And the EFT occurred within the qualifying period
    When the dispute is created
    Then the platform shall identify the case as potentially eligible
    for the 20-business-day initial investigation period
    And calculate the applicable extended investigation deadline

  Scenario: Qualifying foreign-initiated transaction
    Given the alleged error involves a qualifying foreign-initiated EFT
    When the dispute is evaluated
    Then the platform shall identify the case as potentially eligible
    for the 90-calendar-day extended investigation period
    And retain the eligibility reason

  Scenario: Qualifying point-of-sale debit card transaction
    Given the alleged error involves a qualifying point-of-sale debit card transaction
    When the dispute is evaluated
    Then the platform shall identify the case as potentially eligible
    for the 90-calendar-day extended investigation period
    And calculate the applicable deadline

  Scenario: Extended period is not applicable
    Given the transaction does not meet the regulatory criteria for an extended period
    When the dispute is evaluated
    Then the platform shall apply the standard investigation timeframe
    And shall not automatically assign a 90-day deadline

  Scenario: Extended period eligibility requires review
    Given the transaction information is insufficient to establish eligibility
    When the platform evaluates the investigation period
    Then the case shall be flagged for manual review
    And the system shall not assume that the extended period applies


US-006: Monitor and escalate regulatory deadlines
User story

As a disputes team supervisor,
I want the platform to monitor regulatory deadlines and escalate overdue cases,
So that the bank can proactively manage compliance risk.

Acceptance criteria
Feature: Regulatory deadline monitoring

  Scenario: Investigation deadline approaching
    Given a dispute has two business days remaining
    Before its applicable initial investigation deadline
    When the scheduled deadline monitoring process executes
    Then the platform shall generate an upcoming deadline alert
    And notify the assigned case owner

  Scenario: Investigation deadline reached
    Given a dispute has reached its initial investigation deadline
    And the investigation is not complete
    When the deadline monitoring process executes
    Then the platform shall flag the case as requiring immediate action
    And notify the assigned associate and supervisor

  Scenario: Case exceeds regulatory deadline
    Given a dispute has exceeded its applicable regulatory deadline
    And no compliant resolution or applicable extension has been recorded
    When the monitoring process executes
    Then the platform shall flag the case as overdue
    And create a compliance exception
    And retain the overdue duration in the audit history

  Scenario: Case is resolved before the deadline
    Given a dispute has been resolved
    When the deadline monitoring process executes
    Then the platform shall exclude the case from active deadline alerts
    And retain its original regulatory deadlines for audit purposes

  Scenario: Case is reassigned

  Scenario: Case requires additional information
    Given an investigator is reviewing a dispute
    When additional information is required
    Then the investigator shall be able to record the information request
    And update the investigation status

As a disputes investigator,
I want to capture and retain relevant investigation evidence,
So that the bank can substantiate its error determination.

Acceptance criteria
Feature: Investigation evidence

  Scenario: Investigator uploads supporting documentation
    Given a Regulation E dispute is under investigation
    When the investigator uploads supporting documentation
    Then the platform shall associate the document with the case
    And record the document upload date and time
    And record the uploading user
    And retain the document for audit purposes

  Scenario: Investigator records transaction findings

  Scenario: Investigator records a determination

    And record the determination date and time

  Scenario: Investigation evidence is modified
    Given investigation evidence has previously been recorded
    When an authorized user modifies the evidence
    Then the platform shall retain the original audit history
    And record the modification details

EPIC 4 — Provisional credit management
US-010: Determine provisional credit eligibility
User story

As a Regulation E case manager,
I want the platform to determine whether provisional credit is required,
So that eligible consumers receive timely access to disputed funds.

Acceptance criteria
Feature: Provisional credit eligibility

  Scenario: Investigation cannot be completed within 10 business days
    Given a Regulation E dispute is under investigation
    And the bank cannot complete its investigation within 10 business days
    And the applicable provisional credit conditions are satisfied
    When the initial investigation period expires
    Then the platform shall initiate provisional credit processing
    And calculate the amount to be credited
    And record the provisional credit due date

  Scenario: Investigation completed within 10 business days
    Given a Regulation E dispute is under investigation
    When the bank completes its investigation within 10 business days
    Then the platform shall not issue provisional credit solely because
    the initial deadline has been reached
    And shall proceed with the applicable final determination

  Scenario: Consumer has not provided required written confirmation
    Given the consumer provided oral notice of an alleged error
    And the bank properly informed the consumer of its written confirmation requirement
    And the consumer has not provided the required written confirmation
    When the provisional credit eligibility is evaluated
    Then the platform shall identify the written confirmation as outstanding
    And apply the applicable regulatory treatment
    And retain evidence that the consumer was informed of the requirement

  Scenario: Consumer has provided written confirmation
    Given the consumer initially reported the alleged error orally
    And the bank required written confirmation
    When the consumer provides the required written confirmation within
    the applicable timeframe
    Then the platform shall update the confirmation status
    And evaluate provisional credit eligibility accordingly


US-011: Issue provisional credit within the required timeframe
User story

As a disputes operations associate,
I want the platform to issue provisional credit to the consumer's account,
So that the consumer has access to the disputed funds while the investigation continues.

Acceptance criteria
Feature: Provisional credit processing

  Scenario: Standard provisional credit issuance
    Given a dispute requires provisional credit
    And the consumer has satisfied applicable requirements
    When the provisional credit is due
    Then the platform shall submit a credit transaction
    And credit the appropriate consumer account
    And ensure the credit is issued within the applicable deadline
    And record the credit amount
    And record the posting date
    And retain the core banking transaction reference

  Scenario: Provisional credit amount is calculated
    Given a consumer reports an alleged EFT error
    When provisional credit is calculated
    Then the platform shall use the amount of the alleged error
    And apply the applicable regulatory calculation rules
    And include applicable interest where required
    And exclude amounts not supported by the alleged error

  Scenario: Provisional credit is successfully posted
    Given provisional credit has been submitted to the core banking system
    When the core banking system confirms successful posting
    Then the platform shall update the credit status to Posted
    And record the posting confirmation
    And make the funds available for the consumer's use

  Scenario: Provisional credit posting fails
    Given provisional credit is required
    When the core banking system rejects the credit transaction
    Then the platform shall record the posting failure
    And generate an operational exception
    And notify the assigned associate and supervisor
    And initiate the configured retry or remediation process

  Scenario: Provisional credit cannot be restricted
    Given provisional credit has been successfully posted
    When the consumer accesses the credited funds
    Then the funds shall be available for the consumer's use
    And the platform shall not impose a hold solely because the investigation remains open


US-012: Notify consumer of provisional credit
User story

As a disputes operations associate,
I want the platform to notify the consumer when provisional credit is issued,
So that the bank meets its notification obligations.

Acceptance criteria
Feature: Provisional credit notification

  Scenario: Provisional credit notification is generated
    Given provisional credit has been posted
    When the core banking system confirms the posting
    Then the platform shall generate a provisional credit notification
    And include the credited amount
    And include the date of credit
    And provide the applicable information regarding availability of funds

  Scenario: Notification is sent within two business days
    Given provisional credit has been successfully posted
    When the notification process executes
    Then the platform shall send the notification within two business days
    And record the notification date and time
    And record the notification delivery status

  Scenario: Notification delivery fails
    Given a provisional credit notification has been generated
    When the delivery attempt fails
    Then the platform shall record the delivery failure
    And create a notification exception
    And initiate the configured remediation process


EPIC 5 — Final determination and error correction
US-013: Record the final investigation determination
User story

As a disputes investigator,
I want to record whether an alleged EFT error occurred,
So that the bank can complete the Regulation E investigation and determine the appropriate account adjustment.

Acceptance criteria
Feature: Final error determination

  Scenario: Error is confirmed
    Given a Regulation E investigation has been completed
    And the evidence supports the consumer's allegation
    When the investigator records the determination
    Then the case shall be classified as Error Confirmed
    And the investigator shall record the findings
    And the platform shall initiate the applicable account correction

  Scenario: No error is identified
    Given a Regulation E investigation has been completed
    And the evidence does not support the consumer's allegation
    When the investigator records the determination
    Then the case shall be classified as No Error
    And the investigator shall record the rationale
    And the platform shall initiate the applicable consumer notification

  Scenario: Partial error is confirmed
    Given a consumer disputes an EFT amount of $500
    And the investigation confirms an error of $300
    When the investigator records the determination
    Then the platform shall record the confirmed error amount as $300
    And retain the original disputed amount of $500
    And initiate correction for the confirmed error amount
    And retain the rationale for the partial determination

  Scenario: Determination is submitted without supporting rationale
    Given an investigator is completing an investigation
    When the investigator attempts to submit a determination
    And the required rationale is missing
    Then the platform shall prevent submission
    And display a validation message


US-014: Apply final account correction when an error is confirmed
User story

As a disputes operations associate,
I want the platform to correct the consumer's account when an EFT error is confirmed,
So that the consumer receives the appropriate financial adjustment.

Acceptance criteria
Feature: Final account correction

  Scenario: Confirmed error requires account credit
    Given the investigation confirms that an EFT error occurred
    When the final determination is approved
    Then the platform shall calculate the required account adjustment
    And credit the consumer's account within one business day
    And record the adjustment amount
    And record the posting date
    And retain the core banking reference

  Scenario: Provisional credit already covers the confirmed error
    Given provisional credit has already been issued
    And the investigation confirms the full disputed amount as an error
    When the final determination is completed
    Then the platform shall retain the provisional credit as the final credit
    And shall not issue a duplicate credit
    And complete the applicable final notification

  Scenario: Confirmed error is less than provisional credit
    Given provisional credit exceeds the confirmed error amount
    When the final determination is completed
    Then the platform shall calculate the excess provisional credit
    And initiate the applicable recovery process
    And follow the required consumer notification and 1005.11(d)(2) procedures
    And shall not restrict the consumer's use of the remaining provisional credit
    before the applicable recovery period has expired

  Scenario: Error is confirmed and account adjustment fails
    Given the investigation confirms an EFT error
    When the account adjustment fails
    Then the platform shall create a critical operational exception
    And notify the supervisor
    And retain the original correction deadline
    And continue monitoring until the correction is successfully completed


US-015: Handle no-error determinations and provisional credit recovery
User story

As a disputes operations associate,
I want the platform to handle no-error determinations and any provisional credit recovery in accordance with Regulation E,
So that the bank can close investigations without violating consumer notification and funds-availability requirements.

Acceptance criteria
Feature: No-error determination and provisional credit recovery

  Scenario: No error and no provisional credit issued
    Given the investigation determines that no error occurred
    And no provisional credit has been issued
    When the determination is finalized
    Then the platform shall generate a written explanation
    And notify the consumer of the determination
    And advise the consumer of the right to request supporting documentation

  Scenario: No error and provisional credit was issued
    Given the investigation determines that no error occurred
    And provisional credit was previously issued
    When the determination is finalized
    Then the platform shall generate the required written explanation
    And notify the consumer of the determination
    And notify the consumer of the amount and date of any proposed debit
    And inform the consumer of the applicable funds-availability rights
    And initiate the recovery workflow

  Scenario: Recovery occurs after the applicable notification period
    Given the bank has issued the required notice of its determination
    And the applicable five-business-day period has expired
    When the bank initiates recovery of provisional credit
    Then the platform shall permit the applicable debit
    And record the recovery transaction
    And retain the consumer notification evidence

  Scenario: Consumer requests documents following a no-error determination
    Given the bank has provided a no-error determination
    When the consumer requests the documents relied upon in the investigation
    Then the platform shall retrieve the relevant supporting documentation
    And provide copies to the consumer
    And record the request date
    And record the fulfillment date

EPIC 7 — Case closure, audit and compliance reporting
US-019: Close a Regulation E dispute
User story

As a disputes operations associate,
I want to close a Regulation E dispute only after all required resolution activities are complete,
So that the bank maintains complete and compliant case records.

Acceptance criteria
Feature: Regulation E dispute closure

  Scenario: Case is eligible for closure
    Given the investigation is complete
    And the final determination is recorded
    And all required account adjustments are completed
    And required consumer notifications have been generated and sent
    When the associate closes the case

    And record the closure date and time

  Scenario: Case has an outstanding account adjustment

5. Regulatory business rules — configuration reference
I recommend maintaining these as centrally configurable rules rather than hardcoding deadlines in individual workflows.
Rule ID	Business rule	System treatment
RE-001	Consumer notification generally within 60 days after the statement first reflecting the alleged error is sent	Calculate and flag
RE-002	Standard initial investigation period	10 business days
RE-003	Qualifying new-account initial investigation period	20 business days
RE-004	Standard extended investigation period	Up to 45 calendar days
RE-005	Qualifying extended investigation period	Up to 90 calendar days
RE-006	Provisional credit for standard cases	By 10 business days, subject to applicable conditions
RE-007	Provisional credit for qualifying new-account cases	By 20 business days, subject to applicable conditions
RE-008	Provisional credit notification	Within two business days after crediting
RE-009	Consumer availability of provisional credit	Full use of funds
RE-010	Correction following confirmed error	Within one business day after determination
RE-011	Final investigation notification	Within three business days after completing investigation
RE-012	No-error determination	Written explanation and right to request supporting documents
RE-013	Provisional credit recovery	Follow applicable notice and five-business-day requirements
RE-014	Business-day calculation	Institution-specific definition under Regulation E
RE-015	Written confirmation of oral notice	Apply only when properly required and disclosed

Important regulatory distinctions:
- The standard 45-day period is subject to the applicable conditions in §1005.11(c)(2).
- The 90-day period applies to qualifying new-account, foreign-initiated and point-of-sale debit card transactions.
- For qualifying new-account disputes, the initial investigation and provisional credit periods are generally 20 business days, rather than 10.
- When the bank determines that no error occurred, §1005.11(d)(2) imposes specific notice and provisional credit recovery requirements.
- If the investigation establishes a different error amount from the amount alleged, the platform must support partial error determinations.
- A consumer's failure to provide written confirmation should not be treated as an automatic denial of the alleged error.
6. Suggested case lifecycle and workflow
The following represents the recommended end-to-end platform workflow.
Regulation E dispute lifecycle
1. Dispute intake
   
   Capture consumer notification, transaction details, alleged error and notification channel.
2. Eligibility assessment
   
   Determine Regulation E applicability, notification timeliness and applicable investigation period.
3. Deadline calculation
   
   Establish initial and extended investigation deadlines and provisional credit due dates.
4. Investigation
   
   Retrieve transaction evidence, review supporting documents and record investigation findings.
5. Provisional credit decision
   
   Determine whether provisional credit is required and process the applicable account adjustment.
6. Final determination
   
   Record confirmed error, partial error or no error.
7. Financial correction
   
   Complete final account credit or applicable provisional credit recovery.
8. Consumer notification
   
   Send the applicable final determination and supporting explanation.
9. Case closure and audit
   
   Validate outstanding obligations, close the case and retain the complete audit trail.

- 12 CFR §1005.2 — Definitions
- 12 CFR §1005.6 — Liability of consumer for unauthorized transfers

Senior BA recommendation: Before these stories are baselined, obtain Compliance and Legal approval of the deadline calculation matrix, the treatment of late-reported disputes, the oral-notice/written-confirmation workflow, and provisional credit recovery. These are particularly high-risk areas because an otherwise successful operational investigation can still result in a Regulation E violation if the system calculates the wrong deadline or restricts consumer access to funds.

The proposed stories provide a common regulatory framework for debit card and ACH disputes, while allowing transaction-specific investigation and processing rules to remain separate.
