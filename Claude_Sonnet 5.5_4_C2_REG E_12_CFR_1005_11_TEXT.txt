# Regulation E Error Resolution (12 CFR 1005.11): User Stories

**Scope:** Card and ACH disputes platform. Every acceptance criterion traces to a paragraph of § 1005.11 in the text you provided, and the paragraph is cited in each story's tag.

**Assumptions to confirm with Compliance (the text doesn't define these):**
- "Business day" calendar and the cutoff time for "receipt" of a notice.
- "45 days" and "90 days" are treated as calendar days, because the text distinguishes them from "business days."
- References to §§ 1005.6(a), 1005.9, 1005.10(a), 1005.14 and Regulation T are treated as upstream flags or data the platform receives. Their substance is not modeled here.

## Epic 1: Intake and Eligibility

### Story 1: Classify a notice as an error
**Ref: § 1005.11(a)(1)–(2)**

As a disputes analyst, I want each inquiry classified as a covered error type or an excluded inquiry type, so that only covered inquiries enter the error resolution workflow.

```gherkin
Feature: Error classification

  Scenario Outline: Covered error types open an error case
    Given a consumer submits an inquiry
    When the analyst selects the error type "<error_type>"
    Then the platform opens an error resolution case
    And the case records the error type "<error_type>"

    Examples:
      | error_type                                                                    |
      | Unauthorized electronic fund transfer                                         |
      | Incorrect electronic fund transfer to or from the account                     |
      | Omission of a transfer from a periodic statement                              |
      | Computational or bookkeeping error by the institution                         |
      | Incorrect amount of money received from an electronic terminal                |
      | Transfer not identified per § 1005.9 or § 1005.10(a)                          |
      | Request for required documentation or additional information or clarification |

  Scenario Outline: Excluded inquiry types do not open an error case
    Given a consumer submits an inquiry
    When the analyst selects the inquiry type "<inquiry_type>"
    Then the platform does not open an error resolution case
    And the platform records the inquiry as "not an error"

    Examples:
      | inquiry_type                                         |
      | Routine inquiry about account balance                |
      | Request for tax or other recordkeeping information   |
      | Request for duplicate copies of documentation        |

  Scenario: A request made to determine whether an error exists is covered
    Given a consumer asks for information to determine whether an unauthorized transfer occurred
    When the analyst classifies the inquiry
    Then the platform classifies it as a documentation or clarification request under (a)(1)(vii)
    And the platform opens an error resolution case
```

### Story 2: Validate timeliness and content of a notice of error
**Ref: § 1005.11(b)(1)**

As a disputes analyst, I want the platform to check the timing and contents of each oral or written notice, so that every qualifying notice is handled under this section.

```gherkin
Feature: Notice of error validation

  Background:
    Given the platform stores, for each account, the date the periodic statement or passbook documentation was sent

  Scenario: Notice received within 60 days of the statement
    Given the statement first reflecting the alleged error was sent on a given date
    And the notice is received no later than 60 days after that date
    When the analyst records the notice
    Then the platform marks the notice "timely"

  Scenario: Notice received after 60 days
    Given the notice is received more than 60 days after the statement first reflecting the alleged error was sent
    When the analyst records the notice
    Then the platform marks the notice "untimely"
    And the platform requires a documented analyst decision before the case proceeds

  Scenario: Notice must identify the consumer's name and account number
    Given the analyst is recording a notice
    When the consumer's name or account number cannot be identified
    Then the platform blocks case acceptance
    And the platform prompts the analyst to obtain the missing identifier

  Scenario: Notice must state why the consumer believes an error exists
    Given the analyst is recording a notice that is not a documentation or clarification request
    When the analyst saves the notice
    Then the platform requires a stated reason for the alleged error
    And the platform captures the type, date and amount of the error to the extent the consumer can provide them

  Scenario: Type, date and amount are not required for documentation or clarification requests
    Given the notice is a request described in (a)(1)(vii)
    When the analyst saves the notice
    Then the platform does not require type, date or amount

  Scenario Outline: Notices are accepted through either channel
    When a notice of error is received by "<channel>"
    Then the platform records the channel and receipt date-time
    And the platform treats the notice as a valid notice of error if it meets the content requirements

    Examples:
      | channel |
      | oral    |
      | written |
```

### Story 3: Written confirmation of an oral notice
**Ref: § 1005.11(b)(2), (c)(2)(i)(A)**

As a compliance officer, I want the platform to manage the optional written-confirmation requirement, so that required disclosures are given and the effect on provisional credit is handled correctly.

```gherkin
Feature: Written confirmation of oral notice

  Scenario: Institution requires written confirmation
    Given the institution's policy requires written confirmation of oral notices
    When the consumer gives an oral notice of error
    Then the platform informs the consumer that written confirmation is required
    And the platform gives the consumer the address where confirmation must be sent
    And the platform sets a confirmation deadline of 10 business days from the oral notice

  Scenario: Confirmation received in time
    Given written confirmation was required for an oral notice
    When written confirmation is received within 10 business days of the oral notice
    Then the platform marks the confirmation "received"
    And the case continues under the standard time limits

  Scenario: Confirmation not received
    Given written confirmation was required for an oral notice
    And no written confirmation is received within 10 business days of the oral notice
    When the confirmation deadline passes
    Then the platform marks the case "confirmation not received"
    And the platform does not require a provisional credit for that case

  Scenario: Institution does not require written confirmation
    Given the institution does not require written confirmation
    When the consumer gives an oral notice of error
    Then the platform treats the oral notice as sufficient
    And the platform does not set a confirmation deadline
```

### Story 4: Timeliness of notices based on requested documentation
**Ref: § 1005.11(b)(3), (e)**

As a disputes analyst, I want the 60-day window restarted when a notice is based on information the consumer requested, so that follow-on notices are judged timely.

```gherkin
Feature: Notice based on requested documentation or clarification

  Scenario: Follow-on notice within 60 days of the information being sent
    Given a consumer made a documentation or clarification request under (a)(1)(vii)
    And the institution sent the requested information on a given date
    When a notice of error based on that information is received no later than 60 days after that date
    Then the platform marks the notice "timely"

  Scenario: Follow-on notice after 60 days of the information being sent
    Given the institution sent the requested information on a given date
    When a notice of error based on that information is received more than 60 days after that date
    Then the platform marks the notice "untimely"

  Scenario: The platform links the notice to the originating request
    When the analyst records a notice based on requested information
    Then the platform requires a link to the originating documentation request
    And the platform records the date the information was sent
```

## Epic 2: Investigation Timelines and Provisional Credit

### Story 5: Ten-business-day determination, reporting and correction
**Ref: § 1005.11(c)(1)**

As a disputes analyst, I want the platform to track the 10-business-day determination period and the follow-on deadlines, so that investigations are prompt and results are reported and errors corrected on time.

```gherkin
Feature: Ten-business-day investigation

  Scenario: Clock starts on receipt of the notice
    When a notice of error is received
    Then the platform starts the investigation clock at the receipt date
    And the platform sets the determination due date at 10 business days after receipt

  Scenario: Determination within 10 business days
    Given an open case with the 10-business-day period in effect
    When the analyst completes the investigation and records a determination within 10 business days of receipt
    Then the platform sets the reporting due date at 3 business days after the completion date
    And, if an error occurred, the platform sets the correction due date at 1 business day after the determination date

  Scenario: Report the results to the consumer
    Given the investigation is complete
    When the analyst finalizes the result
    Then the platform generates a result notice to the consumer
    And the platform records the date sent
    And the platform flags the case "late" if the notice is sent more than 3 business days after completion

  Scenario: Correct the error
    Given the determination is "error occurred"
    When the correction is posted
    Then the platform records the correction date
    And the platform flags the case "late" if the correction is posted more than 1 business day after the determination

  Scenario: Escalate before the period expires
    Given the investigation is not complete
    When the case is within a configurable number of business days of the 10-business-day deadline
    Then the platform alerts the case owner
    And the platform prompts the analyst to decide whether to invoke the 45-day period
```

### Story 6: Forty-five-day investigation with provisional credit
**Ref: § 1005.11(c)(2)**

As a disputes analyst, I want to extend an investigation to 45 days only when the provisional credit conditions are met, so that the institution stays compliant while it investigates.

```gherkin
Feature: Forty-five-day investigation period

  Scenario: Invoke the 45-day period
    Given the investigation cannot be completed within 10 business days
    When the analyst invokes the 45-day period
    Then the platform sets the determination due date at 45 days from receipt of the notice
    And the platform requires a provisional credit by 10 business days after receipt

  Scenario: Provisional credit posted for the alleged amount
    Given the 45-day period has been invoked
    When the platform posts the provisional credit within 10 business days of receipt
    Then the credit equals the alleged error amount
    And the platform includes interest where applicable
    And the platform records the amount and date of the credit
    And the consumer has full use of the funds during the investigation

  Scenario: Block the 45-day period if the credit cannot be posted in time
    Given the 45-day period is requested
    And no exception under (c)(2)(i) applies
    When the provisional credit has not been posted by 10 business days after receipt
    Then the platform raises a compliance exception
    And the platform flags the case "provisional credit overdue"

  Scenario: Notify the consumer of the provisional credit
    Given a provisional credit was posted
    When the platform posts the credit
    Then the platform sends the consumer notice of the credit amount and date within 2 business days after the credit
    And the platform flags the case "late" if the notice is sent after the second business day

  Scenario: Correct the error and report results
    Given a determination was made within the 45-day period
    When an error is found
    Then the platform sets the correction due date at 1 business day after the determination
    And the platform sets the reporting due date at 3 business days after the investigation completes
    And the result notice includes, if applicable, that the provisional credit has been made final
```

### Story 7: Provisional credit exceptions and the $50 withholding
**Ref: § 1005.11(c)(2)(i)**

As a disputes analyst, I want provisional credit rules to handle the permitted exceptions, so that the right amount is credited, or none is, where the regulation allows.

```gherkin
Feature: Provisional credit exceptions

  Scenario: Withhold up to $50 for a suspected unauthorized transfer
    Given the institution has a reasonable basis for believing an unauthorized transfer occurred
    And the analyst confirms the institution has satisfied the requirements of § 1005.6(a)
    When the platform calculates the provisional credit
    Then the platform permits withholding a maximum of $50 from the amount credited
    And the platform records the amount withheld

  Scenario: Withholding cannot exceed $50
    Given the analyst enters a withheld amount greater than $50
    When the analyst saves the provisional credit
    Then the platform rejects the entry
    And the platform displays "Maximum withholding is $50"

  Scenario: Withholding not available without the stated basis
    Given the analyst has not recorded a reasonable basis for believing an unauthorized transfer occurred
    Or the analyst has not confirmed § 1005.6(a) requirements are satisfied
    When the platform calculates the provisional credit
    Then the platform does not permit any withholding

  Scenario: No provisional credit when required written confirmation is not received
    Given the institution requires written confirmation of an oral notice
    And written confirmation is not received within 10 business days of the oral notice
    When the platform evaluates provisional credit
    Then the platform does not require a provisional credit
    And the platform records the exception reason "written confirmation not received"

  Scenario: No provisional credit for Regulation T accounts
    Given the alleged error involves an account subject to Regulation T
    When the platform evaluates provisional credit
    Then the platform does not require a provisional credit
    And the platform records the exception reason "Regulation T account"
```

### Story 8: Extended time periods
**Ref: § 1005.11(c)(3)**

As a compliance officer, I want the platform to apply extended periods automatically when the stated conditions exist, so that deadlines are set correctly.

```gherkin
Feature: Extension of time periods

  Scenario: 20 business days replaces 10 business days for new accounts
    Given the notice involves an electronic fund transfer to or from the account
    And the transfer occurred within 30 days after the first deposit to the account was made
    When the platform sets investigation deadlines

    And the same 20-business-day period applies to the provisional credit deadline in the 45-day process

  Scenario Outline: 90 days replaces 45 days
    Given the 45-day period has been invoked
    And the transfer "<condition>"
    When the platform sets the determination due date
    Then the platform sets the due date at 90 days from receipt of the notice

    Examples:
      | condition                                                    |
      | was not initiated within a state                             |
      | resulted from a point-of-sale debit card transaction         |
      | occurred within 30 days after the first deposit was made     |

  Scenario: Standard periods apply when no extension condition exists
    Given none of the extension conditions applies
    When the platform sets investigation deadlines
    Then the platform uses 10 business days and 45 days

  Scenario: Record the extension basis
    When the platform applies an extended period
    Then the platform records which condition triggered the extension
```

Feature: Scope of investigation for third-party transfers

    And the transfer is not covered by § 1005.14

  Scenario Outline: Own-records review is not treated as sufficient

    Examples:
      | situation                                                                |

  Scenario: Record the agreement status
    When the analyst saves an investigation for a third-party transfer

### Story 11: Debit a provisional credit after an adverse determination
**Ref: § 1005.11(d)(2)**

As a disputes analyst, I want the debit of a provisional credit to trigger the required notices and a five-business-day honoring period, so that the consumer is protected from overdraft consequences.

```gherkin
Feature: Debiting provisional credit

  Scenario: Notify the consumer when the provisional credit is debited
    Given a provisional credit was previously posted
    And the determination is "no error" or "different error"
    When the platform debits the provisional credit
    Then the platform sends the consumer notice of the date and amount of the debit

  Scenario: Notice of the five-business-day honoring commitment
    When the platform sends the debit notice
    Then the notice states the institution will honor checks, drafts or similar instruments payable to third parties for five business days after the notification
    And the notice states the institution will honor preauthorized transfers for five business days after the notification
    And the notice states the consumer will not be charged as a result of an overdraft for those items

  Scenario: Honor only items that would have been paid
    Given the honoring period is active
    When an item is presented
    And the institution would have paid the item had the provisionally credited funds not been debited
    Then the platform pays the item
    And the platform charges no overdraft fee because of the debit

  Scenario: Items the institution would not have paid
    Given the honoring period is active
    When an item is presented
    And the institution would not have paid it had the provisionally credited funds not been debited
    Then the platform is not required to honor the item

  Scenario: Honoring period is tracked
    When the debit notice is sent
    Then the platform sets the honoring period end at five business days after the notification
    And the platform shows the period on the account and case record

  Scenario: Debit blocked if no provisional credit exists
    Given no provisional credit was posted for the case
    When the analyst attempts to debit a provisional credit
    Then the platform blocks the action
```

### Story 12: Reassertion of the same error
**Ref: § 1005.11(e)**

As a disputes analyst, I want to recognize when a consumer reasserts an error the institution has already fully resolved, so that no duplicate investigation is opened.

```gherkin
Feature: Reassertion of error

  Scenario: Same error reasserted after full compliance
    Given a prior case for the same error was closed
    And the institution recorded full compliance with all error resolution requirements on that case
    When the consumer reasserts the same error
    Then the platform marks the new notice "reassertion: no further responsibilities under § 1005.11"

  Scenario: Reassertion after information provided under (a)(1)(vii)

    When the consumer asserts an error following receipt of that information

  Scenario: Prior case not fully compliant
    Given a prior case for the same error was closed without full compliance recorded
    When the consumer reasserts the same error
    Then the platform does not apply the reassertion exception

  Scenario: Compliance checklist gates the exception
    When the analyst closes a case
    Then the platform requires confirmation that all applicable deadlines, notices and corrections were completed
    And the platform stores the result so the reassertion check can use it
```

## Open Questions for Compliance and Product

1. **Business-day and calendar rules:** Which days count as business days, and what is the receipt cutoff time?
2. **Interest on provisional credit:** The text says "where applicable" without a rule. Which interest logic should the platform use?
3. **Untimely notices (Story 2):** The text states the 60-day condition but not the consequence of missing it. The platform currently requires a documented analyst decision. Confirm this is acceptable.
4. **Stories 6 and 7:** The text does not say what an institution must do if it invokes the 45-day period but misses the credit deadline. The platform flags a compliance exception.
5. **§ 1005.14 and § 1005.6(a):** The regulatory text provided only cross-references these. Confirm which upstream flags the platform should consume.
6. **Card vs. ACH differences:** The text calls out point-of-sale debit card transactions (90-day rule) and transfers not initiated within a state. Confirm the source fields that identify these on each rail.
