Reg E Error Resolution (12 CFR 1005.11): User Stories & Acceptance Criteria

Platform: Card and ACH disputes platform
Prepared by: Business Analysis
Status: Draft for Compliance, Legal, Ops and Engineering review

I wrote this from my knowledge of the regulation and its commentary, without checking current sources. Compliance/Legal should validate every citation and every time-limit parameter before build.

1. Scope and assumptions

In scope

Consumer accounts covered by Reg E: debit card (POS, ATM, online), ACH debits and credits, P2P and other EFTs, and prepaid accounts that meet the §1005.18(e) conditions.
Notice intake, timeliness checks, deadline clocks, provisional credit, investigation, resolution, customer notices, reassertion, and recordkeeping evidence.

Out of scope (route elsewhere)

Credit card billing errors (Reg Z, 12 CFR 1026.13)
Remittance transfers (§1005.33)
Network chargeback and Nacha return mechanics, except where they interface with the above
Initial and annual error-resolution disclosures (§§1005.7(b)(10), 1005.8(b))

Assumptions

A1. All time limits are configuration, not code. They are versioned and effective-dated.
A2. “Business day” follows §1005.2(d) and the bank’s published business-day calendar.
A3. The “date of receipt” is the date the bank first receives notice through any channel, per policy.
A4. Merchant quality or service disputes are not Reg E errors. They stay in the chargeback workflow only.

Key parameters

Parameter	Default	Source
Notice window	60 days after the statement on which the error first appears is sent	§1005.11(b)(1)
Written confirmation of oral notice	10 business days	§1005.11(b)(2)
Investigation (standard)	10 business days	§1005.11(c)(1)
Investigation (extended, provisional credit given)	45 days	§1005.11(c)(2)
Investigation (new account, POS debit, or foreign-initiated)	20 business days, or 90 days with provisional credit	§1005.11(c)(3)
Provisional credit notice	2 business days after credit	§1005.11(c)(2)
Report of results	3 business days after investigation completes	§1005.11(d)
Correct a confirmed error	1 business day after determination	§1005.11(d)(1)
Honor third-party items after reversing provisional credit	5 business days	§1005.11(d)(2)
Evidence retention	2 years	§1005.13

Worked date example (used in the scenarios below)

Notice is received Monday 2026-11-23.
The bank is closed Thursday 11/26 (Thanksgiving) and open Friday 11/27.
Saturdays and Sundays are not business days.
Business day 10 is 2026-12-08, and business day 20 is 2026-12-22.
45 calendar days is 2027-01-07, and 90 calendar days is 2027-02-21.
2. Epic A: Intake and classification
US-01: Capture a notice of error from any channel

As a dispute intake agent (or a customer in self-service),
I want to log a notice of error whether it arrives by phone, branch, mail, secure message, or digital channel,
so that the bank’s Reg E clock starts on the correct date.
Ref: §1005.11(b)(1)

gherkin
Feature: Notice of error intake

  Scenario: Oral notice with sufficient information
    Given a customer calls the contact center on 2026-11-23
    And the customer provides name and account number
    And the customer states why they believe an error exists and the amount, to the extent possible
    When the agent saves the dispute
    Then the platform records the receipt date as 2026-11-23 and the channel as "Phone"
    And the platform creates a Reg E case with status "Intake"
    And the Reg E clock is started from the receipt date

  Scenario: Notice lacks enough information to identify the consumer or account
    Given a notice is received without a name or account number
    When the agent attempts to save the dispute
    Then the platform marks the notice "Incomplete"
    And the platform prompts the agent to collect the missing identifiers
    And the clock does not start until the notice is sufficient
    And an audit entry records what was missing and when it was supplied

  Scenario: Consumer cannot state a specific amount or date
    Given the consumer gives the account, the reason they believe an error exists, and no exact amount
    When the agent saves the dispute
    Then the platform accepts the notice as sufficient
    And the amount field is flagged "To be determined"

  Scenario: Notice arrives through a non-designated channel
    Given a notice arrives at a branch rather than the dispute contact center
    When any employee logs it
    Then the receipt date is the date the branch received it
US-02: Validate notice timeliness

As a dispute analyst,
I want the platform to test whether the notice falls inside the 60-day window,
so that untimely notices are handled consistently and never silently rejected.
Ref: §1005.11(b)(1)(i), (b)(3)

gherkin
Feature: Timeliness of notice

  Scenario Outline: Notice measured against statement date
    Given the periodic statement first reflecting the transaction was sent on <statement_date>
    And the notice was received on <receipt_date>
    When the platform evaluates timeliness
    Then the timeliness result is "<result>"

    Examples:
      | statement_date | receipt_date | result   |
      | 2026-11-01     | 2026-12-31   | Timely   |
      | 2026-11-01     | 2027-01-01   | Untimely |

  Scenario: Untimely notice routed for review, not auto-denied
    Given the timeliness result is "Untimely"
    When the evaluation completes
    Then the case is routed to a Compliance reviewer queue
    And the platform requires a documented decision (accept as extenuating circumstances, or decline with rationale)
    And the platform does not auto-close the case

  Scenario: Extenuating circumstances extension
    Given a consumer reports extended travel or hospitalization prevented timely notice
    When the reviewer records the circumstance and supporting detail
    Then the platform allows the reviewer to treat the notice as timely
    And the extension decision is stored in the audit trail

  Scenario: Request for documentation or clarification
    Given the consumer requested documentation or clarification about an EFT under §1005.11(a)(1)(vi)
    When the bank sends the information on 2026-11-10
    Then the 60-day window for a follow-on notice of error is measured from 2026-11-10
US-03: Classify the dispute as a Reg E error or something else

As a dispute analyst,
I want each dispute classified against the §1005.11(a) error types,
so that only qualifying disputes enter the Reg E workflow, and non-qualifying ones are routed correctly without losing the customer.
Ref: §1005.11(a)

gherkin
Feature: Error classification

  Background:
    Given the Reg E error types are configured as:
      | Unauthorized EFT                                                     |
      | Incorrect EFT to or from the consumer's account                      |
      | EFT omitted from periodic statement                                  |
      | Computational or bookkeeping error by the institution                |
      | Incorrect amount received from an electronic terminal                |
      | EFT not properly identified per §§1005.9 or 1005.10(a)               |
      | Request for documentation or clarification, including whether an error exists |

  Scenario: Qualifying error type selected
    Given an analyst selects "Unauthorized EFT" for a debit card transaction
    When the case is classified
    Then the case enters the Reg E workflow
    And the "Reg E applies" flag is set to true

  Scenario Outline: Items that are not Reg E errors
    Given the consumer describes <situation>
    When the analyst classifies the case
    Then the platform shows "<routing>"

    Examples:
      | situation                                               | routing                                               |
      | a routine balance inquiry                               | Not an error: close with response, log inquiry        |
      | a request for a duplicate statement for tax records     | Not an error: fulfil as service request               |
      | a credit card billing error                             | Route to Reg Z billing error workflow                 |
      | merchant goods or services dissatisfaction              | Route to merchant dispute (chargeback) workflow only  |

  Scenario: Classification is reviewable
    Given a case was classified as "Not an error"
    When a supervisor opens the case
    Then the supervisor can reclassify with a reason code
    And if reclassified into Reg E, the clock uses the original receipt date, not the reclassification date

Open question OQ-1: policy on consumer-induced transfers (scams). Commentary and CFPB guidance treat some fraudulently induced transfers as unauthorized. Legal must set the classification rule before this story is finalized.

US-04: Track written confirmation of oral notices

As a dispute operations manager,
I want the platform to support the optional written-confirmation requirement,
so that provisional credit decisions are lawful and consistent.
Ref: §1005.11(b)(2), (c)(2)

gherkin
Feature: Written confirmation

  Scenario: Agent informs the consumer at the time of oral notice
    Given the bank policy requires written confirmation of oral notices
    When an oral notice is taken
    Then the platform displays a mandatory script stating the requirement and the mailing address or upload channel
    And the agent must acknowledge delivery of the script before saving
    And the confirmation due date is set to 10 business days after the oral notice

  Scenario: Consumer confirms in writing on time
    Given the confirmation was due on 2026-12-08
    When a signed confirmation is received on 2026-12-04
    Then the case records "Confirmation received" with the date
    And the provisional credit obligation remains active

  Scenario: Confirmation not received
    Given the confirmation due date has passed with nothing received
    And the required script was delivered and logged at intake
    When the due date elapses
    Then the platform flags "Provisional credit not required"
    And the investigation continues within the applicable time limit

  Scenario: Script delivery not logged
    Given the required script was not logged at intake
    When the confirmation due date elapses
    Then the platform does not permit withholding provisional credit on that basis
    And the case is flagged for Compliance review
3. Epic B: Deadline engine
US-05: Calculate and display all Reg E deadlines

As a dispute analyst,
I want every statutory deadline calculated automatically from the receipt date and the account’s characteristics,
so that no deadline is missed and every deadline can be shown to an examiner.
Ref: §1005.11(c), (d); §1005.2(d)

gherkin
Feature: Deadline engine

  Background:
    Given the business-day calendar excludes Saturdays, Sundays, and bank holidays
    And 2026-11-26 is a bank holiday and 2026-11-27 is a business day

  Scenario Outline: Investigation deadlines by account and transaction type
    Given a Reg E case with receipt date 2026-11-23
    And the account is "<account_type>" and the transaction is "<txn_type>"
    When the deadlines are calculated
    Then the "Complete investigation or provisionally credit by" date is <bd_deadline>
    And the "Outside investigation limit" date is <outer_deadline>

    Examples:
      | account_type            | txn_type            | bd_deadline | outer_deadline |
      | Established (> 30 days) | ACH debit           | 2026-12-08  | 2027-01-07     |
      | Established (> 30 days) | POS debit card      | 2026-12-08  | 2027-02-21     |
      | Established (> 30 days) | Foreign-initiated   | 2026-12-08  | 2027-02-21     |
      | New (first deposit within 30 days of the transfer) | ACH debit | 2026-12-22 | 2027-02-21 |

  Scenario: Deadlines recalculated when facts change
    Given the analyst corrects the transaction type from "ACH debit" to "POS debit card"
    When the change is saved
    Then the platform recalculates the outer limit
    And logs the previous and new dates with user and timestamp

  Scenario: Calendar changes do not rewrite closed history
    Given a case was closed with deadlines computed under holiday calendar version 12
    When the calendar is updated to version 13
    Then the closed case retains its original calculations
    And the calculation records the calendar version used

  Scenario: Deadline visibility
    Given an open Reg E case
    When any user opens it
    Then the case header shows every applicable deadline with a countdown in business or calendar days as appropriate

OQ-2: confirm whether the bank counts Saturday as a business day under §1005.2(d), and the rule when the 45/90-day date falls on a non-business day (default: no roll-forward).

4. Epic C: Provisional credit
US-06: Issue provisional credit when the investigation will exceed 10 business days

As a dispute analyst,
I want the platform to prompt and execute provisional credit,
so that the consumer has full use of funds and the bank may extend to the 45/90-day limit.
Ref: §1005.11(c)(2)

gherkin
Feature: Provisional credit

  Scenario: Investigation cannot complete in 10 business days
    Given a case with business-day deadline 2026-12-08
    And the investigation is not complete as of 2026-12-07
    When the analyst or the scheduled job reaches the decision point
    Then the platform creates a provisional credit for the alleged error amount, including interest where applicable
    And the credit posts no later than 2026-12-08
    And the case status becomes "Provisionally credited"
    And the extended deadline of 2027-01-07 becomes active

  Scenario: Consumer has full use of provisional funds
    Given a provisional credit has posted
    When the platform applies holds
    Then the platform places no hold on the provisional credit amount
    And the credit is available for withdrawal and transfer

  Scenario: Unauthorized EFT, $50 holdback permitted
    Given the error type is "Unauthorized EFT"
    And Compliance has recorded a reasonable basis for believing the transfer was unauthorized
    And the §1005.6(a) conditions are recorded as satisfied
    When provisional credit is calculated for an alleged error of $300.00
    Then the platform permits a maximum withheld amount of $50.00
    And the provisional credit amount is $250.00
    And the holdback decision and basis are stored in the audit trail

  Scenario: Exception, no provisional credit without written confirmation
    Given written confirmation was required, the required script was logged, and confirmation is overdue
    When the credit decision point is reached
    Then the platform records "Provisional credit not required: no written confirmation"
    And no credit is posted

  Scenario: Exception, Reg T account
    Given the account is subject to Regulation T
    When the credit decision point is reached
    Then the platform records "Provisional credit not required: Reg T"

  Scenario: Investigation completes within 10 business days
    Given the investigation concludes on 2026-12-02 with an error found
    When the case is resolved
    Then no provisional credit is required before resolution
    And the correction follows US-10 timing

  Scenario: Missed provisional credit deadline
    Given the credit has not posted by end of day 2026-12-08
    When the nightly breach check runs
    Then the case is flagged "Regulatory breach"
    And Compliance is notified

As a consumer,
I want to be told that provisional credit has been made, in what amount, and on what date,
so that I understand my funds and the investigation timeline.
Ref: §1005.11(c)(2)(ii)

gherkin
Feature: Provisional credit notice

  Scenario: Notice generated and sent within 2 business days
    Given a provisional credit of $250.00 posted on 2026-12-08
    When the credit posts
    Then the platform generates a notice stating the credit amount and the credit date
    And the notice is sent no later than 2026-12-10
    And the platform records delivery channel, timestamp, and template version

  Scenario: Delivery failure
    Given the notice bounces or fails delivery
    When the delivery failure is detected
    Then the platform creates a task to resend by an alternate channel
    And the failure and cure are logged

  Scenario: Oral notice permitted but must be recorded
    Given policy allows oral notification
    When an agent notifies the consumer by phone
    Then the agent records the date, time, and content
    And a written copy is still sent by default
5. Epic D: Investigation
US-08: Run and document the investigation

As a dispute analyst,
I want a guided investigation workflow with evidence capture,
so that every determination is supportable and consistent.
Ref: §1005.11(c)(1), (c)(4)

gherkin
Feature: Investigation workflow

  Scenario: Required investigation steps by error type
    Given a case classified "Unauthorized EFT"
    When the analyst opens the investigation tab
    Then the platform presents the configured checklist, including account history review, device and channel logs, card or credential status, and customer statement
    And each completed step requires a timestamp, user, and notes

  Scenario: Review of own records permitted for third-party transfers without an agreement
    Given the alleged error concerns a transfer to or from a third party
    And no agreement exists between the bank and that third party for this type of EFT
    When the analyst records the investigation basis
    Then the platform allows "Review of institution records only" as a sufficient basis
    And Compliance can review the conditions for that selection

  Scenario: Broader review otherwise
    Given an agreement exists between the bank and the third party or network for this EFT type
    When the analyst attempts to close the investigation on a records-only basis
    Then the platform requires evidence of the third-party or network inquiry

  Scenario: Cannot close without findings
    Given the analyst has not recorded a determination and rationale
    When the analyst tries to move the case to "Resolution"
    Then the platform blocks the action and lists the missing fields
US-09: Coordinate with card network and ACH processes

As a dispute operations specialist,
I want the Reg E case linked to chargeback and ACH return actions,
so that the customer is made whole on Reg E timelines regardless of recovery from other parties.
Ref: §1005.11; Nacha Operating Rules; network rules

gherkin
Feature: Rails coordination

  Scenario: Customer resolution is not contingent on network recovery
    Given a Reg E error is confirmed on a debit card transaction
    And the chargeback is still pending with the network
    When the correction deadline arrives
    Then the platform requires the customer correction to post on Reg E timing
    And the bank's loss is tracked separately in the recovery ledger

  Scenario: ACH unauthorized debit return
    Given a consumer reports an unauthorized ACH debit
    When the analyst initiates recovery
    Then the platform prompts for a signed written statement of unauthorized debit if the Nacha rules require it
    And it displays the return deadline from the debit's settlement date
    And a missed return window does not extend or alter the Reg E customer deadlines

  Scenario: Recovery outcome recorded
    Given a network chargeback or ACH return completes
    When the outcome is posted
    Then it is linked to the Reg E case
    And the recovery has no effect on the customer's resolution notices

US-11: Resolve when no error is found

As a consumer,

gherkin

  Scenario: Consumer requests supporting documents
    Given a consumer requests the documents the bank relied on

    Then the platform creates a task to deliver copies promptly
    And the task shows the request date and fulfilment date

  Scenario: Same error reasserted after full compliance
    Given case C-1001 was closed with all required notices delivered on time
    When the consumer reasserts the same error
    Then the platform links the new contact to C-1001 and flags "Reassertion"

  Scenario: Prior case was not fully compliant
    Given case C-1001 has an open compliance exception
    When the same error is reasserted

    And it routes the case to Compliance
