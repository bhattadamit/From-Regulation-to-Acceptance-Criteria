To implement Regulation CC next-day availability (**12 CFR § 229.10**) in our deposit hold engine, the stories below break down the statutory requirements into core engine rules. Note that under § 229.10(c)(1)(vii), the minimum next-day availability floor for non-exempt checks (the "$225 rule," inflation-adjusted to **$275** effective July 1, 2025) must also be accounted for alongside full next-day items.

---

### Epic: Reg CC Next-Day Funds Availability (12 CFR § 229.10)

#### Story 1: Electronic Payments and Cash Deposits (12 CFR § 229.10(a)–(b))

**As a** Deposit Operations Manager

**I want** cash deposits made in person and electronic payments (wire transfers and ACH credit transfers) to be granted immediate or next-business-day availability

**So that** the bank complies with 12 CFR § 229.10(a) and § 229.10(b) without placing improper holds on settled funds.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

```gherkin
Feature: Next-Day Availability for Cash and Electronic Payments

  Scenario: Cash deposited in person to a bank employee
    Given a customer has an open transaction account
    When the customer deposits $5,000.00 in cash to a teller on Business Day T
    Then the hold engine shall apply a 0-day hold
    And the full $5,000.00 shall be available no later than the start of Business Day T+1

  Scenario: Cash deposited at a proprietary ATM (not in person to an employee)
    Given a customer has an open transaction account
    When the customer deposits $1,200.00 in cash at a proprietary ATM on Business Day T before the cutoff time
    Then the hold engine shall apply a hold releasing funds no later than Business Day T+2 per 12 CFR 229.10(a)(2)

  Scenario: Incoming wire transfer or ACH credit received
    Given a customer has an open transaction account
    When an incoming Fedwire or ACH credit of $15,000.00 is received by the bank on Business Day T
    Then the hold engine shall apply a 0-day hold
    And the full $15,000.00 shall be available no later than the start of Business Day T+1

```

#### Story 2: U.S. Treasury, USPS Money Orders, and Federal Reserve / FHLB Checks (12 CFR § 229.10(c)(1)(i)–(iii))

**As a** Compliance Officer

**I want** U.S. Treasury checks, U.S. Postal Service money orders, and checks drawn on a Federal Reserve Bank or Federal Home Loan Bank to receive full next-business-day availability when deposited into the payee's account

**So that** federal and quasi-federal instruments meet mandatory Reg CC availability timelines.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

```gherkin
Feature: Next-Day Availability for Federal Instruments

  Scenario: U.S. Treasury check deposited into the payee's account
    Given a U.S. Treasury check for $3,400.00 is payable to the account holder
    And the check is deposited in person or at a proprietary ATM on Business Day T
    When the hold engine evaluates the deposit
    Then the engine shall grant next-business-day availability (T+1) for the full $3,400.00

  Scenario: USPS Money Order or Federal Reserve/FHLB check deposited in person to an employee
    Given a USPS money order or FHLB check for $850.00 is payable to the account holder
    And the item is deposited in person to a bank employee on Business Day T
    When the hold engine evaluates the deposit
    Then the engine shall grant next-business-day availability (T+1) for the full $850.00

  Scenario: USPS Money Order deposited at an ATM (not in person to an employee)
    Given a USPS money order for $850.00 is payable to the account holder
    And the item is deposited at a proprietary ATM on Business Day T
    When the hold engine evaluates the deposit
    Then the engine shall NOT classify the item for mandatory T+1 availability under 229.10(c)(1)(ii)
    And the item shall fall through to standard local check availability rules

```

#### Story 3: State/Local Government Checks and Cashier's, Certified, and Teller's Checks (12 CFR § 229.10(c)(1)(iv)–(v))

**As a** Deposit Operations Manager

**I want** state/local government checks and cashier's, certified, or teller's checks to receive next-day availability only when all statutory conditions (payee match, in-person deposit, geographic/bank location, and special deposit slip if required) are met

**So that** the bank honors Reg CC rules while mitigating counterfeit check fraud on non-qualifying channels.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

```gherkin
Feature: Next-Day Availability for State/Local Government and Official Bank Checks

  Scenario: Qualifying Cashier's, Certified, or Teller's check deposited in person
    Given a cashier's check for $12,000.00 is payable to the account holder
    And the check is deposited in person to a bank employee on Business Day T
    And any bank-required special deposit slip is present
    When the hold engine evaluates the deposit
    Then the engine shall grant next-business-day availability (T+1) for the full $12,000.00

  Scenario: Qualifying State or Local Government check deposited in the same state
    Given a State of Texas check for $2,100.00 is payable to the account holder
    And the deposit is made in person to a bank employee at a branch located in Texas on Business Day T
    And any bank-required special deposit slip is present
    When the hold engine evaluates the deposit
    Then the engine shall grant next-business-day availability (T+1) for the full $2,100.00

  Scenario: Cashier's check deposited via Mobile Remote Deposit Capture (mRDC)
    Given a cashier's check for $4,500.00 is payable to the account holder
    And the check is submitted via Mobile RDC on Business Day T
    When the hold engine evaluates the deposit
    Then the engine shall NOT apply mandatory T+1 availability because the deposit was not made in person to a bank employee
    And the engine shall route the item to standard non-exempt check availability rules

```

#### Story 4: On-Us Checks Deposited at Same-State Branches (12 CFR § 229.10(c)(1)(vi))

**As a** Deposit Product Manager

**I want** checks drawn on our own bank ("on-us" checks) to receive next-business-day availability when deposited at a branch or proprietary ATM in the same state as the paying branch

**So that** internal clearing items adhere to 12 CFR § 229.10(c)(1)(vi).

**Acceptance Criteria:**

```gherkin
Feature: Next-Day Availability for On-Us Checks

  Scenario: On-us check deposited in the same state as the branch holding the payer account
    Given a check for $6,000.00 is drawn on our bank at a branch in Texas
    And the check is deposited into a customer's account at a Texas branch or proprietary ATM on Business Day T
    When the hold engine evaluates the deposit
    Then the engine shall grant next-business-day availability (T+1) for the full $6,000.00

  Scenario: On-us check deposited out-of-state from the payer branch
    Given a check for $6,000.00 is drawn on our bank at a branch in Texas
    And the check is deposited at a branch in California on Business Day T
    When the hold engine evaluates the deposit
    Then the engine shall NOT apply mandatory T+1 availability under 229.10(c)(1)(vi)
    And the item shall be evaluated under standard local check schedules

```

#### Story 5: Minimum Next-Day Availability Floor for Non-Exempt Checks (12 CFR § 229.10(c)(1)(vii))

**As a** Compliance Officer

**I want** the first $275 (current inflation-adjusted statutory amount) of the aggregate daily deposit of non-exempt checks to be made available on the next business day

**So that** customers receive their mandatory minimum liquidity across single or multiple check deposits made on the same banking day.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

```gherkin
Feature: Daily Aggregate Minimum Next-Day Availability ($275 Rule)

  Scenario: Single non-exempt check deposit exceeding $275
    Given a customer has made no prior check deposits on Business Day T
    When the customer deposits a personal check for $1,000.00 on Business Day T
    Then the hold engine shall make $275.00 available on Business Day T+1
    And the remaining $725.00 shall be held according to standard local check availability rules

  Scenario: Multiple non-exempt check deposits on the same business day
    Given a customer deposited a personal check for $150.00 earlier on Business Day T (all $150.00 scheduled for T+1)
    When the customer deposits a second personal check for $400.00 later on Business Day T
    Then the hold engine shall calculate the remaining daily T+1 allowance as $125.00 ($275.00 - $150.00)
    And make $125.00 of the second deposit available on Business Day T+1
    And hold the remaining $275.00 of the second deposit under standard local check availability rules

  Scenario: Single non-exempt check deposit below $275
    Given a customer has made no prior check deposits on Business Day T
    When the customer deposits a personal check for $180.00 on Business Day T
    Then the hold engine shall make the full $180.00 available on Business Day T+1

```

#### Story 6: End-of-Day Cutoff and Exception Hold Override Guardrails (12 CFR § 229.10 & § 229.13)

**As a** Core Banking Systems Architect

**I want** the hold engine to roll deposits received after the channel cutoff time to the next business day and allow § 229.13 exception holds (e.g., New Account, Large Deposit, Reasonable Cause to Doubt Collectibility) to override § 229.10 next-day rules when triggered

**So that** business-day calculations and risk-based holds remain compliant across all deposit channels.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

```gherkin
Feature: Cutoff Time Rollover and Exception Hold Precedence

  Scenario: Qualifying next-day deposit submitted after branch/ATM cutoff time
    Given the branch cutoff time is 4:00 PM local time
    When a customer deposits a $1,000.00 U.S. Treasury check at 4:15 PM on Monday (Business Day T)
    Then the hold engine shall treat the deposit as received on Tuesday (Business Day T+1)
    And make the funds available on Wednesday (Business Day T+2)

  Scenario: Exception hold overrides next-day availability on a Large Deposit
    Given a cashier's check for $15,000.00 meets all criteria for next-day availability under 229.10(c)(1)(v)
    And the bank invokes a Large Deposit exception hold under 12 CFR 229.13(b) (threshold > $6,725)
    When the hold engine evaluates the deposit
    Then the engine shall grant T+1 availability for the first $6,725.00
    And apply an extended exception hold to the remaining $8,275.00 with an automated hold notice trigger

```

### Key Implementation Notes for Engineering & QA

* **Parameterize Thresholds:** Store the **$275** minimum availability floor (229.10(c)(1)(vii)) and **$6,725** large deposit/new account threshold (229.13) in a date-effective configuration table rather than hardcoding them, as the Federal Reserve adjusts these figures for inflation every five years.
* **Channel & Deposit Slip Metadata:** Ensure the intake payload from Teller, ATM, and RDC channels explicitly passes `channel_type`, `in_person_employee_flag`, `deposit_branch_state`, `payer_routing_number`, and `special_deposit_slip_flag` so the rule engine can deterministically evaluate § 229.10(c)(1)(i)–(vi).
