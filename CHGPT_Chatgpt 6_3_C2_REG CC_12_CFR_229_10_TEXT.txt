# **Business Requirements: Regulation CC Next-Day Availability — 12 CFR § 229.10**

**Role:** Senior Business Analyst\
**Business capability:** Deposit Hold Engine\
**Regulatory scope:** 12 CFR § 229.10(a), (b), and (c)\
**Objective:** Implement funds availability rules that determine the latest permissible withdrawal availability date for qualifying cash deposits, electronic payments, and specified check deposits.

## **1. Business requirements and design principles**

The deposit hold engine must:

1. Identify the deposit type and applicable qualification criteria.
2. Determine whether a deposit qualifies for next-business-day availability or second-business-day availability.
3. Calculate the applicable availability deadline using the banking day on which the deposit or electronic payment is received.
4. Apply the special $275 next-day availability provision to qualifying check deposits.
5. Support different treatment for qualifying checks deposited in person and those not deposited in person.
6. Enforce special deposit slip or envelope requirements where the bank requires them.
7. Ensure electronic payments are not considered received until both required receipt conditions are satisfied.

**Terminology**

- **Banking day:** The day on which the depositary bank is considered open for substantially all of its banking activities.
- **Business day:** Used as the regulatory measurement for the availability deadline.
- **Availability deadline:** The latest business day by which funds must be available for withdrawal.

The supplied regulatory text does not define banking day or business day. Their calendar determination is therefore treated as an existing dependency rather than an additional regulatory rule in these stories.

## **Feature 4: $275 next-day availability for other check deposits**

**Regulatory reference:** § 229.10(c)(1)(vii)

**User story**

As a deposit operations stakeholder,\
I want the engine to make the lesser of $275 or the aggregate amount of qualifying customer check deposits available by the next business day,\

# **4. Suggested deposit hold engine decision logic**

The following is a proposed implementation sequence derived from the supplied regulatory text.

```

INPUT:
  Deposit type
  Deposit amount
  Customer identifier
  Account identifier
  Banking day
  In-person deposit indicator
  Deposit made to bank employee indicator
  Check drawer/type
  Payee account indicator
  Depositary bank state
  Issuing government state
  Depositing branch
  Drawee branch
  Branch state
  Check processing region
  Special deposit slip/envelope required indicator
  Special deposit slip/envelope satisfied indicator
  Electronic payment final collection indicator
  Electronic payment account information indicator

PROCESS:

1. IF deposit type = CASH:
     IF deposited in person to bank employee:
         Availability = Next business day
     ELSE:
         Availability = Second business day

2. ELSE IF deposit type = ELECTRONIC PAYMENT:
     IF actually and finally collected funds received
        AND account and amount information received:
         Receipt day = Banking day of receipt
         Availability = Next business day after receipt day
     ELSE:
         Electronic payment not yet received

3. ELSE IF deposit type = CHECK:

     Evaluate § 229.10(c)(1)(i) through (vi):

     IF U.S. Treasury check
        AND account held by payee:
         Availability = Next business day

     ELSE IF USPS money order
        AND account held by payee:
         IF deposited in person to bank employee:
             Availability = Next business day
         ELSE:
             Availability = Second business day

     ELSE IF Federal Reserve Bank or Federal Home Loan Bank check
        AND account held by payee:
         IF deposited in person to bank employee:
             Availability = Next business day
         ELSE:
             Availability = Second business day

     ELSE IF state or local government check
        AND account held by payee
        AND depositary bank located in qualifying state:
         IF special deposit slip/envelope required:
             Evaluate whether requirement is satisfied
         IF deposited in person to bank employee:
             Availability = Next business day
         ELSE:
             Availability = Second business day

     ELSE IF cashier's, certified, or teller's check
        AND account held by payee:
         IF special deposit slip/envelope required:
             Evaluate whether requirement is satisfied
         IF deposited in person to bank employee:
             Availability = Next business day
         ELSE:
             Availability = Second business day

     ELSE IF check deposited at a branch of the depositary bank
        AND drawn on same or another branch of the same bank
        AND both branches are in same state OR same check processing region:
         Availability = Next business day

     ELSE:
         Calculate aggregate qualifying customer check deposits
         across all customer accounts for the banking day.

         Next-day availability amount =
             MIN($275, aggregate qualifying check deposits)

OUTPUT:
  Applicable availability deadline
  Amount subject to next-day availability
  Remaining deposit amount, if applicable

```

## **6. Important regulatory interpretation and scope boundaries**

The following distinctions should be preserved during implementation:

1\. **U.S. Treasury checks:** The supplied text does not impose an in-person deposit requirement for next-day availability under § 229.10(c)(1)(i).

2\. **Other specified official checks:** USPS money orders, Federal Reserve Bank checks, Federal Home Loan Bank checks, government checks, and cashier's/certified/teller's checks have specific in-person requirements for next-day availability. Section (c)(2) provides second-day availability when the specified qualifying checks are not deposited in person.

3\. **Special deposit slips:** The bank may require special slips or envelopes for state/local government checks and cashier's, certified, or teller's checks. If required, the bank must provide them or explain how customers can prepare or obtain them and make them reasonably available.

4\. **$275 aggregation:** The calculation is customer-level, not account-level, and includes checks deposited into all accounts of the customer on any one banking day, provided those checks are not subject to next-day availability under paragraphs (c)(1)(i) through (vi).

5\. **Electronic payment receipt:** Both conditions must be met. Receipt of account information alone, or payment that has not actually and finally been collected, is insufficient.

6\. **Scope limitation:** These stories implement only the supplied text of § 229.10. They do not attempt to implement other Regulation CC provisions, exceptions, or hold extensions.

- The implementation is traceable to the corresponding paragraphs of 12 CFR § 229.10.

**Business analyst recommendation:** Treat the $275 provision as a separate availability allocation capability rather than a standalone deposit classification. This will allow the engine to aggregate eligible check deposits across accounts and allocate the required next-day amount without incorrectly applying the $275 threshold independently to each deposit or account.
