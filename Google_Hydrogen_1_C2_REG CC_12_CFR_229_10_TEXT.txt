### Story 1: Cash Deposits Availability

**As a** Deposit Operations Compliance Officer

**I want** the deposit hold engine to assign funds availability for cash deposits based on whether the deposit was made in person to a bank employee

**So that** the bank complies with Regulation CC § 229.10(a) next-day and second-business-day availability rules.

```gherkin
Feature: Cash Deposit Funds Availability (12 CFR 229.10(a))

  Scenario: Cash deposit made in person to a bank employee
    Given a customer deposits cash into an account on a banking day
    And the deposit is made in person to an employee of the depositary bank
    When the deposit hold engine calculates the funds availability date
    Then the funds must be made available for withdrawal not later than the 1st business day after the banking day of deposit

  Scenario: Cash deposit not made in person to a bank employee
    Given a customer deposits cash into an account on a banking day
    And the deposit is not made in person to an employee of the depositary bank
    When the deposit hold engine calculates the funds availability date
    Then the funds must be made available for withdrawal not later than the 2nd business day after the banking day of deposit

```

### Story 2: Electronic Payments Availability

**As a** Deposit Operations Compliance Officer

**I want** the deposit hold engine to release funds for electronic payments on the next business day only after both collected funds and credit instructions are received

**So that** the bank complies with Regulation CC § 229.10(b).

```gherkin
Feature: Electronic Payment Funds Availability (12 CFR 229.10(b))

  Scenario: Electronic payment received with collected funds and credit information
    Given the bank receives payment in actually and finally collected funds for an electronic payment
    And the bank has received information on the account and amount to be credited on a banking day
    When the deposit hold engine evaluates the electronic payment receipt
    Then the payment is recognized as received on that banking day
    And the funds must be made available for withdrawal not later than the 1st business day after that banking day

  Scenario: Partial receipt of actually and finally collected funds
    Given the bank receives information on the account and amount to be credited for an electronic payment
    And the bank has only received a portion of the payment in actually and finally collected funds
    When the deposit hold engine evaluates the electronic payment receipt
    Then the electronic payment is recognized as received only to the extent of the actually and finally collected funds
    And only that collected amount is scheduled for availability not later than the 1st business day after the banking day of receipt

  Scenario: Credit information received without actually and finally collected funds
    Given the bank receives information on the account and amount to be credited for an electronic payment
    And the bank has not yet received payment in actually and finally collected funds
    When the deposit hold engine evaluates the electronic payment status
    Then the electronic payment is not considered received
    And next-day availability is not triggered until actually and finally collected funds are received

```

### Story 3: Next-Day Availability for U.S. Treasury Checks and On-Us Branch Checks

**As a** Deposit Operations Compliance Officer

**I want** the deposit hold engine to grant next-day availability to eligible U.S. Treasury checks and qualifying same-bank branch checks regardless of deposit channel

**So that** the bank complies with Regulation CC § 229.10(c)(1)(i) and (vi).

```gherkin
Feature: Next-Day Availability for Treasury and On-Us Branch Checks (12 CFR 229.10(c)(1)(i), (vi))

  Scenario: U.S. Treasury check deposited into payee's account
    Given a check is drawn on the Treasury of the United States
    And the check is deposited into an account held by a payee of the check on a banking day
    When the deposit hold engine calculates the funds availability date
    Then the funds must be made available for withdrawal not later than the 1st business day after the banking day of deposit

  Scenario: U.S. Treasury check deposited into a non-payee account
    Given a check is drawn on the Treasury of the United States
    And the check is deposited into an account not held by a payee of the check
    When the deposit hold engine evaluates next-day eligibility under § 229.10(c)(1)(i)
    Then the check does not qualify for mandatory next-day availability under this rule

  Scenario: On-us check deposited at a branch in the same state or check processing region
    Given a check is drawn on a branch of the depositary bank
    And the check is deposited in a branch of the same depositary bank on a banking day
    And both the depositary branch and the drawee branch are located in the same state or the same check processing region
    When the deposit hold engine calculates the funds availability date
    Then the funds must be made available for withdrawal not later than the 1st business day after the banking day of deposit

  Scenario: On-us check deposited at a branch in a different state and different check processing region
    Given a check is drawn on a branch of the depositary bank
    And the check is deposited in a branch of the same depositary bank
    And the depositary branch and the drawee branch are neither in the same state nor in the same check processing region
    When the deposit hold engine evaluates next-day eligibility under § 229.10(c)(1)(vi)
    Then the check does not qualify for mandatory next-day availability under this rule

```

### Story 6: Aggregate $275 Next-Day Check Availability Rule

**As a** Deposit Operations Compliance Officer

**I want** the deposit hold engine to aggregate all non-next-day checks deposited across all of a customer's accounts on a single banking day and make up to $275 available on the next business day

**So that** the bank complies with Regulation CC § 229.10(c)(1)(vii).

```gherkin
Feature: $275 Aggregate Next-Day Check Availability (12 CFR 229.10(c)(1)(vii))

  Scenario: Single or multiple non-next-day checks totaling $275 or less on one banking day
    Given a customer deposits one or more checks not otherwise eligible for next-day availability under § 229.10(c)(1)(i)-(vi)
    And the aggregate amount of those checks deposited on that banking day across all accounts of the customer is $200.00
    When the deposit hold engine calculates next-day availability for the banking day
    Then $200.00 (the lesser of $275 or the aggregate amount) must be made available for withdrawal not later than the 1st business day after the banking day of deposit

  Scenario: Single or multiple non-next-day checks exceeding $275 on one banking day
    Given a customer deposits one or more checks not otherwise eligible for next-day availability under § 229.10(c)(1)(i)-(vi)
    And the aggregate amount of those checks deposited on that banking day across all accounts of the customer is $650.00
    When the deposit hold engine calculates next-day availability for the banking day
    Then $275.00 (the lesser of $275 or the aggregate amount) must be made available for withdrawal not later than the 1st business day after the banking day of deposit

  Scenario: Multiple non-next-day checks deposited across multiple accounts of the same customer on one banking day
    Given a customer holds Account A and Account B at the depositary bank
    And on the same banking day the customer deposits a $150.00 non-next-day check into Account A
    And on the same banking day the customer deposits a $200.00 non-next-day check into Account B
    When the deposit hold engine aggregates non-next-day check deposits across all accounts of the customer for that banking day
    Then the aggregate non-next-day check deposit amount is calculated as $350.00
    And a total of $275.00 across the customer's accounts must be made available for withdrawal not later than the 1st business day after the banking day of deposit

  Scenario: Mix of § 229.10(c)(1)(i)-(vi) next-day checks and other checks on the same banking day
    Given a customer deposits a $1,000.00 U.S. Treasury check into their payee account on a banking day
    And on the same banking day the customer deposits a $400.00 check not subject to next-day availability under § 229.10(c)(1)(i)-(vi)
    When the deposit hold engine calculates next-day availability for the banking day
    Then the $1,000.00 U.S. Treasury check is excluded from the $275 aggregation calculation and is made available in full on the 1st business day
    And $275.00 of the $400.00 check is made available on the 1st business day after the banking day of deposit

```
