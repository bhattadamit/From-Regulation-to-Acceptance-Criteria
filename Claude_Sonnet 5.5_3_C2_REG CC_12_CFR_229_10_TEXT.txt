# Reg CC § 229.10 Next-Day Availability: User Stories and Acceptance Criteria

**Conventions used in the criteria**
- "Deadline" means the latest permitted availability date ("not later than"). The engine may release earlier but never later.
- "Banking day of deposit" is the day the deposit is credited as deposited. "Business day" is as defined by the bank's calendar configuration (see Open Questions).

---

## US-01: Cash deposited in person
**As a** deposit hold engine, **I want** to set the availability deadline for cash deposits made in person to a bank employee **so that** funds are available by the next business day.

```gherkin
Feature: Cash deposits - § 229.10(a)

  Scenario: Cash deposited in person to a bank employee
    Given a cash deposit is made to an account
    And the deposit is made in person to an employee of the depositary bank
    When the deposit is posted on a banking day
    Then the availability deadline is the business day after that banking day

  Scenario: Cash deposited not in person
    Given a cash deposit is made to an account
    And the deposit is not made in person to an employee of the depositary bank
    When the deposit is posted on a banking day
    Then the availability deadline is the second business day after that banking day
```

*(Covers US-02, (a)(2), as the second scenario. It is kept as a separate story in the backlog for traceability.)*

## US-02: Cash deposited not in person
**As a** deposit hold engine, **I want** a separate rule path for cash not deposited in person (for example ATM or mail) **so that** the second-business-day deadline is applied.

```gherkin
  Scenario Outline: Cash deadline by channel
    Given a cash deposit is made on a banking day
    And the "in person to bank employee" indicator is <in_person>
    Then the availability deadline is <deadline>

    Examples:
      | in_person | deadline                                      |
      | true      | business day after the banking day            |
      | false     | second business day after the banking day     |
```

## US-03: Electronic payments
**As a** deposit hold engine, **I want** to treat an electronic payment as received only when both receipt conditions are met **so that** the next-day clock starts on the correct banking day and for the correct amount.

```gherkin
Feature: Electronic payments - § 229.10(b)

  Scenario: Payment received when funds and account information are both present
    Given the bank has received payment in actually and finally collected funds
    And the bank has received information on the account and amount to be credited
    When the electronic payment is recorded on a banking day
    Then the payment is marked "received" on that banking day
    And the availability deadline is the business day after that banking day

  Scenario: Final funds received but no account/amount information
    Given the bank has received payment in actually and finally collected funds
    And the bank has not received information on the account and amount to be credited
    Then the payment is not marked "received"
    And no availability deadline is set

  Scenario: Account/amount information received but funds not yet finally collected
    Given the bank has received information on the account and amount to be credited
    And the bank has not received payment in actually and finally collected funds
    Then the payment is not marked "received"
    And no availability deadline is set

  Scenario: Payment received only in part
    Given the bank has received account and amount information for $1,000.00
    And the bank has received actually and finally collected funds of only $600.00
    Then the payment is treated as received only for $600.00
    And the $600.00 has an availability deadline of the business day after the banking day it was received
    And the remaining $400.00 has no deadline until it is received in finally collected funds
```

## US-04: Treasury checks
**As a** deposit hold engine, **I want** next-business-day availability for U.S. Treasury checks deposited by a payee **so that** § 229.10(c)(1)(i) is met.

```gherkin
Feature: Check deposits, next-day - § 229.10(c)(1)

  Scenario Outline: Check drawn on the Treasury of the United States
    Given a check drawn on the Treasury of the United States
    And the account holder is <payee_status> of the check
    When the check is deposited on a banking day
    Then the next-day rule (c)(1)(i) <applies>

    Examples:
      | payee_status | applies                                                   |
      | a payee      | applies; deadline is the business day after the banking day |
      | not a payee  | does not apply                                            |
```

*Note: the supplied text has no second-day tier for (c)(1)(i), so the channel (in person or not) is irrelevant for this check type.*

## US-10: Lesser of $275 or aggregate check deposits
**As a** deposit hold engine, **I want** to make available the lesser of $275 or the day's aggregate of other check deposits **so that** § 229.10(c)(1)(vii) is met.

```gherkin
  Scenario Outline: Aggregate amount of checks not subject to next-day availability
    Given a customer deposits checks on one banking day to all of the customer's accounts
    And the checks not subject to next-day availability under (c)(1)(i) through (vi) total <aggregate>
    Then the amount available by the business day after that banking day is <next_day_amount>

    Examples:
      | aggregate | next_day_amount |
      | $100.00   | $100.00         |
      | $275.00   | $275.00         |
      | $1,000.00 | $275.00         |

  Scenario: Checks qualifying under (c)(1)(i) through (vi) are excluded from the aggregate
    Given a customer deposits on one banking day:
      | item                                          | amount     |
      | Treasury check, payee deposit                 | $2,000.00  |
      | Personal check not covered by (c)(1)(i)-(vi)  | $500.00    |
    Then the Treasury check is handled under (c)(1)(i)
    And the aggregate for (c)(1)(vii) is $500.00
    And the amount available by the next business day under (c)(1)(vii) is $275.00

  Scenario: Aggregation spans all of the customer's accounts
    Given a customer deposits a $200.00 check to account A on one banking day
    And the same customer deposits a $200.00 check to account B on that banking day
    And both checks are not subject to next-day availability under (c)(1)(i) through (vi)
    Then the aggregate is $400.00
    And the total next-day amount across both accounts is $275.00
```

## US-11: Special deposit slip configuration
**As a** compliance configuration owner, **I want** to configure whether the bank requires special deposit slips or envelopes **so that** the engine applies the slip condition in US-07 and US-08 correctly.

```gherkin
Feature: Special deposit slip - § 229.10(c)(3)

  Scenario: Bank does not require special slips
    Given the bank has not configured a special-slip requirement
    When a state/local government check or a cashier's, certified, or teller's check is deposited
    Then the special deposit slip condition is treated as satisfied

  Scenario: Bank requires special slips and the deposit uses one
    Given the bank requires a special deposit slip or envelope identifying the type of check
    And the deposit used a slip or envelope that identifies the type of check
    Then the special deposit slip condition is satisfied

  Scenario: Bank requires special slips and the deposit does not use one
    Given the bank requires a special deposit slip or envelope identifying the type of check
    And the deposit did not use one
    Then the special deposit slip condition is not satisfied

  Scenario: Enabling the requirement requires a customer-access method
    Given a user attempts to enable the special-slip requirement
    When no method is configured for either providing the slips/envelopes to customers or informing customers how to prepare or obtain them
    Then the configuration is rejected
    And the user is told that one of those methods must be set up and made reasonably available
```

## Open Questions and Assumptions

The regulatory text supplied does not cover several things the engine needs. I did not fill them in from outside sources:

1. **Definitions are missing.** "Business day," "banking day," "check processing region," and "in person to an employee" are not defined in the excerpt. The criteria assume the bank's calendar and channel data already supply these.
2. **No rule for checks that miss every condition.** The text sets no § 229.10 deadline for these, so the Examples show "not determined by § 229.10." You will need a product decision on which other rules or policies apply.
3. **Second-day gaps.** (c)(2) references only (c)(1)(ii) through (v). Treasury checks (i) and same-bank checks (vi) have no second-day tier in this text.
4. **(c)(1)(vii) boundary.** It is unclear whether checks that fall to second-day treatment under (c)(2) count as "not subject to next-day availability under (c)(1)(i) through (vi)" for the aggregate. US-10 assumes they do not qualify for next day, so they are in the aggregate. Confirm this with Compliance or Legal.
5. **Partial electronic receipt.** US-03 applies "only to the extent" language by amount, which is my interpretation.
6. **Local government checks.** The same-state condition for local government checks is read as the state of the issuing unit, per (c)(1)(iv)(B).

I'm not a lawyer, so have Compliance or Legal review these before build.
