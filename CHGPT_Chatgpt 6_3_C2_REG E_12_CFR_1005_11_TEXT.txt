# **Regulation E Error Resolution — User Stories and Gherkin Acceptance Criteria**

**Regulatory scope:** 12 CFR § 1005.11, Procedures for resolving errors\
**Platform scope:** Card and ACH disputes platform\
**Perspective:** Consumer-facing US bank operations and technology implementation

## **1. Implementation approach**

The requirements below translate the supplied regulatory text into functional user stories and testable Gherkin acceptance criteria.

The stories cover the complete error-resolution lifecycle:

1. Error classification and inquiry handling.
2. Notice of error intake and eligibility.
3. Oral notice and written confirmation.
4. Investigation deadlines and extensions.
5. Provisional credit and consumer access to funds.
6. Investigation outcomes and corrections.
7. Consumer notifications and documentation.
8. Provisional credit reversal protections.
9. Reassertion of previously investigated errors.

**Important scope boundary:** Acceptance criteria are based exclusively on the text supplied from § 1005.11. No additional regulatory requirements, operational assumptions, or interpretations from other sections are introduced as independent compliance obligations. Where § 1005.11 references another section, that reference is identified as a dependency rather than an attempt to define its requirements.

# **Epic 1 — Error identification and classification**

## **US-001: Classify consumer-reported errors**

**User story**

As a disputes operations analyst, I want the platform to identify whether a consumer's reported issue meets the definition of an error under Regulation E, so that qualifying notices are processed through the applicable error-resolution workflow.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Regulation E error classification

  Scenario Outline: Classify a qualifying issue as an error
    Given a consumer submits an inquiry concerning an electronic fund transfer
    When the inquiry describes "<error_type>"
    Then the platform shall classify the inquiry as a Regulation E error

    Examples:
      | error_type |
      | Unauthorized electronic fund transfer |
      | Incorrect electronic fund transfer to or from the consumer's account |
      | Omission of an electronic fund transfer from a periodic statement |
      | Computational or bookkeeping error relating to an electronic fund transfer |
      | Incorrect amount of money received from an electronic terminal |
      | Electronic fund transfer not identified in accordance with section 1005.9 or 1005.10(a) |
      | Request for required documentation, additional information, or clarification concerning an electronic fund transfer |

  Scenario Outline: Exclude inquiries that are not errors
    Given a consumer submits an inquiry
    When the inquiry is "<inquiry_type>"
    Then the platform shall not classify the inquiry as a Regulation E error

    Examples:
      | inquiry_type |
      | Routine inquiry about account balance |
      | Request for information for tax or other recordkeeping purposes |
      | Request for duplicate copies of documentation |
```

## **US-002: Capture documentation and clarification requests**

**User story**

As a consumer service representative, I want to record requests for documentation, additional information, or clarification concerning an electronic fund transfer, so that the platform can distinguish qualifying requests from routine inquiries and support subsequent error-resolution notices.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Documentation and clarification requests

  Scenario: Record a qualifying documentation request
    Given a consumer requests documentation required by section 1005.9 or 1005.10(a)
    When the request is submitted
    Then the platform shall classify the request as an error under section 1005.11(a)(1)(vii)

  Scenario: Record a request to determine whether an error exists
    Given a consumer requests additional information or clarification concerning an electronic fund transfer
    When the request is submitted to determine whether an error exists
    Then the platform shall classify the request as an error under section 1005.11(a)(1)(vii)

  Scenario: Distinguish duplicate documentation requests
    Given a consumer requests duplicate copies of documentation
    When the request is submitted
    Then the platform shall not classify the request as an error under section 1005.11(a)(2)
```

# **Epic 2 — Notice of error intake and eligibility**

## **US-003: Accept oral and written notices of error**

**User story**

As a consumer service representative, I want to record oral and written notices of error, so that the bank can initiate the applicable Regulation E error-resolution process regardless of the notice channel.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Notice of error intake

  Scenario Outline: Accept oral or written notice
    Given a consumer reports an alleged electronic fund transfer error
    When the notice is received through "<channel>"
    Then the platform shall create a notice of error record
    And record the date the institution received the notice

    Examples:
      | channel |
      | Oral |
      | Written |
```

## **US-004: Validate the 60-day notice period**

**User story**

As a disputes operations analyst, I want the platform to determine whether a notice of error was received within the applicable 60-day period, so that the bank can identify notices subject to § 1005.11.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Standard notice timeliness

  Scenario: Accept a notice received within 60 days
    Given the institution sent the periodic statement or provided passbook documentation
    And the alleged error was first reflected on that statement or documentation
    When the institution receives the notice no later than 60 days after sending or providing it
    Then the notice shall satisfy the timing requirement

  Scenario: Accept a notice received on the 60th day
    Given the alleged error was first reflected on the periodic statement
    When the institution receives the notice exactly 60 days after sending the statement
    Then the notice shall satisfy the timing requirement

  Scenario: Identify a notice received after 60 days
    Given the alleged error was first reflected on the periodic statement
    When the institution receives the notice more than 60 days after sending the statement
    Then the notice shall be identified as outside the standard notice period
```

## **US-005: Validate required notice information**

**User story**

As a disputes operations analyst, I want the platform to capture and validate the consumer's identifying and error-related information, so that notices meet the content requirements of Regulation E.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Notice information validation

  Scenario: Validate consumer identification
    Given a consumer submits a notice of error
    When the notice includes the consumer's name and account number
    Then the platform shall recognize the identification requirements as satisfied

  Scenario: Identify missing consumer information
    Given a consumer submits a notice of error
    When the notice does not enable the institution to identify the consumer's name or account number
    Then the platform shall identify the missing information

  Scenario: Capture details of the alleged error
    Given a consumer submits a notice of error
    When the notice indicates why the consumer believes an error exists
    Then the platform shall record the stated reason
    And capture the type, date, and amount of the error to the extent possible

  Scenario: Exclude error details requirement for qualifying information requests
    Given a consumer submits a request described under section 1005.11(a)(1)(vii)
    When the platform captures the notice
    Then the platform shall not require the type, date, and amount of an alleged error as notice contents
```

## **US-006: Apply the special timeliness rule for documentation-based notices**

**User story**

As a disputes operations analyst, I want the platform to calculate notice timeliness based on the date requested information was sent when the notice is based on that information, so that the special timing rule is applied.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Documentation-based notice timeliness

  Scenario: Accept notice within 60 days after requested information is sent
    Given a consumer requested documentation, additional information, or clarification
    And the institution sent the requested information
    When the consumer provides a notice of error no later than 60 days after the institution sent that information
    Then the notice shall be considered timely

  Scenario: Identify notice received more than 60 days after requested information is sent
    Given a consumer requested documentation, additional information, or clarification
    And the institution sent the requested information
    When the consumer provides a notice of error more than 60 days after the institution sent that information
    Then the notice shall be identified as outside the special notice period
```

# **Epic 3 — Oral notice and written confirmation**

## **US-007: Manage written confirmation of oral notices**

**User story**

As a disputes operations analyst, I want the platform to support the bank's option to require written confirmation following an oral notice of error, so that the confirmation requirement and applicable deadline are tracked.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Written confirmation of oral notice

  Scenario: Record an oral notice without requiring written confirmation
    Given a consumer provides an oral notice of error
    And the institution does not require written confirmation
    When the oral notice is recorded
    Then the platform shall not require written confirmation

  Scenario: Inform consumer of written confirmation requirement
    Given a consumer provides an oral notice of error
    And the institution requires written confirmation
    When the oral notification is received
    Then the institution shall inform the consumer of the written confirmation requirement
    And provide the address where confirmation must be sent

  Scenario: Track the 10-business-day confirmation period
    Given the institution requires written confirmation of an oral notice
    When the oral notice is received
    Then the platform shall calculate a 10-business-day confirmation deadline

  Scenario: Record written confirmation received within 10 business days
    Given the institution requires written confirmation
    And the consumer provided an oral notice
    When written confirmation is received within 10 business days
    Then the platform shall record the confirmation as received

  Scenario: Identify missing written confirmation
    Given the institution requires written confirmation
    And the consumer provided an oral notice
    When written confirmation is not received within 10 business days
    Then the platform shall identify that the required confirmation was not received
```

# **Epic 4 — Investigation management and regulatory deadlines**

## **US-008: Initiate and complete investigations within 10 business days**

**User story**

As a disputes investigator, I want the platform to establish and track the standard investigation deadline, so that the institution determines whether an error occurred within the applicable regulatory period.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Standard investigation timeline

  Scenario: Establish the standard investigation deadline
    Given the institution receives a notice of error
    When the investigation is initiated
    Then the platform shall calculate a deadline of 10 business days from receipt

  Scenario: Complete investigation within 10 business days
    Given the institution receives a notice of error
    When the investigation determines whether an error occurred within 10 business days
    Then the platform shall record the investigation determination date

  Scenario: Identify an investigation exceeding 10 business days
    Given the institution receives a notice of error
    And the investigation cannot be completed within 10 business days
    When the standard investigation period expires
    Then the platform shall identify the investigation as requiring the extended investigation process
```

## **US-009: Apply the 20-business-day initial investigation period**

**User story**

As a disputes investigator, I want the platform to identify transfers occurring within 30 days after the first deposit to an account, so that the applicable 20-business-day period is used instead of 10 business days.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: New account investigation extension

  Scenario: Apply 20-business-day investigation period
    Given the notice of error involves an electronic fund transfer to or from an account
    And the transfer occurred within 30 days after the first deposit to the account
    When the investigation deadline is calculated
    Then the platform shall calculate 20 business days from receipt of the notice

  Scenario: Apply standard 10-business-day period
    Given the notice of error involves an electronic fund transfer
    And the transfer did not occur within 30 days after the first deposit to the account
    When the investigation deadline is calculated
    Then the platform shall calculate 10 business days from receipt of the notice
```

## **US-010: Apply the extended 45-day or 90-day investigation period**

**User story**

As a disputes investigator, I want the platform to determine the applicable extended investigation deadline based on the transaction characteristics, so that investigations are completed within the permitted extended period.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Extended investigation timelines

  Scenario: Apply 45-day investigation period
    Given the institution cannot complete its investigation within the applicable initial investigation period
    And the alleged error does not meet any of the specified 90-day extension conditions
    When the extended investigation deadline is calculated
    Then the platform shall calculate 45 days from receipt of the notice

  Scenario Outline: Apply 90-day investigation period
    Given the institution cannot complete its investigation within the applicable initial investigation period
    And the alleged error involves an electronic fund transfer that "<condition>"
    When the extended investigation deadline is calculated
    Then the platform shall calculate 90 days from receipt of the notice

    Examples:
      | condition |
      | Was not initiated within a state |
      | Resulted from a point-of-sale debit card transaction |
      | Occurred within 30 days after the first deposit to the account |

  Scenario: Apply 45-day period when no 90-day condition applies
    Given the institution cannot complete its investigation within the applicable initial investigation period
    And the transfer does not meet any of the specified 90-day extension conditions
    When the extended investigation deadline is calculated
    Then the platform shall use the 45-day investigation period
```

## **US-011: Track investigation results and reporting deadlines**

**User story**

As a disputes operations analyst, I want the platform to calculate the deadline for reporting investigation results to the consumer, so that the consumer receives the results within the required period.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Investigation result reporting

  Scenario: Calculate results notification deadline
    Given the institution completes an investigation
    When the investigation completion date is recorded
    Then the platform shall calculate a reporting deadline of three business days after completion

  Scenario: Record results reported within three business days
    Given the institution has completed an investigation
    When the investigation results are reported within three business days
    Then the platform shall record the results reporting date

  Scenario: Identify results reporting outside the deadline
    Given the institution has completed an investigation
    When the results are not reported within three business days
    Then the platform shall identify that the reporting deadline has passed
```

## **US-012: Correct confirmed errors within one business day**

**User story**

As a disputes operations analyst, I want the platform to trigger and track correction of confirmed errors, so that the institution corrects an error within one business day after determining that it occurred.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Confirmed error correction

  Scenario: Establish correction deadline
    Given the investigation determines that an error occurred
    When the determination is recorded
    Then the platform shall calculate a correction deadline of one business day after determination

  Scenario: Record correction within one business day
    Given the institution determines that an error occurred
    When the error is corrected within one business day
    Then the platform shall record the correction date

  Scenario: Identify overdue correction
    Given the institution determines that an error occurred
    When the error has not been corrected within one business day
    Then the platform shall identify that the correction deadline has passed
```

## **US-013: Support investigation using the institution's own records**

**User story**

As a disputes investigator, I want the platform to support an investigation based on the institution's own records when the regulatory conditions are met, so that the investigation approach is consistent with § 1005.11(c)(4).

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Investigation using institution records

  Scenario: Permit review of institution records under qualifying conditions
    Given the alleged error concerns a transfer to or from a third party
    And there is no agreement between the institution and the third party for the type of electronic fund transfer involved
    And the transfer is not covered by section 1005.14
    When the institution reviews its own records
    Then the platform shall allow the investigation to be recorded as based on the institution's own records

  Scenario: Do not apply the own-records provision to section 1005.14 transfers
    Given the alleged transfer is covered by section 1005.14
    When the investigator records the investigation approach
    Then the platform shall not identify review of the institution's own records as sufficient under section 1005.11(c)(4)

  Scenario: Do not apply the own-records provision when a third-party agreement exists
    Given the alleged error concerns a transfer to or from a third party
    And an agreement exists between the institution and the third party for the type of transfer involved
    When the investigator records the investigation approach
    Then the platform shall not identify review of the institution's own records as sufficient under section 1005.11(c)(4)
```

# **Epic 5 — Provisional credit management**

## **US-014: Provisionally credit the consumer's account**

**User story**

As a disputes operations analyst, I want the platform to support provisional credit when the institution cannot complete its investigation within the applicable initial investigation period, so that the consumer receives the required provisional credit.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Provisional credit

  Scenario: Provisionally credit within 10 business days
    Given the institution cannot complete its investigation within 10 business days
    And no provisional credit exception applies
    When the applicable provisional credit deadline is calculated
    Then the platform shall establish a deadline of 10 business days from receipt of the notice

  Scenario: Provisionally credit within 20 business days
    Given the notice involves an electronic fund transfer to or from the account within 30 days after the first deposit
    And the institution cannot complete its investigation within the applicable 20-business-day period
    And no provisional credit exception applies
    When the applicable provisional credit deadline is calculated
    Then the platform shall establish a deadline of 20 business days from receipt of the notice

  Scenario: Include applicable interest
    Given the institution is required to provisionally credit the consumer's account
    And interest is applicable
    When the provisional credit amount is calculated
    Then the provisional credit shall include the amount of the alleged error and applicable interest
```

## **US-015: Apply permitted provisional credit exceptions**

**User story**

As a disputes operations analyst, I want the platform to identify circumstances in which provisional credit is not required, so that the bank can apply the exceptions specified in Regulation E.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Provisional credit exceptions

  Scenario: Apply exception when written confirmation is not received
    Given the institution requires written confirmation of an oral notice of error
    And the institution does not receive written confirmation within 10 business days
    When the provisional credit requirement is evaluated
    Then the platform shall identify that provisional credit is not required under this exception

  Scenario: Apply exception for Regulation T accounts
    Given the alleged error involves an account subject to Regulation T
    When the provisional credit requirement is evaluated
    Then the platform shall identify that provisional credit is not required under this exception

  Scenario: Do not apply exceptions when their conditions are not met
    Given the institution cannot complete the investigation within the applicable initial period
    And the account is not subject to Regulation T
    And the institution does not require written confirmation that remains outstanding
    When the provisional credit requirement is evaluated
    Then the platform shall identify that neither specified exception applies
```

## **US-016: Apply the permitted $50 withholding for qualifying unauthorized transfers**

**User story**

As a disputes operations analyst, I want the platform to calculate a permitted withholding of up to $50 from provisional credit when the specified regulatory conditions are satisfied, so that the provisional credit amount reflects the permitted withholding.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Unauthorized transfer provisional credit withholding

  Scenario: Withhold up to $50 when all conditions are met
    Given the alleged error is an unauthorized electronic fund transfer
    And the institution has a reasonable basis for believing that an unauthorized electronic fund transfer has occurred
    And the institution has satisfied the requirements of section 1005.6(a)
    When provisional credit is calculated
    Then the platform may withhold an amount not exceeding $50 from the amount credited

  Scenario: Do not apply the $50 withholding without a reasonable basis
    Given the alleged error is an unauthorized electronic fund transfer
    And the institution does not have a reasonable basis for believing that an unauthorized electronic fund transfer has occurred
    When provisional credit is calculated
    Then the platform shall not apply the $50 withholding under section 1005.11(c)(2)(i)

  Scenario: Do not apply the $50 withholding when section 1005.6(a) requirements are not satisfied
    Given the alleged error is an unauthorized electronic fund transfer
    And the institution has not satisfied the requirements of section 1005.6(a)
    When provisional credit is calculated
    Then the platform shall not apply the $50 withholding under section 1005.11(c)(2)(i)

  Scenario: Do not exceed the permitted withholding
    Given the institution meets the conditions for the $50 withholding
    When the provisional credit withholding amount is calculated
    Then the withholding amount shall not exceed $50
```

## **US-017: Notify the consumer of provisional credit**

**User story**

As a consumer service representative, I want the platform to generate a notification of provisional credit, so that the consumer receives the credit amount and credit date within the required period.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Provisional credit notification

  Scenario: Notify consumer within two business days
    Given provisional credit has been applied to the consumer's account
    When the credit date and amount are recorded
    Then the platform shall establish a notification deadline of two business days after crediting

  Scenario: Include provisional credit amount and date
    Given provisional credit has been applied
    When the consumer is notified
    Then the notification shall include the amount of provisional credit
    And the date of provisional crediting

  Scenario: Record consumer notification
    Given provisional credit has been applied
    When the consumer notification is sent within two business days
    Then the platform shall record the notification date
```

## **US-018: Provide full use of provisionally credited funds**

**User story**

As a consumer, I want full use of provisionally credited funds during the investigation, so that I can access the credited amount while the bank completes its investigation.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Access to provisional credit

  Scenario: Provide full use of provisionally credited funds
    Given provisional credit has been applied to the consumer's account
    When the investigation remains open
    Then the consumer shall have full use of the provisionally credited funds during the investigation

  Scenario: Confirm access during the investigation
    Given provisional credit has been applied
    And the investigation is ongoing
    When the platform records the provisional credit status
    Then the platform shall identify the credited funds as available for the consumer's full use
```

# **Epic 6 — Investigation outcomes and consumer communications**

## **US-019: Process a determination that an error occurred**

**User story**

As a disputes operations analyst, I want the platform to process a confirmed error determination, so that the error is corrected and the consumer receives the investigation results within the applicable deadlines.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Confirmed error outcome

  Scenario: Process confirmed error
    Given an investigation has been completed
    When the institution determines that an error occurred
    Then the platform shall record the determination
    And establish a correction deadline of one business day
    And establish a results reporting deadline of three business days

  Scenario: Finalize provisional credit when applicable
    Given provisional credit has been applied
    And the institution determines that an error occurred
    When the investigation results are reported
    Then the institution's report shall include notice that provisional credit has been made final where applicable
```

## **US-020: Process a determination that no error occurred**

**User story**

As a disputes operations analyst, I want the platform to support a no-error determination and generate the required written explanation, so that the consumer receives the findings and is informed of the right to request supporting documentation.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: No-error investigation outcome

  Scenario: Generate written explanation of findings
    Given the investigation is complete
    When the institution determines that no error occurred
    Then the investigation results report shall include a written explanation of the institution's findings

  Scenario: Include right to request supporting documents
    Given the institution determines that no error occurred
    When the results report is generated
    Then the report shall inform the consumer of the right to request the documents relied upon in making the determination

  Scenario: Provide requested supporting documents
    Given the institution determines that no error occurred
    And the consumer requests the documents relied upon in making the determination
    When the institution receives the request
    Then the institution shall promptly provide copies of those documents
```

## **US-021: Process a determination that an error occurred differently from the consumer's allegation**

**User story**

As a disputes operations analyst, I want the platform to support determinations where an error occurred in a different manner or amount than alleged, so that the consumer receives an accurate explanation and the required documentation rights.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Different error outcome

  Scenario: Generate written explanation for a different error
    Given the investigation is complete
    When the institution determines that an error occurred in a manner or amount different from that described by the consumer
    Then the investigation results report shall include a written explanation of the institution's findings
    And the report shall note the consumer's right to request the documents relied upon

  Scenario: Provide supporting documents upon request
    Given the institution determined that an error occurred in a different manner or amount
    And the consumer requests the documents relied upon
    When the institution receives the request
    Then the institution shall promptly provide copies of those documents
```

# **Epic 7 — Provisional credit debiting and consumer protections**

## **US-022: Notify the consumer when provisional credit is debited**

**User story**

As a consumer service representative, I want the platform to generate a notification when provisional credit is debited from the consumer's account, so that the consumer receives the date and amount of the debit.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Provisional credit debit notification

  Scenario: Notify consumer of debit date and amount
    Given provisional credit has been applied to the consumer's account
    And the institution subsequently debits the provisionally credited amount
    When the debit is recorded
    Then the institution shall notify the consumer of the date of the debit
    And notify the consumer of the amount debited
```

## **US-023: Provide five-business-day protections following provisional credit debit notification**

**User story**

As a consumer, I want qualifying checks, drafts, and preauthorized transfers to receive the specified protection after notification that provisional credit has been debited, so that these items are honored without an overdraft charge resulting from the debit.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Consumer protections after provisional credit debit

  Scenario: Notify consumer of five-business-day protection
    Given the institution debits a provisionally credited amount
    When the debit notification is generated
    Then the notification shall inform the consumer that the institution will honor qualifying checks, drafts, or similar instruments payable to third parties for five business days after notification
    And the notification shall inform the consumer that the institution will honor qualifying preauthorized transfers for five business days after notification
    And the notification shall state that the consumer will not be charged as a result of an overdraft caused by the debit

  Scenario: Honor qualifying third-party checks during the protection period
    Given the institution has notified the consumer of the provisional credit debit
    And a check, draft, or similar instrument payable to a third party is presented within five business days after notification
    And the institution would have paid the item if the provisionally credited funds had not been debited
    When the item is presented
    Then the institution shall honor the item as specified in the notice

  Scenario: Honor qualifying preauthorized transfers during the protection period
    Given the institution has notified the consumer of the provisional credit debit
    And a preauthorized transfer is presented within five business days after notification
    And the institution would have paid the transfer if the provisionally credited funds had not been debited
    When the transfer is presented
    Then the institution shall honor the transfer as specified in the notice

  Scenario: Limit protection to qualifying items
    Given the institution has notified the consumer of the provisional credit debit
    And a check, draft, similar instrument, or preauthorized transfer is presented
    When the institution would not have paid the item if the provisionally credited funds had not been debited
    Then the institution shall not be required to honor that item under section 1005.11(d)(2)(ii)
```

# **Epic 8 — Reassertion of previously investigated errors**

## **US-024: Manage reassertion of a previously resolved error**

**User story**

As a disputes operations analyst, I want the platform to identify when a consumer reasserts an error that the institution has already fully investigated and resolved, so that the platform can apply the reassertion provisions of Regulation E.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Reassertion of previously investigated errors

  Scenario: Identify a reasserted error after full compliance
    Given the institution has fully complied with the error-resolution requirements
    And the consumer subsequently reasserts the same error
    When the reassertion is received
    Then the platform shall identify the matter as a previously resolved error
    And identify that the institution has no further responsibilities under section 1005.11 for that reassertion

  Scenario: Apply exception for errors asserted following requested information
    Given the institution has fully complied with the error-resolution requirements
    And the consumer previously received information provided under section 1005.11(a)(1)(vii)
    When the consumer asserts an error following receipt of that information
    Then the platform shall not apply the general reassertion exception
```

# **Epic 9 — Cross-platform card and ACH dispute processing**

## **US-025: Apply Regulation E requirements consistently across card and ACH disputes**

**User story**

As a disputes platform product owner, I want Regulation E error-resolution requirements to be applied to qualifying card and ACH electronic fund transfer disputes, so that notices, investigation deadlines, provisional credits, and consumer notifications are managed consistently.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Cross-channel Regulation E processing

  Scenario Outline: Initiate Regulation E workflow for qualifying disputes
    Given a consumer submits a qualifying error notice involving a "<transaction_type>"
    When the platform records the notice
    Then the platform shall initiate the applicable Regulation E error-resolution workflow
    And record the notice receipt date
    And determine the applicable investigation deadline

    Examples:
      | transaction_type |
      | Debit card |
      | ACH |

  Scenario: Apply point-of-sale debit card investigation extension
    Given the alleged error involves a point-of-sale debit card transaction
    And the institution cannot complete its investigation within the applicable initial investigation period
    When the extended investigation deadline is calculated
    Then the platform shall apply the 90-day investigation period

  Scenario: Apply investigation requirements independently of transaction channel
    Given a qualifying electronic fund transfer error notice is received
    When the platform determines the applicable investigation requirements
    Then the platform shall apply the applicable requirements under section 1005.11
    And shall not exclude a qualifying notice solely because it involves an ACH or debit card transaction
```

# **4. Important regulatory dependencies and implementation considerations**

The supplied text references other regulatory provisions. These must be treated as dependencies for a complete implementation.

| Reference       | Dependency                                                                               |
| --------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| § 1005.6(a)     | Requirements that must be satisfied for the permitted $50 provisional credit withholding |
| § 1005.9        | Periodic statements and passbook documentation                                           |
| § 1005.10(a)    | Relevant electronic fund transfer identification and documentation                       |
| § 1005.14       | Transfers excluded from the own-records investigation provision                          |
| 12 CFR Part 220 | Regulation T accounts excluded from the provisional credit requirement                   |

**Specific design considerations:**

1. **Separate investigation and notification clocks.** The platform should maintain independent deadlines for investigation completion, error correction, results reporting, provisional credit, and provisional credit notification.
2. **Support multiple investigation periods.** The platform needs to distinguish the initial 10- or 20-business-day period from the extended 45- or 90-day investigation period.
3. **Preserve the distinction between business days and calendar days.** The 10-, 20-, and 2-business-day requirements should not be treated as calendar-day periods. The 45-, 60-, and 90-day requirements should remain distinct calendar-day periods.
4. **Separate provisional credit from final correction.** Provisional credit and correction of a confirmed error are different events with different deadlines and outcomes.
5. **Maintain evidence of consumer communications.** The platform should retain the information needed to demonstrate notification dates, amounts, written explanations, and document requests.
6. **Support different error outcomes.** The platform must distinguish confirmed errors, no-error determinations, and errors occurring in a different manner or amount.
7. **Avoid assuming that all card disputes are covered.** The platform should determine whether the reported issue meets the definition of an electronic fund transfer error rather than automatically treating every card dispute as a Regulation E error.

# **5. Suggested end-to-end testing scenarios**

These scenarios provide a consolidated view of the most important regulatory workflows.

**Scenario A — ACH error resolved within the standard investigation period**

Standard resolution

1. Consumer submits a timely ACH error notice.
2. Platform validates consumer identification and notice contents.
3. Investigation determines an error occurred within 10 business days.
4. Institution corrects the error within one business day after determination.
5. Institution reports the investigation results within three business days after completion.

**Scenario B — Debit card error requiring extended investigation**

Extended resolution

1. Consumer submits a qualifying debit card error notice.
2. Institution cannot complete the investigation within the applicable initial period.
3. Institution provisionally credits the consumer within the applicable 10- or 20-business-day period, unless an exception applies.
4. Institution notifies the consumer of the provisional credit amount and date within two business days.
5. Consumer receives full use of the provisional funds during the investigation.
6. Institution completes the investigation within the applicable 45- or 90-day period.
7. Institution corrects any confirmed error within one business day and reports results within three business days.

**Scenario C — No error found and provisional credit reversed**

Unfavorable determination

1. Institution completes the investigation and determines that no error occurred.
2. Institution provides a written explanation of its findings and informs the consumer of the right to request supporting documents.
3. Upon request, institution promptly provides copies of the documents relied upon.
4. Institution debits the provisional credit.
5. Institution notifies the consumer of the debit date and amount.
6. Institution informs the consumer of the five-business-day protections for qualifying checks, drafts, and preauthorized transfers.
