### Epic: Regulation CC Next-Day Funds Availability (12 CFR § 229.10)

**Scope & Regulatory Alignment:**

These user stories define the automated rules required for the deposit hold engine to grant **next-business-day availability** (the first business day after the banking day of deposit) for qualifying deposits under **12 CFR § 229.10**, absent any valid Regulation CC exception holds (12 CFR § 229.13).

---

### Story 1: Electronic Payments and Cash Deposits

**User Story**

**As a** Deposit Operations Compliance Manager,

**I want** the deposit hold engine to automatically grant next-business-day availability to cash deposited in person, wire transfers, and ACH credit transfers,

**So that** customers receive timely access to guaranteed electronic and physical currency funds in compliance with 12 CFR § 229.10(a)–(b).

**Acceptance Criteria**

```gherkin
Feature: Next-Day Availability for Cash and Electronic Payments (12 CFR 229.10(a)-(b))

  Background:
    Given the deposit hold engine is evaluating a newly posted deposit
    And no Regulation CC exception holds (12 CFR 229.13) apply to the account or item

  Scenario: Cash deposited in person to a bank employee
    Given a deposit of "Cash" is made on banking day "T"
    And the deposit channel is "In-Person Teller"
    When the deposit hold engine evaluates the deposit
    Then the engine shall set the funds availability date to banking day "T+1"
    And the hold amount shall be "$0.00"

  Scenario: Cash deposited at an unstaffed facility or proprietary ATM
    Given a deposit of "Cash" is made on banking day "T"
    And the deposit channel is "Proprietary ATM" or "Night Depository"
    When the deposit hold engine evaluates the deposit
    Then the engine shall classify the deposit as not made in person to an employee
    And the engine shall set the funds availability date to banking day "T+2"

  Scenario: Wire transfer or ACH credit received
    Given an electronic payment of type "Wire Transfer" or "ACH Credit" is received on banking day "T"
    When the deposit hold engine evaluates the transaction
    Then the engine shall set the funds availability date to banking day "T+1"
    And the hold amount shall be "$0.00"

```

### Story 2: U.S. Treasury, U.S. Postal Service, and Federal Reserve/FHLB Checks

**User Story**

**As a** Deposit Operations Compliance Manager,

**I want** the deposit hold engine to grant next-business-day availability to U.S. Treasury checks, U.S. Postal Service money orders, and Federal Reserve Bank or Federal Home Loan Bank checks when statutory deposit conditions are met,

**So that** federal and central-bank-backed instruments are cleared without unlawful holds under 12 CFR § 229.10(c)(1)(i)–(iii).

**Acceptance Criteria**

```gherkin
Feature: Next-Day Availability for Federal and Postal Instruments (12 CFR 229.10(c)(1)(i)-(iii))

  Background:
    Given the deposit hold engine is evaluating a check deposit made on banking day "T"
    And no Regulation CC exception holds apply

  Scenario: U.S. Treasury check deposited into payee's account via any channel
    Given the check instrument type is "US Treasury Check"
    And the check is deposited into an account held by the named payee
    And the deposit channel is "In-Person Teller", "Proprietary ATM", or "Mobile Remote Deposit"
    When the deposit hold engine evaluates the deposit
    Then the engine shall set the funds availability date to banking day "T+1"

  Scenario: U.S. Postal Service money order deposited in person
    Given the check instrument type is "USPS Money Order"
    And the check is deposited into an account held by the named payee
    And the deposit channel is "In-Person Teller"
    When the deposit hold engine evaluates the deposit
    Then the engine shall set the funds availability date to banking day "T+1"

  Scenario: Federal Reserve Bank or Federal Home Loan Bank check deposited in person
    Given the check instrument type is "Federal Reserve Bank Check" or "FHLB Check"
    And the check is deposited into an account held by the named payee
    And the deposit channel is "In-Person Teller"
    When the deposit hold engine evaluates the deposit
    Then the engine shall set the funds availability date to banking day "T+1"

  Scenario: USPS Money Order or FRB/FHLB check deposited at an ATM (not in person)
    Given the check instrument type is "USPS Money Order", "Federal Reserve Bank Check", or "FHLB Check"
    And the deposit channel is "Proprietary ATM"
    When the deposit hold engine evaluates the deposit
    Then the engine shall not grant T+1 availability under 12 CFR 229.10(c)(1)
    And the engine shall apply the standard second-business-day ("T+2") availability schedule

```

### Story 6: Aggregate Next-Day Availability Rule ($275 Rule)

**User Story**

**As a** Deposit Operations Compliance Manager,

**I want** the deposit hold engine to make the lesser of $275 or the aggregate amount of non-next-day checks deposited on a single banking day available on the next business day,

**So that** customers receive the mandatory minimum next-day liquidity required by 12 CFR § 229.10(c)(1)(vii) (as inflation-adjusted effective July 1, 2025).

**Acceptance Criteria**

```gherkin
Feature: Aggregate $275 Next-Day Availability Rule (12 CFR 229.10(c)(1)(vii))

  Background:
    Given the statutory minimum next-day availability threshold is configured to "$275.00"
    And no Regulation CC exception holds apply

  Scenario: Single standard check deposit exceeding $275.00
    Given a customer deposits a single standard check of "$1,000.00" on banking day "T"
    And the customer has made no other check deposits across any accounts on banking day "T"
    When the deposit hold engine evaluates the deposit
    Then "$275.00" shall be assigned an availability date of banking day "T+1"
    And "$725.00" shall be assigned an availability date of banking day "T+2"

  Scenario: Single standard check deposit less than $275.00
    Given a customer deposits a single standard check of "$180.00" on banking day "T"
    And the customer has made no other check deposits on banking day "T"
    When the deposit hold engine evaluates the deposit
    Then "$180.00" shall be assigned an availability date of banking day "T+1"
    And "$0.00" shall be held for banking day "T+2"

  Scenario: Multiple standard check deposits across multiple accounts on the same banking day
    Given a customer holds "Checking Account A" and "Checking Account B"
    And the customer deposits a "$200.00" standard check into "Checking Account A" at 10:00 AM on banking day "T"
    And the customer deposits a "$300.00" standard check into "Checking Account B" at 2:00 PM on banking day "T"
    When the deposit hold engine evaluates the second deposit
    Then the engine shall recognize "$200.00" of the "$275.00" daily aggregate allowance was already allocated to "Checking Account A"
    And the engine shall allocate only "$75.00" for "T+1" availability on "Checking Account B"
    And the remaining "$225.00" on "Checking Account B" shall be assigned an availability date of banking day "T+2"

  Scenario: Combination of a Next-Day Check and a Standard Check on the same banking day
    Given a customer deposits a "$500.00" U.S. Treasury check (qualifying under 229.10(c)(1)(i)) on banking day "T"
    And the customer also deposits a "$400.00" standard check on banking day "T"
    When the deposit hold engine evaluates both deposits
    Then the full "$500.00" U.S. Treasury check shall be available on banking day "T+1"
    And "$275.00" of the "$400.00" standard check shall be available on banking day "T+1"
    And "$125.00" of the "$400.00" standard check shall be available on banking day "T+2"

```

### Story 7: Banking Day Cutoff & Holiday Calendar Alignment

**User Story**

**As a** Deposit Operations Compliance Manager,

**I want** the deposit hold engine to determine the effective banking day ("T") based on channel-specific cutoff times and the Federal Reserve holiday calendar before calculating "T+1",

**So that** after-hours, weekend, and holiday deposits are anchored to the correct banking day under 12 CFR § 229.2(f)–(g) and § 229.10.

**Acceptance Criteria**

```gherkin
Feature: Banking Day Determination for Next-Day Availability Calculation

  Background:
    Given the branch cutoff time is "4:00 PM" local time
    And the proprietary ATM / Mobile RDC cutoff time is "8:00 PM" local time
    And Monday, May 25, 2026 is configured as a Federal Reserve holiday (Memorial Day)

  Scenario: Cash deposit made before branch cutoff on a regular business day
    Given a customer deposits "$500.00" cash at a branch teller on Wednesday at "3:45 PM"
    When the deposit hold engine determines the banking day
    Then the effective banking day "T" shall be "Wednesday"
    And the next-day availability date "T+1" shall be "Thursday"

  Scenario: Cash deposit made after branch cutoff prior to a weekend and Federal holiday
    Given a customer deposits "$500.00" cash at an extended-hours branch teller on Friday at "4:30 PM"
    And Monday is a Federal Reserve holiday
    When the deposit hold engine determines the banking day
    Then the deposit shall roll to the next business day, setting effective banking day "T" to "Tuesday"
    And the next-day availability date "T+1" shall be "Wednesday"

  Scenario: Wire transfer received on a non-business day (Saturday)
    Given an incoming wire transfer is received on "Saturday"
    When the deposit hold engine determines the banking day
    Then the effective banking day "T" shall be "Monday" (assuming Monday is a business day)
    And the next-day availability date "T+1" shall be "Tuesday"

```

### Implementation & BA Notes for Engineering

* **Exception Hold Precedence (12 CFR § 229.13):** Ensure the rule engine evaluates exception triggers (New Account, Large Deposit >$6,725, Redeposited Check, Repeated Overdraft, Reasonable Cause to Doubt Collectibility, Emergency Conditions) *prior* to finalizing `T+1` release. Note that under New Account rules (§ 229.13(a)), the first $6,725 of next-day checks (Treasury, USPS, FRB/FHLB, state/local, cashier's/certified/teller's) still receives `T+1` availability, while the $275 aggregate rule (§ 229.10(c)(1)(vii)) does **not** apply to new accounts.
* **Parameterization:** Store the `$275.00` aggregate threshold and `$6,725.00` exception thresholds in a versioned configuration table with effective dates, as the Federal Reserve adjusts these figures for inflation every five years (next scheduled adjustment: July 1, 2030).
* **Special Deposit Slips (12 CFR § 229.10(c)(3)):** If the bank elects *not* to require special deposit slips for state/local government or cashier's/certified/teller's checks, Stories 3 and 4 should default `"Special Deposit Slip" indicator` to `Not Required`.
