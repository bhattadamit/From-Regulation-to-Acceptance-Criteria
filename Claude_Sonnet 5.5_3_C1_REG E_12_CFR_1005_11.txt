# Regulation E Error Resolution (12 CFR 1005.11): User Stories and Gherkin Acceptance Criteria

**Prepared as:** Business Analyst draft for Compliance, Product, and Engineering review
**Date:** November 20, 2026

## 1. Scope and Assumptions

**In scope:** Notices of error on EFTs from consumer accounts: debit card (POS, ATM, online), prepaid, and ACH (including preauthorized debits and credits). This includes unauthorized transfers, incorrect amounts, omitted transfers, and computational or bookkeeping errors, as listed in 1005.11(a).

**Out of scope or dependent:**
- Credit card disputes are governed by Reg Z (12 CFR 1026.13), not Reg E. The platform must route them to the Reg Z workflow.
- Consumer liability limits for unauthorized transfers (1005.6) and the network chargeback and representment rules (Visa, Mastercard, Nacha) are separate stories. The Reg E clock runs independently of network timelines.
- Initial and annual error-resolution disclosures (1005.7, 1005.8) are owned by the disclosures team.

**Key regulatory timelines the platform must enforce:**

| Requirement | Standard | Extended (1005.11(c)(3)) |
|---|---|---|
| Consumer notice window | 60 days after the statement or information was sent | Extendable for good cause |
| Investigate and determine | 10 business days | 45 calendar days |
| Investigate and determine (new accounts, POS, foreign-initiated) | 20 business days | 90 calendar days |
| Provisional credit | Within 10 business days of notice (20 for new accounts) | Required if investigation exceeds the standard period |
| Notify consumer of provisional credit | 2 business days after crediting | |
| Report results | 3 business days after completing the investigation | |
| Correct a confirmed error | 1 business day after determining | |
| Records retention | 2 years (1005.13) | |

**Assumptions to confirm:**
- "Business day" follows 1005.2(d): days the bank is open for substantially all business functions. The platform needs a configurable bank business-day calendar rather than a Federal Reserve one.
- "New account" means an account open 30 days or less when the notice is received.
- This draft reflects my understanding of the regulation and Supplement I (Official Interpretations). **Compliance and Legal must validate the final wording and any state-law overlays before build.**

## 2. Actors

Consumer (customer), Contact Center Agent, Disputes Analyst/Investigator, Disputes QA/Compliance Reviewer, Operations/Accounting, System (platform).

## 3. Epics

1. Intake and timeliness
2. Clock management
3. Provisional credit
4. Investigation and determination
5. Resolution and communications
6. Records, controls, and reporting

## EPIC 1: Intake and Timeliness

### US-01: Capture a notice of error through any channel

**As a** consumer, **I want** to report an error by phone, in branch, online, mobile, or in writing, **so that** my notice is recognized and the bank's clock starts no matter how I contact them.

```gherkin
Feature: Notice of error intake

  Background:
    Given a consumer account subject to Regulation E

  Scenario: Oral notice is logged as a valid notice
    Given a customer calls the contact center to report an unrecognized debit card transaction
    When the agent creates a dispute case
    Then the system records the notice received date and time as the case start
    And the notice channel is stored as "Oral - Phone"
    And the case is tagged "Reg E"
    And the disputed transaction, amount, and customer-stated reason are captured

  Scenario: Notice must enable the bank to identify the consumer and the error
    Given a customer provides name, account identifier, and a description of the suspected error
    When the agent completes intake
    Then the system flags the notice as "Sufficient"
    And the case proceeds to timeliness evaluation

  Scenario: Incomplete notice does not defer the clock
    Given a customer provides a description of the error but not the exact amount
    When the agent saves the case
    Then the notice is still logged as received
    And the clock start remains the original notice received date
    And the agent is prompted to obtain missing details during the investigation

  Scenario: Credit card disputes are routed away from Reg E
    Given the disputed transaction is on a credit card account
    When the agent selects the transaction
    Then the system routes the case to the Reg Z dispute workflow
    And no Reg E clock is started
```

### US-02: Classify the error type

**As a** disputes analyst, **I want** the case classified against the error types in 1005.11(a), **so that** the correct investigation path and documentation apply.

```gherkin
Feature: Error type classification

  Scenario Outline: Classify covered error types
    Given a Reg E dispute case is open
    When the analyst selects "<error_type>"
    Then the case is marked as a covered error under 1005.11(a)
    And the "<workflow>" investigation checklist is assigned

    Examples:
      | error_type                                      | workflow             |
      | Unauthorized EFT                                | Unauthorized         |
      | Incorrect EFT to or from the account            | Incorrect amount     |
      | Omission of an EFT from the periodic statement  | Omission             |
      | Computational or bookkeeping error by the bank  | Bank error           |
      | Receipt of incorrect amount from an ATM         | ATM discrepancy      |
      | Request for documentation for tax or recordkeeping | Documentation request (see note) |

  Scenario: Request for information that is not an error
    Given the customer only asks for a copy of documentation, with no assertion of error
    When the analyst classifies the case
    Then the system marks the case "Information request - not a 1005.11 error"
    And no error-resolution clock or provisional credit is created
```

*Note: Confirm with Compliance which routine inquiries, such as requests for documentation or clarification (Comment 11(a)), are excluded and how they are handled.*

### US-03: Evaluate the 60-day notice window

**As a** disputes analyst, **I want** the system to compare the notice date to the 60-day window, **so that** late notices are handled consistently and good-cause extensions are documented.

```gherkin
Feature: Timeliness of notice (1005.11(b)(1))

  Scenario: Notice within 60 days of the statement
    Given the periodic statement showing the transaction was sent on 2026-10-15
    And the notice is received on 2026-11-20
    When the system evaluates timeliness
    Then the case is marked "Timely"
    And the 60-day deadline of 2026-12-14 is stored

  Scenario: Notice after 60 days
    Given the periodic statement showing the transaction was sent on 2026-08-01
    And the notice is received on 2026-11-20
    When the system evaluates timeliness
    Then the case is marked "Outside 60-day window"
    And the analyst is prompted to record whether a good-cause extension applies

  Scenario: Good-cause extension is granted
    Given the notice is outside the 60-day window
    And the customer reports a good reason such as extended hospitalization
    When the analyst selects "Extend for good cause" and enters a justification
    Then the case is treated as timely and the full Reg E clock applies
    And the justification and approver are recorded

  Scenario: Continued unauthorized transfers after the first statement
    Given an unauthorized transfer appears on a later statement of a series of related unauthorized transfers
    When the system evaluates timeliness
    Then the 60-day period is measured from the statement the system is configured to use for that account
    And the system flags the case for Compliance review of the "later transfers" rule
```

*Note: The 1005.6(b) liability rules and the "60 days from the first statement" treatment interact. Compliance should specify the exact rule set.*

### US-04: Require written confirmation for oral notices, where the bank chooses to

**As a** compliance officer, **I want** the bank to request written confirmation of oral notices and track the 10-business-day return window, **so that** the bank preserves its right to withhold provisional credit and to stop the clock when the confirmation is not received.

```gherkin
Feature: Written confirmation of oral notice (1005.11(b)(2))

  Background:
    Given bank policy requires written confirmation of oral notices

  Scenario: Consumer is told about the written confirmation requirement
    Given a customer gives oral notice of an error
    When the case is created
    Then the system records that the agent told the customer to confirm in writing within 10 business days
    And the agent gave the address or electronic method for the confirmation
    And a confirmation request letter or secure message is generated

  Scenario: Written confirmation received in time
    Given written confirmation is received on business day 6
    When the system logs the confirmation
    Then the confirmation status is "Received"
    And the investigation and provisional credit timelines continue unchanged

  Scenario: Written confirmation not received
    Given the bank requested written confirmation
    And 10 business days pass with no confirmation received
    When the system runs the nightly clock process
    Then the confirmation status is set to "Not received"
    And the system marks the case as eligible to skip provisional credit
    And the investigation continues until a determination is made

  Scenario: Agent did not provide the required instruction
    Given the agent did not record the confirmation instruction
    When the system evaluates provisional credit eligibility
    Then the case is NOT eligible to skip provisional credit on the basis of missing confirmation
```

## EPIC 2: Clock Management

### US-05: Calculate and track investigation deadlines

**As a** disputes manager, **I want** deadlines calculated automatically from the notice date using the bank's business-day calendar, **so that** no case misses a regulatory deadline.

```gherkin
Feature: Regulatory clock

  Scenario Outline: Calculate the investigation deadline
    Given a timely Reg E case with notice received on <notice_date>
    And the account is "<account_type>"
    When the system initializes the clock
    Then the 10-business-day deadline (20 for new accounts) is <initial_deadline>
    And the extended outer deadline is <extended_deadline>

    Examples:
      | notice_date | account_type                    | initial_deadline        | extended_deadline          |
      | 2026-11-20  | Standard, ACH                   | 10 business days later  | 45 calendar days later     |
      | 2026-11-20  | New account (30 days or less)   | 20 business days later  | 90 calendar days later     |
      | 2026-11-20  | POS debit                       | 10 business days later  | 90 calendar days later     |
      | 2026-11-20  | Foreign-initiated EFT           | 10 business days later  | 90 calendar days later     |

  Scenario: Business-day calculation excludes bank non-business days
    Given a notice is received on Friday 2026-11-20
    And 2026-11-26 is a bank non-business day
    When the system calculates business day 10
    Then weekends and 2026-11-26 are excluded from the count

  Scenario: Notice received after cutoff or on a non-business day
    Given the bank's configured cutoff is not applicable to notice receipt
    When a notice is received on a Saturday
    Then the system records the actual receipt date
    And business day counting begins on the next business day per the configured rule

  Scenario: Aging alerts
    Given a case is open
    When business day 5, 8, and 9 are reached without a determination
    Then alerts are sent to the assigned analyst and the team lead
    And the case appears on the "At risk" dashboard
```

### US-06: Extend the investigation to 45 or 90 days

**As a** disputes analyst, **I want** to extend an investigation beyond the initial period only when the regulatory conditions are satisfied, **so that** the bank stays compliant.

```gherkin
Feature: Extended investigation (1005.11(c)(2))

  Scenario: Extension allowed when provisional credit has been given
    Given a case has not been resolved by business day 10
    And provisional credit was issued by business day 10
    When the analyst selects "Extend investigation"
    Then the system sets the deadline to 45 calendar days from notice
    And the extension is recorded with the date, user, and reason

  Scenario: Extension to 90 days for qualifying case types
    Given the case is a POS debit, a foreign-initiated transfer, or a new-account notice
    And provisional credit has been issued
    When the analyst selects "Extend investigation"
    Then the outer deadline is set to 90 calendar days from notice

  Scenario: Extension blocked without provisional credit
    Given a case has no provisional credit and the written-confirmation exception does not apply
    When the analyst attempts to extend the investigation
    Then the system blocks the extension
    And displays "Provisional credit is required to extend the investigation"

  Scenario: Extension allowed where provisional credit is excused
    Given the bank requested written confirmation of an oral notice and it was not received in 10 business days
    When the analyst selects "Extend investigation"
    Then the system allows the extension
    And records the basis as "Confirmation not received"
```

*Note: Compliance should confirm treatment of a case where the bank already completed its investigation before day 10.*

## EPIC 3: Provisional Credit

### US-07: Issue provisional credit when required

**As a** consumer, **I want** provisional credit for the disputed amount when the bank cannot resolve my claim in time, **so that** I have use of my funds during the investigation.

```gherkin
Feature: Provisional credit (1005.11(c)(2))

  Scenario: Provisional credit issued within 10 business days
    Given a timely Reg E case remains unresolved on business day 10
    When the system reaches business day 10
    Then a provisional credit equal to the full disputed amount, including any applicable interest, is created
    And the credit is posted to the consumer's account on or before business day 10
    And the funds are available for the consumer's full use

  Scenario: Provisional credit issued early
    Given an analyst determines early that the claim is likely valid
    When the analyst selects "Issue provisional credit now"
    Then the credit is posted immediately and the issue date is recorded

  Scenario: Consumer notified within 2 business days
    Given a provisional credit was posted on 2026-12-04
    When the posting completes
    Then the system generates a notice with the amount and date of the credit
    And the notice states the consumer has full use of the funds during the investigation
    And the notice is delivered no later than 2 business days after posting

  Scenario: Provisional credit not required because confirmation was not received
    Given written confirmation of an oral notice was required and not received within 10 business days
    When the system reaches business day 10
    Then no provisional credit is created
    And the reason "Written confirmation not received" is stored
    And the decision is logged for audit

  Scenario: New account provisional credit timing
    Given the account was opened 25 days before the notice
    When the case is unresolved by business day 10
    Then the system schedules provisional credit by business day 20
    And the case is flagged "New account"

  Scenario: Provisional credit posting failure
    Given the core banking posting call fails
    When the system detects the failure
    Then the case is escalated to Operations with a priority flag
    And an alert is sent before the regulatory deadline
    And the system retries according to the configured policy
```

*Note: New-account rules involve provisional credit for amounts above certain thresholds in some interpretations. Compliance should confirm the exact exceptions, including the 1005.11(c)(2) provisions and Comment 11(c)-2, before build.*

### US-08: Maintain the provisional credit and funds availability

**As an** operations user, **I want** provisional credit tracked as a distinct, reversible ledger item, **so that** finalizing or reversing it is accurate and auditable.

```gherkin
Feature: Provisional credit ledger

  Scenario: Credit is posted with a unique reference
    When a provisional credit is posted
    Then the ledger entry has a dispute case reference, amount, and posting date
    And its status is "Provisional"

  Scenario: Account holder cannot be restricted from using provisional funds
    Given a provisional credit is posted
    When the consumer initiates withdrawals or transfers
    Then the system applies no dispute-related hold on the credited funds

  Scenario: Reconciliation
    When the nightly reconciliation runs
    Then every open provisional credit matches an active dispute case
    And any mismatch creates an exception ticket
```

## EPIC 4: Investigation and Determination

### US-09: Perform and document a good-faith investigation

**As a** disputes analyst, **I want** a structured investigation workflow with required evidence, **so that** each decision is supported and defensible.

```gherkin
Feature: Investigation workflow

  Scenario: Investigation checklist for unauthorized debit card transaction
    Given a case classified "Unauthorized EFT"
    When the analyst opens the case
    Then the checklist includes authorization records, card-present or card-not-present data, device and IP data, prior customer history, and merchant evidence
    And each item requires a result and a note before a determination can be saved

  Scenario: Determination requires evidence
    When the analyst attempts to select a determination without recording evidence reviewed
    Then the system blocks the action
    And displays "Document the evidence reviewed"

  Scenario: Limited review of the bank's own records
    Given the alleged error concerns a transfer to or from a third party
    And bank policy permits a review of its own records for this transfer type
    When the analyst records the investigation scope as "Own records"
    Then the system requires the analyst to confirm the scope is permitted for this error type
    And flags unauthorized-transfer cases for Compliance-approved scope rules

  Scenario: Four-eyes review for denials
    Given the analyst's determination is "No error"
    When the analyst submits the determination
    Then the case moves to QA/Compliance review before any customer notice is sent
    And the reviewer can approve, reject, or request more investigation

  Scenario: Determination within the deadline
    Given the investigation deadline is approaching
    When the analyst submits a determination before the deadline
    Then the determination date and time are recorded and the clock stops
```

### US-10: Handle duplicate notices

**As a** disputes analyst, **I want** duplicate notices recognized, **so that** the bank does not reopen settled matters while meeting its notice obligations.

```gherkin
Feature: Repeat notices (1005.11(d)(2))

  Scenario: Same error reported again after a final determination
    Given a closed case exists for the same account, transaction, and error
    And the new notice contains no new or material information
    When the agent creates a new case
    Then the system flags it as "Potential duplicate"
    And the analyst can mark it "Duplicate - no further investigation required"
    And the system generates a written explanation referencing the prior case and determination

  Scenario: New information changes the outcome
    Given the new notice provides new information about the transaction
    When the analyst marks "New information provided"
    Then the system opens a new investigation with a new clock
```

## EPIC 5: Resolution and Communications

### US-11: Resolve confirmed errors

**As a** consumer, **I want** confirmed errors corrected promptly, **so that** I am made whole and informed.

```gherkin
Feature: Error confirmed (1005.11(c)(1))

  Scenario: Correct the error within 1 business day
    Given the analyst determines on 2026-12-02 that an error occurred
    When the determination is approved
    Then the system schedules the correction, including any applicable interest, within 1 business day of the determination
    And the case is marked "Error confirmed"

  Scenario: Provisional credit becomes final
    Given the case has an existing provisional credit equal to the disputed amount
    When the determination is "Error confirmed"
    Then the provisional credit status changes to "Final"
    And no additional credit is posted

  Scenario: Provisional credit less than the confirmed error amount
    Given the confirmed error amount exceeds the provisional credit
    When the correction is processed
    Then the system posts only the difference
    And the ledger links both entries to the case

  Scenario: Results reported within 3 business days
    Given the investigation is complete
    When the correction is posted
    Then the system generates a result notice to the consumer
    And the notice is sent within 3 business days of completing the investigation
    And the notice states the error was corrected, the amount, and the date

  Scenario: Follow-on charges to be refunded
    Given the error caused overdraft or NSF fees
    When the analyst confirms the error
    Then the system prompts the analyst to review and reverse related fees
    And the reversals are recorded under the case
```

### US-12: Resolve cases where no error is found

**As a** consumer, **I want** a clear written explanation when my claim is denied, **so that** I understand the decision and can request the documents.

```gherkin
Feature: No error found (1005.11(d))

  Scenario: Written explanation within 3 business days
    Given a QA-approved determination of "No error"
    When the determination is finalized
    Then the system generates a written explanation of the findings
    And the explanation is sent within 3 business days of completing the investigation
    And it notes the consumer's right to request the documents the bank relied on

  Scenario: Reversal of provisional credit with notice
    Given a provisional credit exists
    When the bank intends to debit it
    Then the notice states the date and amount of the debit
    And the debit is not executed before the notice is sent

  Scenario: Honoring items for 5 business days
    Given the provisional credit is debited
    And the debit would overdraw the account
    When checks, drafts, or preauthorized transfers payable to third parties are presented within 5 business days of the notice
    Then the system pays them without assessing overdraft or NSF fees
    And the obligation applies only to the extent of the debited provisional credit
    And the consumer must have been notified of the five-business-day period

  Scenario: Provisional credit debit is blocked prior to notice
    Given the no-error notice has not been sent
    When an operations user attempts to reverse the provisional credit
    Then the system blocks the reversal

  Scenario: Consumer requests documents
    Given a consumer requests the documents relied upon
    When the request is logged
    Then the system routes it to the document fulfillment queue
    And copies are provided promptly
    And the request and delivery dates are recorded
```

## EPIC 6: Records, Controls, and Reporting

### US-14: Retain records for two years

**As a** compliance officer, **I want** complete, immutable case records retained for at least two years, **so that** the bank can demonstrate compliance (1005.13).

```gherkin
Feature: Record retention

  Scenario: Retained case artifacts
    When a case is closed
    Then the system retains the notice, timeline, evidence, determination, notices sent, and ledger entries
    And the retention date is the later of 2 years from the closing date or any longer internal or legal-hold period

  Scenario: Audit trail is immutable
    When any case field affecting a deadline or determination is changed
    Then the system logs the user, timestamp, prior value, and new value
    And the log cannot be edited or deleted by application users

  Scenario: Legal hold
    Given a legal hold is placed on a case
    When the retention date arrives
    Then the system does not purge the case
```

### US-15: Monitor compliance and detect risk

**As a** disputes manager and compliance officer, **I want** dashboards and exception reports, **so that** deadline misses and error patterns are caught early.

```gherkin
Feature: Monitoring and reporting

  Scenario: Daily deadline report
    When the daily job runs
    Then it lists open cases by days remaining for each milestone
    And cases with missed deadlines are marked "Regulatory breach"

  Scenario: Breach handling
    Given a regulatory deadline was missed
    When the breach is detected
    Then a compliance incident is created automatically
    And the case requires a documented root cause and remediation, including any customer make-whole action

  Scenario: Management metrics
    When a manager opens the dashboard
    Then it shows volume by error type and channel, average days to resolve, percentage resolved within 10 business days, extension rate, provisional credit totals, and denial and overturn rates

  Scenario: Quality sampling
    Given a monthly QA sample is configured
    When the sample is generated
    Then it includes denied cases and extended cases
    And reviewers record findings against the investigation standard

  Scenario: Role-based access
    Given a user without the Disputes Analyst role
    When they attempt to approve a determination or post a credit
    Then the system denies the action and logs the attempt
```

### US-16: Support the good-faith investigation standard

**As a** compliance officer, **I want** controls that reduce the risk of damages for failing to investigate in good faith (1005.11(e)), **so that** the bank avoids treble damages exposure.

```gherkin
Feature: Good-faith investigation controls

  Scenario: Denial requires a documented reasonable basis
    Given an analyst selects "No error"
    When the analyst saves the determination
    Then the system requires a summary of the evidence supporting the conclusion that no error occurred
    And rejects conclusions based only on the customer's history or a generic reason

  Scenario: Missed deadlines on unauthorized claims are escalated
    Given an unauthorized-transfer case is unresolved at the extended deadline
    When the deadline is reached
    Then the case is escalated to the Compliance officer
    And the provisional credit is not reversed without Compliance approval

  Scenario: Denials are not driven by a fixed quota
    When a manager configures team performance goals
    Then the system does not offer an approval or denial rate as a performance target
```

## 4. Non-Functional Requirements

- **Accuracy:** Deadline calculations must be deterministic and unit-tested against the bank calendar, including leap years, holidays, and month ends.
- **Availability:** The clock engine must run even during platform degradation, with a catch-up process that evaluates missed runs without altering historical dates.
- **Security and privacy:** Encrypt case data and documents. Apply least-privilege access. Mask PAN and account numbers.
- **Auditability:** All time-based decisions are reproducible from the stored timeline.
- **Time zone:** Store timestamps in UTC and apply the bank's official time zone for business-day assignment. Make this configurable and documented.
- **Integrations:** Core banking (ledger posting), card processor, ACH operations (Nacha return handling), document management, notification service, and the Reg Z workflow.

## 5. Open Questions for Compliance and Legal

1. Exact "new account" thresholds and the provisional credit exceptions for new accounts.
2. Which routine inquiries are excluded from "notice of error" (Comment 11(a)).
3. Bank policy on requiring written confirmation of oral notices: always, never, or by case type.
4. The "later transfers" and 60-day interaction for unauthorized series (1005.6 and 1005.11).
5. Treatment of transfers induced by fraud where the consumer initiated the payment (authorized-but-deceived), which is evolving and needs a bank position.
6. Scope of the investigation for third-party or ACH errors when the bank limits review to its own records.
7. State laws or card network rules that are more stringent than Reg E.
8. Business-day definition and cutoff conventions for notice receipt.
