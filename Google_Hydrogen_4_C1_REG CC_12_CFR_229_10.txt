Here are the user stories and Gherkin acceptance criteria required to implement **Regulation CC (12 CFR § 229.10) Next-Day Availability** rules within the core deposit hold engine.

---

### Epic: Regulation CC Next-Day Funds Availability Engine (12 CFR § 229.10)

**Business Objective:** Ensure the deposit hold engine accurately identifies deposits eligible for mandatory next-business-day availability under 12 CFR § 229.10, applies the correct hold release timestamps, and prevents compliance violations—while accounting for standard Reg CC exceptions (e.g., new accounts under § 229.13(a) or exception holds under § 229.13(b)–(f)).

---

### Story 1: Electronic Payments and Cash Deposits (In-Person / Proprietary ATM)

**As a** Deposit Operations Compliance Officer,

**I want** the deposit hold engine to automatically grant next-business-day availability to electronic payments and cash deposits made in person to a bank employee,

**So that** the bank complies with 12 CFR § 229.10(a) and § 229.10(b) without placing erroneous holds on immediately collectible funds.

#### Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Feature: Next-Day Availability for Cash and Electronic Payments (12 CFR 229.10(a)-(b))

  Background:
    Given the deposit hold engine is evaluating a transaction into an active transaction account
    And the account is not subject to a valid Regulation CC exception hold under 12 CFR 229.13

  Scenario Outline: Cash deposited in person to a bank employee
    Given a customer deposits "<amount>" in cash
    And the deposit channel is "<channel>"
    And the deposit is received on "<deposit_day>" at "<deposit_time>" before the branch cutoff time
    When the deposit hold engine evaluates the transaction
    Then no Reg CC hold shall be placed on the deposit
    And the funds availability date shall be set to "<availability_date>" at 09:00 AM local bank time

    Examples:
      | amount   | channel         | deposit_day | deposit_time | availability_date  |
      | $500.00  | Branch Teller   | Tuesday     | 14:30        | Wednesday (Next BD)|
      | $5000.00 | Proprietary ATM | Friday      | 11:00        | Monday (Next BD)   |

  Scenario Outline: Electronic payments (Wire Transfers and ACH Credits)
    Given the bank receives an incoming "<payment_type>" for "<amount>"
    And the settlement funds are received by the bank on "<settlement_day>"
    When the deposit hold engine processes the credit
    Then no Reg CC hold shall be placed on the funds
    And the full "<amount>" shall be made available for withdrawal no later than "<availability_date>"

    Examples:
      | payment_type | amount     | settlement_day | availability_date   |
      | Fedwire      | $25,000.00 | Monday         | Tuesday (Next BD)   |
      | ACH Credit   | $3,200.00  | Thursday       | Friday (Next BD)    |

```

### Story 2: U.S. Treasury, USPS Money Orders, and Federal Reserve/FHLB Checks

**As a** Deposit Operations Compliance Officer,

**I want** the hold engine to grant next-business-day availability to U.S. Treasury checks, U.S. Postal Service money orders, and checks drawn on a Federal Reserve Bank or Federal Home Loan Bank when deposited into the payee's account,

**So that** the bank satisfies the strict payee-specific criteria under 12 CFR § 229.10(c)(1)(i)–(iii).

#### Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Feature: Next-Day Availability for Federal Government and Postal Instruments (12 CFR 229.10(c)(1)(i)-(iii))

  Background:
    Given an established customer account (open > 30 calendar days)
    And no Reg CC exception holds (12 CFR 229.13) are triggered

  Scenario Outline: Government and Postal instruments deposited into the named payee's account
    Given a check of type "<instrument_type>" for "<amount>"
    And the check is deposited via "<channel>"
    And the named payee on the check matches the account holder ("<payee_match>")
    When the deposit hold engine evaluates the item
    Then the engine shall assign a next-business-day availability schedule
    And the hold release timestamp shall be set to the start of the next business day

    Examples:
      | instrument_type         | amount    | channel       | payee_match |
      | U.S. Treasury Check     | $1,850.00 | Branch Teller | True        |
      | U.S. Treasury Check     | $1,850.00 | Proprietary ATM| True       |
      | USPS Money Order        | $450.00   | Branch Teller | True        |
      | Federal Reserve Check   | $10,000.00| Branch Teller | True        |
      | Federal Home Loan Check | $7,500.00 | Branch Teller | True        |

  Scenario: USPS Money Order or Federal Bank check NOT deposited in person to a bank employee
    Given a "USPS Money Order" for "$600.00" payable to the account holder
    And the deposit channel is "Proprietary ATM" (not in person to a bank employee)
    When the deposit hold engine evaluates the item
    Then the item shall NOT receive mandatory 229.10(c)(1) next-day availability
    And the engine shall route the item to second-business-day availability under 12 CFR 229.10(c)(2)

```

*(Note: Under 12 CFR § 229.10(c)(1)(i), U.S. Treasury checks qualify for next-day availability even if deposited at a proprietary ATM, whereas USPS money orders and FRB/FHLB checks require in-person deposit to a bank employee—otherwise falling under § 229.10(c)(2) for second-business-day availability.)*

### Story 3: State/Local Government, Cashier's, Certified, and Teller's Checks (with Special Deposit Slip Logic)

**As a** Core Banking Product Owner,

**I want** the deposit hold engine to evaluate instrument type, issuing jurisdiction, payee match, in-person presentation, and special deposit slip usage for state/local government checks and bank-issued checks,

**So that** the bank accurately enforces the conditional next-day rules under 12 CFR § 229.10(c)(1)(iv)–(v).

#### Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Feature: Conditional Next-Day Availability for State/Local and Official Bank Checks (12 CFR 229.10(c)(1)(iv)-(v))

  Background:
    Given an established customer account (open > 30 calendar days)
    And no Reg CC exception holds (12 CFR 229.13) are triggered

  Scenario Outline: In-person deposit of State/Local or Official Bank checks meeting all 229.10(c)(1) criteria
    Given a check of type "<check_type>" for "<amount>"
    And the check is payable to the account holder
    And the deposit is made in person to a bank employee
    And the same-state requirement is "<same_state_met>"
    And the special deposit slip requirement is "<special_slip_used>"
    When the deposit hold engine evaluates the item
    Then the engine shall grant next-business-day availability for the full "<amount>"

    Examples:
      | check_type             | amount    | same_state_met | special_slip_used |
      | State Government Check | $2,400.00 | True (Same State) | True (or Not Required) |
      | Local Government Check | $1,100.00 | True (Same State) | True (or Not Required) |
      | Cashier's Check        | $15,000.00| N/A            | True (or Not Required) |
      | Certified Check        | $8,000.00 | N/A            | True (or Not Required) |
      | Teller's Check         | $5,500.00 | N/A            | True (or Not Required) |

  Scenario: Bank requires a special deposit slip, and the customer omits it
    Given the bank's Reg CC policy enforces special deposit slips for "Cashier's Checks"
    And a customer deposits a "Cashier's Check" for "$4,000.00" in person using a standard deposit slip
    When the deposit hold engine evaluates the item
    Then the engine shall downgrade the item from 229.10(c)(1) next-day availability
    And apply second-business-day availability under 12 CFR 229.10(c)(2)

```

### Story 4: "On-Us" Checks Drawn on the Same Bank

**As a** Deposit Operations Compliance Officer,

**I want** the hold engine to grant next-business-day availability to checks drawn on our own bank (including branches in the same state or same check-processing region),

**So that** internal clearing items adhere to 12 CFR § 229.10(c)(1)(vi).

#### Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Feature: Next-Day Availability for On-Us Checks (12 CFR 229.10(c)(1)(vi))

  Scenario: On-Us check deposited at a branch or proprietary ATM
    Given a check drawn on an internal routing transit number (RTN) belonging to this bank
    And the check is deposited via "<channel>" before the daily cutoff time
    And the account is not subject to a Reg CC exception hold
    When the deposit hold engine evaluates the item
    Then the engine shall identify the item as an "On-Us" check under 12 CFR 229.10(c)(1)(vi)
    And set the full deposit amount to be available on the next business day

    Examples:
      | channel         |
      | Branch Teller   |
      | Proprietary ATM |

```

*(Note: Unlike Treasury or Cashier's checks, § 229.10(c)(1)(vi) does **not** require the depositor to be the named payee for an On-Us check to qualify for next-day availability.)*

### Story 5: The Mandatory "$275 Rule" (First $275 of Non-Next-Day Check Deposits)

**As a** Deposit Operations Compliance Officer,

**I want** the deposit hold engine to aggregate all non-next-day checks deposited on a single banking day and release up to **$275** (the inflation-adjusted statutory amount effective July 1, 2025) on the next business day,

**So that** the bank complies with 12 CFR § 229.10(c)(1)(vii) without over-releasing funds across multiple deposits on the same day.

#### Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Feature: Mandatory Next-Day Release of First $275 on Check Deposits (12 CFR 229.10(c)(1)(vii))

  Background:
    Given an established customer account (open > 30 calendar days)
    And no Reg CC exception holds (12 CFR 229.13) apply to the account or items
    And the statutory 229.10(c)(1)(vii) threshold is configured to "$275.00"

  Scenario: Single check deposit exceeding $275
    Given the customer has made "$0.00" in prior check deposits on the current banking day
    And the customer deposits a local personal check for "$1,000.00" via "Branch Teller"
    When the deposit hold engine calculates the hold schedule
    Then "$275.00" shall be made available on the next business day (Day 1)
    And the remaining "$725.00" shall be placed on a standard 2-business-day hold (Day 2)

  Scenario: Multiple check deposits on the same banking day (Aggregate Cap Enforcement)
    Given the customer deposited a personal check for "$200.00" at 10:00 AM on Tuesday
    And the engine already scheduled "$200.00" for next-business-day release (Wednesday)
    When the customer deposits a second personal check for "$500.00" at 2:00 PM on Tuesday into the same account
    Then the engine shall calculate the remaining daily 229.10(c)(1)(vii) allowance as "$75.00" ($275.00 - $200.00)
    And release only "$75.00" from the second check on Wednesday (Next BD)
    And hold the remaining "$425.00" until Thursday (Second BD)

  Scenario: Deposit consisting of both a 229.10(c)(1) Next-Day check and a standard check
    Given a customer deposits a "$1,000.00" U.S. Treasury check (eligible under 229.10(c)(1)(i))
    And in the same transaction deposits a "$600.00" personal check
    When the deposit hold engine evaluates the combined deposit
    Then the full "$1,000.00" Treasury check shall be available on the next business day
    And an additional "$275.00" from the personal check shall ALSO be available on the next business day (Total Day 1 = $1,275.00)
    And the remaining "$325.00" of the personal check shall be available on the second business day

```

### BA Implementation Notes & Edge Cases for Engineering

1. **Parameterized Thresholds:** Ensure the `$275.00` value under 12 CFR § 229.10(c)(1)(vii) (which increased from $225 on July 1, 2025, per Dodd-Frank 5-year inflation adjustments) is stored as a date-effective configuration parameter rather than hardcoded, as the next statutory adjustment is scheduled for July 1, 2030.
2. **Remote Deposit Capture (RDC) Scope:** Reg CC's mandatory availability schedules technically apply to *paper* checks and electronic payments; however, if your bank contractually extends Reg CC schedules to Mobile RDC in your Deposit Account Agreement, confirm whether RDC routes through a separate channel rule table.
3. **Precedence of Exception Holds (§ 229.13):** Stories 1–5 assume standard processing. A separate epic should govern § 229.13 overrides (e.g., New Accounts open < 30 days, where the $275 rule does **not** apply and only the first $6,725 of Treasury/Cashier's/Official checks gets next-day availability).
