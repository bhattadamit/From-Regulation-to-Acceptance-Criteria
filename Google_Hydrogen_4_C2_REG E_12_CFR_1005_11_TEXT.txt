### US-1: Error Classification and Intake Validation

**As a** Disputes Intake Specialist,

**I want** the platform to classify incoming consumer notices against Regulation E error definitions and validate required notice elements,

**So that** covered electronic fund transfer (EFT) errors are routed into the Reg E resolution workflow while non-covered inquiries are excluded.

#### Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Feature: Regulation E Error Classification and Intake Validation

  Scenario: Classify covered EFT error types into Reg E dispute workflow
    Given a consumer submits an oral or written notice regarding an electronic fund transfer
    When the notice alleges one of the following:
      | Covered Error Category                                                                                       |
      | Unauthorized electronic fund transfer                                                                        |
      | Incorrect electronic fund transfer to or from the consumer's account                                         |
      | Omission of an electronic fund transfer from a periodic statement                                            |
      | Computational or bookkeeping error made by the financial institution relating to an EFT                      |
      | Consumer's receipt of an incorrect amount of money from an electronic terminal                               |
      | EFT not identified in accordance with § 1005.9 or § 1005.10(a)                                               |
      | Request for § 1005.9 or § 1005.10(a) documentation or additional EFT information/clarification               |
    Then the system classifies the case as a Regulation E covered error

  Scenario: Exclude non-covered inquiries from Reg E dispute workflow
    Given a consumer contacts the bank regarding their account
    When the inquiry is solely one of the following:
      | Non-Covered Inquiry Type                                        |
      | Routine inquiry about the consumer's account balance            |
      | Request for information for tax or other recordkeeping purposes |
      | Request for duplicate copies of documentation                   |
    Then the system does not classify the inquiry as a Regulation E error

  Scenario: Validate required consumer notice contents for standard error claims
    Given a consumer submits an oral or written notice of a covered error other than a documentation or clarification request
    When the intake agent captures the notice
    Then the system requires:
      | Required Element                                                                    |
      | Consumer's name and account number (or information enabling identification of both) |
      | Reason why the consumer believes an error exists                                    |
      | Type, date, and amount of the alleged error (captured to the extent possible)       |

  Scenario: Validate required consumer notice contents for documentation or clarification requests
    Given a consumer submits an oral or written request for § 1005.9/§ 1005.10(a) documentation or additional EFT clarification
    When the intake agent captures the request
    Then the system requires identification of the consumer's name and account number
    And the system does not require the type, date, and amount of an error

```

### US-2: Notice Timeliness and Written Confirmation Tracking

**As a** Disputes Operations Analyst,

**I want** the platform to evaluate the 60-day notice window and track optional 10-business-day written confirmation requirements,

**So that** timely claims receive full Reg E protections and oral-notice confirmation rules are enforced accurately.

#### Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Feature: Notice Timeliness and Written Confirmation Tracking

  Scenario: Evaluate 60-day timely notice window from periodic statement or passbook documentation
    Given an alleged EFT error is first reflected on a periodic statement or passbook documentation required by § 1005.9
    When the consumer's oral or written notice of error is received no later than 60 calendar days after the statement was sent or passbook documentation was provided
    Then the system flags the error notice as timely under § 1005.11(b)(1)(i)

  Scenario: Evaluate 60-day timely notice window following a documentation or clarification request
    Given a consumer previously requested documentation or clarification under § 1005.11(a)(1)(vii)
    And the institution sent the requested information
    When the consumer submits a subsequent notice of error based on that information no later than 60 calendar days after the information was sent
    Then the system flags the subsequent error notice as timely under § 1005.11(b)(3)

  Scenario: Require written confirmation for an oral notice of error
    Given a consumer submits an oral notice of error
    And the institution elects to require written confirmation
    When the oral notice is logged
    Then the system prompts the agent to inform the consumer of the written confirmation requirement and provide the mailing/submission address during the oral notification
    And the system starts a 10-business-day timer for receipt of written confirmation

```

### US-3: Investigation SLA Calculation and Extensions

**As a** Disputes Case Manager,

**I want** the platform to automatically calculate standard and extended investigation deadlines based on transaction and account attributes,

**So that** investigators complete determinations within mandatory Regulation E timeframes.

#### Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Feature: Investigation SLA Calculation and Extensions

  Scenario: Set standard 10-business-day and 45-calendar-day investigation timelines
    Given a timely notice of error is received
    And the transaction did not occur within 30 days after the first deposit to the account
    And the transaction was initiated within a state
    And the transaction did not result from a point-of-sale debit card transaction
    When the case is opened
    Then the system sets the initial investigation and provisional credit deadline to 10 business days from receipt of the notice
    And sets the extended investigation deadline to 45 calendar days from receipt of the notice

  Scenario: Apply 20-business-day initial deadline for new accounts
    Given a timely notice of error involves an EFT to or from the account within 30 calendar days after the first deposit to the account was made
    When the case is opened
    Then the system sets the initial investigation and provisional credit deadline to 20 business days from receipt of the notice (in place of 10 business days)

  Scenario: Apply 90-calendar-day extended deadline for qualifying transactions
    Given a timely notice of error is received
    When the alleged error involves an EFT that meets at least one of the following conditions:
      | Qualifying Extension Condition                                              |
      | Was not initiated within a state                                            |
      | Resulted from a point-of-sale debit card transaction                        |
      | Occurred within 30 calendar days after the first deposit to the account     |
    Then the system sets the extended investigation deadline to 90 calendar days from receipt of the notice (in place of 45 calendar days)

  Scenario: Limit investigation scope to internal records for non-agreement third-party transfers
    Given an alleged error concerns a transfer to or from a third party
    And the transfer is not covered by § 1005.14
    And no agreement exists between the institution and the third party for the type of EFT involved
    When the investigator conducts the investigation
    Then the system allows the investigator to complete the investigation based on a review of the institution's own records

```

### US-4: Provisional Credit Calculation, Eligibility, and Notification

**As a** Disputes Operations Specialist,

**I want** the platform to manage provisional credit eligibility, optional $50 withholding, and consumer notifications,

**So that** consumers receive timely access to disputed funds when investigations exceed the initial business-day window.

#### Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Feature: Provisional Credit Calculation, Eligibility, and Notification

  Scenario: Issue full provisional credit when investigation extends past initial business-day window
    Given an investigation is not completed within 10 business days (or 20 business days for new accounts)
    And the account is not subject to Regulation T (12 CFR part 220)
    And written confirmation was either not required or was received within 10 business days of an oral notice
    When the 10-business-day (or 20-business-day) deadline arrives
    Then the system posts a provisional credit for the full amount of the alleged error, including applicable interest
    And grants the consumer full use of the credited funds during the investigation
    And generates a notice to the consumer within 2 business days of crediting stating the amount and date of the provisional credit

  Scenario: Withhold up to $50 on eligible unauthorized EFT claims
    Given an investigation extends past the initial 10-business-day (or 20-business-day) window
    And the institution has a reasonable basis for believing an unauthorized EFT occurred
    And the institution has satisfied the requirements of § 1005.6(a)
    When the system calculates the provisional credit
    Then the system allows withholding a maximum of $50.00 from the provisional credit amount

  Scenario: Suppress provisional credit when required written confirmation is not received
    Given the institution required written confirmation within 10 business days of an oral notice of error
    And the institution informed the consumer of the requirement and provided the address at the time of oral notice
    When 10 business days elapse without receipt of written confirmation
    Then the system suppresses provisional credit
    And keeps the investigation active through the applicable 45-day or 90-day investigation period

  Scenario: Suppress provisional credit for Regulation T accounts
    Given the alleged error involves an account subject to Regulation T (12 CFR part 220)
    When the investigation extends beyond 10 business days (or 20 business days)
    Then the system suppresses provisional credit

```

### US-5: Error Confirmed — Final Resolution, Correction, and Reporting

**As a** Disputes Investigator,

**I want** the platform to enforce 1-business-day error correction and 3-business-day consumer reporting deadlines when an error is confirmed,

**So that** confirmed errors are remediated and communicated within statutory timeframes.

#### Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Feature: Final Resolution and Reporting When Error Occurred as Alleged

  Scenario: Correct confirmed error within one business day
    Given the investigator determines that an error occurred as alleged
    When the determination date is recorded
    Then the system sets a mandatory task to correct the error (or finalize an existing provisional credit, including applicable interest) within 1 business day of the determination date

  Scenario: Report confirmed error results to consumer within three business days
    Given the investigator completes the investigation and determines an error occurred
    When the investigation completion date is recorded
    Then the system generates and dispatches the investigation results notice to the consumer within 3 business days of completing the investigation
    And includes notice that any provisional credit has been made final, if applicable

```

### US-6: No Error or Different Error — Written Explanation, Document Rights, and Provisional Credit Reversal

**As a** Disputes Investigator,

**I want** the platform to generate a compliant written explanation and manage provisional credit reversal with 5-business-day overdraft protection,

**So that** adverse or modified determinations meet all Regulation E notice and item-honoring obligations.

#### Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Feature: Adverse or Modified Determination and Provisional Credit Reversal

  Scenario: Send written explanation and document request rights within three business days
    Given the investigator completes the investigation
    And determines either that no error occurred or that an error occurred in a manner or amount different from that described by the consumer
    When the determination is recorded
    Then the system generates a report of results within 3 business days of completing the investigation
    And includes a written explanation of the institution's findings
    And states the consumer's right to request copies of the documents relied upon in making the determination

  Scenario: Fulfill consumer request for relied-upon documents
    Given the consumer received a no-error or different-error determination notice
    When the consumer requests copies of the documents the institution relied upon
    Then the system logs a high-priority fulfillment task to promptly provide copies of those documents to the consumer

  Scenario: Reverse provisional credit and notify consumer of debit and 5-business-day item-honoring window
    Given the consumer previously received a provisional credit
    And the investigation concludes that no error occurred (or an error occurred for a lesser amount)
    When the institution debits the provisionally credited amount
    Then the system sends a notice to the consumer stating:
      | Required Reversal Notice Element                                                                                                                               |
      | Date and amount of the debiting                                                                                                                                |
      | Institution will honor checks, drafts, or similar instruments payable to third parties and preauthorized transfers for 5 business days after the notification   |
      | Honored items during those 5 business days will not incur an overdraft charge resulting from the debit                                                         |

  Scenario: Honor third-party items and preauthorized transfers without overdraft fees for five business days
    Given a provisional credit reversal notice has been issued to the consumer
    When a check, draft, similar instrument payable to a third party, or preauthorized transfer is presented within 5 business days after the notification
    And the item would have been paid if the provisionally credited funds had not been debited
    Then the system honors the item
    And suppresses any overdraft charge resulting from the debiting of the provisional credit

```

### US-7: Reassertion of Previously Resolved Error

**As a** Disputes Intake Specialist,

**I want** the platform to identify when a consumer reasserts a previously resolved error and check whether it follows a documentation/clarification request,

**So that** duplicate claims are closed without redundant investigation unless triggered by newly provided § 1005.11(a)(1)(vii) records.

#### Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Feature: Reassertion of Error

  Scenario: Close reasserted error when prior investigation fully complied with § 1005.11
    Given a consumer submits a notice of error for a transaction previously investigated and closed
    And the audit log confirms the institution fully complied with all requirements of § 1005.11 during the prior resolution
    And the prior case was not solely a request for documentation or clarification under § 1005.11(a)(1)(vii)
    When the reasserted claim is evaluated
    Then the system flags the claim as a reasserted error with no further institution responsibilities under § 1005.11

  Scenario: Process error asserted after receipt of requested documentation or clarification
    Given a consumer previously submitted a request for documentation or clarification under § 1005.11(a)(1)(vii)
    And the institution provided the requested information
    When the consumer asserts an error following receipt of that information within 60 days of the institution sending it
    Then the system opens a full Regulation E error investigation under § 1005.11

```
