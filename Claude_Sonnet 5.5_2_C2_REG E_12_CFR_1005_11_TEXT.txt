# Regulation E Error Resolution (12 CFR 1005.11): User Stories and Acceptance Criteria

All criteria are based only on the § 1005.11 text you provided. Items the text refers to but does not define are listed as open items at the end rather than assumed.

**Personas:** Dispute Analyst (analyst), Consumer (represented via the intake channel), Compliance Officer, System (platform behavior).

---

## Epic 1: Intake and Classification

### US-01: Classify an inquiry as an error (§ 1005.11(a))
**As a** dispute analyst, **I want** each inquiry classified as a covered error type or an excluded inquiry **so that** only covered errors enter the Reg E clock and workflow.

```gherkin
Feature: Error classification

  Scenario Outline: Covered error types open a Reg E case
    Given a consumer inquiry concerning an electronic fund transfer
    When the analyst selects the error type "<error_type>"
    Then the platform creates a Reg E error case
    And the case records the error type "<error_type>"

    Examples:
      | error_type                                                                  |
      | Unauthorized electronic fund transfer                                       |
      | Incorrect electronic fund transfer to or from the account                   |
      | Omission of an electronic fund transfer from a periodic statement           |
      | Computational or bookkeeping error by the institution                       |
      | Incorrect amount of money received from an electronic terminal              |
      | Electronic fund transfer not identified per § 1005.9 or § 1005.10(a)        |
      | Request for documentation or clarification (§ 1005.9 or § 1005.10(a))       |

  Scenario Outline: Excluded inquiries do not open a Reg E case
    Given a consumer inquiry
    When the analyst selects the inquiry type "<inquiry_type>"
    Then the platform does not start any error resolution clock
    And the inquiry is logged as "Not a Reg E error"

    Examples:
      | inquiry_type                                      |
      | Routine inquiry about account balance             |
      | Request for tax or other recordkeeping information|
      | Request for duplicate copies of documentation     |

  Scenario: A documentation request made to determine whether an error exists
    Given a consumer requests information to determine whether an error exists under (a)(1)(i) through (vi)
    When the analyst classifies the request
    Then the platform classifies it as a covered error under (a)(1)(vii)
    And it is not classified as an excluded inquiry
```

## Epic 2: Notice of Error

### US-02: Validate timeliness and content of a notice of error (§ 1005.11(b)(1))
**As a** dispute analyst, **I want** the platform to check whether a notice meets the timing and content conditions **so that** every qualifying oral or written notice triggers the required procedures.

```gherkin
Feature: Notice of error validation

  Background:
    Given the platform records the date the institution sent the periodic statement
      or provided passbook documentation on which the alleged error first appears

  Scenario: Notice received within 60 days
    Given the periodic statement was sent on day 0
    When an oral or written notice is received on or before day 60
    Then the platform marks the notice as timely

  Scenario: Notice received after 60 days
    Given the periodic statement was sent on day 0
    When a notice is received on day 61
    Then the platform marks the notice as untimely under (b)(1)(i)
    And the case is routed to the analyst for review before it is closed as outside Reg E timing

  Scenario Outline: Both oral and written notices are accepted
    When the consumer submits a notice via "<channel>"
    Then the platform treats it as a notice of error for § 1005.11 purposes

    Examples:
      | channel |
      | Oral    |
      | Written |

  Scenario: Notice identifies consumer and account
    When the notice enables the institution to identify the consumer's name and account number
    Then the platform marks the identification condition as met

  Scenario: Notice does not enable identification
    When the notice does not enable the institution to identify the consumer's name and account number
    Then the platform marks the identification condition as not met
    And the analyst is prompted to obtain the missing information

  Scenario: Notice states why the consumer believes an error exists
    When the notice indicates why the consumer believes an error exists
    Then the platform captures the reason
    And the platform captures the type, date, and amount of the error to the extent possible

  Scenario: Documentation or clarification request needs no error reason
    Given the notice is a request described in (a)(1)(vii)
    Then the platform does not require the reason, type, date, or amount fields to be completed
```

### US-03: Record the notice receipt date as the clock start (§ 1005.11(b)(1), (c))
**As a** compliance officer, **I want** the date the institution receives each notice captured precisely **so that** all downstream deadlines are measured from receipt.

```gherkin
Feature: Receipt date capture

  Scenario: Receipt date is recorded and locked
    When a notice of error is received through any channel
    Then the platform records the receipt date and time
    And the receipt date is the start date for all § 1005.11(c) deadlines
    And any later change to the receipt date requires a documented reason and an audit entry
```

### US-04: Written confirmation of oral notice (§ 1005.11(b)(2))
**As a** dispute analyst, **I want** the platform to support an optional written-confirmation requirement **so that** disclosures and deadlines are handled correctly when we require it.

```gherkin
Feature: Written confirmation of oral notice

  Scenario: Institution requires written confirmation
    Given the institution's configuration requires written confirmation of oral notices
    When a consumer gives an oral notice of error
    Then the platform prompts the analyst to inform the consumer of the requirement
    And the platform prompts the analyst to give the address where confirmation must be sent
    And the platform records that the disclosure was given, with date and time
    And the platform sets the confirmation deadline at 10 business days after the oral notice

  Scenario: Written confirmation received in time
    Given written confirmation was required
    When the confirmation is received within 10 business days of the oral notice
    Then the case proceeds under the normal § 1005.11(c) procedures

  Scenario: Written confirmation not received in time
    Given written confirmation was required and disclosed to the consumer
    When 10 business days pass after the oral notice without receipt of confirmation
    Then the platform flags the case as eligible for the (c)(2)(i)(A) provisional credit exception

  Scenario: Requirement not disclosed
    Given the institution requires written confirmation
    And the required disclosure and address were not recorded at the time of the oral notice
    Then the platform does not allow the (c)(2)(i)(A) exception to be applied
    And the platform alerts the analyst and compliance
```

### US-05: Notice following a documentation or clarification request (§ 1005.11(b)(3))
**As a** dispute analyst, **I want** the 60-day window to run from the date we send requested information **so that** follow-on notices are judged timely correctly.

```gherkin
Feature: Notice based on requested documentation or clarification

  Scenario: Notice received within 60 days of sending the requested information
    Given a notice of error is based on documentation or clarification the consumer requested under (a)(1)(vii)
    And the institution sent that information on day 0
    When the notice is received on or before day 60
    Then the platform marks the notice as timely

  Scenario: Notice received after 60 days of sending the requested information
    Given the institution sent the requested information on day 0
    When the notice is received on day 61
    Then the platform marks the notice as untimely under (b)(3)
```

## Epic 3: Investigation Timelines

### US-06: Ten-business-day investigation (§ 1005.11(c)(1))
**As a** dispute analyst, **I want** the platform to run the 10-business-day track **so that** we determine, report, and correct on time.

```gherkin
Feature: Ten-business-day investigation

  Scenario: Deadline calculation
    When a valid notice of error is received on a given date
    Then the platform sets the determination deadline at 10 business days after receipt
    And the platform shows the investigation as "prompt" with analyst work due immediately

  Scenario: Error determined within 10 business days
    Given the institution determines within 10 business days that an error occurred
    Then the platform sets the correction deadline at one business day after the determination
    And the platform sets the reporting deadline at three business days after the investigation is completed

  Scenario: Reporting after investigation
    When the analyst completes the investigation
    Then the platform sets the result-report deadline at three business days after completion
    And the platform blocks case closure until the report is logged as sent

  Scenario: Deadline warnings
    Given a case is open on the 10-business-day track
    When the determination deadline is within 2 business days
    Then the platform alerts the assigned analyst and the team lead
```

### US-07: Forty-five-day investigation with provisional credit (§ 1005.11(c)(2))
**As a** dispute analyst, **I want** to move a case to the 45-day track only when all conditions are met **so that** the extension is compliant.

```gherkin
Feature: Forty-five-day investigation

  Scenario: Investigation cannot be completed in 10 business days
    Given the investigation will not be complete within 10 business days of receipt
    When the analyst elects the 45-day track
    Then the platform sets the investigation deadline at 45 days from receipt of the notice
    And the platform requires provisional credit to be posted within 10 business days of receipt

  Scenario: Provisional credit posted
    When a provisional credit is posted
    Then the amount equals the alleged error amount
    And the platform includes interest where applicable
    And the platform sets the consumer notification deadline at two business days after the provisional credit
    And the platform records the posting date

  Scenario: Consumer notified of provisional credit
    When the analyst sends the provisional credit notice
    Then the notice states the amount and the date of the provisional credit
    And the notice states that the consumer has full use of the funds during the investigation
    And the platform records the notice date

  Scenario: Funds availability during investigation
    Given a provisional credit has been posted
    Then the platform places no hold or restriction on the provisionally credited funds

  Scenario: Provisional credit deadline missed
    Given a case is on the 45-day track
    When 10 business days pass after receipt with no provisional credit posted
    And no exception in (c)(2)(i)(A) or (B) applies
    Then the platform raises a compliance breach alert
    And the platform prevents the case from using the 45-day extension
```

### US-08: Provisional credit exceptions and the $50 withholding (§ 1005.11(c)(2)(i))
**As a** compliance officer, **I want** exceptions applied only on documented grounds **so that** provisional credit is never improperly withheld.

```gherkin
Feature: Provisional credit exceptions

  Scenario: Withholding up to $50 on a suspected unauthorized transfer
    Given the alleged error is an unauthorized electronic fund transfer
    And the analyst records a reasonable basis for believing an unauthorized transfer occurred
    And the analyst confirms the institution satisfied the requirements of § 1005.6(a)
    When the provisional credit is calculated
    Then the platform permits a withholding of no more than $50 from the credited amount

  Scenario Outline: The $50 withholding is blocked when a condition is missing
    Given the alleged error is an unauthorized electronic fund transfer
    And "<missing_condition>"
    When the provisional credit is calculated
    Then the platform does not permit any amount to be withheld

    Examples:
      | missing_condition                                            |
      | no reasonable basis has been recorded                        |
      | satisfaction of § 1005.6(a) has not been confirmed           |

  Scenario: Withholding cap
    When an analyst enters a withholding greater than $50
    Then the platform rejects the entry

  Scenario: Exception for missing written confirmation
    Given the institution required written confirmation and disclosed it per (b)(2)
    And the written confirmation was not received within 10 business days of the oral notice
    Then the platform allows the analyst to skip the provisional credit
    And the platform records the exception reason "(c)(2)(i)(A)"

  Scenario: Exception for Regulation T accounts
    Given the alleged error involves an account subject to Regulation T
    Then the platform allows the analyst to skip the provisional credit
    And the platform records the exception reason "(c)(2)(i)(B)"

  Scenario: No exception applies
    Given neither exception applies
    Then the platform requires provisional credit within 10 business days of receipt
```

### US-09: Correct the error and report results (§ 1005.11(c)(2)(iii)-(iv))
**As a** dispute analyst, **I want** correction and reporting deadlines tracked on the 45-day track **so that** we meet them after determination.

```gherkin
Feature: Correction and reporting on the 45-day track

  Scenario: Error found
    When the institution determines that an error occurred
    Then the platform sets the correction deadline at one business day after the determination

  Scenario: Reporting results
    When the investigation is completed
    Then the platform sets the reporting deadline at three business days after completion
    And the report includes notice that the provisional credit has been made final, if applicable
```

### US-10: Extended time periods (§ 1005.11(c)(3))
**As a** compliance officer, **I want** the platform to apply extended periods automatically when the case facts qualify **so that** timelines are accurate.

```gherkin
Feature: Extended time periods

  Scenario: 20 business days replaces 10 business days
    Given the notice involves an electronic fund transfer to or from the account within 30 days after the first deposit to the account was made
    Then the platform uses 20 business days in place of 10 business days
    And this applies to the (c)(1) determination deadline and the (c)(2) provisional credit deadline

  Scenario Outline: 90 days replaces 45 days
    Given the notice involves an electronic fund transfer that "<condition>"
    And the case is on the 45-day track
    Then the platform uses 90 days in place of 45 days for completing the investigation

    Examples:
      | condition                                                              |
      | was not initiated within a state                                       |
      | resulted from a point-of-sale debit card transaction                   |
      | occurred within 30 days after the first deposit to the account was made|

  Scenario: Both extensions apply
    Given the transfer occurred within 30 days after the first deposit
    Then the platform uses 20 business days in place of 10 business days
    And the platform uses 90 days in place of 45 days

  Scenario: Extension flags are visible and auditable
    When any extension is applied
    Then the case shows which condition triggered it
    And the audit log records who confirmed the facts supporting it
```

### US-11: Investigation scope for third-party transfers (§ 1005.11(c)(4))
**As a** dispute analyst, **I want** to limit a review to our own records only when the regulatory conditions are met **so that** we document the basis for a records-only review.

```gherkin
Feature: Review of own records

  Scenario: Records-only review is permitted
    Given the alleged error concerns a transfer to or from a third party
    And there is no agreement between the institution and the third party for that type of electronic fund transfer
    And the transfer is not covered by § 1005.14
    When the analyst selects "Review of own records only"
    Then the platform records both conditions as confirmed
    And the review is treated as satisfying the investigation requirement

  Scenario Outline: Records-only review is blocked
    Given the analyst selects "Review of own records only"
    And "<condition>"
    Then the platform rejects the selection
    And the analyst is prompted to perform a fuller investigation

    Examples:
      | condition                                                          |
      | the transfer is not to or from a third party                       |
      | an agreement exists with the third party for that transfer type    |
      | the transfer is covered by § 1005.14                               |
```

## Epic 4: Determination Outcomes

### US-12: Written explanation when no error or a different error is found (§ 1005.11(d)(1))
**As a** dispute analyst, **I want** the result letter to meet the additional content requirements **so that** we comply when the finding differs from the consumer's claim.

```gherkin
Feature: Adverse or different findings

  Scenario Outline: Additional procedures apply
    Given the investigation outcome is "<outcome>"
    Then the platform applies the (d) procedures in addition to (c)

    Examples:
      | outcome                                                              |
      | No error occurred                                                    |
      | An error occurred in a manner different from that described          |
      | An error occurred in an amount different from that described         |

  Scenario: Result report content
    When the analyst prepares the result report
    Then the report includes a written explanation of the institution's findings
    And the report notes the consumer's right to request the documents the institution relied on
    And the platform blocks sending until both elements are present

  Scenario: Consumer requests documents
    Given the consumer requests the documents the institution relied on
    Then the platform creates a task to provide copies promptly
    And the platform records the request date and the date copies were provided
```

### US-13: Debit a provisional credit (§ 1005.11(d)(2))
**As a** dispute analyst, **I want** reversal of a provisional credit to trigger the required notices and honoring period **so that** the consumer is protected.

```gherkin
Feature: Debiting a provisional credit

  Scenario: Notice of debit
    Given a provisional credit exists and the institution determined no error or a different error occurred
    When the platform debits the provisionally credited amount
    Then the platform sends the consumer a notice of the date and amount of the debiting

  Scenario: Notice of honoring items
    When the debit notice is generated
    Then it states that the institution will honor checks, drafts, or similar instruments payable to third parties
    And it states that the institution will honor preauthorized transfers
    And it states that no overdraft charge will result from honoring them
    And it states that the honoring period is five business days after the notification
    And the notice specifies the items that will be honored

  Scenario: Honoring scope
    Given the notice period is running
    When an item is presented
    Then the platform honors the item only if the institution would have paid it
      had the provisionally credited funds not been debited
    And no overdraft fee is charged as a result of honoring it

  Scenario: Debit blocked without notice
    When an analyst attempts to debit a provisional credit
    And the debit notice has not been generated
    Then the platform blocks the debit
```

### US-14: Reassertion of the same error (§ 1005.11(e))
**As a** dispute analyst, **I want** repeat claims identified **so that** we apply the no-further-responsibility rule only where it is allowed.

```gherkin
Feature: Reassertion of error

  Scenario: Same error reasserted after full compliance
    Given a prior case for the same error was closed with all § 1005.11 requirements documented as met
    When the consumer reasserts the same error
    Then the platform flags the new inquiry as a reassertion
    And the platform does not require a new investigation under § 1005.11
    And the analyst's decision is recorded

  Scenario: Prior case was not fully compliant
    Given a prior case has any unmet § 1005.11 requirement
    When the consumer reasserts the same error
    Then the platform treats the notice as a new notice of error

  Scenario: Error asserted after information requested under (a)(1)(vii)
    Given the consumer asserts an error following receipt of information provided under (a)(1)(vii)
    Then the platform treats the notice as a new notice of error
    And the reassertion exception does not apply
```

## Open Items (not resolvable from the provided text)

1. **"Business day" and "day" counting:** The text does not define business days, how day 60 or day 45 is counted, or the cutoff time for receipt. The platform needs a confirmed calendar rule from Compliance.
2. **§ 1005.6(a):** This section is referenced for the $50 withholding but not provided. US-08 treats it as an analyst-confirmed flag.
3. **§§ 1005.9, 1005.10(a), 1005.14:** These are referenced but not provided. Periodic statement and passbook timing, and the § 1005.14 carve-out, need source definitions.
4. **"Within a state" and "point-of-sale debit card transaction":** The text does not define these. Card and ACH transaction attributes must be mapped to them.
5. **Interest on provisional credit:** The text says "where applicable" without a method or rate.
6. **Notice delivery:** The text does not prescribe channel or format (written or oral) for most consumer notices.
7. **Reasonable basis for unauthorized transfers:** The text gives no criteria, so analyst guidance must be defined separately.
8. **Written explanation and "promptly":** No time limit is given for providing documents on request; "promptly" needs an internal SLA.
9. **Same-error test:** The text does not say how to decide that a reasserted error is "the same" (US-14).

I can convert these into a traceability matrix (story to paragraph) or add non-functional requirements next if that would help.
