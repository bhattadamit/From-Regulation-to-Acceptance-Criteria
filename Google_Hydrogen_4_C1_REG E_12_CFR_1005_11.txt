Below are core user stories with Gherkin acceptance criteria for implementing Regulation E (12 CFR § 1005.11) error resolution in a US retail banking card and ACH disputes platform.

---

### Epic: Regulation E Error Resolution Compliance (12 CFR § 1005.11)

#### Story 1: Dispute Intake & Covered Transaction Qualification

**As a** Dispute Intake Agent (or Digital Self-Service Portal),

**I want** the system to evaluate incoming card and ACH disputes against Regulation E eligibility rules upon submission,

**So that** compliant SLA timers and statutory workflows are automatically triggered for covered Electronic Fund Transfers (EFTs).

```gherkin
Feature: Regulation E Dispute Qualification and SLA Timer Initialization

  Scenario: Qualifying consumer debit card or ACH transaction triggers Reg E workflow
    Given a consumer checking or savings account holder submits a dispute
    And the disputed transaction is an Electronic Fund Transfer (debit card, ATM, or consumer ACH)
    And the notice is received within 60 days of the periodic statement transmittal date showing the error
    When the dispute case is created
    Then the system flags the case as "Reg E Eligible"
    And sets the "Initial Investigation Deadline" to 10 business days from the receipt date
    And sets the "Extended Investigation Deadline" to 45 calendar days from the receipt date

  Scenario: Non-consumer account or non-EFT transaction bypasses Reg E workflow
    Given a business account holder submits a dispute for a commercial ACH debit
    When the dispute case is created
    Then the system flags the case as "Non-Reg E (NACHA / Commercial)"
    And does not apply Regulation E provisional credit timers

  Scenario: Oral notice triggers written confirmation request option
    Given a consumer notifies the bank orally of an alleged EFT error
    When the intake agent logs the oral notification
    Then the system starts the 10-business-day investigation clock immediately upon oral receipt
    And generates an optional "Request for Written Confirmation" task due within 10 business days

```

#### Story 2: Provisional Credit Issuance & Extended Investigation (10-Day / 45-Day Rule)

**As a** Disputes Operations Specialist,

**I want** the system to enforce provisional credit issuance if an investigation cannot be completed within 10 business days,

**So that** the bank remains compliant when extending the investigation window to 45 calendar days.

```gherkin
Feature: Provisional Credit and 45-Day Investigation Extension

  Scenario: Investigation unresolved at Business Day 10 triggers provisional credit
    Given a Reg E eligible dispute is in "Open - Under Investigation" status
    And the current date reaches Business Day 10 from the notice receipt date
    And the consumer provided timely written confirmation (if required by bank policy)
    When the daily SLA batch job executes
    Then the system automatically posts a provisional credit for the full disputed amount (including related bank fees)
    And updates the case investigation deadline to 45 calendar days from the initial notice date
    And triggers the "Provisional Credit & Extension Notice" to be sent within 2 business days of crediting

  Scenario: Provisional credit withheld due to missing written confirmation
    Given the bank required written confirmation within 10 business days of an oral error notice
    And the consumer failed to provide written confirmation by Business Day 10
    When the daily SLA batch job executes
    Then the system does not issue provisional credit
    And maintains the 45-calendar-day investigation deadline to complete the investigation and report results

  Scenario: Extended 20-business-day / 90-calendar-day timers for new accounts, POS, or foreign transactions
    Given a Reg E eligible dispute involves a transaction initiated within 30 days of the first deposit to a new account
    Or the transaction was a Point-of-Sale (POS) debit card transaction or initiated outside the United States
    When the dispute case is created
    Then the system sets the "Initial Investigation Deadline" to 20 business days
    And sets the "Extended Investigation Deadline" to 90 calendar days

```

#### Story 3: Final Determination — Error Confirmed (Final Credit)

**As a** Disputes Investigator,

**I want** to finalize a claim when an EFT error is confirmed,

**So that** the consumer receives full restitution (including interest and fees) and required regulatory notice within statutory timelines.

```gherkin
Feature: Resolution of Confirmed Regulation E Error

  Scenario: Error confirmed within 10 business days (no prior provisional credit)
    Given a Reg E dispute is under investigation within the initial 10-business-day window
    When the investigator selects disposition "Error Confirmed"
    Then the system posts a final credit for the disputed amount plus any lost interest and bank-imposed overdraft/NSF fees
    And closes the case within 1 business day of the determination
    And sends the "Final Resolution - Error Corrected" notice promptly

  Scenario: Error confirmed during extended window (provisional credit already issued)
    Given provisional credit was previously posted to the consumer's account
    When the investigator selects disposition "Error Confirmed" on or before Day 45 (or Day 90)
    Then the system converts the provisional credit into a final credit
    And posts any remaining owed interest or bank-assessed fees within 1 business day
    And sends the "Provisional Credit Made Final" notice to the consumer

```

#### Story 4: Final Determination — No Error Found & Provisional Credit Reversal

**As a** Disputes Investigator,

**I want** to close a claim when no error is found and manage the compliant reversal of any provisional credit,

**So that** the consumer receives proper explanation, document access rights, and advance notice before funds are debited.

```gherkin
Feature: Resolution When No Error or Different Error is Found

  Scenario: No error found with provisional credit reversal and 5-business-day protection
    Given provisional credit was previously issued on a Reg E dispute
    When the investigator selects disposition "No Error Found"
    Then the system sends a written "Notice of Final Determination & Reversal" within 3 business days of completing the investigation
    And the notice informs the consumer of their right to request copies of documents relied upon
    And the notice states the exact date and amount of the upcoming debit
    And the system schedules the provisional credit reversal debit for 5 business days after the notice date
    And the system honors checks, preauthorized debits, and third-party drafts without charging overdraft fees during those 5 business days

  Scenario: Consumer requests investigation documentation
    Given a "No Error Found" determination notice has been issued
    When the consumer requests copies of the documents relied upon in the investigation
    Then the system creates a high-priority "Document Fulfillment" task
    And tracks SLA compliance to ensure prompt delivery of the documentation package

```

#### Story 5: Consumer Liability Tiering for Unauthorized Transfers (12 CFR § 1005.6)

**As a** Disputes System Calculation Engine,

**I want** to calculate maximum consumer liability based on access device loss/theft reporting timelines,

**So that** the bank does not unlawfully hold consumers liable beyond statutory caps.

```gherkin
Feature: Regulation E Consumer Liability Calculation

  Scenario: Lost or stolen debit card reported within 2 business days
    Given a consumer reports a lost or stolen debit card within 2 business days of learning of the loss/theft
    When unauthorized transactions occurred prior to notice
    Then the system caps maximum consumer liability at the lesser of $50 or the amount of unauthorized transfers

  Scenario: Lost or stolen debit card reported after 2 business days (up to 60 days from statement)
    Given a consumer learns of a lost or stolen debit card and reports it more than 2 business days later
    When unauthorized transactions occurred after the 2-business-day window
    Then the system caps maximum consumer liability at $500 (up to $50 for the first 2 days, plus remaining unauthorized transfers up to $500 total)

  Scenario: Unauthorized ACH or card-not-present transfer with no physical card loss
    Given a consumer reports an unauthorized EFT where no access device was lost or stolen (e.g., ACH account takeover or skimming)
    And the consumer reports the transfer within 60 days of the periodic statement transmittal date
    When liability is calculated
    Then the system sets maximum consumer liability to $0

```

### Key Implementation Notes for Engineering & QA

* **Business Day Calendar:** Ensure the SLA engine excludes Saturdays, Sundays, and Federal Reserve holidays per 12 CFR § 1005.2(d).
* **Overdraft Protection Hold:** During the 5-business-day post-reversal notice window (Story 4), the core banking ledger integration must suppress NSF/OD fees caused by honoring third-party items against the provisionally credited balance.
* **Audit Logging:** Every timestamp (oral notice, written confirmation receipt, provisional credit posting, notice dispatch) must be immutably logged for CFPB/OCC examination readiness.
