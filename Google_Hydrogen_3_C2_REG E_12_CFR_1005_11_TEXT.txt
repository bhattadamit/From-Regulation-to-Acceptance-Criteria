### Epic: Regulation E Error Resolution (12 CFR § 1005.11) — Card & ACH Disputes Platform

### Story 1: Error Classification & Covered Inquiry Intake

**As a** Disputes Intake Specialist

**I want** the system to classify consumer submissions as covered Regulation E errors or excluded inquiries based on § 1005.11(a)

**So that** only eligible electronic fund transfer (EFT) claims enter the Regulation E resolution workflow.

#### Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Feature: Regulation E Error Eligibility Classification (§ 1005.11(a))

  Scenario Outline: Classify covered EFT error types
    Given a consumer submits a dispute or inquiry regarding an electronic fund transfer
    When the intake specialist selects the claim category "<claim_category>"
    Then the system flags the case as "Reg E Covered = True"
    And initiates the § 1005.11 error resolution workflow

    Examples:
      | claim_category                                                                 |
      | Unauthorized electronic fund transfer                                          |
      | Incorrect electronic fund transfer to or from the consumer's account           |
      | Omission of an electronic fund transfer from a periodic statement              |
      | Computational or bookkeeping error by the institution relating to an EFT       |
      | Receipt of an incorrect amount of money from an electronic terminal            |
      | EFT not identified in accordance with § 1005.9 or § 1005.10(a)                 |
      | Request for § 1005.9/§ 1005.10(a) documentation or EFT clarification           |

  Scenario Outline: Exclude non-covered routine inquiries
    Given a consumer contacts the bank regarding an electronic fund transfer or account
    When the intake specialist categorizes the request as "<excluded_inquiry>"
    Then the system flags the case as "Reg E Covered = False"
    And does not trigger Regulation E statutory timers or provisional credit workflows

    Examples:
      | excluded_inquiry                                       |
      | Routine inquiry about the consumer's account balance   |
      | Request for information for tax or recordkeeping       |
      | Request for duplicate copies of documentation          |

```

### Story 2: Notice of Error Validation, Timeliness & Written Confirmation

**As a** Disputes Intake Specialist

**I want** the platform to validate notice completeness, enforce the 60-day filing window, and manage oral notice written confirmation rules per § 1005.11(b)

**So that** timely and actionable error notices are properly logged and tracked.

#### Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Feature: Notice of Error Intake and Validation (§ 1005.11(b))

  Scenario: Validate required fields for a standard notice of error
    Given a consumer submits an oral or written notice of error not categorized as a documentation/clarification request
    When the specialist attempts to submit the dispute intake form
    Then the system requires the consumer's name and account number
    And requires the reason why the consumer believes an error exists
    And provides fields to capture the type, date, and amount of the error to the extent possible

  Scenario: Validate required fields for a documentation or clarification request (§ 1005.11(a)(1)(vii))
    Given a consumer submits a request for documentation or clarification under § 1005.11(a)(1)(vii)
    When the specialist submits the intake form
    Then the system requires the consumer's name, account number, and reason for the request
    And allows submission without requiring the specific type, date, or amount of an error

  Scenario: Evaluate timeliness within 60 days of periodic statement or passbook documentation
    Given a periodic statement or passbook documentation reflecting the alleged error was sent/provided on "<statement_date>"
    When the institution receives the notice of error on "<receipt_date>"
    Then the system calculates the elapsed calendar days as "<days_elapsed>"
    And marks the notice as "<timeliness_status>"

    Examples:
      | statement_date | receipt_date | days_elapsed | timeliness_status |
      | 2026-09-01     | 2026-10-31   | 60           | Timely            |
      | 2026-09-01     | 2026-11-01   | 61           | Untimely          |

  Scenario: Evaluate timeliness for a follow-up error notice after documentation/clarification delivery
    Given the consumer previously requested documentation or clarification under § 1005.11(a)(1)(vii)
    And the institution sent the requested information on "<info_sent_date>"
    When the consumer's subsequent notice of error is received on "<notice_receipt_date>"
    Then the system marks the notice as "<timeliness_status>"

    Examples:
      | info_sent_date | notice_receipt_date | timeliness_status |
      | 2026-09-10     | 2026-11-09          | Timely            |
      | 2026-09-10     | 2026-11-10          | Untimely          |

  Scenario: Require written confirmation for an oral notice of error
    Given a consumer provides an oral notice of error
    And the bank's policy requires written confirmation
    When the intake specialist logs the oral notice
    Then the system prompts the specialist to inform the consumer of the written confirmation requirement and provide the mailing/submission address
    And sets a "Written Confirmation Due Date" of 10 business days from the oral notice receipt date

```

```gherkin

  Scenario Outline: Calculate initial investigation and provisional credit SLA (10 vs. 20 business days)

    When the system generates the investigation SLAs
    Then the initial investigation / provisional credit deadline is set to "<initial_sla>"

    Examples:
      | first_deposit_relation                   | initial_sla      |

  Scenario Outline: Calculate extended investigation SLA when unresolved within initial period (45 vs. 90 days)

    When the system transitions the case to the extended investigation phase
    Then the final investigation completion deadline is set to "<extended_sla>" from the receipt date of the notice of error
```

### Story 4: Provisional Credit Issuance, Withholding Rules & Consumer Notification

**As a** Disputes Investigator

**I want** the system to govern provisional credit eligibility, enforce the $50 maximum withholding rule, grant full use of funds, and trigger the 2-business-day notice per § 1005.11(c)(2)

**So that** consumers receive timely provisional relief when an investigation extends past the initial window.

#### Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Feature: Provisional Credit Processing and Notification (§ 1005.11(c)(2))

  Scenario: Issue provisional credit when investigation extends beyond the initial business-day window
    Given an investigation cannot be completed within the applicable initial window (10 or 20 business days)
    And no provisional credit exception applies
    When the provisional credit workflow executes on or before the initial business-day deadline
    Then the system credits the consumer's account for the full amount of the alleged error plus applicable interest
    And grants the consumer full use of the credited funds during the remainder of the investigation
    And schedules a consumer notification task due within 2 business days after the provisional crediting containing the amount and date of the credit

  Scenario: Apply maximum $50 withholding for unauthorized EFT
    Given the dispute is classified as an unauthorized electronic fund transfer
    And the investigator records a reasonable basis for believing an unauthorized EFT occurred
    And the system confirms § 1005.6(a) requirements are satisfied
    When the investigator calculates the provisional credit amount
    Then the system allows a maximum withholding of $50.00 from the alleged error amount
    And blocks any withholding amount greater than $50.00

  Scenario: Suppress provisional credit when required written confirmation is not received
    Given the bank required written confirmation within 10 business days of an oral notice of error
    And the consumer was informed of the requirement and provided the submission address at intake
    When 10 business days elapse without receipt of the written confirmation
    Then the system waives the provisional credit requirement while keeping the investigation active

  Scenario: Suppress provisional credit for Regulation T accounts
    Given the alleged error involves an account flagged as subject to Regulation T (12 CFR part 220)
    When the investigation extends beyond the initial business-day window
    Then the system does not issue provisional credit

```

### Story 5: Investigation Scope & Third-Party Record Review Rule

**As a** Disputes Investigator

**I want** the platform to determine when a review of the bank's own records satisfies the investigation requirement under § 1005.11(c)(4)

**So that** third-party transfers without an institutional agreement are investigated efficiently and compliantly.

#### Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Feature: Four-Walls (Own Records) Investigation Scope (§ 1005.11(c)(4))

  Scenario: Satisfy investigation via review of institution's own records
    Given an alleged error concerns a transfer to or from a third party
    And the transfer is not covered by § 1005.14
    And the bank has no agreement with that third party for the type of EFT involved
    When the investigator reviews the bank's internal records regarding the alleged error
    Then the system allows the investigator to complete the investigation based solely on the internal record review

  Scenario: Require external/network investigation when an agreement exists or § 1005.14 applies
    Given an alleged error concerns a transfer to or from a third party
    When an agreement exists between the bank and the third party for that EFT type OR the transfer is covered by § 1005.14
    Then the system prevents closing the investigation solely on an internal record review without external verification steps

```

### Story 6: Error Confirmed — Account Correction & Final Resolution Notice

**As a** Disputes Investigator

**I want** the system to enforce the 1-business-day correction SLA and 3-business-day reporting SLA when an error is confirmed per § 1005.11(c)(1) and (c)(2)(iii)–(iv)

**So that** confirmed errors are remediated and reported within statutory deadlines.

#### Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Feature: Confirmed Error Correction and Reporting (§ 1005.11(c)(1) & (c)(2)(iii)-(iv))

  Scenario: Correct error and report results when no provisional credit was issued
    Given the investigator determines on Day T that an error occurred
    And no provisional credit was previously applied
    When the case disposition is saved as "Error Occurred"
    Then the system creates an account adjustment task due within 1 business day of Day T to correct the error (including applicable interest)
    And creates a consumer results notification task due within 3 business days after completing the investigation

  Scenario: Finalize provisional credit and report results when error is confirmed during extended investigation
    Given provisional credit was previously issued to the consumer's account
    And the investigator determines on Day T that the error occurred as alleged
    When the case disposition is saved as "Error Occurred"
    Then the system changes the provisional credit status to "Final" within 1 business day of Day T
    And generates a consumer results notice due within 3 business days after completing the investigation stating that the provisional credit has been made final

```

### Story 7: Adverse Determination (No Error or Different Error) & Provisional Credit Reversal

**As a** Disputes Investigator

**I want** the platform to generate compliant adverse determination notices, track document requests, and enforce the 5-business-day overdraft/honor protection upon debiting provisional credit per § 1005.11(d)

**So that** denials and partial adjustments follow all consumer notification and item-honoring protections.

#### Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Feature: No Error / Different Error Procedures and Provisional Credit Reversal (§ 1005.11(d))

  Scenario: Generate adverse determination report within 3 business days
    Given the investigator completes the investigation and determines that "No Error Occurred" OR "Different Error Occurred" (in manner or amount)
    When the investigator submits the determination
    Then the system generates a written results report due within 3 business days after completing the investigation
    And the written report includes a written explanation of the institution's findings
    And the written report includes a notice of the consumer's right to request the documents relied upon in making the determination

  Scenario: Fulfill consumer request for relied-upon documents
    Given an adverse or modified determination notice was sent to the consumer
    When the consumer requests copies of the documents relied upon by the institution
    Then the system logs a high-priority "Document Fulfillment" task to promptly provide copies of the relied-upon documents

  Scenario: Debit provisional credit and apply 5-business-day item-honoring protection
    Given provisional credit was previously applied to the consumer's account
    And the investigation concludes that no error occurred (or a smaller error occurred requiring a debit of provisional funds)
    When the system debits the provisionally credited amount from the consumer's account
    Then the system generates a consumer notice stating the date and amount of the debiting
    And the notice informs the consumer that for 5 business days after the notification, the bank will honor checks, drafts, or similar instruments payable to third parties and preauthorized transfers without charging an overdraft fee
    And the core banking integration honors qualifying third-party items and preauthorized transfers for 5 business days after notification up to the balance that would have existed had the provisional credit not been debited, suppressing overdraft fees on those items

```

### Story 8: Reassertion of Previously Resolved Error

**As a** Disputes Intake Specialist

**I want** the system to identify reasserted claims for previously resolved errors and distinguish between closed claims and new claims arising from § 1005.11(a)(1)(vii) disclosures per § 1005.11(e)

**So that** the bank avoids duplicate investigations while preserving consumer rights after receiving requested documentation.

#### Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Feature: Reassertion of Error Handling (§ 1005.11(e))

  Scenario: Reject duplicate reassertion of a fully resolved error
    Given a prior Regulation E error claim for the same EFT was resolved in full compliance with § 1005.11
    And the prior claim was not solely a request for documentation or clarification under § 1005.11(a)(1)(vii)
    When the consumer attempts to reassert the same error
    Then the system flags the submission as a "Prior Resolved Error Reassertion"
    And indicates that the institution has no further Regulation E obligations under § 1005.11

  Scenario: Allow error assertion following fulfillment of a § 1005.11(a)(1)(vii) request
    Given a prior claim was a request for documentation or clarification under § 1005.11(a)(1)(vii)
    And the institution provided the requested information to the consumer
    When the consumer asserts an error based on that provided information within 60 days of the information being sent
    Then the system accepts the submission as a new timely Regulation E error notice
    And initiates the standard § 1005.11 investigation workflow

```
