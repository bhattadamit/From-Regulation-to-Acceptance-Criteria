# **Regulation E Error Resolution — User Stories and Gherkin Acceptance Criteria**

**Regulatory scope:** 12 CFR § 1005.11, Procedures for resolving errors\
**Platform:** Card and ACH Disputes Platform\
**Perspective:** US financial institution\
**Source constraint:** All acceptance criteria below are derived exclusively from the regulatory text supplied in the request. No additional regulatory requirements, operational assumptions, or external interpretations are introduced.

## **1. Scope and implementation approach**

The platform must support the end-to-end Regulation E error resolution lifecycle, including:

1. Error classification and inquiry eligibility.
2. Consumer notice intake and timeliness validation.
3. Oral notice and written confirmation management.
4. Investigation deadlines and extensions.
5. Provisional credit eligibility, calculation, and communication.
6. Investigation findings and account corrections.
7. Consumer notification and document requests.
8. Provisional credit debiting and five-business-day protections.
9. Reassertion of previously resolved errors.

### **Key regulatory parameters**

| Regulatory requirement                             | Standard period                           | Applicable exception                                                 |
| -------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Consumer notice deadline                           | 60 days after statement/documentation     | 60 days after requested information is sent for § 1005.11(a)(1)(vii) |
| Written confirmation after oral notice             | 10 business days                          | If institution requires confirmation                                 |
| Initial investigation and determination            | 10 business days                          | 20 business days for qualifying new accounts                         |
| Provisional credit                                 | Within 10 business days                   | 20 business days for qualifying new accounts                         |
| Extended investigation                             | 45 calendar days                          | 90 calendar days for specified transfers                             |
| Consumer notification of provisional credit        | Within 2 business days after crediting    | None stated                                                          |
| Consumer notification of investigation results     | Within 3 business days after completion   | None stated                                                          |
| Error correction                                   | Within 1 business day after determination | None stated                                                          |
| Consumer protection after provisional credit debit | 5 business days after notification        | None stated                                                          |

**Important implementation distinction:** The 10-, 20-, and 45-/90-day periods must not be treated as interchangeable. The 20-business-day extension applies to the applicable 10-business-day periods, while the 90-day extension applies to the 45-day investigation period.

For this backlog, business-day and calendar-day calculations should be maintained as distinct deadline types.

## **2. Epic 1 — Error classification and eligibility**

### **US-001: Identify qualifying Regulation E errors**

**User story**

As a disputes operations user, I want the platform to identify whether a consumer's claim qualifies as an error under Regulation E, so that eligible notices enter the error resolution process.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Regulation E error classification

  Scenario Outline: Classify a qualifying error
    Given a consumer submits a claim concerning "<error_type>"
    When the platform evaluates the claim
    Then the claim must be classified as a Regulation E error

    Examples:
      | error_type                                      |
      | Unauthorized electronic fund transfer           |
      | Incorrect electronic fund transfer              |
      | Omission of transfer from periodic statement    |
      | Computational or bookkeeping error              |
      | Incorrect amount received from electronic terminal |
      | Electronic fund transfer not properly identified |
      | Request for required documentation or clarification |

  Scenario: Classify an inquiry that is not an error
    Given a consumer submits a routine inquiry about account balance
    When the platform evaluates the inquiry
    Then the inquiry must not be classified as a Regulation E error

  Scenario Outline: Exclude other non-error inquiries
    Given a consumer submits a request for "<request_type>"
    When the platform evaluates the request
    Then the request must not be classified as a Regulation E error

    Examples:
      | request_type                            |
      | Tax or other recordkeeping information  |
      | Duplicate copies of documentation      |
```

**Regulatory reference:** § 1005.11(a)(1)–(2)

### **US-002: Capture consumer notice information**

**User story**

As a disputes operations user, I want the platform to capture the required consumer notice information, so that the institution can determine whether the notice qualifies for Regulation E error resolution.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Consumer notice information

  Scenario: Accept an oral notice of error
    Given a consumer provides an oral notice of error
    When the institution receives the notice
    Then the platform must record the notice as received

  Scenario: Accept a written notice of error
    Given a consumer provides a written notice of error
    When the institution receives the notice
    Then the platform must record the notice as received

  Scenario: Capture required consumer identification
    Given the institution receives a notice of error
    When the notice is recorded
    Then the consumer's name must be identifiable
    And the consumer's account number must be identifiable

  Scenario: Capture details of the alleged error
    Given the institution receives a notice of error
    When the notice is recorded
    Then the platform must capture why the consumer believes an error exists
    And capture the type of error to the extent possible
    And capture the date of the error to the extent possible
    And capture the amount of the error to the extent possible

  Scenario: Apply exception for documentation or clarification requests
    Given the consumer submits a request described under Section 1005.11(a)(1)(vii)
    When the platform records the request
    Then the platform must not require the type, date, and amount of the alleged error
    As a condition for recording that request
```

**Regulatory reference:** § 1005.11(b)(1)

### **US-003: Validate notice timeliness**

**User story**

As a disputes operations user, I want the platform to validate the date a consumer's notice was received against the applicable regulatory deadline, so that notices are evaluated using the correct 60-day period.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Consumer notice timeliness

  Scenario: Notice received within 60 days of periodic statement
    Given a periodic statement first reflects the alleged error
    And the institution sent the statement
    When the consumer's notice is received no later than 60 days after the statement was sent
    Then the notice must satisfy the timing requirement

  Scenario: Notice received after 60 days of periodic statement
    Given a periodic statement first reflects the alleged error
    And the institution sent the statement
    When the consumer's notice is received more than 60 days after the statement was sent
    Then the notice does not satisfy the timing requirement in Section 1005.11(b)(1)(i)

  Scenario: Notice received within 60 days of passbook documentation
    Given passbook documentation first reflects the alleged error
    When the consumer's notice is received no later than 60 days after the documentation was provided
    Then the notice must satisfy the timing requirement

  Scenario: Notice based on requested documentation or clarification
    Given the consumer requested documentation or clarification under Section 1005.11(a)(1)(vii)
    And the institution sent the requested information
    When the consumer submits a notice of error no later than 60 days after the information was sent
    Then the notice must be considered timely

  Scenario: Notice based on requested documentation received after 60 days
    Given the institution sent information requested under Section 1005.11(a)(1)(vii)
    When the consumer submits a notice of error more than 60 days after the information was sent
    Then the notice does not satisfy the timing requirement in Section 1005.11(b)(3)
```

**Regulatory reference:** § 1005.11(b)(1)(i), (b)(3)

## **3. Epic 2 — Oral notice and written confirmation**

### **US-004: Manage written confirmation of oral notice**

**User story**

As a disputes operations user, I want the platform to support written confirmation requirements following oral notices, so that the institution can exercise its option to require confirmation within the prescribed period.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Written confirmation of oral notice

  Scenario: Institution does not require written confirmation
    Given a consumer provides an oral notice of error
    And the institution does not require written confirmation
    When the notice is recorded
    Then the platform must not require written confirmation

  Scenario: Institution requires written confirmation
    Given a consumer provides an oral notice of error
    And the institution requires written confirmation
    When the oral notice is recorded
    Then the consumer must be informed of the written confirmation requirement
    And the consumer must be provided with the address where confirmation must be sent

  Scenario: Written confirmation received within 10 business days
    Given the institution requires written confirmation of an oral notice
    When written confirmation is received within 10 business days of the oral notice
    Then the platform must record the confirmation as received

  Scenario: Written confirmation not received within 10 business days
    Given the institution requires written confirmation of an oral notice
    And written confirmation has not been received within 10 business days
    When the institution evaluates provisional credit eligibility
    Then the institution may rely on the exception to provisional credit under Section 1005.11(c)(2)(i)(A)
```

**Regulatory reference:** § 1005.11(b)(2), (c)(2)(i)(A)

## **4. Epic 3 — Investigation and regulatory deadlines**

### **US-005: Calculate initial investigation deadline**

**User story**

As a disputes operations user, I want the platform to calculate the applicable initial investigation deadline, so that investigations are completed within the required regulatory timeframe.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Initial investigation deadline

  Scenario: Standard investigation deadline
    Given the institution receives a notice of error
    And the transfer does not qualify for an extended initial period
    When the platform calculates the investigation deadline
    Then the investigation deadline must be 10 business days after receipt

  Scenario: New account investigation deadline
    Given the institution receives a notice of error
    And the alleged transfer occurred within 30 days after the first deposit to the account
    When the platform calculates the investigation deadline
    Then the investigation deadline must be 20 business days after receipt

  Scenario: Determine error within initial investigation period
    Given the institution receives a notice of error
    When the institution investigates the alleged error
    Then the institution must determine whether an error occurred within the applicable 10 or 20 business day period

  Scenario: Report investigation results within three business days
    Given the institution has completed its investigation
    When the platform calculates the results notification deadline
    Then the deadline must be within three business days after investigation completion
```

**Regulatory reference:** § 1005.11(c)(1), (c)(3)(i)

### **US-006: Support extended investigations**

**User story**

As a disputes operations user, I want the platform to support extended investigation timelines when the institution cannot complete its investigation within the initial period, so that the institution can use the extended timeframe while satisfying the applicable provisional credit requirements.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Extended investigation period

  Scenario: Standard extended investigation
    Given the institution cannot complete its investigation within 10 business days
    And the alleged transfer does not qualify for a 90-day investigation period
    When the institution elects to use the extended investigation period
    Then the investigation must be completed within 45 days from receipt of the notice of error

  Scenario: Extended investigation for qualifying new account transfer
    Given the institution cannot complete its investigation within the initial period
    And the alleged transfer occurred within 30 days after the first deposit to the account
    When the institution elects to use the extended investigation period
    Then the investigation must be completed within 90 days from receipt of the notice of error

  Scenario: Extended investigation for transfer not initiated within a state
    Given the institution cannot complete its investigation within 10 business days
    And the alleged transfer was not initiated within a state
    When the institution elects to use the extended investigation period
    Then the investigation must be completed within 90 days from receipt of the notice of error

  Scenario: Extended investigation for point-of-sale debit card transaction
    Given the institution cannot complete its investigation within 10 business days
    And the alleged transfer resulted from a point-of-sale debit card transaction
    When the institution elects to use the extended investigation period
    Then the investigation must be completed within 90 days from receipt of the notice of error

  Scenario: Complete extended investigation within applicable period
    Given the institution has elected to use an extended investigation period
    When the investigation is completed
    Then the platform must validate completion against the applicable 45-day or 90-day deadline
```

**Regulatory reference:** § 1005.11(c)(2), (c)(3)(ii)

### **US-007: Determine investigation methodology**

**User story**

As a disputes operations user, I want the platform to recognize when reviewing the institution's own records satisfies the investigation requirements, so that eligible third-party transfer disputes can be resolved using the permitted investigation approach.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Investigation methodology

  Scenario: Institution records review satisfies investigation requirement
    Given the alleged error concerns a transfer to or from a third party
    And there is no agreement between the institution and the third party for the type of electronic fund transfer involved
    And the transfer is not covered by Section 1005.14
    When the institution reviews its own records concerning the alleged error
    Then that review satisfies the investigation requirement under Section 1005.11(c)(4)

  Scenario: Own-records review exception does not apply
    Given the alleged error concerns a transfer to or from a third party
    And an agreement exists between the institution and the third party for the type of electronic fund transfer involved
    When the institution evaluates its investigation methodology
    Then the institution cannot rely on the own-records-only provision in Section 1005.11(c)(4)

  Scenario: Own-records review exception does not apply to covered transfers
    Given the alleged error concerns a transfer to or from a third party
    And the transfer is covered by Section 1005.14
    When the institution evaluates its investigation methodology
    Then the institution cannot rely on the own-records-only provision in Section 1005.11(c)(4)
```

**Regulatory reference:** § 1005.11(c)(4)

## **5. Epic 4 — Provisional credit management**

### **US-008: Calculate and issue provisional credit**

**User story**

As a disputes operations user, I want the platform to calculate and issue provisional credit for eligible unresolved disputes, so that consumers receive access to the alleged error amount during the extended investigation period.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Provisional credit

  Scenario: Provisional credit required for extended investigation
    Given the institution cannot complete its investigation within the initial 10 business day period
    And the institution elects to use the extended investigation period
    And no provisional credit exception applies
    When the platform processes the dispute
    Then the consumer's account must be provisionally credited
    And the credit must equal the amount of the alleged error
    And applicable interest must be included
    And the provisional credit must be issued within 10 business days of receipt of the notice

  Scenario: Provisional credit for qualifying new account
    Given the alleged transfer occurred within 30 days after the first deposit to the account
    And the institution elects to use the extended investigation period
    When the platform calculates the provisional credit deadline
    Then the provisional credit must be issued within 20 business days of receipt of the notice

  Scenario: Withhold maximum permitted amount for qualifying unauthorized transfer
    Given the alleged error is an unauthorized electronic fund transfer
    And the institution has a reasonable basis for believing an unauthorized electronic fund transfer occurred
    And the institution has satisfied the requirements of Section 1005.6(a)
    When the institution calculates provisional credit
    Then the institution may withhold an amount from the credit
    And the withheld amount must not exceed $50

  Scenario: Do not apply unauthorized transfer withholding without required conditions
    Given the alleged error is an unauthorized electronic fund transfer
    And the institution does not satisfy the requirements for withholding under Section 1005.11(c)(2)(i)
    When the institution calculates provisional credit
    Then the platform must not apply the $50 withholding exception

  Scenario: No provisional credit when required written confirmation is not received
    Given the institution requires written confirmation of an oral notice
    And written confirmation is not received within 10 business days
    When the institution evaluates provisional credit eligibility
    Then the institution need not provisionally credit the consumer's account

  Scenario: No provisional credit for Regulation T account
    Given the alleged error involves an account subject to Regulation T
    When the institution evaluates provisional credit eligibility
    Then the institution need not provisionally credit the consumer's account
```

**Regulatory reference:** § 1005.11(c)(2)(i), (c)(3)(i)

### **US-009: Notify consumer of provisional credit**

**User story**

As a disputes operations user, I want the platform to notify consumers when provisional credit is applied, so that consumers are informed of the amount and date of the credit and can use the funds during the investigation.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Provisional credit notification

  Scenario: Notify consumer of provisional credit
    Given provisional credit has been applied to the consumer's account
    When the platform generates the provisional credit notification
    Then the notification must include the provisional credit amount
    And the notification must include the provisional credit date
    And the notification must be provided within two business days after crediting

  Scenario: Provide full use of provisionally credited funds
    Given provisional credit has been applied to the consumer's account
    When the investigation remains open
    Then the consumer must have full use of the provisionally credited funds
```

**Regulatory reference:** § 1005.11(c)(2)(ii)

## **6. Epic 5 — Investigation outcomes and corrections**

### **US-010: Correct confirmed errors**

**User story**

As a disputes operations user, I want the platform to initiate correction when an investigation confirms an error, so that the consumer's account is corrected within the regulatory timeframe.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Confirmed error correction

  Scenario: Correct error after initial investigation
    Given the institution determines that an error occurred
    When the determination is recorded
    Then the institution must correct the error within one business day after the determination

  Scenario: Correct error after extended investigation
    Given the institution determines that an error occurred during an extended investigation
    When the determination is recorded
    Then the institution must correct the error within one business day after the determination

  Scenario: Report confirmed error investigation results
    Given the institution has completed its investigation
    And the institution determines that an error occurred
    When the results are communicated to the consumer
    Then the results must be reported within three business days after investigation completion
```

**Regulatory reference:** § 1005.11(c)(1), (c)(2)(iii)–(iv)

### **US-011: Communicate no-error or different-error findings**

**User story**

As a disputes operations user, I want the platform to generate the required written explanation when the institution determines that no error occurred or that the error differed from the consumer's allegation, so that the consumer receives the investigation findings and document request rights.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: No-error and different-error investigation outcomes

  Scenario: Communicate no-error determination
    Given the institution completes its investigation
    And determines that no error occurred
    When the institution reports the investigation results
    Then the report must include a written explanation of the institution's findings
    And the report must explain the consumer's right to request copies of documents relied upon

  Scenario: Communicate different error determination
    Given the institution completes its investigation
    And determines that an error occurred in a manner or amount different from the consumer's allegation
    When the institution reports the investigation results
    Then the report must include a written explanation of the institution's findings
    And the report must explain the consumer's right to request copies of documents relied upon

  Scenario: Consumer requests supporting documents
    Given the institution has provided a written explanation of its investigation findings
    And the consumer requests copies of documents relied upon
    When the institution receives the request
    Then the institution must promptly provide copies of the documents

  Scenario: Report findings within required timeframe
    Given the institution has completed its investigation
    And the institution determines that no error occurred or a different error occurred
    When the institution reports the investigation results
    Then the results must be reported within three business days after investigation completion
```

**Regulatory reference:** § 1005.11(c)(1), (c)(2)(iv), (d)(1)

### **US-012: Finalize provisional credit**

**User story**

As a disputes operations user, I want the platform to finalize provisional credit when appropriate, so that the account reflects the final investigation outcome and the consumer receives the required notification.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Provisional credit finalization

  Scenario: Make provisional credit final
    Given provisional credit has been applied to the consumer's account
    And the institution determines that an error occurred
    When the institution finalizes the provisional credit
    Then the platform must record the provisional credit as final
    And the consumer's investigation results notification must indicate that provisional credit has been made final, if applicable

  Scenario: Report final provisional credit status
    Given the institution has completed its investigation
    And provisional credit has been made final
    When the institution reports the investigation results
    Then the notification must be sent within three business days after investigation completion
    And the notification must identify the provisional credit as final
```

**Regulatory reference:** § 1005.11(c)(2)(iii)–(iv)

## **7. Epic 6 — Provisional credit debit and consumer protections**

### **US-013: Manage provisional credit debit**

**User story**

As a disputes operations user, I want the platform to manage provisional credit debits and generate the required consumer notifications, so that the institution satisfies the notification and payment protections required when provisionally credited funds are debited.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Provisional credit debit

  Scenario: Notify consumer when provisional credit is debited
    Given provisional credit has been applied to the consumer's account
    When the institution debits the provisionally credited amount
    Then the consumer must be notified of the debit date
    And the consumer must be notified of the debit amount

  Scenario: Notify consumer of five-business-day payment protection
    Given provisional credit has been applied to the consumer's account
    When the institution debits the provisionally credited amount
    Then the consumer must be notified that the institution will honor checks, drafts, or similar instruments payable to third parties
    And the consumer must be notified that the institution will honor preauthorized transfers
    And the notification must state that these protections apply for five business days after notification
    And the notification must state that the consumer will not incur charges as a result of an overdraft caused by the debit

  Scenario: Honor qualifying third-party payment instruments
    Given the institution has debited provisionally credited funds
    And the consumer has received notification of the debit
    When a check, draft, or similar instrument payable to a third party is presented within five business days after notification
    Then the institution must honor the instrument
    If the institution would have paid the instrument had the provisionally credited funds not been debited

  Scenario: Honor qualifying preauthorized transfers
    Given the institution has debited provisionally credited funds
    And the consumer has received notification of the debit
    When a preauthorized transfer is presented within five business days after notification
    Then the institution must honor the transfer
    If the institution would have paid the transfer had the provisionally credited funds not been debited

  Scenario: Apply overdraft charge protection
    Given the institution has debited provisionally credited funds
    And the consumer presents a qualifying payment instrument or preauthorized transfer within five business days after notification
    When the institution honors the payment
    Then the consumer must not be charged as a result of an overdraft caused by the debit of provisional credit
```

**Regulatory reference:** § 1005.11(d)(2)(i)–(ii)

## **8. Epic 7 — Reassertion of previously resolved errors**

### **US-014: Manage reasserted error claims**

**User story**

As a disputes operations user, I want the platform to identify previously resolved error claims when consumers reassert them, so that the institution can apply the appropriate repeat-claim handling requirements.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Reassertion of error

  Scenario: Consumer reasserts an error after full regulatory compliance
    Given the institution has fully complied with the error resolution requirements
    And the consumer subsequently reasserts the same error
    And the original error was not asserted following receipt of information under Section 1005.11(a)(1)(vii)
    When the platform identifies the reasserted error
    Then the institution must have no further responsibilities under Section 1005.11 for that reassertion

  Scenario: Consumer reasserts an error following requested information
    Given the consumer previously requested information or clarification under Section 1005.11(a)(1)(vii)
    And the consumer asserts an error following receipt of the requested information
    When the platform identifies the assertion
    Then the exception to the general reassertion rule must be recognized
    And the platform must not automatically treat the assertion as a reassertion for which the institution has no further responsibilities

  Scenario: Institution has not fully complied with error resolution requirements
    Given the consumer reasserts an error
    And the institution has not fully complied with the error resolution requirements
    When the platform evaluates the reassertion
    Then the platform must not apply the no-further-responsibilities provision in Section 1005.11(e)
```

**Regulatory reference:** § 1005.11(e)

## **11. Regulatory deadline and decision logic**

The following decision flow consolidates the principal timing and provisional credit requirements.

**Step 1 — Receive notice**

Record oral or written notice and evaluate the identification, content, and applicable 60-day timing requirements.

**Step 2 — Determine initial deadline**

- Standard account: 10 business days.
- Qualifying transfer within 30 days after first deposit: 20 business days.

**Step 3 — Determine whether investigation can be completed**

Yes

Complete investigation and determine whether an error occurred within the applicable initial period.

No

Institution may use the extended investigation period, subject to applicable provisional credit requirements.

**Step 4 — Apply extended investigation period**

- Standard: 45 days from notice receipt.
- Qualifying transfer: 90 days from notice receipt.

**Step 5 — Apply provisional credit requirements**

- Credit alleged error amount, including applicable interest.
- Apply permitted maximum $50 withholding only when all stated conditions are met.
- Observe the applicable 10- or 20-business-day deadline.
- Notify consumer within two business days after crediting.
- Provide full use of funds during investigation.

**Step 6 — Complete investigation**

- Confirmed error: correct within one business day.
- No error/different error: provide written explanation and document-request rights.

**Step 7 — Communicate results**

Report results within three business days after investigation completion.

**Step 8 — If provisional credit is debited**

Notify consumer of date and amount, and provide the required five-business-day payment protections.

## **13. Key business analyst clarification points**

Before finalizing the functional specification and handing the backlog to engineering, I would resolve these implementation questions:

1\. **Day-count conventions:** The regulation specifies business days for certain deadlines and days for the 45-/90-day investigation periods. The deadline engine must preserve these distinctions. The supplied text does not specify holiday-calendar or cutoff-time conventions.

2\. **Initial versus extended investigation:** The 45-/90-day provisions are available when the institution cannot complete its investigation within the initial period. They are not a replacement for the initial investigation requirement.

3\. **Provisional credit withholding:** The maximum $50 withholding is conditional on both a reasonable basis for believing an unauthorized transfer occurred and satisfaction of § 1005.6(a). It is not a universal deductible.

4\. **Provisional credit exceptions:** The platform must distinguish the written-confirmation exception and Regulation T account exception from ordinary provisional credit processing.

5\. **Different-error outcomes:** The institution must provide a written explanation and document-request rights when the investigation finds either no error or an error in a different manner or amount.

6\. **Reassertion:** The exception relating to an error asserted following receipt of information under § 1005.11(a)(1)(vii) must remain distinguishable from ordinary reassertions.

7\. **Scope exclusions:** Transfers covered by § 1005.14 are excluded from the own-records-only investigation provision in § 1005.11(c)(4). They should not be excluded from the entire Regulation E error resolution process based solely on this provision.
