### US-01: Error Intake, Classification, and Timeliness Validation

**Regulatory Reference:** 12 CFR § 1005.11(a)(1)–(2), (b)(1), (b)(3), (e)

**As a** Dispute Intake Specialist,

**I want** the platform to validate consumer error notices against covered error types, timeliness windows, and required consumer details,

**So that** valid Regulation E claims enter the resolution workflow with accurate statutory deadlines and non-covered inquiries are filtered out.

#### Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Feature: Regulation E Error Intake and Validation

  Scenario Outline: Classify covered error types under § 1005.11(a)(1)
    Given a consumer submits an oral or written notice of error
    When the intake specialist selects "<Error_Category>"
    Then the system classifies the case as a covered Regulation E error
    And enables the error resolution SLA workflow

    Examples:
      | Error_Category                                                                      |
      | Unauthorized electronic fund transfer                                               |
      | Incorrect electronic fund transfer to or from the consumer's account                |
      | Omission of an electronic fund transfer from a periodic statement                   |
      | Computational or bookkeeping error by the institution relating to an EFT            |
      | Receipt of an incorrect amount of money from an electronic terminal                 |
      | EFT not identified in accordance with § 1005.9 or § 1005.10(a)                      |
      | Request for documentation/clarification required by § 1005.9 or § 1005.10(a)        |

  Scenario Outline: Exclude non-covered inquiries under § 1005.11(a)(2)
    Given a consumer contacts the bank regarding an electronic fund transfer
    When the inquiry is logged as "<Inquiry_Type>"
    Then the system flags the request as excluded from Regulation E error resolution procedures

    Examples:
      | Inquiry_Type                                                  |
      | Routine inquiry about the consumer's account balance          |
      | Request for information for tax or other recordkeeping purposes |
      | Request for duplicate copies of documentation                 |

  Scenario: Validate required notice contents for standard error claims under § 1005.11(b)(1)(ii)-(iii)
    Given a consumer submits a notice of error for categories (a)(1)(i) through (a)(1)(vi)
    When the specialist attempts to submit the intake record
    Then the system requires the consumer's name and account number
    And requires the reason the consumer believes an error exists
    And provides fields to capture the type, date, and amount of the error to the extent possible

  Scenario: Validate required notice contents for documentation/clarification requests under § 1005.11(b)(1)(iii)
    Given a consumer submits a request for documentation or clarification under § 1005.11(a)(1)(vii)
    When the specialist attempts to submit the intake record
    Then the system requires the consumer's name and account number
    And does not block submission if the type, date, and amount of an error are omitted

  Scenario: Calculate 60-day timely filing window from periodic statement or passbook under § 1005.11(b)(1)(i)
    Given the periodic statement or passbook documentation reflecting the alleged error was sent or provided on "<Statement_Date>"
    When the consumer's notice of error is received on "<Receipt_Date>"
    Then the system calculates the elapsed calendar days between "<Statement_Date>" and "<Receipt_Date>"
    And marks the notice as "Timely" if received on or before day 60
    And marks the notice as "Untimely (Over 60 Days)" if received after day 60

  Scenario: Calculate 60-day timely filing window following a documentation/clarification response under § 1005.11(b)(3)
    Given the consumer previously requested documentation or clarification under § 1005.11(a)(1)(vii)
    And the bank sent the requested information on "<Info_Sent_Date>"
    When the consumer submits a subsequent notice of error on "<Receipt_Date>"
    Then the system calculates timeliness as 60 calendar days from "<Info_Sent_Date>"

  Scenario: Prevent duplicate error resolution workflow on reasserted errors under § 1005.11(e)
    Given a prior error claim for the same transaction was resolved in full compliance with § 1005.11
    And the prior claim was not solely a request for documentation or clarification under § 1005.11(a)(1)(vii)
    When the consumer attempts to reassert the same error
    Then the system alerts the specialist that the institution has no further error resolution responsibilities under § 1005.11

```

### US-02: Written Confirmation Management for Oral Error Notices

**Regulatory Reference:** 12 CFR § 1005.11(b)(2), (c)(2)(i)(A)

**As a** Dispute Intake Specialist,

**I want** the system to track written confirmation requirements when a consumer reports an error orally,

**So that** the consumer receives timely instructions and the platform accurately determines provisional credit eligibility.

#### Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Feature: Written Confirmation Requirement for Oral Notices

  Scenario: Trigger written confirmation disclosure upon oral notice under § 1005.11(b)(2)
    Given a consumer submits an oral notice of error
    And bank policy requires written confirmation for the claim
    When the intake record is saved
    Then the system prompts the specialist to inform the consumer of the written confirmation requirement during the interaction
    And displays the designated mailing/submission address to provide to the consumer
    And sets a "Written Confirmation Due Date" of 10 business days from the oral notice receipt date

  Scenario: Withhold provisional credit if required written confirmation is not received within 10 business days under § 1005.11(c)(2)(i)(A)
    Given the bank required written confirmation for an oral notice of error
    And the 10-business-day deadline has elapsed without receipt of written confirmation
    When the investigation extends beyond 10 business days
    Then the system suppresses mandatory provisional credit issuance
    And keeps the investigation SLA active through the applicable 45-day or 90-day investigation deadline

```

### US-03: Dynamic Investigation SLA and Deadline Calculation

**Regulatory Reference:** 12 CFR § 1005.11(c)(1), (c)(2), (c)(3)(i)–(ii)

**As a** Disputes Operations Manager,

**I want** the platform to automatically calculate initial and extended investigation deadlines based on account age, transaction type, and origin,

**So that** investigators meet all statutory deadlines without manual date math.

#### Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Feature: Regulation E Investigation SLA Calculation

  Scenario: Assign standard 10-business-day and 45-calendar-day SLAs under § 1005.11(c)(1)-(2)
    Given a timely notice of error is received
    And the transaction did not occur within 30 days of the first deposit to the account
    And the transaction was initiated within a U.S. state
    And the transaction did not result from a point-of-sale (POS) debit card transaction
    When the case SLA timer initializes
    Then the initial investigation and provisional credit deadline is set to 10 business days from receipt
    And the extended investigation deadline is set to 45 calendar days from receipt

  Scenario: Apply 20-business-day and 90-calendar-day extensions for new accounts under § 1005.11(c)(3)(i)-(ii)(C)
    Given a timely notice of error is received
    And the alleged error involves an EFT that occurred within 30 days after the first deposit to the account was made
    When the case SLA timer initializes
    Then the initial investigation and provisional credit deadline is set to 20 business days from receipt
    And the extended investigation deadline is set to 90 calendar days from receipt

  Scenario Outline: Apply 90-calendar-day extended investigation window for POS debit and foreign transactions under § 1005.11(c)(3)(ii)(A)-(B)
    Given a timely notice of error is received
    And the transaction occurred more than 30 days after the first deposit to the account
    When the transaction meets the condition "<Extension_Trigger>"
    Then the initial investigation and provisional credit deadline remains 10 business days from receipt
    And the extended investigation deadline is set to 90 calendar days from receipt

    Examples:
      | Extension_Trigger                                         |
      | Transaction was not initiated within a state              |
      | Transaction resulted from a point-of-sale debit card EFT  |

```

### US-04: Provisional Credit Issuance, Exclusions, and Consumer Notification

**Regulatory Reference:** 12 CFR § 1005.11(c)(2)(i)–(ii), (c)(3)(i)

**As a** Dispute Investigator,

**I want** the platform to manage provisional credit calculations, statutory exceptions, and 2-business-day consumer notices,

**So that** consumers receive full use of disputed funds on time when investigations cannot be resolved within the initial window.

#### Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Feature: Provisional Credit Processing and Notification

  Scenario: Issue provisional credit when investigation exceeds initial window under § 1005.11(c)(2)(i)-(ii)
    Given an investigation is still open approaching day 10 business days (or day 20 business days for new accounts)
    And no statutory exception applies
    When provisional credit is triggered
    Then the system credits the consumer's account for the full amount of the alleged error plus applicable interest no later than the 10th (or 20th) business day
    And grants the consumer full use of the credited funds during the investigation
    And generates a consumer notification stating the amount and date of the provisional credit to be sent within 2 business days after crediting

  Scenario: Withhold up to $50 on unauthorized EFTs under § 1005.11(c)(2)(i)
    Given the bank has a reasonable basis for believing an unauthorized EFT occurred
    And the investigator confirms the institution has satisfied the disclosure/liability requirements of § 1005.6(a)
    When provisional credit is calculated
    Then the system allows withholding a maximum of $50.00 from the provisionally credited amount

  Scenario: Exempt Regulation T accounts from provisional credit under § 1005.11(c)(2)(i)(B)
    Given the alleged error involves an account subject to Regulation T (12 CFR part 220)
    When the investigation extends beyond 10 business days
    Then the system exempts the case from mandatory provisional crediting

```

### US-05: Investigation Scope for Third-Party Transfers

**Regulatory Reference:** 12 CFR § 1005.11(c)(4)

**As a** Dispute Investigator,

**I want** the system to identify when an internal records review satisfies the investigation requirement for third-party transfers,

**So that** investigations are not needlessly delayed awaiting external parties with whom the bank has no agreement.

#### Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Feature: Third-Party Transfer Investigation Scope

  Scenario: Limit investigation to internal records when no third-party agreement exists under § 1005.11(c)(4)
    Given the alleged error is not covered by § 1005.14
    And the alleged error concerns a transfer to or from a third party
    And the system verifies there is no agreement between the bank and the third party for that type of EFT
    When the investigator completes a review of the bank's own records regarding the alleged error
    Then the system allows the investigator to mark the investigation scope as complete under § 1005.11(c)(4)

```

### US-06: Resolution When Error Is Confirmed (Final Credit & Reporting)

**Regulatory Reference:** 12 CFR § 1005.11(c)(1), (c)(2)(iii)–(iv)

**As a** Dispute Investigator,

**I want** the platform to enforce 1-business-day error correction and 3-business-day result reporting deadlines once an error is confirmed,

**So that** the consumer is made whole and notified promptly.

#### Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Feature: Confirmed Error Resolution and Reporting

  Scenario: Correct confirmed error and report results when no provisional credit was issued under § 1005.11(c)(1)
    Given the investigator determines on "<Determination_Date>" that the alleged error occurred
    And no provisional credit was previously issued
    When the determination is recorded
    Then the system creates a mandatory task to credit/correct the consumer's account (including applicable interest) within 1 business day of "<Determination_Date>"
    And creates a mandatory task to report the investigation results to the consumer within 3 business days after completing the investigation

  Scenario: Finalize provisional credit and report results when error is confirmed under § 1005.11(c)(2)(iii)-(iv)
    Given provisional credit was previously issued to the consumer's account
    And the investigator determines on "<Determination_Date>" that the alleged error occurred
    When the determination is recorded
    Then the system converts the provisional credit to final within 1 business day of "<Determination_Date>" (including any remaining balance/interest if up to $50 was withheld)
    And generates a results notice to be sent within 3 business days after completing the investigation explicitly stating that the provisional credit has been made final

```

### US-07: Adverse Determination (No Error or Different Error) and Provisional Credit Reversal

**Regulatory Reference:** 12 CFR § 1005.11(c)(1), (c)(2)(iv), (d)(1)–(2)

**As a** Dispute Investigator,

**I want** the platform to generate a written explanation with document-request rights and enforce a 5-business-day overdraft protection period when reversing provisional credit,

**So that** adverse determinations and credit clawbacks strictly comply with § 1005.11(d).

#### Acceptance Criteria

```gherkin
Feature: Adverse Determination and Provisional Credit Debiting

  Scenario: Issue written explanation and document-request rights under § 1005.11(d)(1)
    Given the investigator determines that no error occurred or that an error occurred in a manner or amount different from that described by the consumer
    When the investigation is completed
    Then the system requires a written explanation of the bank's findings in the results report
    And includes a mandatory notice of the consumer's right to request copies of the documents the bank relied upon in making its determination
    And schedules the report to be sent within 3 business days after completing the investigation

  Scenario: Track consumer requests for relied-upon documents under § 1005.11(d)(1)
    Given an adverse determination notice was sent to the consumer
    When the consumer requests copies of the documents relied upon by the bank
    Then the system logs a fulfillment task to promptly provide copies of those documents to the consumer

  Scenario: Debit provisional credit and trigger 5-business-day item honoring protection under § 1005.11(d)(2)
    Given provisional credit was previously applied to the consumer's account
    And the bank determines that no error occurred or a different error amount occurred
    When the bank debits the provisionally credited amount from the consumer's account on "<Debit_Date>"
    Then the system sends a notice to the consumer stating the date ("<Debit_Date>") and exact amount of the debiting
    And notifies the consumer that the bank will honor checks, drafts, or similar instruments payable to third parties and preauthorized transfers from the account without overdraft charges for 5 business days after the notification
    And applies a 5-business-day overdraft fee waiver and item-honoring flag on the account for items that would have been paid had the provisionally credited funds not been debited

```
