# Regulation CC Next-Day Availability (12 CFR 229.10): User Stories and Acceptance Criteria

**Product area:** Deposit Hold Engine
**Prepared:** Friday, November 20, 2026
**Status:** Draft for Compliance, Product and Engineering review

---

## 1. Regulatory basis and design assumptions

**What §229.10 requires, as it drives the stories:**

| Deposit type | Required availability | Source / note |
|---|---|---|
| Cash, made in person to a bank employee | Next business day after the banking day of deposit. | §229.10(a)(1) |
| Cash, not made in person to an employee (e.g., proprietary ATM) | No later than the second business day. | §229.10(a)(2) |
| Electronic payments (wire, ACH credit) | Next business day after the banking day the bank received the payment. | §229.10(b). ACH debits are not "electronic payments" under Reg CC. |
| Certain checks (Treasury, USPS money order, Federal Reserve / FHLB, state/local government, cashier's / certified / teller's, on-us) | Next business day, subject to per-category conditions | §229.10(c)(1)(i)–(vi). The Fed compliance guide indicates non-in-person deposits of several of these types may extend to the second business day. |
| First $275 of other check deposits | The minimum amount is $275 effective July 1, 2025, and must be available the next business day. | §229.10(c)(1)(vii); the aggregate is per customer per banking day |

**Dollar thresholds (effective July 1, 2025):** Minimum amount $275, cash withdrawal amount $550, new-account amount $6,725, large-deposit threshold $6,725, repeatedly-overdrawn threshold $6,725. The regulation adjusts these every five years, so the next adjustment is **July 1, 2030**. Only the $275 minimum is in scope here; the others are listed so the parameter model covers them.

**Assumptions**
- **A1.** "Next-day" means the next *business day* after the *banking day* of deposit, not the next calendar day.
- **A2.** Rules are implemented as effective-dated configuration, not hard-coded constants.
- **A3.** The engine produces a *regulatory maximum hold* for each item. Exception-hold logic (§229.13), the nonproprietary ATM rule (§229.12(f)) and hold notices (§229.13(g)) are separate epics. This epic provides the hand-off interface only.
- **A4.** Scope is consumer and commercial transaction accounts as defined in §229.2(a).
- **A5.** Category-level conditions (payee account, special deposit slip, in-person) are stored as flags on each check category. Compliance must validate the flag values against §229.10(c) text and commentary before release (see Open Questions).

---

## 2. Epic

**EPIC-RCC-10: Make the deposit hold engine compute and enforce Regulation CC §229.10 next-day availability.**

**Business value:** Eliminates regulatory exposure (EFA Act civil liability and examiner findings) from over-holding next-day-eligible funds. Gives Operations and Compliance an explainable, auditable decision for every hold.

## 3. User stories

### US-01 Banking day, business day and cutoff calendar

**As a** deposit operations analyst
**I want** the engine to determine the banking day and next business day for each deposit
**so that** every availability date is measured the way Regulation CC defines it.

**Notes:** Business days exclude Saturdays, Sundays and federal holidays. A deposit received after the cutoff is treated as received the next banking day. Regulation CC (§229.19(a)) allows cutoffs no earlier than 2 p.m. for staffed facilities and 12 noon for ATMs. Cutoffs are configurable per channel.

```gherkin
Feature: Banking day and business day determination

  Background:
    Given the federal holiday calendar for 2026 is loaded
    And the teller channel cutoff is 17:00 local time
    And the ATM channel cutoff is 14:00 local time

  Scenario: Deposit before cutoff on a banking day
    Given a deposit is received via teller on Friday 2026-11-20 at 10:15
    When the engine determines the banking day
    Then the banking day is 2026-11-20
    And the next business day is Monday 2026-11-23

  Scenario: Deposit after cutoff is treated as next banking day
    Given a deposit is received via teller on Friday 2026-11-20 at 17:30
    When the engine determines the banking day
    Then the banking day is Monday 2026-11-23
    And the next business day is Tuesday 2026-11-24

  Scenario: Federal holiday is skipped
    Given a deposit is received via teller on Wednesday 2026-11-25 at 11:00
    When the engine determines the next business day
    Then the next business day is Friday 2026-11-27
    And 2026-11-26 is not counted as a business day

  Scenario: Deposit received on a non-banking day
    Given a deposit is received via ATM on Saturday 2026-11-21 at 09:00
    When the engine determines the banking day
    Then the banking day is Monday 2026-11-23

  Scenario: Cutoff differs by channel
    Given a deposit is received via ATM on Monday 2026-11-23 at 15:00
    When the engine determines the banking day
    Then the banking day is Tuesday 2026-11-24
```

### US-02 Cash deposit availability

**As a** compliance officer
**I want** cash deposits to be available by the regulatory deadline according to how they were deposited
**so that** we comply with §229.10(a).

```gherkin
Feature: Cash deposit availability per 12 CFR 229.10(a)

  Background:
    Given the business-day calendar and cutoffs from US-01

  Scenario Outline: Cash availability by deposit method
    Given a cash deposit of <amount> is made via <method> on Monday 2026-11-16 before cutoff
    When the hold engine calculates availability
    Then the funds are available on <available_date>
    And the decision record cites "12 CFR 229.10(a)(<para>)"

    Examples:
      | amount   | method                            | available_date | para |
      | $1,000   | teller, in person to an employee  | 2026-11-17     | 1    |
      | $10,000  | teller, in person to an employee  | 2026-11-17     | 1    |
      | $1,000   | proprietary ATM                   | 2026-11-18     | 2    |

  Scenario: No exception hold may be applied to cash
    Given a cash deposit of $50,000 is made in person to an employee
    And the large-deposit exception flag is evaluated as true
    When the hold engine calculates availability
    Then no exception hold is applied to the cash
    And the funds are available on the next business day
```

### US-03 Electronic payment availability

**As a** deposit operations analyst
**I want** incoming wires and ACH credits to be available by the next business day after receipt
**so that** we meet §229.10(b) and do not misclassify ACH debits.

**Notes:** An electronic payment is "received" when the bank has both collected funds and the account and amount information (§229.10(b)(2)). Many institutions make these funds available sooner. The engine enforces the regulatory ceiling, and a bank-policy override may only be *earlier*.

```gherkin
Feature: Electronic payment availability per 12 CFR 229.10(b)

  Scenario Outline: Wire and ACH credit availability
    Given an incoming <type> of $25,000 is received on Tuesday 2026-11-17
    And the bank has received collected funds and the account and amount information
    When the hold engine calculates availability
    Then the funds are available no later than 2026-11-18

    Examples:
      | type             |
      | wire transfer    |
      | ACH credit       |

  Scenario: Payment received without account information
    Given an incoming wire is received on 2026-11-17 with collected funds
    But the account number is missing
    When the hold engine evaluates the payment
    Then the payment is not treated as "received" for Regulation CC purposes
    And the item is routed to the exceptions queue with reason "INSUFFICIENT_RECEIPT_DATA"

  Scenario: ACH debit is not an electronic payment
    Given a customer deposit is funded by an originated ACH debit
    When the hold engine classifies the item
    Then the item is not classified as "ELECTRONIC_PAYMENT"
    And the item is evaluated under the check/ACH-debit hold rules instead

  Scenario: Electronic payments are not eligible for exception holds
    Given an incoming ACH credit is received for a new account
    When the hold engine calculates availability
    Then no §229.13 exception hold is applied
```

### US-04 Classify checks eligible for next-day availability

**As a** compliance officer
**I want** each deposited check classified into the §229.10(c)(1) next-day categories
**so that** eligible items are never held beyond the next business day.

**Categories (configurable table):** U.S. Treasury check; USPS money order; Federal Reserve Bank or FHLB check; state or local government check; cashier's, certified or teller's check; check drawn on the depositary bank itself (on-us).

```gherkin
Feature: Next-day check classification per 12 CFR 229.10(c)(1)(i)-(vi)

  Background:
    Given the next-day check category table is loaded and effective

  Scenario Outline: Eligible check categories receive next-day availability
    Given a <category> of $4,000 is deposited in person to a teller on Monday 2026-11-16
    And all conditions for the category are met
    When the hold engine classifies and calculates availability
    Then the item class is "NEXT_DAY_<code>"
    And the funds are available on 2026-11-17

    Examples:
      | category                         | code            |
      | U.S. Treasury check              | TREASURY        |
      | USPS money order                 | USPS_MO         |
      | Federal Reserve Bank check       | FRB             |
      | state or local government check  | STATE_LOCAL     |
      | cashier's check                  | CASHIERS        |
      | certified check                  | CERTIFIED       |
      | teller's check                   | TELLERS         |
      | on-us check                      | ON_US           |

  Scenario: Item type cannot be determined
    Given a check is deposited whose type cannot be determined from the MICR line or teller input
    When the hold engine classifies the item
    Then the item is classified as "STANDARD_CHECK"
    And the decision record flags "CLASSIFICATION_UNCERTAIN"
    And the item is queued for operations review within the same business day

  Scenario: Teller manually reclassifies an item
    Given a teller marks a check as "CASHIERS" at the point of deposit
    When the engine receives the deposit
    Then the classification is recorded with the teller ID and timestamp
    And a supervisor approval is required if the item amount exceeds $10,000
```

### US-05 Enforce category conditions (payee account, special deposit slip, in-person)

**As a** compliance officer
**I want** the engine to apply each category's conditions
**so that** next-day treatment is granted only when the regulation requires it, and never withheld when it does.

**Notes:** Some categories require the check to be deposited to the payee's account. A bank may require a special deposit slip for certain categories. Several categories may extend to the second business day when not deposited in person to an employee. Flags per category are data-driven (Assumption A5).

```gherkin
Feature: Conditions on next-day check categories

  Scenario: Cashier's check deposited in person to the payee's account
    Given a cashier's check of $8,000 payable to the account holder
    And it is deposited in person to a teller on Monday 2026-11-16
    When the hold engine calculates availability
    Then the funds are available on 2026-11-17

  Scenario: Cashier's check not deposited in person
    Given a cashier's check of $8,000 payable to the account holder
    And it is deposited at a proprietary ATM on Monday 2026-11-16 before cutoff
    When the hold engine calculates availability
    Then the funds are available no later than 2026-11-18
    And the decision record cites "12 CFR 229.10(c)(2)"

  Scenario: Check payable to a third party
    Given a cashier's check payable to someone other than the account holder
    When the hold engine classifies the item
    Then the "deposited to payee's account" condition is not met
    And the item is evaluated as "STANDARD_CHECK"
    And the $275 minimum in US-07 still applies

  Scenario: Bank requires a special deposit slip
    Given the bank policy requires a special deposit slip for state or local government checks
    And a customer deposits a state government check without the special slip
    When the hold engine calculates availability
    Then next-day treatment is not applied to the item
    And the item is evaluated as "STANDARD_CHECK"
    And the decision record shows "SPECIAL_SLIP_NOT_PRESENTED"

  Scenario: U.S. Treasury check has no in-person requirement
    Given a U.S. Treasury check is deposited via mobile remote deposit capture
    When the hold engine calculates availability
    Then the funds are available on the next business day
```

### US-06 Account for the $275 minimum amount

**As a** customer
**I want** at least the first $275 of my non-next-day check deposits available the next business day
**so that** I have access to a minimum amount of funds quickly.

**Notes:** The $275 is an aggregate per customer per banking day, across all of the customer's accounts at the bank (§229.10(c)(1)(vii)(B)). It applies only to checks *not* already subject to next-day availability under (c)(1)(i)–(vi).

```gherkin
Feature: $275 minimum amount per 12 CFR 229.10(c)(1)(vii)

  Background:
    Given the minimum amount parameter is $275 effective 2025-07-01

  Scenario: Single check under the minimum
    Given a customer deposits one personal check of $200 on Monday 2026-11-16
    When the hold engine calculates availability
    Then the full $200 is available on 2026-11-17

  Scenario: Single check over the minimum
    Given a customer deposits one personal check of $1,000 on Monday 2026-11-16
    When the hold engine calculates availability
    Then $275 is available on 2026-11-17
    And the remaining $725 is available per the standard schedule under §229.12

  Scenario: Aggregation across multiple checks
    Given a customer deposits personal checks of $200 and $300 in one account on 2026-11-16
    When the hold engine calculates availability
    Then $275 in total is available on 2026-11-17
    And the remaining $225 is available per the standard schedule

  Scenario: Aggregation across multiple accounts
    Given a customer with accounts A and B deposits a $150 check to A and a $400 check to B on 2026-11-16
    When the hold engine calculates availability
    Then $275 in total is available on 2026-11-17 across both accounts
    And the allocation follows the configured allocation rule

  Scenario: Next-day-eligible items do not consume the minimum
    Given a customer deposits a $5,000 cashier's check meeting all conditions
    And a $400 personal check on the same banking day
    When the hold engine calculates availability
    Then the $5,000 is available on the next business day as a next-day item
    And $275 of the personal check is also available on the next business day
    And the remaining $125 is available per the standard schedule

  Scenario: Aggregation resets each banking day
    Given a customer deposits $400 on Monday 2026-11-16
    And another $400 on Tuesday 2026-11-17
    When the hold engine calculates availability
    Then a separate $275 minimum is applied to each banking day's deposits

  Scenario: Threshold read from effective-dated configuration
    Given a deposit is processed with a banking day of 2026-11-16
    When the engine reads the minimum amount
    Then it uses the value effective on 2026-11-16
    And does not use a hard-coded constant
```

### US-07 Mixed-item deposits and per-item allocation

**As a** deposit operations analyst
**I want** a deposit containing several item types to be broken down and evaluated per item
**so that** each item receives the correct availability and the total reconciles.

```gherkin
Feature: Mixed deposit decomposition

  Scenario: Deposit with cash and checks
    Given a teller deposit on Monday 2026-11-16 contains $500 cash, a $3,000 Treasury check and an $800 personal check
    When the hold engine calculates availability
    Then the $500 cash is available on 2026-11-17
    And the $3,000 Treasury check is available on 2026-11-17
    And $275 of the personal check is available on 2026-11-17
    And $525 of the personal check is available per the standard schedule
    And the sum of all availability tranches equals the $4,300 deposit total

  Scenario: Item returned after next-day funds were released
    Given $275 of a personal check was released on 2026-11-17
    And the check is later returned unpaid
    When the item return is posted
    Then the return is processed per the existing returned-item procedure
    And the decision record for the original availability remains unchanged
```

### US-08 Regulatory ceiling and bank policy

**As a** product owner
**I want** the engine to accept bank-policy overrides that make funds available *earlier*, but never later than Regulation CC allows
**so that** business policy cannot cause a violation.

```gherkin
Feature: Regulatory maximum hold ceiling

  Scenario: Bank policy gives faster availability
    Given bank policy makes ACH credits available the same day
    When an ACH credit is received on 2026-11-16
    Then the funds are available on 2026-11-16

  Scenario: Configuration attempts to extend beyond the regulatory maximum
    Given an operations user submits a rule change delaying cash deposits to the third business day
    When the change is validated
    Then the system rejects it with error "EXCEEDS_REGCC_MAXIMUM"
    And the rejection is written to the configuration audit log

  Scenario: Manual hold placement on a next-day-eligible item
    Given a user attempts to place a manual hold longer than the next business day on a Treasury check
    When the user submits the hold
    Then the engine requires a documented exception basis under §229.13
    And the hold is blocked if no valid exception basis is selected
```

**I want** Reg CC thresholds maintained as effective-dated, approval-controlled parameters
**so that** the July 1, 2030 inflation adjustment can be implemented without a code release.

```gherkin
  Scenario: Seeded parameters
    Given the parameter table is initialized
    Then it contains for effective date 2025-07-01:
      | parameter                  | citation               | value  |
      | minimum_amount             | 229.10(c)(1)(vii)      | 275    |
      | cash_withdrawal_amount     | 229.12(d)              | 550    |
      | new_account_amount         | 229.13(a)(1)(ii)       | 6725   |
      | large_deposit_threshold    | 229.13(b)              | 6725   |
      | repeated_overdraft_amount  | 229.13(d)(2)           | 6725   |
    And it retains the prior values effective 2020-07-01 for historical reproduction

  Scenario: Four-eyes approval for parameter change
    Given a user creates a new minimum_amount value effective 2030-07-01
    When the user submits the change
    Then it requires approval by a second user with the "Compliance Approver" role
    And it does not take effect until approved

  Scenario: Historical re-calculation
    Given an examiner requests the availability decision for a deposit dated 2024-03-04
    When the engine replays the decision
    Then it applies the $225 minimum amount in effect on that date
```

### US-10 Decision record and audit trail

**As a** compliance officer or examiner
**I want** every availability decision stored with its inputs, rules and result
**so that** we can evidence compliance and answer customer disputes.

```gherkin
Feature: Availability decision audit record

  Scenario: Decision record contents
    Given any deposit has been evaluated by the hold engine
    Then an immutable decision record exists containing:
      | field                     |
      | deposit ID and item IDs   |
      | channel and cutoff applied|
      | banking day and timestamp |
      | item classification       |
      | regulatory citation       |
      | rule version and parameter version |
      | availability date per tranche |
      | any override or policy applied |

  Scenario: Record retention
    Given a decision record was created
    Then it is retained for at least the period set by the bank's records retention schedule
    And it cannot be edited or deleted by application users

  Scenario: Customer inquiry
    Given a customer disputes the availability date of a deposit
    When a service agent opens the deposit
    Then the agent sees a plain-language explanation of the decision
    And the explanation cites the applicable rule category
```

### US-11 Hand-off to exception-hold and other availability modules

**As an** engineer
**I want** a defined interface between next-day logic and the exception-hold, nonproprietary-ATM and new-account modules
**so that** rule precedence is deterministic and out-of-scope rules are not duplicated.

```gherkin
Feature: Rule precedence and module hand-off

  Scenario: Engine output contract
    Given the next-day module evaluates a deposit
    Then it returns for each item:
      | field                     |
      | item_class                |
      | next_day_amount           |
      | next_day_date             |
      | remainder_amount          |
      | remainder_routing         |
    And "remainder_routing" is one of "STANDARD_SCHEDULE_229_12", "EXCEPTION_REVIEW_229_13" or "NONE"

  Scenario: Cash and electronic payments bypass exception logic
    Given an item is classified as cash or ELECTRONIC_PAYMENT
    Then the engine never routes it to the exception-hold module

  Scenario: Nonproprietary ATM deposit
    Given a deposit is made at a nonproprietary ATM
    Then the next-day module does not calculate availability
    And routes the deposit to the §229.12(f) module

  Scenario: New account
    Given the account is within the new-account period
    Then the next-day module hands the deposit to the §229.13(a) module
    And does not apply the $275 minimum unless Compliance has confirmed it applies
```

## 4. Non-functional requirements

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| NFR-1 | Availability calculation completes in under 200 ms at p95 per deposit. |
| NFR-2 | Calculation is deterministic and reproducible: the same inputs plus the same rule and parameter version always give the same output. |
| NFR-3 | Channel cutoffs, holiday calendar and thresholds change through configuration with approval, with no deployment. |
| NFR-4 | Test suite covers every Gherkin scenario above, including holiday-boundary and cutoff-boundary dates (e.g., Thanksgiving week, year-end, leap day). |
| NFR-5 | Monthly report of items held beyond next-day treatment, for Compliance QA sampling. |

## 5. Open questions for Compliance and Legal

1. **Category flags.** Confirm for each §229.10(c)(1) category whether payee-account, special-deposit-slip and in-person conditions apply, and which categories may extend to the second business day under §229.10(c)(2) (Assumption A5).
2. **$275 allocation rule.** The regulation does not specify which account or item receives the $275 when a customer deposits to several accounts. Decide the bank's rule (e.g., earliest deposit or largest item first) and document it in the funds availability policy.
3. **Minimum amount and exception holds.** Confirm whether the $275 must still be released when a §229.13 exception hold (large deposit, repeated overdraft, reasonable cause, emergency) applies.
4. **New accounts.** Confirm treatment of the $275 minimum and the $6,725 new-account amount in the new-account module.
5. **Remote deposit capture.** Confirm "in person" treatment for mobile deposits for each category.
6. **Business-day opening time.** Confirm the time at which next-day funds become available for withdrawal (start of business vs. a fixed posting time) and align it with the customer disclosure.
7. **Disclosure alignment.** Confirm that the published funds availability policy (§229.15–229.16) matches the engine's configured rules.
