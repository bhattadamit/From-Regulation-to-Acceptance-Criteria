# **Business Requirements: Regulation CC Next-Day Availability**

**Deposit Hold Engine | 12 CFR § 229.10**

Regulatory compliance

Business requirements

US banking

## **1. Business objective**

Implement Regulation CC next-business-day funds availability requirements in the bank's deposit hold engine to ensure eligible deposits receive the availability treatment required under **12 CFR § 229.10**.

The solution must:

- Identify deposits that qualify for mandatory next-business-day availability.
- Calculate the applicable availability date using the bank's business calendar and banking-day determination.
- Prevent discretionary holds from delaying funds beyond the regulatory deadline.
- Apply the correct rules to cash, electronic payments, qualifying checks, and the minimum next-day amount for other check deposits.
- Maintain an auditable record of eligibility decisions, availability calculations, and overrides.
- Integrate with deposit processing, core banking, and customer-facing funds availability systems.

**Primary regulatory reference:** 12 CFR § 229.10, Next-day availability.

The requirements below are written as implementation-ready backlog items. They distinguish mandatory regulatory behavior from configurable bank policy.

## **2. Regulatory requirements and scope**

The engine should support the following categories:

| Deposit category                            | Required availability                                                                               |
| ------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Electronic payments                         | No later than the next business day after the banking day of receipt                                |
| Cash deposited in person to a bank employee | No later than the next business day after the banking day of deposit                                |
| U.S. Treasury checks                        | Next business day, subject to applicable eligibility conditions                                     |
| U.S. Postal Service money orders            | Next business day, subject to applicable eligibility conditions                                     |
| Federal Reserve Bank checks                 | Next business day, subject to applicable eligibility conditions                                     |
| Federal Home Loan Bank checks               | Next business day, subject to applicable eligibility conditions                                     |
| State and local government checks           | Next business day, subject to applicable eligibility conditions                                     |
| Cashier's, certified, and teller's checks   | Next business day, subject to applicable eligibility conditions                                     |
| Other qualifying check deposits             | First $275 of aggregate qualifying check deposits per banking day, subject to applicable conditions |

**Important regulatory distinction:** The first $275 provision is a minimum next-day availability requirement for qualifying check deposits that are not otherwise entitled to next-day availability. It is not a substitute for the full next-day availability requirements applicable to the specified check categories.

The $275 threshold reflects the inflation-adjusted amount effective July 1, 2025. Regulatory threshold maintenance should be configurable rather than hardcoded.

## **3. User stories and Gherkin acceptance criteria**

### **US-01: Identify eligible next-day deposit categories**

**As a** deposit operations system,\
**I want** to identify deposits qualifying for next-business-day availability under Regulation CC,\
**So that** eligible funds receive the required availability treatment regardless of the bank's standard hold schedule.

**Business rules:**

- Classify deposits by payment instrument and deposit channel.
- Identify qualifying check types using instrument attributes and available validation data.
- Apply the regulatory eligibility conditions for each check category.
- Do not rely solely on a teller-entered instrument description when more reliable instrument classification data is available.

**Acceptance criteria (Gherkin):**

```
Feature: Identify Regulation CC next-day eligible deposits

  Scenario Outline: Identify qualifying check types
    Given a customer deposits a "<check_type>"
    And the check satisfies the applicable Regulation CC eligibility conditions
    When the deposit is evaluated by the hold engine
    Then the deposit should be classified as "NEXT_DAY_ELIGIBLE"
    And the applicable regulatory availability schedule should be assigned

    Examples:
      | check_type                  |
      | US Treasury check           |
      | USPS money order            |
      | Federal Reserve Bank check  |
      | Federal Home Loan Bank check|
      | State or local government check |
      | Cashier's check             |
      | Certified check             |
      | Teller's check              |

  Scenario: Nonqualifying check is not classified as next-day eligible
    Given a customer deposits a personal check
    And the check does not qualify under the specified check categories
    When the deposit is evaluated
    Then the check should not be classified as a specified next-day check
    And the standard availability rules should be evaluated
    And the applicable minimum next-day amount should be considered

  Scenario: Instrument classification is unavailable
    Given a check deposit has an unknown instrument classification
    When the hold engine evaluates the deposit
    Then the engine should not assume the check qualifies for next-day availability
    And the deposit should be routed for appropriate classification
    And the bank must preserve the applicable regulatory availability deadline
```

### **US-02: Provide next-day availability for cash deposits**

**As a** deposit customer,\
**I want** cash deposited in person to a bank employee to be available by the next business day,\
**So that** my cash deposit is not subjected to an impermissible extended hold.

**Business rules:**

- Cash must be deposited in person to a bank employee.
- Availability must be no later than the business day following the banking day of receipt.
- The engine must distinguish employee-assisted deposits from other deposit channels.

**Acceptance criteria:**

```
Feature: Next-day availability for cash deposits

  Scenario: Cash deposited in person during a banking day
    Given a customer deposits cash in person to a bank employee
    And the deposit is received on a banking day
    When the deposit is evaluated by the hold engine
    Then the cash deposit should be classified as "NEXT_DAY_ELIGIBLE"
    And the availability date should be the next business day
    And no standard extended hold should be applied to the cash

  Scenario: Cash deposited on a Friday
    Given a customer deposits cash in person to a bank employee on Friday
    And Monday is the next business day
    When the deposit is evaluated
    Then the availability date should be Monday

  Scenario: Cash deposited through a non-employee channel
    Given a customer deposits cash through a channel that does not qualify as an in-person deposit to an employee
    When the deposit is evaluated
    Then the engine should not automatically apply the employee-assisted cash rule
    And the applicable channel-specific regulatory rules should be evaluated
```

### **US-03: Provide next-day availability for electronic payments**

**As a** deposit customer,\
**I want** funds received through qualifying electronic payments to be available by the next business day,\
**So that** electronic funds are not subjected to inappropriate check deposit holds.

**Business rules:**

- Recognize qualifying electronic payments.
- Determine the banking day on which the bank receives the electronic payment.
- Calculate availability no later than the following business day.
- Do not apply check collection or check hold schedules to qualifying electronic payments.

**Acceptance criteria:**

```
Feature: Next-day availability for electronic payments

  Scenario: Electronic payment received on a banking day
    Given the bank receives a qualifying electronic payment on Tuesday
    And Wednesday is a business day
    When the payment is processed by the hold engine
    Then the funds should be available no later than Wednesday
    And the deposit should be classified as "ELECTRONIC_PAYMENT"
    And no check deposit hold should be applied

  Scenario: Electronic payment received before a weekend
    Given the bank receives a qualifying electronic payment on Friday
    And Monday is the next business day
    When the payment is evaluated
    Then the funds should be available no later than Monday

  Scenario: Electronic payment received on a bank holiday
    Given the bank receives an electronic payment on a day that is not a banking day
    When the payment is assigned to a banking day under applicable processing rules
    Then the availability date should be calculated using that banking day
    And the funds should be available no later than the following business day
```

### **US-04: Apply next-day availability to qualifying checks**

**As a** deposit customer,\
**I want** qualifying government and bank-issued checks to receive next-business-day availability,\
**So that** the bank complies with the special availability requirements for specified checks.

**Business rules:**

The engine must evaluate applicable conditions, including:

- Whether the check belongs to a qualifying instrument category.
- Whether the check is payable to the customer or an eligible joint account holder.
- Whether the deposit was made in person to a bank employee, where required.
- Whether the check is properly endorsed.
- Whether additional category-specific conditions apply.

For example, state and local government checks have additional requirements concerning the location of the governmental unit and the bank.

**Acceptance criteria:**

```
Feature: Next-day availability for specified checks

  Scenario: Qualifying U.S. Treasury check
    Given a customer deposits a U.S. Treasury check
    And the check satisfies applicable payee and endorsement requirements
    And the deposit channel satisfies applicable requirements
    When the hold engine evaluates the check
    Then the check should be classified as "NEXT_DAY_ELIGIBLE"
    And the availability date should be no later than the next business day

  Scenario: Qualifying cashier's check
    Given a customer deposits a cashier's check
    And the check is payable to an eligible account holder
    And the check is properly endorsed
    And the deposit is made in person to a bank employee
    When the deposit is evaluated
    Then the check should qualify for next-day availability
    And the availability date should be no later than the next business day

  Scenario: State government check fails geographic eligibility
    Given a customer deposits a state government check
    And the check does not satisfy the applicable geographic eligibility condition
    When the deposit is evaluated
    Then the check should not qualify under the state or local government check category
    And the applicable alternative availability requirements should be evaluated

  Scenario: Check is not payable to an eligible account holder
    Given a customer deposits a cashier's check
    And the check is not payable to an eligible account holder
    When the deposit is evaluated
    Then the check should not qualify for the specified-check next-day treatment
    And the applicable alternative availability rules should be evaluated

  Scenario: Qualifying check is deposited through an ineligible channel
    Given a customer deposits a qualifying cashier's check
    And the deposit channel does not satisfy the applicable in-person requirement
    When the deposit is evaluated
    Then the engine should not automatically apply the specified-check next-day treatment
    And the applicable channel-specific availability requirements should be evaluated
```

### **US-05: Apply the minimum next-day availability amount to other check deposits**

**As a** deposit customer,\
**I want** at least the regulatory minimum amount of eligible check deposits to be available the next business day,\
**So that** the bank meets the minimum next-day availability requirement for check deposits not otherwise entitled to full next-day availability.

**Business rules:**

- Apply the $275 minimum to qualifying check deposits.
- Calculate the amount at the account level using aggregate eligible check deposits for the banking day.
- Do not apply the minimum twice to multiple checks deposited into the same account on the same banking day.
- Exclude checks already entitled to full next-day availability from consuming the minimum amount.
- Apply the minimum to the qualifying portion of the aggregate deposit, subject to the regulation's conditions.

**Acceptance criteria:**

```
Feature: Minimum next-day availability for other check deposits

  Scenario: Single eligible check deposit exceeds the minimum
    Given a customer deposits a personal check for $1,000
    And the check qualifies for the minimum next-day availability requirement
    And the customer has no other relevant check deposits that banking day
    When the hold engine evaluates the deposit
    Then $275 should be available no later than the next business day
    And the remaining $725 should be evaluated under the applicable availability schedule

  Scenario: Multiple eligible checks deposited on the same banking day
    Given a customer deposits three eligible personal checks
    And the check amounts are $100, $200, and $300
    And all checks are deposited into the same account on the same banking day
    When the hold engine calculates next-day availability
    Then the aggregate next-day minimum should be $275
    And the engine should not apply a separate $275 minimum to each check

  Scenario: Eligible check deposit is less than the minimum
    Given a customer deposits an eligible personal check for $150
    When the hold engine evaluates the deposit
    Then the entire $150 should be available no later than the next business day

  Scenario: Specified next-day checks do not consume the minimum
    Given a customer deposits a qualifying U.S. Treasury check for $500
    And the customer deposits an eligible personal check for $500
    And both deposits are made into the same account on the same banking day
    When the hold engine calculates availability
    Then the full $500 Treasury check should be available no later than the next business day
    And the $275 minimum should also be applied to the eligible personal check
    And the minimum amount should not be reduced by the Treasury check

  Scenario: Multiple deposits are processed separately
    Given a customer makes two eligible check deposits into the same account
    And both deposits are received on the same banking day
    When the deposits are processed in separate transactions
    Then the engine should maintain a cumulative daily minimum availability calculation
    And the total minimum applied should not exceed the applicable regulatory amount
```

### **US-06: Calculate next-business-day availability correctly**

**As a** deposit operations system,\
**I want** the hold engine to calculate availability dates using the bank's banking-day calendar,\
**So that** weekends, holidays, and deposit receipt times do not result in incorrect availability dates.

**Business rules:**

- Use the bank's configured business-day and banking-day calendar.
- Distinguish banking days from calendar days.
- Apply the bank's compliant cutoff time and receipt determination rules.
- Account for weekends, federal holidays, and applicable bank closures.
- A deposit received after a valid cutoff may be treated as received on the next banking day.

**Acceptance criteria:**

```
Feature: Calculate regulatory next-day availability dates

  Scenario: Deposit received on Friday
    Given a qualifying deposit is received on Friday
    And Monday is a business day
    When the engine calculates availability
    Then the regulatory availability date should be Monday

  Scenario: Deposit received before a holiday
    Given a qualifying deposit is received on the business day before a bank holiday
    And the following day is not a business day
    When the engine calculates availability
    Then the availability date should be the next applicable business day

  Scenario: Deposit received after the applicable cutoff
    Given a qualifying deposit is presented after the bank's applicable cutoff time
    And the cutoff is compliant with Regulation CC
    When the engine determines the banking day of receipt
    Then the deposit should be assigned to the next banking day
    And the availability date should be no later than the business day following that banking day

  Scenario: Deposit received before the applicable cutoff
    Given a qualifying deposit is presented before the applicable cutoff time
    When the engine determines the banking day of receipt
    Then the deposit should be assigned to the current banking day
    And the availability date should be no later than the next business day
```

### **US-07: Prevent standard holds from overriding mandatory next-day availability**

**As a** deposit operations manager,\
**I want** the hold engine to prioritize mandatory Regulation CC availability requirements over standard deposit hold policies,\
**So that** customers are not subject to holds that violate regulatory deadlines.

**Business rules:**

- Regulatory availability requirements must take precedence over ordinary bank hold policies.
- Standard risk-based hold rules must not delay mandatory next-day funds.
- Applicable Regulation CC exceptions must be separately evaluated and authorized.
- Any exception must be supported by a valid reason, applicable regulatory provision, and required documentation.

**Acceptance criteria:**

```
Feature: Regulatory availability takes precedence over standard holds

  Scenario: Standard hold conflicts with next-day availability
    Given a deposit qualifies for mandatory next-day availability
    And the standard deposit hold policy specifies a two-business-day hold
    When the hold engine calculates availability
    Then the regulatory next-day availability requirement should take precedence
    And the applicable funds should be available no later than the next business day

  Scenario: Risk score recommends an extended hold
    Given a qualifying next-day deposit has a high-risk customer risk score
    And the standard risk model recommends a five-business-day hold
    When the hold engine evaluates the deposit
    Then the risk recommendation must not independently override mandatory next-day availability
    And the applicable regulatory availability deadline must be preserved

  Scenario: Regulatory exception is applicable
    Given a qualifying check deposit meets the requirements for a Regulation CC exception
    And the exception is supported by an authorized exception determination
    When the hold engine evaluates the deposit
    Then the engine should apply the permitted exception treatment
    And calculate availability using the applicable exception schedule
    And record the regulatory exception reason

  Scenario: No regulatory exception is applicable
    Given a deposit qualifies for next-day availability
    And no applicable Regulation CC exception has been established
    When the hold engine evaluates the deposit
    Then the engine should apply the mandatory next-day availability requirement
    And reject any standard hold extension that would delay those funds
```

**Implementation note:** Exceptions under 12 CFR § 229.13 must be evaluated by their actual applicability. They must not be treated as blanket permission to delay cash or electronic payments, or to disregard the specific requirements of § 229.10.

### **US-08: Support partial availability and hold allocation**

**As a** deposit operations system,\
**I want** the hold engine to allocate deposit amounts between next-day availability and subsequent availability dates,\
**So that** the customer receives the required portion of the deposit on time while the remaining amount follows the applicable schedule.

**Business rules:**

- Support partial availability for mixed deposits.
- Maintain individual availability amounts and dates.
- Ensure the total allocated amount equals the original deposit amount.
- Prevent duplicate availability allocations.
- Maintain a clear relationship between the deposit, its components, and the resulting availability schedule.

**Acceptance criteria:**

```
Feature: Allocate deposit amounts across availability dates

  Scenario: Mixed deposit containing qualifying and nonqualifying checks
    Given a customer deposits a qualifying Treasury check for $500
    And a personal check for $1,000
    And both checks are deposited into the same account
    When the hold engine calculates availability
    Then $500 should be available no later than the next business day
    And at least $275 of the eligible personal check should be available no later than the next business day
    And the remaining personal check balance should follow the applicable availability schedule

  Scenario: Deposit availability allocations reconcile
    Given a deposit contains multiple instruments
    When the hold engine creates the availability schedule
    Then the sum of all scheduled availability amounts should equal the deposit amount
    And no amount should be allocated more than once
    And each allocation should have an associated availability date

  Scenario: Availability amount is updated after deposit correction
    Given a deposit has an existing next-day availability allocation
    And an authorized correction changes the deposit amount
    When the deposit is recalculated
    Then the engine should recalculate the availability allocations
    And preserve the applicable regulatory minimum
    And record the original and revised allocation details
```

### **US-09: Maintain an auditable record of next-day availability decisions**

**As a** compliance officer,\
**I want** the deposit hold engine to record the rationale behind each Regulation CC availability decision,\
**So that** the bank can demonstrate compliance during internal audits, regulatory examinations, and customer disputes.

**Business rules:**

The audit record should include:

- Deposit transaction identifier.
- Customer and account identifiers, subject to access controls.
- Deposit date and time.
- Banking day of receipt.
- Deposit channel.
- Instrument type.
- Regulatory eligibility determination.
- Eligibility or ineligibility reason code.
- Original deposit amount.
- Amount subject to next-day availability.
- Calculated availability date.
- Applicable hold schedule.
- Exception code, if applicable.
- Rule version and processing timestamp.
- User or system identifier responsible for any override.

**Acceptance criteria:**

```
Feature: Audit Regulation CC availability decisions

  Scenario: Successful next-day eligibility determination
    Given a deposit qualifies for next-day availability
    When the hold engine completes its evaluation
    Then an audit record should be created
    And the record should contain the deposit classification
    And the record should contain the regulatory eligibility determination
    And the record should contain the next-day availability amount and date
    And the record should identify the applicable rule version

  Scenario: Deposit does not qualify for next-day availability
    Given a deposit does not meet the applicable eligibility requirements
    When the hold engine evaluates the deposit
    Then an audit record should be created
    And the record should identify the reason for ineligibility
    And the applicable alternative availability schedule should be recorded

  Scenario: Authorized exception is applied
    Given a qualifying check deposit is subject to a valid regulatory exception
    When the exception is applied
    Then the audit record should identify the exception provision
    And record the exception reason
    And record the revised availability date
    And identify the user or system responsible for the determination

  Scenario: Audit history is retained after recalculation
    Given a deposit has an existing availability determination
    And the deposit is subsequently recalculated
    When the revised availability determination is saved
    Then the original determination should remain available in the audit history
    And the revised determination should be separately recorded
```

### **US-10: Support configurable regulatory thresholds and rule maintenance**

**As a** deposit product administrator,\
**I want** Regulation CC thresholds and eligibility rules to be configurable,\
**So that** the bank can implement regulatory changes without requiring a software release for every threshold adjustment.

**Business rules:**

- Maintain the minimum next-day availability amount as a configurable parameter.
- Maintain effective dates for regulatory changes.
- Support historical rule versions.
- Prevent retroactive changes to completed deposit determinations unless an authorized recalculation is initiated.
- Restrict configuration changes to authorized roles.

**Acceptance criteria:**

```
Feature: Maintain configurable Regulation CC rules

  Scenario: Update the minimum next-day availability threshold
    Given the current minimum next-day availability threshold is $275
    And an authorized administrator updates the threshold to $300
    And the new threshold has a future effective date
    When the effective date is reached
    Then the hold engine should use $300 for eligible deposits
    And deposits processed before the effective date should retain the prior threshold

  Scenario: Unauthorized user attempts to change a threshold
    Given a user does not have regulatory configuration privileges
    When the user attempts to modify the next-day availability threshold
    Then the change should be rejected
    And the attempted modification should be logged

  Scenario: Historical deposit is reviewed
    Given a deposit was processed under a previous regulatory rule version
    When an auditor retrieves the deposit's availability determination
    Then the engine should display the rule version applicable at processing time
    And the original availability calculation should be reproducible
```

### **US-11: Integrate next-day availability with downstream banking systems**

**As a** deposit operations system,\
**I want** the hold engine to communicate next-day availability decisions to downstream banking systems,\
**So that** the correct funds availability is reflected consistently across customer channels and core banking records.

**Business rules:**

- Send the availability schedule to the core banking system.
- Update applicable account-level holds and available balances.
- Ensure customer-facing systems receive the correct availability date and amount.
- Prevent duplicate availability postings.
- Support retry and reconciliation processes for failed messages.

**Acceptance criteria:**

```
Feature: Integrate next-day availability with downstream systems

  Scenario: Next-day availability is successfully communicated
    Given a deposit qualifies for next-day availability
    When the hold engine completes its determination
    Then the availability schedule should be transmitted to the core banking system
    And the next-day availability amount should be reflected in the account records
    And the customer-facing availability information should be updated

  Scenario: Downstream system is unavailable
    Given a deposit qualifies for next-day availability
    And the core banking system is unavailable
    When the hold engine attempts to transmit the availability schedule
    Then the transmission should be queued for retry
    And the original regulatory availability deadline should be preserved
    And an operational alert should be generated if the deadline is at risk

  Scenario: Duplicate availability message is received
    Given an availability instruction has already been successfully processed
    When the same instruction is received again
    Then the downstream system should not duplicate the availability posting
    And the original transaction reference should be retained

  Scenario: Availability reconciliation identifies a discrepancy
    Given the hold engine and core banking system contain different availability amounts
    When the reconciliation process executes
    Then the discrepancy should be identified
    And the transaction should be routed for investigation
    And the applicable regulatory availability deadline should remain protected
```

## **5. Recommended decision table for the hold engine**

This is the proposed high-level decision hierarchy for implementation.

| Processing sequence | Condition                                     | Engine action                                                    |
| ------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1                   | Qualifying electronic payment                 | Apply § 229.10(a)                                                |
| 2                   | Qualifying cash deposit in person to employee | Apply § 229.10(b)                                                |
| 3                   | Qualifying specified next-day check           | Apply § 229.10(c)                                                |
| 4                   | Other eligible check deposit                  | Apply applicable minimum next-day availability requirement       |
| 5                   | Applicable regulatory exception               | Apply permitted exception treatment                              |
| 6                   | No next-day requirement identified            | Apply other applicable Regulation CC and bank availability rules |

**Important:** The implementation must distinguish the applicability of exceptions from the initial eligibility determination. In particular, the engine must not allow an exception applicable to certain checks to inadvertently extend the availability of cash or electronic payments.

## **7. Key implementation considerations and regulatory controls**

Before development sign-off, I would require agreement on these areas:

**1. Distinguish availability from settlement.**

The availability date is the date funds must be available for withdrawal under Regulation CC. It should not be confused with the date the check clears, settles, or is finally paid.

**2. Define deposit receipt consistently.**

The engine needs an authoritative determination of when a deposit is received. Cutoff times, branch operating hours, ATM deposits, and electronic payment receipt rules must be incorporated.

**3. Implement exceptions carefully.**

Regulation CC § 229.13 contains exceptions to certain availability requirements. The engine should maintain exception-specific eligibility, notice, and availability rules rather than a generic exception flag.

**4. Maintain effective-dated thresholds.**

The $275 minimum should be stored in a regulatory parameter table with effective dates. The bank should have a controlled process to implement future inflation adjustments.

**5. Preserve customer protection.**

The engine must calculate availability at the account level and ensure that separate deposit transactions cannot inadvertently bypass the aggregate minimum availability requirement.

- No qualifying deposit receives availability later than its applicable regulatory deadline.
