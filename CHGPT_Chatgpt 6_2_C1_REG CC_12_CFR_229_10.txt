# **Business requirements: Regulation CC next-day availability**

**12 CFR § 229.10 — Deposit Hold Engine Implementation**

Regulatory compliance

Business requirements

Deposit operations

## **1. Business objective**

Implement Regulation CC next-day availability requirements in the bank's deposit hold engine to ensure that eligible deposits receive the required funds availability, regardless of the standard or exception hold rules configured for the account.

The deposit hold engine must identify deposits subject to mandatory next-business-day availability, calculate the required availability date, apply the appropriate availability amount, and prevent conflicting hold rules from delaying funds beyond the regulatory deadline.

**Regulatory reference:** 12 CFR § 229.10, Next-day availability.

**Important implementation assumption:** The requirements below use the Regulation CC threshold effective July 1, 2025: **$275 for the first-day availability requirement** under § 229.10(c)(1)(vii). The bank should validate this threshold against the applicable regulatory schedule and effective date before production deployment.

## **2. Scope and regulatory requirements**

The implementation should address the following categories:

| Deposit category                            | Regulatory treatment                                                             |
| ------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Electronic payments                         | Availability no later than the business day following the banking day of receipt |
| Cash deposited in person to a bank employee | Next-business-day availability                                                   |
| U.S. Treasury checks                        | Next-business-day availability, subject to eligibility requirements              |
| U.S. Postal Service money orders            | Next-business-day availability, subject to eligibility requirements              |
| State and local government checks           | Next-business-day availability, subject to eligibility requirements              |
| Cashier's checks                            | Next-business-day availability, subject to eligibility requirements              |
| Certified checks                            | Next-business-day availability, subject to eligibility requirements              |
| Teller's checks                             | Next-business-day availability, subject to eligibility requirements              |
| First $275 of qualifying check deposits     | Next-business-day availability under § 229.10(c)(1)(vii)                         |

The engine must also account for the eligibility conditions in § 229.10(c), including payee, endorsement, deposit method, and account requirements where applicable.

The implementation must distinguish mandatory next-day availability from the bank's standard funds availability policy and applicable exception-hold provisions under § 229.13.

## **3. Epic: Regulation CC next-day availability**

**Epic ID:** DEP-REGCC-001

**Epic description:** As a bank, we need the deposit hold engine to identify deposits qualifying for next-day availability under Regulation CC § 229.10, so that customers receive access to funds within the required regulatory timeframe.

**Business outcomes:**

- Comply with mandatory next-day funds availability.
- Prevent standard and exception holds from improperly delaying qualifying funds.
- Apply the $275 first-day availability requirement correctly.
- Support business-day, holiday, and cutoff calculations.
- Maintain an auditable record of availability decisions.
- Support regulatory reporting and compliance testing.

# **4. User stories and Gherkin acceptance criteria**

## **US-001: Identify deposits eligible for next-day availability**

**Priority:** Critical

**As a** deposit operations system,\
**I want** to identify deposits qualifying for next-day availability under Regulation CC § 229.10,\
**So that** the correct availability treatment is applied before calculating deposit holds.

### **Acceptance criteria**

**AC-001.1 — Identify eligible deposit categories**

```
Feature: Identify Regulation CC next-day eligible deposits

  Scenario Outline: Identify deposits qualifying for next-day availability
    Given a customer deposits funds into an eligible account
    And the deposit has been classified as "<deposit_type>"
    And all applicable regulatory eligibility conditions are satisfied
    When the deposit hold engine evaluates the deposit
    Then the deposit must be classified as "REG_CC_NEXT_DAY"
    And the applicable next-business-day availability rule must be selected

    Examples:
      | deposit_type              |
      | Electronic payment        |
      | Cash deposit              |
      | US Treasury check         |
      | USPS money order          |
      | State government check    |
      | Local government check    |
      | Cashier's check           |
      | Certified check           |
      | Teller's check            |
      | On-us check               |
```

**AC-001.2 — Ineligible deposits**

```
Scenario: Deposit does not qualify for mandatory next-day treatment
  Given a deposit is not eligible under Regulation CC 229.10
  When the deposit hold engine evaluates the deposit
  Then the deposit must not be classified as REG_CC_NEXT_DAY
  And the standard funds availability rules must be evaluated
  And any applicable exception hold rules must be evaluated
```

**AC-001.3 — Missing deposit classification**

```
Scenario: Deposit classification is missing
  Given a deposit is received without a valid deposit classification
  When the deposit hold engine evaluates the deposit
  Then the engine must not assume the deposit is eligible for next-day availability
  And the deposit must be routed for classification or exception handling
  And the evaluation outcome must be recorded
```

**Business rule:** Eligibility must be determined using the applicable regulatory conditions, not solely the deposit instrument description. For example, the system must consider whether the check is payable to the depositor and whether the required deposit and endorsement conditions have been met.

## **US-007: Prevent conflicting standard and exception holds**

**Priority:** Critical

**As a** deposit operations system,\
**I want** mandatory Regulation CC availability rules to take precedence over conflicting standard hold configurations,\
**So that** eligible funds are not held beyond the regulatory deadline.

### **Acceptance criteria**

**AC-007.1 — Standard hold conflicts with next-day availability**

```
Feature: Next-day availability rule precedence

  Scenario: Standard hold conflicts with mandatory next-day availability
    Given a customer account has a standard two-business-day hold
    And the customer deposits a qualifying next-day availability check
    When the deposit hold engine evaluates the deposit
    Then the qualifying amount must be available by the next business day
    And the standard hold must not delay that amount
    And any remaining amount must follow applicable availability rules
```

**AC-007.2 — Large deposit exception**

```
Scenario: Large deposit exception applies
  Given a customer makes qualifying check deposits
  And the aggregate daily deposit amount exceeds the applicable
      large deposit exception threshold
  When the deposit hold engine evaluates the deposit
  Then the engine must identify the applicable large deposit exception
  And must preserve the availability required under Section 229.10
  And must calculate any permissible additional hold
      under Section 229.13
```

**AC-007.3 — New account exception**

```
Scenario: Deposit made to a new account
  Given a customer deposits a qualifying check into a new account
  And the account qualifies for the new account exception under Section 229.13
  When the deposit hold engine evaluates the check
  Then the engine must apply the applicable new account exception rules
  And must identify check categories that retain mandatory next-day treatment
  And must not automatically apply the standard $275 treatment
      without evaluating the new account exception
```

**AC-007.4 — Repeated overdrafts exception**

```
Scenario: Repeated overdrafts exception applies
  Given a customer account qualifies for an applicable repeated overdrafts exception
  And a qualifying check is deposited
  When the deposit hold engine evaluates the deposit
  Then the engine must evaluate the applicable Section 229.13 exception
  And apply only the holds permitted by Regulation CC
  And preserve any availability that remains mandatory
```

**AC-007.5 — Emergency exception**

```
Scenario: Emergency exception is activated
  Given the bank has an approved emergency exception
  And the emergency exception is applicable to the deposit
  When the deposit hold engine evaluates the deposit
  Then the engine must apply the authorized emergency availability treatment
  And record the applicable exception reason
  And retain the regulatory and operational audit trail
```

**Important design consideration:** Do not implement a simplistic rule that § 229.10 always overrides § 229.13. Certain § 229.13 exceptions modify the normal availability requirements. The engine needs a regulatory decision matrix that evaluates the applicable exception, the deposit category, and any funds that remain protected by next-day requirements.

# **6. Recommended business rule precedence**

The following is a proposed decision framework for the deposit hold engine. It should be validated against the complete applicable Regulation CC requirements before implementation.

## **Deposit availability decision flow**

**1. Receive deposit**

Capture account, amount, instrument, channel and receipt timestamp

**2. Determine Regulation CC eligibility**

Evaluate § 229.10(a), (b), and (c)

**3. Evaluate applicable exceptions**

Evaluate new accounts, large deposits, repeated overdrafts and emergency exceptions under § 229.13

**4. Calculate required availability**

Apply protected next-day amounts and any permissible exception holds

**5. Generate availability schedule**

Assign amounts and availability dates to each deposit component

**6. Validate and audit**

Validate regulatory deadlines, reconcile amounts and record the decision

# **9. Regulatory and implementation considerations**

Before finalizing the requirements for development sign-off, I would call out five important considerations.

1\. **Do not confuse next-day availability with same-day availability.** Section 229.10 generally requires availability by the next business day for the covered categories. Electronic payments have their own receipt and availability rules.

2\. **Do not apply $275 independently to every check.** The first-day availability requirement is subject to aggregate daily calculation rules.

3\. **Do not assume every government or official check automatically qualifies.** The applicable payee, endorsement, deposit-method and other eligibility conditions must be validated.

4\. **Do not treat § 229.10 and § 229.13 as mutually exclusive.** The engine must implement the interaction between mandatory availability and permissible exception holds, including the special treatment of new accounts.

5\. **Do not hardcode regulatory thresholds.** Maintain effective-dated regulatory parameters so future threshold adjustments can be implemented accurately.

The final implementation should also be reviewed against the bank's Regulation CC disclosures, operational procedures, and applicable state-law requirements.
