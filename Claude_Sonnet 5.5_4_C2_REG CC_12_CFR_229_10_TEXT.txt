# Regulation CC Next-Day Availability: User Stories and Acceptance Criteria

**Scope:** 12 CFR 229.10, using only the regulatory text you provided.
**Notation:** "Banking day D" is the banking day on which the deposit is made. "BD+1" and "BD+2" mean the first and second business day after D.

## 1. Assumptions and open items

The provided text does not define these terms, so the criteria treat them as inputs to the engine. They need confirmation from Compliance before build.

| # | Item | How the criteria handle it |
|---|------|----------------------------|
| A1 | "Business day" and "banking day" are not defined in the excerpt. | Calendar logic is a dependency. The criteria state deadlines as "Nth business day after banking day D." |
| A2 | "In person to an employee" is not defined. | The engine needs a deposit-channel attribute: `IN_PERSON_TO_EMPLOYEE` or `NOT_IN_PERSON`. |
| A3 | "Check processing region" and "same state" are not defined. | The engine needs branch attributes for state and check processing region. |
| A4 | (c)(2) covers only checks under (c)(1)(ii)–(v) deposited not in person. The excerpt gives no deadline for a not-in-person Treasury check (c)(1)(i) or on-us check (c)(1)(vi). | Story 7 flags this as a gap. No availability rule is invented for it. |
| A5 | The excerpt covers next-day availability only. It says nothing about other holds, exceptions or longer schedules. | Checks that don't qualify get no deadline here, other than the (c)(1)(vii) partial amount. |
| A6 | The rules say funds must be available "not later than" a deadline. | The engine treats the deadline as the maximum hold. A bank may release earlier. |

## 2. Epic

**As the bank, we need the deposit hold engine to compute and enforce the latest permitted availability date for cash, electronic payment and certain check deposits under 12 CFR 229.10, so that we comply with Regulation CC.**

## 3. User stories

### US-01: Cash deposited in person

**As a** deposit operations analyst
**I want** cash deposits made in person to an employee to be available by the next business day
**So that** we comply with § 229.10(a)(1).

```gherkin
Feature: Next-day availability for in-person cash deposits (§ 229.10(a)(1))

  Scenario: In-person cash deposit is available by the next business day
    Given a customer deposits cash into an account on banking day D
    And the deposit channel is "in person to an employee of the depositary bank"
    When the deposit hold engine calculates the availability date
    Then the funds must be available for withdrawal no later than business day D+1
    And the engine records the rule applied as "229.10(a)(1)"

  Scenario: Engine does not set an availability date later than the maximum
    Given an in-person cash deposit made on banking day D
    When the engine assigns an availability date
    Then the assigned date is never later than business day D+1
```

```gherkin
Feature: Availability for cash deposits not made in person (§ 229.10(a)(2))

    Examples:
      | amount | in_person | days |
      | 500.00 | true      | 1    |
      | 500.00 | false     | 2    |
```

### US-03: Electronic payments, general rule

**As a** payments operations analyst
**I want** electronic payments received for deposit to be available by the business day after the banking day the bank received them
**So that** we comply with § 229.10(b)(1).

```gherkin
Feature: Next-day availability for electronic payments (§ 229.10(b)(1))

  Scenario: Electronic payment received on banking day R
    Given an electronic payment is received for deposit into an account
    And the bank is deemed to have received the payment on banking day R
    When the engine calculates the availability date
    Then the funds must be available no later than business day R+1
    And the engine records the rule applied as "229.10(b)(1)"

  Scenario: The availability clock is measured from the receipt day, not the origination day
    Given an electronic payment was originated before banking day R
    And the bank is deemed to have received it on banking day R
    When the engine calculates the availability date
    Then the clock starts from banking day R
```

### US-04: Determining when an electronic payment is "received"

**As a** payments operations analyst
**I want** the engine to treat an electronic payment as received only when both finally collected funds and account and amount information are in hand, and only to the extent funds are collected
**So that** the availability clock starts correctly under § 229.10(b)(2).

```gherkin
Feature: Receipt of an electronic payment (§ 229.10(b)(2))

  Scenario: Both conditions met, so the payment is received
    Given the bank has received payment in actually and finally collected funds
    And the bank has received information on the account and amount to be credited
    When the engine evaluates the electronic payment
    Then the payment is marked "received"
    And the receipt banking day is set to the banking day on which the second of the two conditions was satisfied

  Scenario: Funds collected but account and amount information missing
    Given the bank has received payment in actually and finally collected funds
    And the bank has not received information on the account and amount to be credited
    When the engine evaluates the electronic payment
    Then the payment is not marked "received"
    And no availability clock is started

  Scenario: Information received but funds not actually and finally collected
    Given the bank has received information on the account and amount to be credited
    And the bank has not received payment in actually and finally collected funds
    When the engine evaluates the electronic payment
    Then the payment is not marked "received"
    And no availability clock is started

  Scenario: Payment received only to the extent of finally collected funds
    Given the payment information states an amount of $10,000.00
    And the bank has received actually and finally collected funds of $6,000.00
    And the bank has received information on the account and amount to be credited
    When the engine evaluates the electronic payment
    Then $6,000.00 is marked "received"
    And the next-day availability rule applies only to $6,000.00
    And the remaining $4,000.00 is not treated as received under § 229.10(b)

  Scenario: Remaining funds later finally collected
    Given $6,000.00 of a $10,000.00 electronic payment was received on banking day R
    When the bank later receives the remaining $4,000.00 in actually and finally collected funds on banking day R2
    Then the additional $4,000.00 is marked "received" on banking day R2
    And it must be available no later than business day R2+1
```

### US-05: Checks eligible for next-day availability, government and bank-issued items

**As a** check operations analyst
**I want** the engine to classify checks under § 229.10(c)(1)(i)–(v) and set next-business-day availability when every listed condition is met
**So that** these checks are released on time.

```gherkin
Feature: Next-day availability for certain checks (§ 229.10(c)(1)(i)–(v))

  Background:
    Given a check is deposited into an account on banking day D

  Scenario: U.S. Treasury check deposited by a payee
    Given the check is drawn on the Treasury of the United States
    And the account is held by a payee of the check
    When the engine calculates the availability date
    Then the funds must be available no later than business day D+1
    And the engine records the rule applied as "229.10(c)(1)(i)"

  Scenario: U.S. Treasury check deposited into an account not held by a payee
    Given the check is drawn on the Treasury of the United States
    And the account is not held by a payee of the check
    When the engine evaluates the check
    Then "229.10(c)(1)(i)" is not applied

  Scenario: USPS money order deposited in person by a payee
    Given the item is a U.S. Postal Service money order
    And the account is held by a payee of the money order
    And the deposit is made in person to an employee of the depositary bank
    When the engine calculates the availability date
    Then the funds must be available no later than business day D+1
    And the engine records the rule applied as "229.10(c)(1)(ii)"

  Scenario: Federal Reserve Bank or Federal Home Loan Bank check deposited in person by a payee
    Given the check is drawn on a Federal Reserve Bank or a Federal Home Loan Bank
    And the account is held by a payee of the check
    And the deposit is made in person to an employee of the depositary bank
    When the engine calculates the availability date
    Then the funds must be available no later than business day D+1
    And the engine records the rule applied as "229.10(c)(1)(iii)"

  Scenario: State or local government check meeting all conditions
    Given the check is drawn by a state or a unit of general local government
    And the account is held by a payee of the check
    And the depositary bank is located in the state that issued the check, or in the same state as the issuing unit of local government
    And the deposit is made in person to an employee of the depositary bank
    And a special deposit slip or envelope is either not required by the bank or was used
    When the engine calculates the availability date
    Then the funds must be available no later than business day D+1
    And the engine records the rule applied as "229.10(c)(1)(iv)"

  Scenario: State or local government check deposited at a bank in a different state
    Given the check is drawn by a state or a unit of general local government
    And the depositary bank is not located in the issuing state, or the state of the issuing local government
    When the engine evaluates the check
    Then "229.10(c)(1)(iv)" is not applied

  Scenario: State or local government check where a required special slip was not used
    Given the bank requires a special deposit slip or envelope under § 229.10(c)(3)
    And the check is drawn by a state or a unit of general local government
    And the deposit was made without the special slip or envelope
    When the engine evaluates the check
    Then "229.10(c)(1)(iv)" is not applied

  Scenario Outline: Cashier's, certified or teller's check
    Given the check is a <check_type>
    And the account is held by a payee: <payee>
    And the deposit is made in person to an employee of the depositary bank: <in_person>
    And the special slip condition is satisfied: <slip_ok>
    When the engine evaluates the check
    Then "229.10(c)(1)(v)" applies: <applies>
    And when it applies, funds must be available no later than business day D+1

    Examples:
      | check_type        | payee | in_person | slip_ok | applies |
      | cashier's check   | true  | true      | true    | true    |
      | certified check   | true  | true      | true    | true    |
      | teller's check    | true  | true      | true    | true    |
      | cashier's check   | false | true      | true    | false   |
      | certified check   | true  | false     | true    | false   |
      | teller's check    | true  | true      | false   | false   |
```

*"Slip condition satisfied" means the bank does not require a special slip, or it requires one and it was used.*

### US-06: On-us branch checks

**As a** check operations analyst
**I want** checks deposited at one branch and drawn on the same or another branch of our bank to be available the next business day when both branches are in the same state or check processing region
**So that** we comply with § 229.10(c)(1)(vi).

```gherkin
Feature: Next-day availability for same-bank branch checks (§ 229.10(c)(1)(vi))

  Scenario Outline: Check deposited at a branch and drawn on a branch of the same bank
    Given a check is deposited in a branch of the depositary bank on banking day D
    And the check is drawn on the same or another branch of the same bank
    And the branches are in the same state: <same_state>
    And the branches are in the same check processing region: <same_region>
    When the engine calculates the availability date
    Then "229.10(c)(1)(vi)" applies: <applies>
    And when it applies, funds must be available no later than business day D+1

    Examples:
      | same_state | same_region | applies |
      | true       | false       | true    |
      | false      | true        | true    |
      | true       | true        | true    |
      | false      | false       | false   |

  Scenario: Check not deposited in a branch of the depositary bank
    Given a check drawn on a branch of the depositary bank
    And the check was not deposited in a branch of the depositary bank
    When the engine evaluates the check
    Then "229.10(c)(1)(vi)" is not applied
```

### US-08: Special deposit slip or envelope

**As a** compliance officer
**I want** the engine to support an optional special-slip requirement for state or local government checks and for cashier's, certified and teller's checks, and to enforce it only when the bank has met its obligations
**So that** we comply with § 229.10(c)(3).

```gherkin
Feature: Special deposit slip or envelope (§ 229.10(c)(3))

  Scenario: Bank does not require a special slip
    Given the bank's configuration has "special slip required" set to false
    When the engine evaluates a state or local government check, or a cashier's, certified or teller's check
    Then the slip condition is treated as satisfied

  Scenario: Bank requires a slip and the customer used it
    Given "special slip required" is true
    And the bank has met the provision requirements below
    And the deposit used a special slip or envelope identifying the type of check
    When the engine evaluates the check
    Then the slip condition is satisfied

  Scenario: Bank requires a slip and the customer did not use it
    Given "special slip required" is true
    And the bank has met the provision requirements below
    And the deposit did not use the special slip or envelope
    When the engine evaluates the check
    Then the slip condition is not satisfied
    And paragraphs (c)(1)(iv) and (c)(1)(v) are not applied

  Scenario Outline: The slip requirement can be enforced only if the bank has made it available
    Given "special slip required" is true
    And the bank provides the slip or envelope to customers: <provides>
    And the bank informs customers how to prepare or obtain it and makes it reasonably available: <informs>
    When an administrator saves the configuration
    Then the configuration is accepted: <accepted>

    Examples:
      | provides | informs | accepted |
      | true     | false   | true     |
      | false    | true    | true     |
      | false    | false   | false    |

  Scenario: Slip requirement does not apply to other check types
    Given "special slip required" is true
    When the engine evaluates a U.S. Treasury check, a USPS money order, a Federal Reserve or FHLB check, or an on-us branch check
    Then the slip condition is not evaluated
```

### US-09: Partial next-day availability, the lesser of $275 or aggregate non-qualifying checks

**As a** deposit operations analyst
**I want** the engine to make the lesser of $275 or the day's aggregate of non-qualifying check deposits available the next business day
**So that** we comply with § 229.10(c)(1)(vii).

```gherkin
Feature: Next-day availability of the lesser of $275 or the aggregate (§ 229.10(c)(1)(vii))

  Scenario Outline: Amount made available next business day
    Given on banking day D a customer deposits checks to one or more of the customer's accounts
    And the aggregate of those checks not subject to next-day availability under (c)(1)(i) through (vi) is $<aggregate>
    When the engine calculates the next-day amount
    Then $<available> must be available no later than business day D+1
    And the engine records the rule applied as "229.10(c)(1)(vii)"

    Examples:
      | aggregate | available |
      | 100.00    | 100.00    |
      | 275.00    | 275.00    |
      | 275.01    | 275.00    |
      | 1,000.00  | 275.00    |

  Scenario: Aggregation across all accounts of the customer
    Given a customer has two accounts
    And on banking day D the customer deposits a $200.00 non-qualifying check to account 1
    And on banking day D the customer deposits a $200.00 non-qualifying check to account 2
    When the engine calculates the next-day amount
    Then the aggregate considered is $400.00
    And a total of $275.00 must be available no later than business day D+1 across the customer's accounts

  Scenario: Checks qualifying under (c)(1)(i) through (vi) are excluded from the aggregate
    Given on banking day D a customer deposits a $5,000.00 check qualifying under "229.10(c)(1)(v)"
    And the customer deposits a $150.00 check not subject to (c)(1)(i) through (vi)
    When the engine calculates the (c)(1)(vii) amount
    Then the aggregate is $150.00
    And the $5,000.00 check follows its own next-day rule

  Scenario: Aggregation is per banking day
    Given on banking day D a customer deposits a $300.00 non-qualifying check
    And on banking day D+1 the customer deposits a $300.00 non-qualifying check
    When the engine calculates the (c)(1)(vii) amounts
    Then each banking day is evaluated separately
    And $275.00 is available for the first deposit by business day D+1
    And $275.00 is available for the second deposit by the business day after banking day D+1
```

### US-10: Rule precedence, audit and traceability

**As a** compliance officer
**I want** the engine to choose the correct rule, record why, and never extend a hold beyond the maximum
**So that** we can evidence compliance.

```gherkin
Feature: Rule selection and audit trail

  Scenario: Item meets more than one rule
    Given an item qualifies under more than one paragraph of § 229.10
    When the engine calculates the availability date
    Then the earliest availability date among the applicable rules is used

  Scenario: Audit record
    Given the engine has calculated an availability date for any deposit
    Then the audit record contains the deposit ID, banking day D, deposit channel, item type, account-holder payee status, rule citation applied, amount made available and availability deadline
    And the record is retained and retrievable by deposit ID

  Scenario: No rule applies
    Given an item matches none of the rules in § 229.10(a), (b) or (c)
    When the engine evaluates the item
    Then no next-day deadline is assigned under § 229.10
    And the item is routed per open item A5
    And, for checks, the (c)(1)(vii) amount is still evaluated
```
