# Regulation E Error Resolution (12 CFR 1005.11): Requirements Pack

**Platform:** Card and ACH Disputes Platform
**Author:** Business Analysis
**Date:** Friday, November 20, 2026
**Status:** Draft for Compliance, Legal, Operations and Engineering review

## 1. Scope and assumptions

**In scope**
- Consumer error notices for debit card, ACH and other EFTs on consumer asset accounts, as defined in 1005.11(a).
- Intake, clocking, provisional credit, investigation, resolution, notices, reassertion, records and reporting.
- Alignment with Nacha rules (unauthorized consumer ACH returns) and card network chargeback timelines.

**Out of scope or routed elsewhere**
- Credit card billing disputes (Reg Z, 12 CFR 1026.13) are routed to the existing credit dispute process.
- Prepaid accounts (1005.18) are an open question (see section 5).
- Remittance transfer errors (Subpart B, 1005.33) are a separate effort.

**Assumptions**
- **A1.** "Business day" follows 1005.2(d): any day the bank's offices are open for substantially all business functions. The platform needs a configurable bank business-day calendar.
- **A2.** "Days" in the 45-day and 90-day limits are calendar days.
- **A3.** Day 0 is the day the bank receives the notice. Business-day counts begin the next business day.
- **A4.** Citations are at the paragraph level. Compliance should confirm sub-paragraph references and the official Supplement I commentary before release.

**Worked timing example (used in the scenarios below).** Notice received Fri 2026-11-20, standard debit card transaction, bank open Nov 27 (closed Thu Nov 26 for Thanksgiving):

| Milestone | Date |
|---|---|
| 10th business day (provisional credit deadline, or investigation deadline if resolved in 10) | Mon 2026-12-07 |
| 45th calendar day | Mon 2027-01-04 |
| 90th calendar day (POS, foreign-initiated, new account) | Thu 2027-02-18 |

## 3. User stories

### Epic A: Intake and eligibility

#### US-01: Log a notice of error from any channel
*Ref: 1005.11(b)*

**As a** dispute intake specialist, **I want** to record a customer's oral or written notice of error from any channel, **so that** the regulatory clock starts on the true receipt date.

```gherkin
Feature: Notice of error intake

  Background:
    Given the bank business-day calendar is loaded
    And the intake channels are phone, branch, secure message, mail, and digital banking

  Scenario: Oral notice by phone starts the clock
    Given a customer calls on "2026-11-20" at 12:15 Central
    And the customer states a debit card charge of "$250.00" on "2026-11-12" was not authorized
    When the specialist saves the notice
    Then the system creates a case with receipt date "2026-11-20"
    And records the channel as "Phone" and the notice type as "Oral"
    And stores the receipt timestamp as immutable
    And starts the regulatory clock

  Scenario: Any reasonable form of notice is accepted
    Given a customer sends a free-text secure message describing an unrecognized ACH debit
    When the specialist logs the message
    Then the system does not require a specific form or template
    And creates a case with receipt date equal to the message timestamp

  Scenario: Notice received on a non-business day
    Given a notice arrives by secure message on Saturday "2026-11-21"
    When the case is created
    Then the receipt date is recorded as "2026-11-21"
    And business-day counting begins on the next business day, "2026-11-23"

  Scenario: Back-dating is blocked
    Given a specialist attempts to edit the receipt date of an existing case
    Then the system rejects the edit
    And writes a failed-edit entry to the audit log
```

#### US-02: Capture minimum notice content without rejecting thin claims
*Ref: 1005.11(b)*

**As a** dispute intake specialist, **I want** guided capture of the customer's name, account, reason and transaction details, **so that** the bank can investigate and a claim isn't wrongly rejected for missing details.

```gherkin
Feature: Notice content capture

  Scenario: Complete notice
    Given a customer provides name, account number, reason for the error, and the type, date and amount of the transfer
    When the specialist saves the notice
    Then the case status is "Open - Investigation Pending"

  Scenario: Notice missing transaction details
    Given a customer provides name and account but cannot recall the exact amount or date
    When the specialist saves the notice
    Then the system suggests candidate transactions from the account within the last 90 days
    And the case is created with status "Open - Clarification Needed"
    And the regulatory clock continues to run
    And the system does not allow the case to be closed as "Rejected - Incomplete"

  Scenario: Customer cannot be identified
    Given the caller cannot be authenticated
    When the specialist completes the intake
    Then the system flags the case "Identity Unverified"
    And routes it to a supervisor for a compliance decision
    And does not discard the notice
```

#### US-03: Evaluate timeliness (60-day window) and extenuating circumstances
*Ref: 1005.11(b)(1) and commentary*

**As a** dispute analyst, **I want** the platform to compute the 60-day window from the statement date and allow documented extensions, **so that** timeliness decisions are consistent and defensible.

```gherkin
Feature: Notice timeliness

  Scenario: Notice within 60 days of the statement
    Given the periodic statement showing the transaction was sent on "2026-11-01"
    And the notice is received on "2026-11-20"
    Then the system sets timeliness to "Timely"
    And the 60-day deadline is "2026-12-31"

  Scenario: Notice after 60 days with extenuating circumstances
    Given the statement was sent on "2026-08-01"
    And the notice is received on "2026-11-20"
    And the customer reports a hospitalization during the window
    When the analyst records the extenuating circumstance and supporting note
    Then the system sets timeliness to "Timely - Extended"
    And requires a second-level approver before proceeding
    And the case is investigated under standard Reg E timelines

  Scenario: Notice after 60 days without extenuating circumstances
    Given the notice is untimely and no extenuating circumstance is recorded
    When the analyst proposes denial for untimeliness
    Then the system requires supervisor approval
    And generates a denial notice for the analyst to review
    And records that the transaction remains eligible for review under other applicable rules, such as Reg E 1005.6 and network or Nacha return rights

  Scenario: Multiple statements
    Given the same error first appears on a statement dated "2026-10-05" and recurs on "2026-11-05"
    Then the system measures the 60 days from the first statement on which the error appears
```

#### US-04: Written confirmation of oral notice
*Ref: 1005.11(b)*

**As a** dispute analyst, **I want** the platform to track a written-confirmation requirement when the bank uses one, **so that** the bank only withholds provisional credit when it is entitled to.

```gherkin
Feature: Written confirmation of oral notice

  Background:
    Given the bank policy "Require written confirmation of oral notice" is enabled

  Scenario: Requirement disclosed at time of oral notice
    Given a customer gives oral notice on "2026-11-20"
    When the specialist reads the required disclosure script
    And states the confirmation deadline and the mailing address
    Then the system records the disclosure as given
    And sets the confirmation due date to the 10th business day, "2026-12-07"

  Scenario: Confirmation not received in time
    Given the disclosure was recorded
    And no written confirmation is received by "2026-12-07"
    When the deadline passes
    Then the system marks "Written confirmation overdue"
    And suspends the provisional credit obligation for this case
    But the investigation clock and the 45-day resolution deadline continue to run

  Scenario: Disclosure not given
    Given the disclosure was not recorded at intake
    Then the system does not allow the "no provisional credit due to missing confirmation" path
    And the case follows the standard provisional credit rules

  Scenario: Confirmation received late but before provisional credit decision
    Given written confirmation arrives after the due date
    Then the case is routed to a supervisor with an advisory to reinstate provisional credit
```

#### US-05: Classify error type and route by product
*Ref: 1005.11(a)*

**As a** dispute analyst, **I want** each case classified against the Reg E error categories and routed by product, **so that** non-Reg E disputes are not handled under the wrong rules and Reg E errors are never missed.

```gherkin
Feature: Error classification and routing

  Scenario Outline: Reg E error categories
    Given a notice describing "<situation>"
    When the analyst selects the error category
    Then the system sets regulation to "Reg E"
    And the category to "<category>"

    Examples:
      | situation                                      | category                           |
      | card used by someone else                      | Unauthorized EFT                   |
      | ACH debit for the wrong amount                 | Incorrect EFT                      |
      | debit missing from statement                   | Omitted EFT                        |
      | bank mis-posted a transfer                     | Computational or bookkeeping error |
      | ATM dispensed less cash than requested         | Incorrect amount from terminal     |
      | request for documentation to check for error   | Documentation or clarification     |

  Scenario: Credit card dispute routed away
    Given the transaction was on a credit card account
    Then the system routes the case to the "Reg Z billing error" workflow
    And does not start a Reg E clock

  Scenario: Routine inquiry is not an error
    Given the customer only asks for a balance or a duplicate statement copy
    And does not want to determine whether an error exists
    Then the system records "Inquiry - Not a Reg E error"
    And does not start a Reg E clock

  Scenario: Documentation request to check for error is a Reg E error
    Given the customer asks for a copy of a receipt to determine whether an error exists
    Then the system classifies it as "Documentation or clarification"
    And starts the Reg E clock

  Scenario: Reclassification keeps the original clock
    Given a case was classified as "Inquiry" and later reclassified as a Reg E error
    Then the clock start date is the original receipt date, not the reclassification date
```

### Epic B: Clock and deadline engine

#### US-06: Regulatory deadline engine
*Ref: 1005.11(c), 1005.2(d)*

**As a** dispute operations manager, **I want** deadlines computed from the bank business-day calendar, **so that** no case misses a Reg E limit.

```gherkin
Feature: Deadline calculation

  Background:
    Given the bank is closed on "2026-11-26" and open on "2026-11-27"

  Scenario: Standard case deadlines
    Given a standard case with receipt date "2026-11-20"
    Then the "10-business-day" date is "2026-12-07"
    And the "45-day" date is "2027-01-04"

  Scenario: Bank holiday excluded
    Given the bank is also closed on "2026-11-27"
    Then the "10-business-day" date is "2026-12-08"

  Scenario: Calendar change recalculates open cases
    Given open cases exist with computed deadlines
    When an administrator adds a new bank closure date
    Then the system recomputes deadlines for all affected open cases
    And records the old and new values in the audit log

  Scenario: Internal target dates are earlier than regulatory dates
    Given the bank configures an internal investigation target of 7 business days
    Then the system shows both the internal target and the regulatory deadline
    And alerts are driven by the internal target

  Scenario: Alerts
    Given a case is within 2 business days of any regulatory deadline
    Then the system raises a warning alert to the case owner and team lead
    When a regulatory deadline passes with the milestone incomplete
    Then the system raises a breach alert to Compliance
    And flags the case "Regulatory Deadline Missed"
```

#### US-07: Extended timelines for special case types
*Ref: 1005.11(c)(3)*

**As a** dispute analyst, **I want** the platform to apply the 20-business-day and 90-day limits where allowed, **so that** extended timelines are applied only for eligible cases.

```gherkin
Feature: Extended investigation periods

  Scenario Outline: Eligibility for extended limits
    Given a case where "<condition>"
    When the analyst saves the case attributes
    Then the provisional credit deadline is "<provisional_credit_limit>"
    And the maximum investigation period is "<investigation_limit>"

    Examples:
      | condition                                              | provisional_credit_limit | investigation_limit |
      | standard transaction on an established account         | 10 business days         | 45 days             |
      | point-of-sale debit transaction                        | 10 business days         | 90 days             |
      | transfer initiated outside the United States           | 10 business days         | 90 days             |
      | account opened fewer than 30 days before the notice    | 20 business days         | 90 days             |

  Scenario: New account determination
    Given the first deposit was made on "2026-11-05"
    And the notice is received on "2026-11-20"
    Then the system marks the account as "New account"
    And shows the supporting first-deposit date on the case

  Scenario: Eligibility must be evidenced
    Given an analyst selects an extended timeline
    Then the system requires the eligibility basis to be selected from a list
    And a standard-timeline case cannot be switched to an extended timeline without that basis
```

### Epic C: Provisional credit

#### US-08: Provisional credit when the investigation exceeds 10 business days
*Ref: 1005.11(c)(2)*

**As a** dispute analyst, **I want** automatic provisional credit and notice when an investigation will exceed the standard period, **so that** customers have use of their funds and the bank remains compliant.

```gherkin
Feature: Provisional credit

  Scenario: Provisional credit issued by day 10
    Given a standard case received "2026-11-20"
    And the investigation is not complete by "2026-12-04"
    And no written-confirmation exclusion applies
    When the day-9 checkpoint runs
    Then the system queues a provisional credit for the full alleged error amount
    And any related fees and interest, where the account bears interest
    And posts the credit no later than "2026-12-07"
    And makes the funds fully available to the customer

  Scenario: Notice of provisional credit
    Given provisional credit was posted on "2026-12-07"
    Then the system generates a notice to the customer within 2 business days, by "2026-12-09"
    And the notice states the amount and the date of the credit

  Scenario: Investigation completed within 10 business days
    Given the investigation concludes on business day 8
    Then the system does not require a provisional credit
    And proceeds to resolution under US-12 or US-13

  Scenario: Amount differs from alleged amount
    Given the customer alleges "$250.00" but the disputed item is "$205.00"
    Then the system requires the analyst to confirm the amount before posting
    And logs the basis for the credited amount

  Scenario: Provisional credit exclusion due to missing written confirmation
    Given written confirmation was required, disclosed, and not received within 10 business days
    Then the system does not post the provisional credit
    And shows the exclusion basis on the case
    And requires a supervisor sign-off

  Scenario: Provisional credit blocked by a system error
    Given the core posting call fails
    Then the system retries and escalates to the operations queue within 1 hour
    And flags a potential regulatory breach if the deadline is within 1 business day
```

### Epic D: Investigation

#### US-09: Investigation workflow
*Ref: 1005.11(c), commentary to 11(c)*

**As a** dispute analyst, **I want** a structured investigation workflow, **so that** each decision is evidence-based and defensible.

```gherkin
Feature: Investigation workflow

  Scenario: Investigation checklist
    Given a case is in "Investigation"
    Then the system presents a checklist including:
      | Review of the bank's own account and transaction records        |
      | Review of authorization, settlement and processing data         |
      | Review of prior and similar claims on the account               |
      | Review of customer contact history and fraud indicators         |
      | Outreach to the customer for clarification, if needed           |
    And each item requires a status and a note before the case can move to a decision

  Scenario: Third-party records not required
    Given the transfer involves a third party
    And the bank has no agreement with that party for this type of EFT
    When the analyst completes review of the bank's own records
    Then the system allows the checklist to be marked complete
    But records the basis as "Own-records review sufficient"

  Scenario: Decision requires documented evidence
    Given the analyst selects "No error occurred"
    Then the system requires at least one evidence attachment or system reference
    And a free-text rationale of at least 100 characters

  Scenario: Second-level review for denials
    Given the analyst proposes a "No error" determination
    Then the case is routed for a second-level review before any notice is released
    And the reviewer cannot be the same user as the analyst

  Scenario: Investigation cannot be closed after deadline without a recorded reason
    Given the case is past the 45-day or 90-day regulatory limit
    Then the system blocks closure until Compliance records the breach reason
```

#### US-10: ACH-specific handling
*Ref: 1005.11 with Nacha Operating Rules*

**As a** dispute analyst, **I want** ACH cases to capture the data needed for returns and authorization checks, **so that** the bank recovers funds where eligible without delaying the Reg E process.

```gherkin
Feature: ACH error handling

  Scenario: Unauthorized ACH debit - Written Statement of Unauthorized Debit
    Given the case is an unauthorized consumer ACH debit
    When the analyst requests a Written Statement of Unauthorized Debit from the customer
    Then the system tracks the statement as "Requested", "Received" or "Overdue"
    And the case retains its Reg E clock regardless of the statement status

  Scenario: Return eligibility check
    Given an ACH debit with settlement date "2026-11-10"
    When the analyst opens the return panel
    Then the system shows the return-eligibility window under current Nacha rules
    And proposes a return reason code from the allowed list
    And states whether the return window is still open

  Scenario: Return window expired
    Given the return window for the ACH debit has closed
    Then the system does not offer an ACH return
    But the Reg E investigation and provisional credit rules still apply
    And the analyst can record an alternative recovery path, such as a warranty claim, subject to Operations rules

  Scenario Outline: Return reasons mapped to the dispute reason
    Given the customer states "<dispute_reason>"
    Then the system suggests the return reason code "<code>"

    Examples:
      | dispute_reason                              | code |
      | never authorized the debit                  | R10  |
      | authorization was revoked before the debit  | R07  |
      | amount differs from the authorization       | R10  |

  Scenario: Customer revoked authorization
    Given the customer says they cancelled the recurring authorization with the originator
    Then the system captures the cancellation date and evidence
    And prompts the analyst to evaluate the case under Reg E and Nacha revocation rules
```

*The reason-code mapping above is illustrative and must be validated against the current Nacha rulebook before build.*

#### US-11: Card-specific handling and network coordination
*Ref: 1005.11 with network rules*

**As a** dispute analyst, **I want** card network chargeback steps tracked inside the Reg E case, **so that** network deadlines never delay or replace Reg E obligations.

```gherkin
Feature: Card dispute and network coordination

  Scenario: Reg E timeline governs the customer outcome
    Given a debit card case with an open network chargeback
    Then the system calculates the Reg E deadlines independently of network deadlines
    And the customer outcome is never made conditional on the network outcome

  Scenario: Network deadline conflict
    Given the network chargeback deadline falls before the Reg E investigation deadline
    Then the system alerts the analyst to file the chargeback by the network date
    And the Reg E clock is unaffected

  Scenario: Chargeback lost
    Given the network rejects the chargeback
    And the Reg E investigation concluded that an error occurred
    Then the bank still corrects the customer's account under US-12
    And logs the loss as a bank charge-off or recovery item for Finance

  Scenario: ATM incorrect amount
    Given the customer says an ATM dispensed less than the amount debited
    Then the system requests the ATM journal and cash reconciliation as evidence
    And attaches them to the case
```

### Epic E: Resolution and notices

#### US-12: Error confirmed: correct, credit and notify
*Ref: 1005.11(c)(1), 1005.11(d)*

**As a** dispute analyst, **I want** the platform to enforce correction timing when an error is found, **so that** the customer is made whole on time.

```gherkin
Feature: Error confirmed

  Scenario: Correct the error within 1 business day
    Given the analyst determines an error occurred on Wednesday "2026-12-02"
    When the decision is approved
    Then the system schedules the correcting entry for no later than "2026-12-03"
    And includes any applicable interest and fees charged because of the error

  Scenario: Convert provisional credit to final
    Given provisional credit of "$250.00" was previously posted
    When the error is confirmed
    Then the system marks the provisional credit "Final"
    And does not post a second credit for the same amount

  Scenario: Notify within 3 business days of completing the investigation
    Given the investigation completed on "2026-12-02"
    Then the system generates a resolution notice
    And the notice must be sent by "2026-12-07"
    And the notice states that the error was corrected and the amount and date of correction

  Scenario: Different error than the one alleged
    Given the investigation finds an error different from the one reported
    Then the system records both the alleged and the confirmed error types
    And corrects the confirmed error
    And the notice explains the difference

  Scenario: Correction failure
    Given the core system rejects the correcting entry
    Then the system escalates to the operations queue immediately
    And flags the case "Correction Pending - At Risk"
```

#### US-13: No error: explain, reverse provisional credit with protections
*Ref: 1005.11(d)*

**As a** dispute analyst, **I want** compliant notices and reversal handling when no error is found, **so that** the customer is informed and protected from avoidable overdraft consequences.

```gherkin
Feature: No error found

  Scenario: Written explanation
    Given the second-level reviewer approves a "No error" determination on "2026-12-10"

    And the notice states the customer's right to request the documents the bank relied on
    And must be sent within 3 business days, by "2026-12-15"

  Scenario: Copies of documents on request
```

#### US-14: Reassertion of the same error

#### US-15: Unauthorized EFT liability determination
*Ref: 1005.6 (adjacent requirement)*

**As a** dispute analyst, **I want** consumer liability calculated when an EFT is confirmed unauthorized, **so that** the customer is charged only what Reg E permits.

```gherkin
Feature: Consumer liability for unauthorized EFTs

  Scenario Outline: Liability tiers
    Given an unauthorized EFT involving an access device
    And the customer reported the loss or theft "<timing>"
    Then the system calculates maximum consumer liability of "<liability>"

    Examples:
      | timing                                                         | liability                       |
      | within 2 business days of learning of the loss or theft         | lesser of $50 or amount taken   |
      | after 2 business days but within 60 days of statement           | up to $500                      |
      | after 60 days of the statement on which the transfer appeared   | may exceed $500 for later items |

  Scenario: No access device involved
    Given the unauthorized transfer did not involve an access device, such as an ACH debit from a compromised account number
    Then the system applies the reporting-based rule tied to the 60-day statement window
    And does not apply the access-device tiers

  Scenario: Liability decision is auditable
    Given a liability amount is calculated
    Then the system stores the inputs, the tier applied and the reviewer
    And Compliance can override only with a recorded reason
```

*Compliance should confirm the tier logic and any bank policy that is more generous than the regulation (for example, network zero-liability programs).*

## 4. Non-functional requirements

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| NFR-01 | Deadline calculations are deterministic, tested against a golden dataset (holidays, weekends, leap years, month ends), and have zero tolerance for off-by-one errors. |
| NFR-02 | Time zones: receipt timestamps are stored in UTC with the bank's business-day cutoff applied in Central Time. The cutoff policy must be confirmed (see section 5). |
| NFR-03 | Core posting integration is idempotent. Retries must never double-credit. |
| NFR-04 | Provisional credit posting and notice generation have a 99.9% monthly success rate, with failures alerted within 15 minutes. |
| NFR-05 | PII and account data are encrypted at rest and in transit. Evidence attachments are access-controlled by role. |
| NFR-06 | All regulatory rules (day counts, tiers, windows) are configuration-driven with change control, so rule changes don't require code releases. |
