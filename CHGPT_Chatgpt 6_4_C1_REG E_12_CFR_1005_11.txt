# **Regulation E Error Resolution — User Stories and Gherkin Acceptance Criteria**

**Platform:** Card and ACH Disputes\
**Regulatory scope:** Electronic Fund Transfer Act (EFTA), Regulation E, 12 CFR § 1005.11\
**Role:** Senior Business Analyst, U.S. Banking\
**Objective:** Implement a compliant, auditable, end-to-end error-resolution workflow for consumer electronic fund transfer (EFT) disputes across debit card and ACH channels.

## **1. Business objectives**

The disputes platform must:

1. Capture and classify consumer-reported EFT errors.
2. Calculate and enforce regulatory investigation deadlines.
3. Provide provisional credit when required.
4. Conduct reasonable investigations and retain supporting evidence.
5. Communicate investigation outcomes within prescribed timeframes.
6. Provide consumers with investigation results and supporting documentation upon request.
7. Maintain a complete audit trail demonstrating regulatory compliance.
8. Support exceptions, extensions, and special timing rules without inadvertently extending deadlines.

## **2. Regulatory requirements and business rules**

The following requirements form the foundation of the backlog.

| Requirement                     | Regulatory rule                                                                                                                                                  | Platform implication                                                      |
| ------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Error notification              | Consumer generally must notify the institution within 60 days after transmittal of the periodic statement reflecting the alleged error.                          | Capture statement date, notification date, and timeliness determination.  |
| Acknowledgment                  | Written acknowledgment generally required within 10 business days if the institution does not resolve the error within that period.                              | Generate acknowledgment workflow and communication.                       |
| Investigation                   | Investigate promptly and determine whether an error occurred.                                                                                                    | Establish case ownership, investigation tasks, and evidence requirements. |
| Initial resolution              | Generally resolve within 10 business days.                                                                                                                       | Calculate initial investigation deadline.                                 |
| Provisional credit              | If unable to complete investigation within 10 business days, provisionally credit the account within the applicable timeframe, subject to regulatory exceptions. | Automate provisional credit eligibility and posting.                      |
| Extended investigation          | Generally up to 45 calendar days, subject to applicable exceptions.                                                                                              | Support extended investigation deadlines.                                 |
| Special exceptions              | Certain POS debit card, new account, and foreign-initiated EFT investigations may qualify for up to 90 calendar days, subject to applicable conditions.          | Apply special deadline rules based on transaction characteristics.        |
| Provisional credit availability | Generally make provisional funds available within the applicable timeframe, subject to permitted exceptions.                                                     | Track credit posting and availability separately.                         |
| Resolution notice               | Notify the consumer of the investigation results within three business days after completing the investigation.                                                  | Generate outcome notifications.                                           |
| Provisional credit reversal     | If no error occurred, provide written explanation before debiting provisional credit and comply with applicable notice and access requirements.                  | Implement controlled credit reversal.                                     |
| Documentation                   | Provide requested supporting documentation relied upon in the investigation.                                                                                     | Maintain retrievable evidence and document delivery.                      |

**Important design principle:** Regulatory deadlines must be driven by a centralized rules engine rather than hardcoded independently in card and ACH workflows.

The requirements below distinguish between regulatory obligations and recommended operational controls. Final implementation should be validated against the current regulation, applicable official interpretations, and bank legal/compliance policy.

## **3. Product scope and assumptions**

Before implementation, establish the following boundaries.

| Area            | Included                                                                                                                               | Excluded or separately governed                                                  |
| --------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Card            | Consumer debit card, ATM, and covered prepaid card EFTs                                                                                | Credit card billing disputes governed by Regulation Z                            |
| ACH             | Consumer ACH debit and credit transfers that qualify as EFTs                                                                           | Commercial/business accounts unless separately covered by bank policy            |
| Dispute types   | Unauthorized transfers, incorrect amounts, duplicate transfers, missing transfers, bookkeeping errors, and other qualifying EFT errors | Merchant dissatisfaction or service complaints that do not constitute EFT errors |
| Intake channels | Phone, branch, online banking, mobile, written correspondence                                                                          | Internal-only fraud alerts that have not yet become consumer error notifications |
| Resolution      | Investigation, provisional credit, final credit/debit, consumer notification                                                           | Network chargeback processes as a substitute for Regulation E compliance         |

**Important distinction:** A card dispute is not automatically a Regulation E dispute. Credit card disputes generally fall under Regulation Z, while consumer debit card transactions generally fall under Regulation E. The platform must route cases based on the underlying account, product, and transaction type.

The requirements below assume that the platform supports both oral and written consumer notifications and maintains separate regulatory and operational timestamps.

# **4. Functional user stories and Gherkin acceptance criteria**

## **Epic 1 — Dispute intake and error classification**

### **US-001: Capture consumer error notification**

**User story**

As a disputes operations user, I want to capture a consumer's notification of an EFT error, so that the bank can establish the regulatory notification date and initiate the error-resolution process.

**Business rules**

- The notification date is the date the consumer notifies the institution, not necessarily the date the case is entered into the platform.
- Both oral and written notifications must be supported.
- The platform must retain the original notification channel and date.
- The platform must distinguish the notification date from the transaction date and case creation date.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Capture consumer error notification

  Scenario: Consumer reports an error by telephone
    Given a consumer contacts the bank by telephone
    And identifies an electronic fund transfer
    When the disputes representative records the complaint
    Then the platform shall create a dispute case
    And capture the date of consumer notification
    And capture the notification channel as "Oral"
    And capture the affected account and transaction
    And record the representative identifier
    And timestamp the case creation event

  Scenario: Consumer submits a written error notification
    Given a consumer submits an error notification
    When the notification is received by the bank
    Then the platform shall capture the receipt date
    And classify the notification channel as "Written"
    And preserve the original notification
    And associate the notification with the dispute case

  Scenario: Case entry occurs after notification
    Given a consumer reported an error on November 2
    And the representative enters the case on November 4
    When the dispute is created
    Then the regulatory notification date shall be November 2
    And the case creation date shall be November 4
    And the two dates shall remain independently auditable
```

### **US-002: Identify and classify Regulation E errors**

**User story**

As a disputes analyst, I want the platform to classify reported transactions and error types, so that the correct regulatory investigation workflow is initiated.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Classify Regulation E errors

  Scenario Outline: Classify an eligible EFT error
    Given a consumer reports an electronic fund transfer error
    When the analyst selects "<error_type>"
    Then the platform shall classify the case as a Regulation E candidate
    And initiate the applicable investigation workflow
    And retain the selected error classification

    Examples:
      | error_type                         |
      | Unauthorized EFT                   |
      | Incorrect EFT amount               |
      | Incorrect date or posting          |
      | Duplicate EFT                      |
      | EFT not credited to account        |
      | Incorrect EFT to or from account   |
      | Bookkeeping error                  |
      | Other alleged EFT error            |

  Scenario: Credit card dispute is submitted
    Given a consumer reports a credit card billing dispute
    When the platform identifies the underlying product as a credit card
    Then the platform shall route the case to the Regulation Z workflow
    And shall not automatically apply Regulation E deadlines

  Scenario: Commercial ACH account is identified
    Given a consumer reports an ACH transaction error
    And the affected account is identified as a commercial account
    When the platform evaluates regulatory applicability
    Then the platform shall apply the configured commercial-account policy
    And shall not automatically classify the case as a consumer Regulation E case
```

### **US-003: Calculate the 60-day notification period**

**User story**

As a disputes analyst, I want the platform to determine whether a consumer reported an error within the applicable notification period, so that the bank can evaluate its regulatory obligations.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Determine notification timeliness

  Scenario: Notification received within 60 days
    Given a periodic statement was transmitted on October 1
    And the consumer reports an error on November 15
    When the platform evaluates notification timeliness
    Then the notification shall be classified as within 60 days
    And the notification shall be eligible for normal investigation

  Scenario: Notification received after 60 days
    Given a periodic statement was transmitted on October 1
    And the consumer reports an error after the applicable 60-day period
    When the platform evaluates notification timeliness
    Then the notification shall be flagged as potentially untimely
    And the calculation shall be retained in the audit history
    And the case shall be routed for policy review

  Scenario: Multiple periodic statements exist
    Given the account has multiple periodic statements
    When the consumer reports an error
    Then the platform shall identify the first applicable statement
    And calculate the notification period using that statement's transmittal date
    And retain the statement reference used in the calculation

  Scenario: Notification is potentially untimely
    Given the platform determines that the notification is outside the applicable period
    When the case is reviewed
    Then the platform shall not automatically reject the consumer's claim
    And shall support review of the consumer's circumstances
    And shall record the approval or rejection rationale
```

**Implementation note:** The 60-day rule should be evaluated using the statement that first reflects the alleged error, with the actual transmittal date. Late reporting must not automatically result in case rejection; the statutory liability provisions and applicable exceptions require separate evaluation.

## **Epic 2 — Regulatory deadline management**

### **US-004: Calculate the initial investigation deadline**

**User story**

As a disputes operations manager, I want the platform to automatically calculate the initial investigation deadline, so that all disputes are resolved within applicable regulatory timeframes.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Calculate initial investigation deadline

  Scenario: Standard Regulation E dispute
    Given a valid consumer error notification is received
    And the transaction is not subject to a special timing exception
    When the platform calculates the investigation deadline
    Then the initial investigation deadline shall be 10 business days
    And the deadline shall be calculated from the notification date
    And the deadline shall be stored on the dispute case

  Scenario: New account investigation
    Given a consumer reports an error involving an EFT
    And the EFT occurred within 30 days after the first deposit
    When the platform evaluates the applicable timing rules
    Then the platform shall identify the new-account exception
    And calculate the applicable 20-business-day initial period
    And flag the case for extended investigation handling

  Scenario: Special 90-calendar-day investigation period
    Given a dispute involves an eligible point-of-sale debit card transaction
    Or a qualifying foreign-initiated EFT
    Or an eligible new-account transaction
    When the platform calculates the investigation deadline
    Then the platform shall identify the applicable special exception
    And calculate the applicable 90-calendar-day period
    And retain the reason for the exception

  Scenario: Business day calculation
    Given a dispute has a regulatory deadline
    When the platform calculates the deadline
    Then it shall use the institution's configured Regulation E business-day calendar
    And exclude days that are not business days under the applicable definition
    And retain the calendar version used for calculation
```

### **US-005: Manage regulatory deadlines and escalations**

**User story**

As a disputes manager, I want automated deadline monitoring and escalation, so that approaching or missed regulatory deadlines are identified before they result in compliance violations.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Monitor regulatory deadlines

  Scenario: Deadline approaching
    Given a dispute has an outstanding investigation deadline
    And the deadline is approaching
    When the configured escalation threshold is reached
    Then the platform shall notify the assigned investigator
    And notify the responsible team lead
    And record the escalation event

  Scenario: Regulatory deadline missed
    Given a dispute has exceeded its regulatory deadline
    And the investigation remains incomplete
    When the deadline monitoring process executes
    Then the platform shall flag the case as overdue
    And generate a compliance exception
    And notify the disputes manager
    And preserve the original deadline

  Scenario: Case reassignment
    Given a dispute is reassigned to another investigator
    When the reassignment is completed
    Then the original regulatory deadline shall remain unchanged
    And the new investigator shall receive the outstanding tasks
    And the reassignment shall be recorded in the audit history

  Scenario: Deadline recalculation
    Given a dispute has an established regulatory deadline
    When a case attribute is changed
    Then the platform shall recalculate the deadline only when the change affects an applicable regulatory rule
    And preserve the previous deadline and calculation history
```

### **US-006: Generate written acknowledgment**

**User story**

As a disputes operations user, I want the platform to generate a written acknowledgment when required, so that the bank satisfies Regulation E acknowledgment requirements.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Written acknowledgment

  Scenario: Investigation cannot be completed within 10 business days
    Given a consumer has reported an EFT error
    And the bank cannot complete the investigation within 10 business days
    When the acknowledgment requirement is evaluated
    Then the platform shall generate a written acknowledgment
    And schedule delivery within the applicable 10-business-day period
    And record the generation and delivery dates

  Scenario: Investigation completed within 10 business days
    Given a consumer has reported an EFT error
    When the bank completes the investigation within 10 business days
    Then the platform shall not require a separate acknowledgment solely under the extended-investigation provision

  Scenario: Consumer notification was oral
    Given the consumer initially reported an error orally
    And written confirmation is required under the applicable procedure
    When the platform processes the oral notification
    Then it shall generate the required written-confirmation instructions
    And track the confirmation requirement separately
    And prevent unauthorized use of the confirmation requirement to delay required regulatory action
```

## **Epic 3 — Provisional credit management**

### **US-007: Determine provisional credit eligibility**

**User story**

As a disputes analyst, I want the platform to determine whether provisional credit is required, so that consumers receive access to disputed funds when required by Regulation E.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Provisional credit eligibility

  Scenario: Investigation cannot be completed within the initial period
    Given a consumer reports an eligible EFT error
    And the investigation cannot be completed within the applicable initial period
    When the platform evaluates provisional credit eligibility
    Then the platform shall determine whether provisional credit is required
    And calculate the applicable provisional credit deadline
    And create a provisional credit task

  Scenario: Investigation completed within initial period
    Given a consumer reports an EFT error
    And the investigation is completed within the applicable initial period
    When the platform determines that no error occurred
    Then provisional credit shall not be required solely because a dispute was opened

  Scenario: Oral notification and written confirmation
    Given a consumer initially reports an error orally
    And the bank has provided the required written confirmation instructions
    And the consumer has not provided written confirmation within the applicable 10-business-day period
    When the platform evaluates provisional credit eligibility
    Then it shall evaluate the permitted exception under Regulation E
    And record the facts supporting the determination
    And route the decision for appropriate controls
```

### **US-008: Post provisional credit**

**User story**

As a disputes operations user, I want the platform to initiate provisional credit automatically when required, so that the consumer receives the disputed funds within regulatory timeframes.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Provisional credit posting

  Scenario: Standard provisional credit
    Given a dispute qualifies for provisional credit
    When the applicable provisional credit deadline is reached
    Then the platform shall initiate provisional credit posting
    And use the amount of the alleged error as the provisional credit amount
    And associate the credit transaction with the dispute case
    And record the posting date and amount

  Scenario: New-account provisional credit
    Given the dispute qualifies for the new-account timing exception
    When provisional credit is required
    Then the platform shall calculate the applicable 20-business-day provisional credit deadline
    And initiate provisional credit within the required timeframe

  Scenario: Provisional credit cannot be posted
    Given provisional credit is required
    When the posting interface returns an error
    Then the platform shall mark the posting as failed
    And generate an immediate operational exception
    And retry according to approved posting controls
    And escalate before the regulatory deadline is missed

  Scenario: Provisional credit successfully posted
    Given the posting system confirms a successful credit
    When the platform receives the posting confirmation
    Then the dispute status shall update to "Provisional Credit Posted"
    And the transaction reference shall be stored
    And the consumer notification workflow shall be initiated
```

### **US-009: Make provisional credit available and notify the consumer**

**User story**

As a consumer, I want access to provisional credit while my dispute is being investigated, so that I am not unnecessarily deprived of the disputed funds.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Provisional credit availability

  Scenario: Provisional credit successfully posted
    Given provisional credit has been granted
    When the bank confirms the credit
    Then the platform shall initiate the applicable consumer notification
    And notify the consumer within two business days after granting provisional credit
    And ensure the funds are available as required by Regulation E

  Scenario: Provisional credit posting is pending
    Given provisional credit is required
    And the posting transaction is pending
    When the consumer notification workflow executes
    Then the platform shall not represent the funds as available until availability is confirmed
    And shall generate an operational exception if the required availability deadline is at risk
```

## **Epic 4 — Investigation and evidence management**

### **US-010: Assign and manage investigation tasks**

**User story**

As a disputes investigator, I want the platform to generate investigation tasks based on the dispute type, so that the bank performs a consistent and reasonable investigation.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Investigation workflow

  Scenario: ACH dispute investigation
    Given a dispute is classified as an ACH error
    When the investigation workflow is initiated
    Then the platform shall create applicable ACH investigation tasks
    And identify the relevant transaction details
    And request available supporting ACH evidence
    And assign an investigator

  Scenario: Debit card dispute investigation
    Given a dispute is classified as a debit card error
    When the investigation workflow is initiated
    Then the platform shall create applicable card investigation tasks
    And identify the transaction and authorization details
    And request available supporting card evidence
    And assign an investigator

  Scenario: Investigator requests additional information
    Given an investigation is in progress
    When the investigator identifies missing evidence
    Then the platform shall create an evidence request task
    And record the evidence requested
    And track the request status
    And retain the regulatory deadline
```

### **US-011: Capture investigation evidence and decision rationale**

**User story**

As a compliance officer, I want all evidence and investigation decisions retained in the dispute record, so that the bank can demonstrate that it performed a reasonable investigation.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Evidence management

  Scenario: Evidence is attached to a dispute
    Given an investigation is in progress
    When an investigator uploads supporting evidence
    Then the platform shall associate the evidence with the dispute
    And record the evidence source
    And record the upload timestamp
    And record the user who uploaded the evidence

  Scenario: Investigator completes investigation
    Given all required investigation activities are complete
    When the investigator records the outcome
    Then the platform shall require an investigation rationale
    And capture the evidence relied upon
    And capture the determination date
    And prevent closure without a recorded outcome

  Scenario: Evidence is modified or removed
    Given evidence is associated with a dispute
    When an authorized user modifies its status or removes it
    Then the platform shall record the action
    And retain the applicable audit history
    And preserve evidence retention requirements
```

### **US-012: Determine final investigation outcome**

**User story**

As a disputes investigator, I want to record whether an EFT error occurred, so that the platform can execute the appropriate financial and notification actions.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Investigation determination

  Scenario: Error confirmed
    Given the investigation establishes that an EFT error occurred
    When the investigator records the final determination
    Then the platform shall classify the outcome as "Error Confirmed"
    And calculate the required correction amount
    And initiate the appropriate account correction
    And trigger the consumer notification workflow

  Scenario: No error identified
    Given the investigation establishes that no EFT error occurred
    When the investigator records the final determination
    Then the platform shall classify the outcome as "No Error"
    And require an explanation of the investigation findings
    And initiate the consumer notification workflow
    And evaluate whether provisional credit reversal is applicable

  Scenario: Different or partial error identified
    Given the investigation establishes an error different from the one alleged
    Or establishes an error involving a different amount
    When the investigator records the determination
    Then the platform shall capture the actual error
    And calculate the appropriate correction
    And generate the required written explanation
    And retain the original allegation separately from the final determination
```

## **Epic 5 — Final resolution, account correction, and consumer notifications**

### **US-013: Complete account correction when an error is confirmed**

**User story**

As a disputes operations user, I want the platform to correct confirmed EFT errors promptly, so that the consumer's account reflects the appropriate financial adjustment.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Correct confirmed EFT errors

  Scenario: Full error confirmed
    Given the investigation establishes that the alleged EFT error occurred
    When the investigator records the determination
    Then the platform shall calculate the amount required to correct the error
    And initiate the correction within one business day after determining the error
    And record the correction transaction reference
    And update the case status to "Resolved - Error Confirmed"

  Scenario: Partial error confirmed
    Given the investigation establishes that only part of the alleged amount is incorrect
    When the investigator records the determination
    Then the platform shall calculate the confirmed error amount
    And initiate the appropriate account correction
    And generate an explanation of the partial determination
    And retain the calculation supporting the adjustment

  Scenario: Correction posting fails
    Given an error has been confirmed
    When the account correction transaction fails
    Then the platform shall retain the confirmed investigation outcome
    And generate a financial posting exception
    And escalate the failed correction
    And continue monitoring the correction until successfully posted

  Scenario: Correction successfully posted
    Given the correction has been initiated
    When the core banking system confirms posting
    Then the platform shall capture the posting date
    And capture the correction amount
    And associate the posting reference with the dispute
```

### **US-014: Generate final resolution notification**

**User story**

As a disputes operations user, I want the platform to automatically generate the required final investigation notification, so that consumers receive timely and understandable resolution information.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Final resolution notification

  Scenario: Error confirmed
    Given the investigation determines that an error occurred
    When the investigation is completed
    Then the platform shall generate a notification describing the error
    And include the amount of the correction
    And include the correction posting information when available
    And initiate delivery within three business days after completing the investigation

  Scenario: No error identified
    Given the investigation determines that no error occurred
    When the investigation is completed
    Then the platform shall generate a written explanation
    And describe the investigation findings
    And explain why the bank determined that no error occurred
    And notify the consumer within three business days after completing the investigation

  Scenario: Different error identified
    Given the investigation identifies an error different from the alleged error
    When the final notification is generated
    Then the notification shall describe the actual error identified
    And explain the determination
    And identify the applicable correction

  Scenario: Notification delivery failure
    Given a final notification has been generated
    When the delivery provider returns a failure
    Then the platform shall record the delivery failure
    And initiate the approved notification recovery process
    And escalate unresolved delivery failures
    And preserve the original notification deadline
```

### **US-015: Reverse provisional credit when no error is found**

**User story**

As a disputes operations user, I want the platform to manage provisional credit reversals in accordance with Regulation E, so that funds are recovered appropriately without violating consumer notification and access requirements.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Provisional credit reversal

  Scenario: No error determined and provisional credit exists
    Given the investigation concludes that no error occurred
    And provisional credit was previously granted
    When the final determination is approved
    Then the platform shall generate a written explanation
    And initiate the required notice before debiting provisional credit
    And calculate the earliest permitted debit date
    And retain the notice delivery evidence

  Scenario: Five-business-day consumer access period
    Given the consumer has been notified that provisional credit will be debited
    When the reversal process is scheduled
    Then the platform shall preserve the consumer's opportunity to withdraw the funds without charge
    And shall not debit the provisional credit before the applicable five-business-day period expires

  Scenario: Reversal is executed
    Given the applicable consumer access period has expired
    And all required reversal conditions have been satisfied
    When the reversal is executed
    Then the platform shall debit the provisional credit amount
    And capture the debit posting reference
    And record the debit date and amount
    And update the case status to "Resolved - No Error"

  Scenario: Reversal posting fails
    Given a provisional credit reversal is due
    When the core banking system rejects the debit
    Then the platform shall record the failed reversal
    And generate an operational exception
    And prevent duplicate reversal attempts
    And retain the investigation outcome independently of the posting status
```

### **US-016: Provide supporting documentation upon consumer request**

**User story**

As a consumer, I want to obtain the documents the bank relied upon during its investigation, so that I can understand and review the resolution of my dispute.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Consumer supporting documentation

  Scenario: Consumer requests investigation documentation
    Given an investigation has been completed
    And the consumer requests copies of documents relied upon
    When the request is recorded
    Then the platform shall create a documentation fulfillment task
    And identify the evidence relied upon
    And make the relevant documentation retrievable
    And record the request date and fulfillment date

  Scenario: Documentation is provided
    Given a consumer has requested supporting documentation
    When the documents are delivered

  Scenario: Documentation is unavailable

    And notify the responsible operations team
    And track the task until resolved
```

## **Epic 6 — Audit, compliance, and management reporting**

### **US-017: Maintain a complete regulatory audit trail**

**User story**

As a compliance officer, I want a complete, immutable history of all material dispute events, so that the bank can demonstrate compliance with Regulation E.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Regulatory audit trail

  Scenario: Dispute event is recorded
    Given a dispute exists
    When a user or system performs a material action
    Then the platform shall record the event
    And capture the event timestamp
    And capture the acting user or system
    And capture the previous and new values when applicable

  Scenario: Regulatory deadline is calculated
    Given a dispute is subject to Regulation E
    When a regulatory deadline is calculated
    Then the platform shall record the applicable rule
    And record the calculation inputs
    And record the resulting deadline
    And retain the calculation history

  Scenario: Case is closed
    Given all applicable resolution activities are complete
    When the dispute is closed
    Then the platform shall preserve the full investigation history
    And preserve all consumer notifications
    And preserve financial posting references
    And retain the record according to the bank's approved retention schedule

  Scenario: User attempts to modify a regulatory event
    Given a regulatory event has been recorded
    When a user attempts to modify the event
    Then the platform shall prevent unauthorized modification
    And retain the original event
    And record authorized corrections as separate audit events
```

  Scenario: Drill down into an exception

# **5. Non-functional and technical user stories**

These requirements ensure that the platform is operationally reliable and that compliance controls cannot be bypassed by technical failures.

### **US-020: Centralized Regulation E rules engine**

**User story**

As a product owner, I want all Regulation E deadline and eligibility calculations centralized, so that card and ACH workflows apply consistent regulatory rules.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Centralized Regulation E rules engine

  Scenario: Card dispute invokes regulatory calculation
    Given a debit card dispute qualifies for Regulation E
    When the dispute is submitted for deadline calculation
    Then the platform shall invoke the centralized rules engine
    And return the applicable deadlines
    And return the applicable regulatory exception

  Scenario: ACH dispute invokes regulatory calculation
    Given an ACH dispute qualifies for Regulation E
    When the dispute is submitted for deadline calculation
    Then the platform shall invoke the same centralized rules engine
    And apply the applicable ACH transaction attributes
    And return the applicable deadlines

  Scenario: Regulatory rule changes
    Given an approved regulatory rule change is configured
    When the new rule becomes effective
    Then the rules engine shall apply the new rule to eligible cases
    And retain the previous rule version
    And preserve existing case calculation history

  Scenario: Rule calculation cannot be completed
    Given a dispute requires a regulatory deadline calculation
    When the rules engine is unavailable
    Then the platform shall not silently assign an assumed deadline
    And generate a system exception
    And initiate the approved fallback procedure
```

### **US-021: Ensure idempotent financial transactions**

**User story**

As a payments operations manager, I want provisional credits, corrections, and reversals to be processed without duplicate postings, so that consumer accounts are not incorrectly credited or debited.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Financial transaction idempotency

  Scenario: Duplicate provisional credit request
    Given a provisional credit has already been successfully posted
    When the platform receives a duplicate posting request
    Then the platform shall identify the existing transaction
    And prevent duplicate credit posting
    And associate the existing posting reference with the dispute

  Scenario: Duplicate reversal request
    Given a provisional credit reversal has already been posted
    When another reversal request is submitted
    Then the platform shall prevent duplicate debit posting
    And return the existing transaction status

  Scenario: Core banking interface is unavailable
    Given a required financial adjustment is initiated
    When the core banking interface becomes unavailable
    Then the platform shall retain the pending transaction
    And retry according to approved recovery controls
    And reconcile the transaction against the core banking ledger
```

### **US-022: Support role-based access and segregation of duties**

**User story**

As an information security officer, I want access to dispute actions controlled by user roles, so that sensitive consumer information and financial adjustments are appropriately protected.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Role-based dispute access

  Scenario: Authorized investigator accesses dispute
    Given a user has an authorized investigator role
    When the user opens an assigned dispute
    Then the platform shall grant access according to role permissions

  Scenario: Unauthorized user attempts financial adjustment
    Given a user does not have financial adjustment permissions
    When the user attempts to initiate a provisional credit or reversal
    Then the platform shall reject the action
    And record the unauthorized attempt

  Scenario: Supervisor approves own restricted action
    Given segregation-of-duties controls prohibit self-approval
    When an investigator attempts to approve their own restricted determination
    Then the platform shall prevent the approval
    And require an authorized independent approver
```

### **US-023: Reconcile dispute transactions with core banking**

**User story**

As a reconciliation analyst, I want the disputes platform to reconcile financial adjustments against core banking transactions, so that all dispute-related credits and debits are accounted for.

**Acceptance criteria**

```
Feature: Dispute financial reconciliation

  Scenario: Successful reconciliation
    Given the dispute platform has initiated a financial adjustment
    And the core banking system has posted the adjustment
    When reconciliation executes
    Then the platform shall match the dispute reference
    And match the amount and posting date
    And mark the transaction as reconciled

  Scenario: Amount mismatch
    Given a dispute adjustment exists
    When the core banking posting amount differs from the expected amount
    Then the platform shall create a reconciliation exception
    And prevent the exception from being silently closed

  Scenario: Missing posting
    Given the disputes platform records a successful posting request
    When no corresponding core banking posting is identified
    Then the platform shall flag the transaction as unreconciled
    And assign it for investigation
```

# **6. Recommended dispute lifecycle and status model**

The following is a recommended logical state model. The implementation should keep investigation status, regulatory status, and financial posting status separate rather than attempting to represent every combination through one case status.

**Dispute lifecycle**

1. **Received**

   Consumer notification captured; regulatory notification date established.
2. **Classified**

   Product, transaction, error type, and Regulation E applicability determined.
3. **Investigation in progress**

   Investigator assigned; deadlines calculated; evidence gathered.
4. **Acknowledgment required / sent**

   Written acknowledgment and oral-notification confirmation controls tracked.
5. **Provisional credit pending / posted**

   Eligibility, posting, and consumer notification tracked independently.
6. **Investigation completed**

   Error confirmed, no error, or different/partial error established.
7. **Financial correction / reversal pending**

   Account adjustment processed and reconciled.
8. **Consumer notification completed**

   Final investigation outcome and applicable explanation delivered.
9. **Resolved / closed**

   Required regulatory, financial, and operational activities completed.

**Recommended independent status dimensions**

| Dimension            | Example statuses                                          |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Investigation        | Open, In Progress, Pending Evidence, Completed            |
| Regulatory           | Within SLA, Approaching Deadline, Overdue, Exception      |
| Provisional credit   | Not Required, Eligible, Pending, Posted, Failed, Reversed |
| Financial correction | Not Required, Pending, Posted, Failed, Reconciled         |
| Notification         | Pending, Generated, Sent, Delivered, Failed               |
| Documentation        | Not Requested, Requested, Fulfilled                       |

This separation is important. For example, an investigation can be completed while the account correction is still pending. Closing the investigation should not incorrectly imply that the financial obligation has been fulfilled.

# **7. Regulatory deadline matrix for development and QA**

This is the key reference table I would give the development team for configuring the rules engine.

| Regulatory event                   | Standard timing            | Exception / condition                                                                                     |
| ---------------------------------- | -------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Consumer notification              | Generally 60 calendar days | From transmittal of the periodic statement first reflecting the alleged error                             |
| Initial investigation              | 10 business days           | 20 business days for qualifying new accounts                                                              |
| Written acknowledgment             | 10 business days           | Required when investigation cannot be completed within the applicable initial period                      |
| Provisional credit                 | Within 10 business days    | 20 business days for qualifying new accounts                                                              |
| Final investigation                | 45 calendar days           | 90 calendar days for qualifying POS debit card, foreign-initiated EFT, and new-account cases              |
| Error correction                   | 1 business day             | After determining that an error occurred                                                                  |
| Final outcome notice               | 3 business days            | After completing the investigation                                                                        |
| Provisional credit notice          | 2 business days            | After granting provisional credit                                                                         |
| Provisional credit reversal access | 5 business days            | Consumer must have the required opportunity to withdraw funds without charge after notice of the reversal |

**Critical interpretation for engineering:**

- The 45/90-day extended investigation period does not mean the bank can wait 45/90 days to provide provisional credit.
- The provisional credit deadline remains tied to the applicable 10/20-business-day initial period, subject to permitted exceptions.
- The 90-day exception is not a blanket extension for all card or ACH disputes.
- New-account status must be determined using the regulatory definition and transaction timing, not simply the account's current age.
- A business day must be determined using the Regulation E definition and the institution's applicable operating calendar, not simply by subtracting weekends.
- Written confirmation requirements for oral notifications must be separately controlled.

# **8. Key regulatory controls and design decisions**

I recommend explicitly addressing the following items during solution design. They are common sources of defects in Regulation E implementations.

| Control                | Required design decision                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ---------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Oral notification      | Support oral claims without making written submission a general prerequisite for investigation.                                                                                          |
| Written confirmation   | Track the requirement to provide written confirmation instructions following oral notification. Apply the specific provisional-credit exception only where its conditions are satisfied. |
| New account            | Determine whether the EFT occurred within 30 days after the first deposit, as required for the applicable timing exception.                                                              |
| Special 90-day cases   | Identify qualifying POS debit card transactions, foreign-initiated EFTs, and new-account EFTs.                                                                                           |
| Partial credit         | Support cases where the confirmed error amount differs from the amount alleged.                                                                                                          |
| Multiple transactions  | Support one claim involving multiple EFTs, with transaction-level evidence and deadline calculations where necessary.                                                                    |
| Statement availability | Capture statement transmittal date and evidence supporting the 60-day calculation.                                                                                                       |
| Provisional credit     | Distinguish credit initiation, posting, and consumer availability.                                                                                                                       |
| No-error outcome       | Require written explanation and controlled provisional credit reversal.                                                                                                                  |
| Consumer access        | Preserve the applicable five-business-day withdrawal opportunity without charge after notice of a provisional credit debit.                                                              |
| Evidence requests      | Support consumer requests for copies of documents relied upon in the investigation.                                                                                                      |
| Exceptions             | Maintain an auditable exception process without automatically resetting regulatory deadlines.                                                                                            |

## **13. Final business analyst recommendation**

For a bank implementing this across card and ACH, I would make three architectural decisions mandatory before development begins:

**1. Build one Regulation E compliance engine.**

Card and ACH should share the same regulatory deadline, eligibility, notification, and audit services. Channel-specific investigation procedures should remain separate.

**2. Separate investigation resolution from financial resolution.**

A case can have a completed investigation but an outstanding financial correction, provisional credit reversal, or notification. These must have separate completion criteria.

**3. Treat regulatory deadlines as non-negotiable control dates.**

Reassignment, missing evidence, system outages, approval delays, and internal SLAs must not silently reset or extend regulatory deadlines.

**One important legal-review caveat:** This backlog is a functional requirements baseline, not a legal opinion. Before production sign-off, Bank Legal and Compliance should validate each timing rule, exception, and notification template against the current text of § 1005.11 and its official commentary. Particular attention should be given to the interaction between the 10/20-business-day provisional-credit requirements and the 45/90-calendar-day investigation periods.
